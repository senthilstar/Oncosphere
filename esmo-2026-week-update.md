## How the ten trials fit together

**Presidential Symposium I** spans adjuvant, perioperative and maintenance treatment:

- **INTerpath-001:** Following complete resection of stage IIB–IV cutaneous melanoma, adjuvant intismeran autogene plus pembrolizumab yielded estimated recurrence-free survival (RFS) at 24 months of **82.2% versus 70.0%** with placebo plus pembrolizumab. <a class="cite" href="https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2609762">[NEJM 2026]</a> The reported overall RFS HR was **0.58** (95% CI 0.45–0.75); the journal analysis also reported time-dependent effects, with HR 0.99 (95% CI 0.66–1.49) before six months and 0.42 (95% CI 0.30–0.58) thereafter. <a class="cite" href="https://www.merck.com/news/merck-and-moderna-will-present-results-from-landmark-phase-3-interpath-001-trial-for-intismeran-autogene-plus-keytruda-pembrolizumab-at-esmo-2026-presidential-symposium/">[Merck, sponsor-reported 2026]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2609762">[NEJM 2026]</a> Overall survival (OS) data remain immature. <a class="cite" href="https://www.merck.com/news/merck-and-moderna-will-present-results-from-landmark-phase-3-interpath-001-trial-for-intismeran-autogene-plus-keytruda-pembrolizumab-at-esmo-2026-presidential-symposium/">[Merck, sponsor-reported 2026]</a>
- **VOLGA:** In muscle-invasive bladder cancer when cisplatin was unsuitable or declined, perioperative durvalumab with neoadjuvant enfortumab vedotin was compared with cystectomy followed by observation or permitted adjuvant therapy. Event-free survival (EFS) HR was **0.51** (95% CI 0.38–0.67), OS HR **0.58** (95% CI 0.40–0.83), and estimated EFS at 24 months 63.5% versus 45.1%; local pathological complete response was 45% versus 10%. <a class="cite" href="https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2608323">[NEJM 2026]</a> The US label includes neoadjuvant durvalumab with enfortumab vedotin followed by adjuvant durvalumab after radical cystectomy for adults with muscle-invasive bladder cancer who are ineligible for or refuse cisplatin-based chemotherapy. <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/761069s059lbl.pdf">[FDA 2026]</a>
- **DeLLphi-305:** After initial chemoimmunotherapy without progression in extensive-stage small-cell lung cancer, tarlatamab plus durvalumab maintenance produced median OS of **21.1 versus 12.7 months** with durvalumab alone (HR **0.42**, 95% CI 0.32–0.54); progression-free survival (PFS) HR was 0.34. <a class="cite" href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42929702/">[NEJM 2026]</a> Survival was measured from maintenance randomisation, and these are interim results. <a class="cite" href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42929702/">[NEJM 2026]</a>
- **DASL-HiCaP:** For prostate cancer at high risk of recurrence, adding darolutamide to androgen deprivation and definitive or postoperative radiotherapy improved conventional-imaging metastasis-free survival at interim analysis (HR **0.42**, 95% CI 0.27–0.66; four-year estimates 95% versus 88%). <a class="cite" href="https://www.bayer.com/en-us/united-states/news-and-stories/2026/metastasis-nubeqa">[Bayer, sponsor-reported 2026]</a> OS follow-up continues (HR 0.88, 95% CI 0.49–1.59). <a class="cite" href="https://www.bayer.com/en-us/united-states/news-and-stories/2026/metastasis-nubeqa">[Bayer, sponsor-reported 2026]</a>

**Presidential Symposium II** compared investigational regimens with active treatment:

- **Krascendo 1:** In previously treated advanced KRAS G12C-mutated non-small-cell lung cancer (NSCLC), divarasib improved median PFS versus sotorasib or adagrasib (**9.8 versus 5.7 months**; HR 0.58, 95% CI 0.44–0.76) and median OS (**17.7 versus 12.0 months**; HR 0.56, 95% CI 0.41–0.78). <a class="cite" href="https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2605703">[NEJM 2026]</a> Most patients in the control group received sotorasib. <a class="cite" href="https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2605703">[NEJM 2026]</a>
- **SAFFRON:** After progression on osimertinib in EGFR-mutant advanced NSCLC, eligibility required MET staining intensity 3+ in at least 90% of tumour cells, MET amplification of at least 10 gene copies by FISH, or both. <a class="cite" href="https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2611335">[NEJM 2026]</a> Osimertinib plus savolitinib improved median PFS versus platinum–pemetrexed (**7.6 versus 5.4 months**; HR 0.55, 95% CI 0.42–0.72); final median OS was **17.8 versus 12.9 months** (HR 0.68, 95% CI 0.50–0.91). <a class="cite" href="https://www1.hkexnews.hk/listedco/listconews/sehk/2026/1026/2026102600045.pdf">[HUTCHMED, sponsor-reported 2026]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2611335">[NEJM 2026]</a>
- **CLARITY-Gastric01:** In previously treated advanced gastric, gastro-oesophageal junction or oesophageal adenocarcinoma, the trial defined CLDN18.2 positivity as staining of at least 25% of tumour cells at any intensity. <a class="cite" href="https://www.astrazeneca-us.com/media/press-releases/2026/Sonesitatug-vedotin-reduced-the-risk-of-death-by-42-percent-in-2nd-and-later-line-CLDN18-2-positive-advanced-gastric-GEJ-cancers-in-CLARITY-Gastric01.html">[AstraZeneca, sponsor-reported 2026]</a> Sonesitatug vedotin improved the primary OS endpoint versus investigator’s choice after at least two prior lines (**10.0 versus 5.0 months**; HR 0.47, 95% CI 0.36–0.62). <a class="cite" href="https://www.astrazeneca-us.com/media/press-releases/2026/Sonesitatug-vedotin-reduced-the-risk-of-death-by-42-percent-in-2nd-and-later-line-CLDN18-2-positive-advanced-gastric-GEJ-cancers-in-CLARITY-Gastric01.html">[AstraZeneca, sponsor-reported 2026]</a> Overall-population OS favoured the antibody–drug conjugate (10.6 versus 6.4 months; HR 0.58, 95% CI 0.47–0.73), but the other primary endpoint, centrally assessed PFS in that population, did not meet its significance threshold (HR 0.77, 95% CI 0.62–0.96). <a class="cite" href="https://www.astrazeneca-us.com/media/press-releases/2026/Sonesitatug-vedotin-reduced-the-risk-of-death-by-42-percent-in-2nd-and-later-line-CLDN18-2-positive-advanced-gastric-GEJ-cancers-in-CLARITY-Gastric01.html">[AstraZeneca, sponsor-reported 2026]</a>
- **HARMONi-GI1:** In previously untreated advanced biliary tract cancer in China, ivonescimab plus gemcitabine–cisplatin improved interim median OS versus durvalumab with the same chemotherapy (**14.9 versus 12.1 months**; HR 0.75, 95% CI 0.61–0.92). <a class="cite" href="https://akesobio.com/en/media/akeso-news/20261025/">[Akeso, sponsor-reported 2026]</a>

**Outside the Presidential sessions**, the [HEALER and OptiTROP-Lung06 note]({{ '/esmo-2026-china-phase-3-healer-optitrop-lung06/' | relative_url }}) covers two trials conducted in China:

- **HEALER:** In first-line HER2-positive advanced breast cancer, anbenitamab plus albumin-bound docetaxel was compared with trastuzumab, pertuzumab and docetaxel. Estimated PFS at 24 months was **58.5% versus 34.4%** (HR 0.46, 95% CI 0.37–0.56); grade 3 or higher adverse events occurred in 45.9% versus 34.4%. <a class="cite" href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42929681/">[NEJM 2026]</a> Both the HER2-directed treatment and the taxane formulation differed between arms; OS follow-up remains important. <a class="cite" href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42929681/">[NEJM 2026]</a>
- **OptiTROP-Lung06:** In untreated advanced nonsquamous NSCLC without targetable alterations and with PD-L1 tumour proportion score &lt;1%, sacituzumab tirumotecan plus pembrolizumab improved median PFS versus platinum–pemetrexed plus pembrolizumab (**15.0 versus 5.7 months**; HR 0.49, 95% CI 0.36–0.65). <a class="cite" href="https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2611280">[NEJM 2026]</a> OS was immature (HR 0.58, 95% CI 0.38–0.88). <a class="cite" href="https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2611280">[NEJM 2026]</a>

## What the forest plot and quiz add

The [forest plot]({{ '/esmo-2026-forest-plot/' | relative_url }}) places headline endpoints and OS estimates in separate panels. Grey rows identify results that were not significant or not formally tested. Use it to recall the estimates, not to compare treatments across trials: populations, endpoints and follow-up differ.

The [quiz]({{ '/esmo-2026-quiz/' | relative_url }}) tests eligibility, endpoints and interpretation; explanations link back to the trial pages. The linked summaries and graphic reflect their own publication dates; check the reports above for subsequent evidence.
