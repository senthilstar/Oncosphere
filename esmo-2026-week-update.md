## How the ten trials fit together

**Presidential Symposium I** (LBA1–4) was about **curative-intent and first-line maintenance wins** with mixed maturity on overall survival:

- **INTerpath-001** (adjuvant resected stage IIB–IV melanoma): individualised mRNA neoantigen therapy (intismeran autogene) + pembrolizumab improved RFS — HR **0.58** (95% CI 0.45–0.75); 24-month RFS 82.2% vs 70.0%. OS immature (HR 0.77, not significant). Benefit appeared after the six-month immunisation course. Source page: Presidential Symposium I (Merck/Moderna release; accepted by *NEJM*).
- **VOLGA** (cisplatin-ineligible/refusing MIBC): perioperative durvalumab + neoadjuvant enfortumab vedotin cut event risk — EFS HR **0.51** (0.38–0.67); OS HR **0.58** (0.40–0.83). 24-month EFS 63.5% vs 45.1%; pCR 45% vs 10%. Published in *NEJM* with ESMO (Powles et al.).
- **DeLLphi-305** (ES-SCLC first-line maintenance): tarlatamab + durvalumab vs durvalumab alone — median OS **21.1 vs 12.7 months**, OS HR **0.42** (0.32–0.54); PFS HR 0.34. Published in *NEJM* (Pérol et al.).
- **DASL-HiCaP** (very high-risk localised prostate, with RT + ADT): darolutamide improved MFS — HR **0.42** (0.27–0.66); 4-year MFS 95% vs 88%. OS not significant (HR 0.88). Abstract/sponsor data so far.

**Presidential Symposium II** (LBA5–8) was different: **every trial beat an active control on overall survival**, not placebo:

- **Krascendo 1** (pretreated KRAS G12C NSCLC): divarasib vs sotorasib/adagrasib — PFS **9.8 vs 5.7 months** (HR 0.58); OS **17.7 vs 12.0 months** (HR 0.56). *NEJM* simultaneous (Skoulidis et al.).
- **SAFFRON** (EGFR-mutant NSCLC progressing on osimertinib with MET overexpression/amplification on post-progression biopsy): osimertinib + savolitinib vs platinum–pemetrexed — PFS **7.6 vs 5.4 months** (HR 0.55); final OS **17.8 vs 12.9 months** (HR 0.68). *NEJM*.
- **CLARITY-Gastric 01** (CLDN18.2+ gastro-oesophageal, ≥1 prior line): sonesitatug vedotin vs investigator’s choice — dual-primary ITT PFS not met (HR 0.77); OS benefit driven by **3L+** — **10.0 vs 5.0 months** (HR 0.47). AstraZeneca release / OncLive detail.
- **HARMONi-GI1** (first-line biliary, all-China): ivonescimab + GemCis vs durvalumab + GemCis — OS **14.9 vs 12.1 months** (HR 0.75); PFS 8.4 vs 5.8 months (HR 0.61). First phase 3 to beat the TOPAZ-1 regimen on the figures cited on the site.

**Outside the Presidential slot but into *NEJM*** — the 2 November note on [HEALER and OptiTROP-Lung06]({{ '/esmo-2026-china-phase-3-healer-optitrop-lung06/' | relative_url }}):

- **HEALER** (first-line HER2+ advanced breast): anbenitamab + albumin-bound docetaxel vs THP — 24-month PFS **58.5% vs 34.4%**; HR for progression or death **0.46** (0.37–0.56). OS immature. China-only; two variables changed at once (bispecific + nab-docetaxel).
- **OptiTROP-Lung06** (PD-L1 TPS &lt;1% non-squamous NSCLC, driver-negative): sacituzumab tirumotecan + pembrolizumab vs chemo + pembrolizumab — median PFS **15.0 vs 5.7 months** (HR **0.49**). OS immature (descriptive HR 0.58).

Together these ten trials cover melanoma adjuvant mRNA, perioperative bladder ADC–IO, SCLC bispecific maintenance, high-risk prostate ARPI intensification, next-generation KRAS G12C and EGFR/MET combinations, a CLDN18.2 ADC, a PD-1×VEGF bispecific in biliary cancer, a HER2 biparatopic first-line challenge to THP, and a TROP2 ADC–IO challenge to chemo-IO in PD-L1-negative lung cancer.

## What the forest plot and quiz add

The [forest-plot page]({{ '/esmo-2026-forest-plot/' | relative_url }}) (3 Nov) puts the same hazard ratios on one educational SVG: headline end points (RFS/EFS/OS/MFS/PFS as reported) and a separate OS panel. Grey rows flag end points that did not meet significance or were not formally tested (for example CLARITY ITT PFS, and immature OS for INTerpath-001 and DASL-HiCaP). It is for **scale and memory**, not head-to-head comparison — populations, end points and follow-up differ.

The [trial quiz]({{ '/esmo-2026-quiz/' | relative_url }}) (2 Nov) asks ten questions keyed to those same pages (setting, timing of benefit, EFS/OS HRs, MET-altered eligibility, CLDN18.2 OS locus, HEALER/OptiTROP PFS). Answers stay in the browser; every explanation links back to the source post.
