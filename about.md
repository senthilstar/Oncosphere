# About Oncosphere

Oncosphere is an independent educational blog by **Dr. Senthil Kumar**, a medical oncologist working in Tamil Nadu government service in Chennai.

**What you'll find:** concise, referenced summaries of practice-changing trials, guideline updates (NCCN, ESMO, ASCO, ICMR) and plain-language explainers for patients and families. A new post is planned most Mondays.

**Editorial principles**
- Every factual claim is linked to a primary source (journal, regulator or guideline body).
- Opinions are labelled as opinion.
- Numbers are not invented; if something cannot be verified it is stated.
- No patient-identifiable information or patient images are published.
- Views are personal and do not represent the Government of Tamil Nadu or any institution.

**Advertising:** the site may display Google AdSense ads to cover running costs. Advertisers have no influence on content. See the [Privacy Policy]({{ '/privacy/' | relative_url }}).

**How posts are written**
1. Primary sources first: the full paper (NEJM, Lancet, JCO, Annals of Oncology), regulator notices (US FDA, CDSCO) and guidelines (NCCN, ESMO, ASCO, ICMR).
2. Each post starts with a **bottom-line box**, then telegraphic evidence with citations next to the numbers, then guideline context and practical caveats.
3. Every post ends with **"What it means for India"** — what is deliverable in Indian public and private hospitals, and where access or cost is a barrier.
4. Indian approvals and prices change often; where they cannot be verified from an official source, posts say "check current availability" instead of quoting a number.

**Corrections and updates**
Posts are reviewed when major new data appear (for example final overall-survival results) and carry an "updated" date. If you spot an error, please write in via the [Contact]({{ '/contact/' | relative_url }}) page; corrections are made promptly and noted.

**Who this site is for**
- Oncologists, residents and fellows who want a quick, referenced summary of a trial.
- General physicians and nurses caring for people with cancer.
- Patients and families who want to understand the evidence behind a treatment — always together with their own doctor.

**What this site is not**
It is not a substitute for a consultation, a second opinion or a tumour board. No individual medical advice is given by email. In an emergency, go to the nearest hospital.

**Conflicts of interest:** none declared relating to this site.
