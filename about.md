# About Dr. Senthil

Dr. Senthil is a medical oncologist working in the Tamil Nadu government health service in Chennai. Oncosphere is his personal, non-commercial space to share:

- **Trial and approval updates** — concise, sourced summaries of new evidence for fellow clinicians and trainees.
- **Patient guides** — plain-language explanations to help patients and families understand cancer treatment and prepare questions for their doctor.
- **Perspectives** — cancer history and thoughts on how treatment paradigms evolve.

Follow on X: [@esenthil84](https://x.com/esenthil84)

<p class="note">Oncosphere does not offer individual consultations or second opinions online, and no patient details are ever published here. For care, please visit your treating hospital.</p>
