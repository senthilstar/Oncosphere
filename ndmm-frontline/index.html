Frontline Therapy of Newly Diagnosed Multiple Myeloma (NDMM)

An evidence review for medical oncology trainees and faculty, with an Indian / resource-limited perspective

Evidence current to 10 November 2026 | Numbers in [square brackets] refer to the reference list (Section 12)

How to use this document

• Sections 1–6 give the evidence (trial design, primary endpoint, headline effect size with its source). Sections 7–9 give guidelines, the Indian context and a bedside algorithm with dosing. Section 10 has exam points and self-test questions.

• Labels used: [Approved] = regulatory approval verified (FDA/EC); [Reported] = peer-reviewed or congress data; [Ongoing] = no randomised efficacy results yet.

• Hazard ratios (HR) are for progression or death (PFS) unless stated. MRD = measurable (minimal) residual disease, at 10⁻⁵ unless stated.

• Drug doses are for teaching. Check the current label, renal and hepatic function, and the local formulary before prescribing.

Ten-line executive summary

• Anti-CD38 quadruplets are the global standard for fit patients: D-VRd (PERSEUS) or Isa-VRd (GMMG-HD7) before ASCT. For transplant-ineligible patients under 80: Isa-VRd (IMROZ) or D-VRd (CEPHEUS). For older or frail patients: D-Rd (MAIA), which gave a median OS of 90.3 months.

• PERSEUS: 48-month PFS 84.3% vs 67.7% (HR 0.42); MRD− 75.2% vs 47.5% [13]. FDA approved D-VRd for transplant-eligible NDMM on 30 Jul 2024 and for transplant-ineligible NDMM on 27 Jan 2026 [14,30].

• ASCT still improves PFS but not OS in the triplet era (IFM 2009, DETERMINATION) [18,19]. Whether ASCT is still needed after a quadruplet is being tested in MRD-adapted trials (MIDAS, MASTER) [24,25].

• Lenalidomide maintenance is the backbone. Adding daratumumab (PERSEUS D-R) deepens and sustains MRD negativity, and dara can be stopped once MRD negativity is sustained.

• IMS/IMWG 2025 high-risk definition: del(17p) >20% and/or TP53 mutation; t(4;14)/t(14;16)/t(14;20) only when 1q+ and/or del(1p32) co-occur; 1p32 combinations; β2M ≥5.5 mg/L with normal creatinine [4].

• FDA ODAC voted 12–0 (12 Apr 2024) to accept MRD as an accelerated-approval endpoint in myeloma [36].

• Frontline CAR-T (CARTITUDE-5/6) and bispecifics (MajesTEC-7, MajesTEC-4) have no randomised results yet [26].

• Smouldering myeloma: daratumumab for high-risk SMM (AQUILA) was FDA-approved on 6 Nov 2025 [7].

• In India, the PMJAY package list covers VRd/VCd/VTd at low per-cycle rates. Daratumumab SC is costly (indicative ₹2.0–2.3 lakh per vial), and no CDSCO-approved biosimilar existed as of May 2026 [49,50]. VRd ± ASCT with lenalidomide maintenance therefore stays the pragmatic public-sector standard.

• Supportive care has evidence behind it: levofloxacin for 12 weeks (TEAMM), risk-adapted VTE prophylaxis (IMPEDE; note the −3 points for Asian ethnicity), zoledronate or denosumab, SC weekly bortezomib, acyclovir, and HBV screening before anti-CD38 therapy.

1. Diagnosis, staging and risk stratification

Work-up checklist: CBC, creatinine/CrCl, calcium, albumin, LDH, β2-microglobulin, SPEP + IFE, 24-h UPEP + IFE, serum FLC, bone marrow aspirate and trephine, FISH on CD138-sorted plasma cells (purity ≥70% recommended by IMS/IMWG) [4], NGS for TP53 where available, and imaging with whole-body low-dose CT (WB-LDCT) or PET-CT (MRI of spine/pelvis if those are negative). Add cardiac biomarkers and NT-proBNP if AL amyloidosis is suspected.

1.3 IMS/IMWG 2025 consensus definition of high-risk MM [4]

Published in J Clin Oncol 2025;43:2739–51. High-risk MM = any one of:

  • del(17p) in >20% clonal fraction and/or TP53 mutation (NGS). Biallelic TP53 loss (~5%) carries the worst prognosis.
  • t(4;14), t(14;16) or t(14;20) co-occurring with 1q+ (gain ≥3 copies or amplification ≥4) and/or del(1p32). This is a key change: a translocation on its own no longer counts as high-risk.
  • Monoallelic del(1p32) with 1q+, or biallelic del(1p32).
  • β2-microglobulin ≥5.5 mg/L with normal creatinine (<1.2 mg/dL).

1q+ alone (seen in ~35–37% of NDMM) is not high-risk unless combined with one of the above. The panel recommends FISH on plasma-cell-enriched samples with ≥70% purity, and NGS in all patients [4].

Mayo supplemental criteria (Leukemia 2025 review): primary plasma cell leukaemia, NDMM with extramedullary disease, and a high plasma-cell S-phase fraction also count as high-risk. "Double-hit" means ≥2 IMS/IMWG high-risk abnormalities, excluding the β2M criterion. These patients form the "ultra-high-risk" group that most trials (e.g., MASTER with ≥2 HRCA [24]) show still does poorly with current quadruplets.

1.5 High-risk smouldering myeloma (brief)

  • AQUILA (phase 3, n = 390): SC daratumumab monotherapy for up to 36 months vs active monitoring. With 65.2 months median follow-up, PFS (progression to active MM or death) HR 0.49 (95% CI 0.36–0.67); 5-y PFS 63.1% vs 40.8%; deaths 7.7% vs 13.3% (HR 0.52; 95% CI 0.27–0.98); 5-y OS 93.0% vs 86.9% [7]. [Approved] FDA, 6 Nov 2025, for high-risk SMM only [7].
  • ECOG E3A06 (phase 3): lenalidomide vs observation in intermediate/high-risk SMM. 3-y PFS 91% vs 66% [8].
  • Practice point: apply Mayo 2018 "20/2/20" risk (BMPC >20%, M-protein >2 g/dL, FLC ratio >20). Before calling a patient SMM, rule out MDEs with WB-LDCT/PET and MRI.

In practice they are kept for high-risk disease or trials.

  • Bottom line: upfront ASCT adds about 1.5–2 years of PFS but no proven OS gain in the triplet era. It remains standard (NCCN, EHA-EMN) for fit patients, especially when quadruplets or long-term anti-CD38 therapy are not affordable. In the Indian public sector, ASCT is among the most cost-effective "deepeners" available.
  • Deferred ASCT is acceptable for standard-risk patients who prefer it, provided stem cells are collected and stored.
  • Tandem ASCT: consider for IMS high-risk disease (EMN02 benefit; StaMINA neutral). Many centres reserve it for patients who are MRD-positive after the first ASCT or who have ultra-high-risk disease.
  • Conditioning: melphalan 200 mg/m² (140 mg/m² if age >70 or CrCl <30–40 mL/min).

2.3 Consolidation

Two cycles of the induction regimen after ASCT were part of PERSEUS, GRIFFIN and CASSIOPEIA, which makes it the de facto standard with a quadruplet. The independent value of VRd consolidation is inconsistent across trials: EMN02 showed a PFS benefit, while StaMINA showed none [20,21]. Use it when following the PERSEUS schedule. It is optional if MRD-negative after a triplet + ASCT and resources are limited.

2.5 MRD-guided therapy in transplant-eligible patients

  • MASTER (phase 2, Dara-KRd → ASCT → MRD-adapted consolidation; therapy stopped after 2 consecutive MRD− results at 10⁻⁵): 3-y PFS 88.4% with 0 HRCA, 78.9% with 1 HRCA and 50.0% with ≥2 HRCA [24]. Lesson: stopping works in standard-risk disease, but ultra-high-risk disease relapses despite MRD negativity.
  • MIDAS (phase 3): in patients MRD-negative after Isa-KRd, ASCT did not increase 10⁻⁶ MRD negativity. In MRD-positive patients, tandem ASCT added nothing over single ASCT + Isa-KRd [25]. PFS is awaited before ASCT can be omitted routinely.
  • PERSEUS sustained-MRD-driven dara discontinuation: with longer follow-up, most eligible D-VRd patients stopped daratumumab and few needed to restart (company/congress data; see [13]).

3.1 Choosing among D-Rd, D-VRd and Isa-VRd

  • Fit/intermediate patients under 80 (most IMROZ/CEPHEUS patients): a quadruplet gives more MRD negativity and PFS, but costs more neuropathy, infections and treatment-related deaths. IMROZ excluded patients over 80. Fatal treatment-emergent AEs were 11.0% with Isa-VRd vs 5.5% with VRd, mostly infections, partly reflecting longer exposure [31]. Pick patients with care.
  • Older (≥75) or intermediate-fit patients: D-Rd is the "no-PI" option with the longest OS follow-up (median 7.5 y). Its benefit holds in patients ≥80 y [28].
  • Frail patients: use dex-sparing D-R (IFM2017-03): dexamethasone 20 mg weekly only in cycles 1–2, then stopped [33]. Alternatives are RVd-lite or Rd with reduced dexamethasone.
  • Bortezomib: give it SC and once weekly in the elderly (lower neuropathy, comparable efficacy) [40].

4. High-risk disease and special populations

4.1 High-risk / ultra-high-risk

  • Principles: use a quadruplet induction (D-VRd; consider carfilzomib-based Isa-KRd/Dara-KRd in a trial or where available), ASCT (consider tandem), and PI-IMiD ± anti-CD38 maintenance until progression. Enrol in trials.
  • PERSEUS and GMMG-HD7 subgroups: the benefit in high-risk cytogenetics was smaller and less certain. GMMG-HD7 found no clear MRD/PFS advantage in R-ISS III or high-risk subgroups [15]. Small subgroups give wide CIs, so do not over-interpret.
  • Carfilzomib quadruplets in high-risk disease: GMMG-CONCEPT (Isa-KRd) and MASTER reported high MRD-negativity rates, but ≥2 HRCA still had 3-y PFS of only ~50% in MASTER [24].
  • BENEFIT high-risk subgroup: 48-mo PFS 63.1% (Isa-VRd) vs 40.2% (Isa-Rd), which argues for keeping the PI in high-risk elderly patients (IMS 2026 congress data).

4.2 Primary plasma cell leukaemia (pPCL)

  • IMWG 2021 definition: ≥5% circulating plasma cells in the peripheral smear of a patient otherwise meeting MM criteria [41].
  • Treat as ultra-high-risk: rapid cytoreduction with a bortezomib-based quadruplet (± cyclophosphamide/anthracycline-containing regimens such as VDT-PACE if fulminant), then ASCT (tandem commonly used) and maintenance with PI + IMiD ± anti-CD38. Allogeneic SCT only for selected young patients in a trial. Median survival is still short, so trial referral is preferred.

4.3 Renal impairment / cast nephropathy

  • Start bortezomib + dexamethasone (40 mg D1–4) immediately with no dose adjustment. Bortezomib-based triplets (VCd, VTd) or D-VCd/D-VTd are suitable at any CrCl, including dialysis. Daratumumab needs no renal dose adjustment.
  • Lenalidomide: adjust for CrCl (e.g., 10 mg daily at CrCl 30–60; 15 mg every 48 h, or 5 mg daily per the current label, at <30; 5 mg after dialysis on dialysis days). Avoid nephrotoxins, NSAIDs and IV contrast; hydrate; treat hypercalcaemia.
  • High-cutoff haemodialysis (MYRE, JAMA 2017): no significant improvement in dialysis independence at 3 months; not routine [51].

4.4 Extramedullary disease (EMD)

  • Distinguish paraskeletal (bone-related, better prognosis) from true EMD (soft tissue, haematogenous; poor prognosis). Baseline PET-CT is essential; use it for response assessment.
  • Treat as high-risk: quadruplet, ASCT ± tandem, and consider RT for symptomatic masses. Anti-CD38 regimens alone work less well in true EMD.

4.5 Elderly/frail and AL amyloidosis overlap

  • Elderly: see Section 3. Prioritise quality of life, oral regimens, reduced dexamethasone, and SC weekly bortezomib.
  • MM with AL amyloidosis: stage cardiac involvement (NT-proBNP, troponin, Mayo 2004/2012). Dara-VCd (ANDROMEDA) is the standard for AL (haematological CR 53.3% vs 18.1%, NEJM 2021). Use caution with high-dose dex and fluid; ASCT only in carefully selected patients.

  • IMWG 2016 criteria [35]: MRD− by NGF/NGS at ≥10⁻⁵ in patients with CR. Sustained MRD− = negative on two assessments ≥1 year apart. Imaging + MRD− = MRD− plus disappearance of PET-avid lesions.

  • Timing in practice: end of induction (optional), about 100 days post-ASCT, then yearly during maintenance in patients in CR. Always take the first-pull aspirate for MRD (haemodilution gives false negatives).
  • MRD-adapted trials: MASTER, MIDAS, PERSEUS (dara stop), GEM2014MAIN, DRAMMATIC (SWOG S1803; MRD-based maintenance cessation, ongoing), MRD2STOP.
  • Caution: MRD negativity is strongly prognostic, but treatment changes driven by MRD status have not yet been proven to improve OS. High-risk patients who are MRD− still relapse more often.

8. Indian and resource-limited context

8.1 Access and financing

  • PMJAY / AB-PMJAY packages (HBP codes, as compiled on a public Indian haematology resource [49]): "Multiple myeloma: bortezomib + lenalidomide + dexamethasone, per cycle ×6" and "bortezomib + cyclophosphamide + dexamethasone, per cycle ×6" are each listed at ₹5,000/cycle, VTD for PCL/MM at ₹20,000 (max 6 cycles), MPT, thalidomide-dex, MP and zoledronic acid (₹2,000/month, max 12). Pre-authorisation needs BM report, SPEP and CRAB documentation, and post-treatment drug batch barcodes. Rates differ between HBP versions and states, so confirm current HBP 2022 rates with the scheme.
  • Tamil Nadu CMCHIS: medical oncology is a major expense head (The Hindu, 2025), and high-end procedures such as transplants are funded above the usual cap through a corpus fund [48]. I could not verify a myeloma-specific CMCHIS package or an ASCT-for-myeloma package from a primary government source. Check with the CMCHIS empanelment desk (cmchistn.com) before counselling patients.
  • Daratumumab cost: indicative Indian price ~₹2.0–2.3 lakh per 1800 mg SC vial (private provider listing, 2026) [49]. As of the CDSCO SEC (Oncology) meeting of 20 May 2026, no daratumumab biosimilar was approved in India; Zydus ZRC-3296 had permission only for a phase 3 trial [50]. A PERSEUS-style induction + consolidation needs 16 SC doses (≈ ₹32–37 lakh at these prices) before maintenance, which is out of reach for most public-sector patients.
  • Generic lenalidomide, bortezomib, thalidomide and pomalidomide are made by multiple Indian manufacturers and are widely available at a fraction of originator prices. Some are on Jan Aushadhi and state (TNMSC) procurement lists. Check the local tender list.

10. Open questions and future directions

  • Is ASCT still needed after quadruplet induction? MIDAS (MRD-adapted) and CARTITUDE-6 (cilta-cel vs ASCT) will answer this. Until then, ASCT remains standard [25,26].
  • Anti-CD38 choice and duration: dara vs isa have not been compared head to head. Maintenance duration (PERSEUS stop rules) and SC isatuximab are open questions.
  • Carfilzomib vs bortezomib backbone in quadruplets: IsKia shows deeper MRD with Isa-KRd [16]. No phase 3 comparison of D-VRd vs D-KRd exists.
  • Frontline T-cell redirection: CARTITUDE-5/6, MajesTEC-7, MajesTEC-4 and MagnetisMM trials are all ongoing. The key issues are infections, cost, and whether early use beats sequencing.
  • MRD-guided de-escalation (DRAMMATIC, MRD2STOP, PERSEUS stop rule) and MRD as a regulatory endpoint after ODAC 2024 [36].
  • Ultra-high-risk disease: novel combinations (bispecific + quadruplet, cereblon E3 ligase modulators such as iberdomide/mezigdomide in maintenance), allogeneic approaches, and genomic risk-adapted trials.
  • Interception: AQUILA has made treatment of high-risk SMM a reality [7]. Trials of curative-intent treatment for SMM (e.g., CAR-T in high-risk SMM) are underway.
  • Equity: Indian randomised data, biosimilar daratumumab (phase 3 under way in India [50]), local MRD by NGF, and scheme coverage of anti-CD38 therapy.

11. Exam-oriented key points

  • SLiM-CRAB: S ≥60% BMPC; Li FLC ratio ≥100; M >1 MRI focal lesion ≥5 mm.
  • IMS/IMWG 2025 high-risk: del(17p) >20% ± TP53 mut; IgH translocation plus 1q+/del(1p32); del(1p32) combinations; β2M ≥5.5 with normal creatinine. t(4;14) alone is no longer high-risk.
  • R2-ISS weights: ISS III 1.5, ISS II 1, del17p 1, LDH 1, t(4;14) 1, 1q+ 0.5.
  • PERSEUS: D-VRd ×4 → ASCT → D-VRd ×2 → D-R. 4-y PFS 84.3% vs 67.7%; HR 0.42; MRD− 75.2% vs 47.5%; FDA 30 Jul 2024.
  • IMROZ: Isa-VRd in TI ≤80 y; 5-y PFS 63.2% vs 45.2%; HR 0.60; FDA 20 Sep 2024.
  • CEPHEUS: D-VRd in TI/deferred; MRD− 60.9% vs 39.4%; HR 0.57; FDA 27 Jan 2026.
  • MAIA: D-Rd median OS 90.3 vs 64.1 mo (HR 0.67); median PFS 61.9 vs 34.4 mo.
  • ASCT: IFM 2009 and DETERMINATION show a PFS benefit (50 vs 36; 67.5 vs 46.2 mo) but no OS benefit.
  • Tandem ASCT: EMN02 positive (HR 0.74); StaMINA negative.
  • Len maintenance: OS benefit in meta-analysis (HR 0.75).
  • AQUILA: dara in HR-SMM, HR 0.49; FDA 6 Nov 2025. E3A06: len in SMM, 3-y PFS 91% vs 66%.
  • TEAMM: levofloxacin ×12 weeks lowers febrile episodes/death (19% vs 27%).
  • IMPEDE VTE: Asian ethnicity −3; IMiD +4; ≥4 → anticoagulant.
  • pPCL (IMWG 2021): ≥5% circulating plasma cells.
  • FDA ODAC 12 Apr 2024: MRD accepted as accelerated-approval endpoint (12–0).

11.1 Self-test questions with brief answers

Question

Model answer

Why is t(4;14) alone no longer "high-risk" under IMS/IMWG 2025?

Modern PI-based quadruplets largely overcome isolated t(4;14). Data from IFM, GEM and CoMMpass show poor outcomes only when 1q+ or del(1p32) co-occur [4].

What is the difference between R-ISS and R2-ISS?

R2-ISS adds 1q+ and weights each factor additively, splitting the large R-ISS II group into low-intermediate and intermediate-high groups [3].

Name the trial that led to FDA approval of D-VRd in transplant-eligible NDMM and its primary endpoint.

PERSEUS; PFS (HR 0.42). Approved 30 July 2024 [13,14].

Does ASCT improve OS in the novel-agent era?

Not in randomised trials with a triplet backbone (IFM 2009, DETERMINATION). It improves PFS by ~1.5–2 years. EMN02 suggested an OS benefit versus VMP intensification [18–20].

Which trial supports a dexamethasone-sparing approach in frail patients?

IFM2017-03: dara-len with dex only for 2 cycles vs Rd; median PFS 53.4 vs 22.5 mo; OS HR 0.52 [33].

What did BENEFIT show?

In TI patients aged 65–79, adding bortezomib to Isa-Rd doubled 18-month MRD negativity (53% vs 26%). The 48-month PFS difference was not significant overall but was large in high-risk disease [32].

How would you treat a 62-year-old with NDMM and creatinine 6 mg/dL?

Start bortezomib-dex immediately (dex 40 mg D1–4), add cyclophosphamide/thalidomide or daratumumab. Hydrate and avoid nephrotoxins. Adjust lenalidomide once CrCl is known. ASCT with Mel140 remains possible even on dialysis.

What sensitivity defines MRD negativity per IMWG and which methods are used?

≥10⁻⁵ by NGF (EuroFlow) or NGS (clonoSEQ). 10⁻⁶ is increasingly reported. Combine with PET-CT for imaging MRD [35].

Why give levofloxacin prophylaxis?

The early infection-related death peak occurs in the first 3 months. TEAMM showed fewer febrile episodes or deaths (HR 0.66) without more resistant infections [37].

What is the role of daratumumab in maintenance after CASSIOPEIA?

Dara q8w for 2 years improved PFS vs observation (HR 0.49), mainly after VTd. The benefit after D-VTd was small, which suggests the importance of anti-CD38 exposure at some point [11].

How does daratumumab interfere with blood banking and SPEP?

It binds CD38 on RBCs, giving pan-reactive indirect Coombs results (use DTT-treated cells or a pre-treatment phenotype). It is an IgG-κ antibody, so it can mimic a residual M-protein in IgG-κ myeloma.

What is the current status of frontline CAR-T in myeloma?

Investigational only. CARTITUDE-5 (TI) and CARTITUDE-6 (TE vs ASCT) are ongoing, with no efficacy results as of Nov 2026 [26].

Define plasma cell leukaemia per IMWG 2021.

≥5% circulating plasma cells on peripheral blood smear in a patient with symptomatic MM [41].

Which smouldering myeloma patients should receive therapy?

High-risk SMM (e.g., Mayo 20/2/20 with ≥2 factors). Daratumumab monotherapy for 3 years (AQUILA; FDA-approved Nov 2025) or lenalidomide (E3A06). Otherwise observe [7,8].
