# Historical perspectives in oncology

A concise timeline of changes in cancer prevention and treatment.

## 1890s — Surgery and hormones {#halsted-beatson}

Radical mastectomy shaped breast surgery, while ovarian removal showed that some breast cancers respond to hormonal change.

## 1896–1900s — From shadows to light: X-rays, radium and radiotherapy {#x-rays-radium}

X-rays (1895) and radium (1898) led to early external radiation and radium brachytherapy. <a class="cite" href="https://www.cancer.gov/about-nci/advisory-boards/ctac/working-groups/radiation-oncology-working-group/november-2020.pdf">[NCI 2020]</a>

## 1917–1930s — The Radium Girls {#radium-girls}

Dial painters who lip-pointed radium brushes developed jaw necrosis and cancers; their legal cases helped advance workplace protections. [Source](https://blogs.loc.gov/headlinesandheroes/2019/03/radium-girls-living-dead-women/)

## 1940s — Nitrogen mustard and antifolates {#chemotherapy}

Nitrogen mustard and the antifolate aminopterin helped establish chemotherapy; aminopterin induced temporary remissions in childhood acute leukaemia. <a class="cite" href="https://www.cancer.gov/research/progress/250-years-milestones">[NCI milestones]</a>

## 1978 — Cisplatin and testicular cancer {#cisplatin}

Cisplatin, vinblastine and bleomycin produced remissions in disseminated testicular cancer; cisplatin-based combinations made cure possible for many. <a class="cite" href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/71004/">[Ann Intern Med 1977]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/1700077/">[J Clin Oncol 1990]</a>

## 1998 — Trastuzumab: targeted therapy arrives {#trastuzumab}

The FDA approved trastuzumab for HER2-overexpressing metastatic breast cancer; adding it to first-line chemotherapy improved survival. <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/appletter/1998/trasgen092598L.htm">[FDA 1998]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11248153/">[NEJM 2001]</a>

## 2001 — Imatinib for CML {#imatinib}

Oral imatinib inhibits BCR-ABL in CML, demonstrating driver-directed therapy. <a class="cite" href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11287972/">[NEJM 2001]</a>

## 2006 — HPV vaccine {#hpv-vaccine}

HPV vaccination prevents infections that can lead to cervical and other cancers; vaccination is a pillar of cervical cancer elimination in India. <a class="cite" href="https://www.fda.gov/files/vaccines%2C%20blood%20%26%20biologics/published/Package-Insert---Gardasil.pdf">[FDA label]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://gco.iarc.who.int/media/elimination_tool/factsheets/356-IND-india.pdf">[IARC India]</a>

## 2011 — Ipilimumab: checkpoint era {#ipilimumab}

Ipilimumab improved survival in previously treated metastatic melanoma, establishing checkpoint blockade as a treatment approach. <a class="cite" href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20525992/">[NEJM 2010]</a>

## 2017 — Tissue-agnostic: MSI-H/dMMR {#tissue-agnostic}

The FDA granted pembrolizumab its first tissue-agnostic accelerated approval for unresectable or metastatic MSI-H/dMMR solid tumours progressing after prior treatment with no satisfactory alternatives; colorectal cancer had additional prior-treatment criteria. <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2017/125554Orig1s034.pdf">[FDA 2017]</a>

## 2020s — ADCs, organ preservation, ctDNA {#adc-ctdna}

Trastuzumab deruxtecan improved survival in previously treated HER2-low metastatic breast cancer (DESTINY-Breast04). <a class="cite" href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35665782/">[NEJM 2022]</a> In DYNAMIC, postoperative circulating tumour DNA guided adjuvant chemotherapy in stage II colon cancer, reducing chemotherapy use without compromising recurrence-free survival. <a class="cite" href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40055522/">[Nat Med 2025]</a> Dostarlimab can permit organ preservation after clinical complete response in selected stage II/III MSI-H/dMMR rectal cancers, with close surveillance.
