# Historical perspectives in oncology

From shadows to light — how cancer care evolved. Telegraphic; sources linked where available.

## 1890s — Halsted and Beatson: surgery and hormones {#halsted-beatson}

Radical mastectomy set the surgical template; Beatson's oophorectomy showed breast cancer could respond to hormone removal — the first systemic idea.

## 1896–1900s — From shadows to light: X-rays, radium and radiotherapy {#x-rays-radium}

Röntgen's X-rays (1895) and the Curies' radium (1898) birthed radiotherapy within years. [Source](https://www.cancer.gov/about-cancer/treatment/types/radiation-therapy)

## 1917–1930s — The Radium Girls {#radium-girls}

Dial painters who lip-pointed radium brushes developed jaw necrosis and cancers; their lawsuits shaped occupational-safety law. [Source](https://blogs.loc.gov/headlinesandheroes/2019/03/radium-girls-living-dead-women/)

## 1940s — Nitrogen mustard and Farber's antifolates {#chemotherapy}

Mustard derivatives and Farber's aminopterin remissions in childhood leukaemia opened the chemotherapy era.

## 1978 — Cisplatin and testicular cancer {#cisplatin}

Cisplatin turned metastatic testicular cancer into a largely curable disease.

## 1998 — Trastuzumab: targeted therapy arrives {#trastuzumab}

HER2 antibody plus chemotherapy improved survival in HER2+ metastatic breast cancer (Slamon, NEJM 2001). [Source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11248153/)

## 2001 — Imatinib for CML {#imatinib}

A pill targeting BCR-ABL — the proof-of-concept for driver-targeted therapy (Druker, NEJM 2001). [Source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11287972/)

## 2006 — HPV vaccine {#hpv-vaccine}

First vaccine aimed at preventing a cancer; central to India's cervical-cancer elimination plans.

## 2011 — Ipilimumab: checkpoint era {#ipilimumab}

First checkpoint inhibitor to improve survival in melanoma (NEJM 2011). [Source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21639810/)

## 2017 — Tissue-agnostic: MSI-H/dMMR {#tissue-agnostic}

Pembrolizumab approved by biomarker, not organ — rooted in Le et al., NEJM 2015. [Source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26028255/)

## 2020s — ADCs, organ preservation, ctDNA {#adc-ctdna}

T-DXd, dostarlimab organ-sparing in dMMR rectal cancer, ctDNA-guided care. [Source](/dostarlimab-msi-h-rectal-cancer/)

## India note
Cancer history in India runs through Tata Memorial (1941) and the Adyar Cancer Institute, Chennai (1954) — affordable, evidence-led care remains the thread.
