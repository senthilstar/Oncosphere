<h2>How to read it</h2>
<ul>
<li>A diamond left of the 1.0 line means fewer events in the experimental arm; the horizontal bar is the 95% CI. A bar that crosses 1.0 is not significant.</li>
<li>Smallest headline HRs: DeLLphi-305 OS and DASL-HiCaP MFS (both 0.42). Largest that still met its end point: HARMONi-GI1 OS (0.75).</li>
<li>CLARITY-Gastric 01 is shown twice: the 3L+ OS dual primary was met, the ITT PFS dual primary was not.</li>
<li>DASL-HiCaP OS (0.88) and INTerpath-001 OS (0.77) are immature and not significant; a big MFS or RFS gain is not yet a survival gain.</li>
</ul>
