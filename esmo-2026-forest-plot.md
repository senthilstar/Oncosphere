<h2>How to read it</h2>
<ul>
<li>The diamond marks the hazard ratio (HR), and the bar its confidence interval (CI). Values below the no-difference line favour the experimental arm for the specified event. A CI crossing the line is compatible with no difference; a CI wholly below it does not by itself establish statistical significance when a stricter boundary applies or the result has not been formally tested.</li>
<li>INTerpath-001’s single RFS HR summarises a time-varying effect: the published piecewise HR was 0.99 (95% CI 0.66–1.49) before 6 months and 0.42 (0.30–0.58) thereafter. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2609762">[NEJM 2026]</a></li>
<li>DeLLphi-305 OS and DASL-HiCaP metastasis-free survival have the same headline HR (0.42), but assess different events in different populations. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2609482">[NEJM 2026]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://www.bayer.com/en-us/united-states/news-and-stories/2026/metastasis-nubeqa">[Bayer 2026]</a></li>
<li>CLARITY-Gastric 01 met its later-line OS primary end point. Intention-to-treat progression-free survival (PFS), the other primary end point, did not meet the prespecified significance boundary despite a CI below the no-difference line. <a class="cite" href="https://www.astrazeneca-us.com/media/press-releases/2026/Sonesitatug-vedotin-reduced-the-risk-of-death-by-42-percent-in-2nd-and-later-line-CLDN18-2-positive-advanced-gastric-GEJ-cancers-in-CLARITY-Gastric01.html">[AZ 2026, sponsor-reported]</a></li>
<li>OS follow-up continues in DASL-HiCaP and INTerpath-001; their metastasis-free and recurrence-free survival gains do not yet establish an OS benefit. <a class="cite" href="https://www.bayer.com/en-us/united-states/news-and-stories/2026/metastasis-nubeqa">[Bayer 2026, sponsor-reported]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://docs.publicnow.com/viewDoc?filename=178244%5CEXT%5CBE30DCA6A1EBAD5B10C57D354064ABFE8165B757_379E2CD359D32405C0CA6688BA53E37FF1CB86A9.PDF">[ESMO presentation 2026]</a></li>
</ul>
