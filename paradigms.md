## Recent updates {#this-week}
A selection of recent articles and regulatory updates, current to 25 Nov 2026.

- [MATTERHORN]({{ '/durvalumab-flot-perioperative-gastric-gej-matterhorn/' | relative_url }}) — perioperative durvalumab plus FLOT improved event-free and overall survival in resectable gastric/GEJ adenocarcinoma.
- [KEYNOTE-905 and KEYNOTE-B15]({{ '/enfortumab-vedotin-pembrolizumab-perioperative-mibc-keynote-905/' | relative_url }}) — perioperative enfortumab vedotin plus pembrolizumab improved survival in cystectomy candidates.
- [First-line metastatic colorectal cancer]({{ '/first-line/metastatic-colorectal-cancer/' | relative_url }}) — assess resectability, fitness, MMR, RAS, BRAF V600E and sidedness; HER2 and KRAS G12C guide later options. Includes India access considerations.
- [First-line advanced hepatocellular carcinoma]({{ '/first-line/advanced-hepatocellular-carcinoma/' | relative_url }}) — Child-Pugh class, endoscopy and immunotherapy eligibility decide between atezolizumab–bevacizumab, STRIDE, nivolumab–ipilimumab and lenvatinib or sorafenib; sequencing and India access.
- [First-line advanced gastric / GEJ adenocarcinoma]({{ '/first-line/advanced-gastric-gej-adenocarcinoma/' | relative_url }}) — test HER2, PD-L1 CPS, MMR and CLDN18.2; choose PD-1, HER2-directed or zolbetuximab combinations; sequencing and India access.
- [PSMAddition]({{ '/lutetium-psma-hormone-sensitive-prostate-cancer-psmaddition/' | relative_url }}) — Lu-PSMA-617 added to ADT and an androgen receptor pathway inhibitor in PSMA-positive metastatic hormone-sensitive prostate cancer improved radiographic PFS; overall survival remains immature.
- **FDA approvals:** giredestrant plus everolimus for ER-positive, HER2-negative, ESR1-mutated advanced breast cancer after endocrine therapy; evERA median PFS 10 vs 5.5 months, HR 0.38. <a class="cite" href="https://www.fda.gov/drugs/cancer-approvals/fda-approves-giredestrant-combination-everolimus-er-positive-her2-negative-esr1-mutated-locally">[FDA 2026]</a> Neladalkib received accelerated approval for ALK-positive advanced NSCLC after alectinib, or after at least two ALK inhibitors and platinum chemotherapy; ALKOVE-1 response rates were 49% and 32%, respectively. <a class="cite" href="https://www.fda.gov/drugs/cancer-approvals/fda-grants-accelerated-approval-neladalkib-previously-treated-locally-advanced-or-metastatic-alk">[FDA 2026]</a>
- [Deep-dive quiz]({{ '/deep-dive-quiz-november-2026/' | relative_url }}) — questions on November trial designs, findings and a recent approval.
- [ATOMIC]({{ '/atezolizumab-adjuvant-dmmr-colon-cancer-atomic/' | relative_url }}) — adding atezolizumab to adjuvant mFOLFOX6 improved disease-free survival in resected stage III dMMR colon cancer; overall survival remains immature.
- [SERENA-6]({{ '/camizestrant-esr1-ctdna-switch-serena-6/' | relative_url }}) — switch from an aromatase inhibitor to camizestrant while continuing CDK4/6 inhibition after ctDNA detects ESR1 mutation, before radiological progression; survival and optimal switch timing remain uncertain.
- [TALAPRO-3]({{ '/talazoparib-enzalutamide-hrr-mhspc-talapro-3/' | relative_url }}) — talazoparib plus enzalutamide improved radiographic PFS in HRR-mutated metastatic hormone-sensitive prostate cancer; survival is immature and anaemia requires monitoring.
- [ROSELLA]({{ '/relacorilant-platinum-resistant-ovarian-cancer-rosella/' | relative_url }}) — relacorilant plus nab-paclitaxel improved survival in platinum-resistant ovarian cancer; FDA use requires prior bevacizumab. Plan blood-count monitoring and glucocorticoid management.
- [ACTION-1]({{ '/ryz101-action-1-actinium-dotatate-gep-net/' | relative_url }}) — sponsor-reported positive interim PFS for actinium-225 DOTATATE after lutetium-177 in SSTR-positive gastroenteropancreatic neuroendocrine tumours; quantitative efficacy and safety results awaited.
- [NHS-Galleri]({{ '/nhs-galleri-multi-cancer-early-detection-trial/' | relative_url }}) — multi-cancer blood screening did not meet its primary endpoint of reducing stage III–IV cancers; mortality benefit remains unestablished.
- [RASolute 302]({{ '/daraxonrasib-pancreatic-cancer-rasolute-302/' | relative_url }}) — daraxonrasib after treatment for metastatic pancreatic cancer: survival, preventive skin and mouth care, and Indian access.
- **FDA approvals:** [subcutaneous amivantamab plus hyaluronidase]({{ '/rybrevant-faspro-hnscc-fda-approval/' | relative_url }}) received accelerated approval for recurrent or metastatic head and neck squamous cell cancer after platinum and anti-PD-(L)1 therapy, excluding HPV-associated oropharyngeal cancer. <a class="cite" href="https://www.fda.gov/drugs/cancer-approvals/fda-grants-accelerated-approval-amivantamab-and-hyaluronidase-lpuj-recurrent-or-metastatic-head-and">[FDA 2026]</a> [Pimicotinib]({{ '/pimicotinib-tgct-fda-approval/' | relative_url }}) is approved for symptomatic tenosynovial giant cell tumour when surgery may worsen function or cause severe morbidity. <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/220414s000lbl.pdf">[FDA 2026]</a>
- **Muscle-invasive bladder cancer:** neoadjuvant durvalumab plus enfortumab vedotin followed by adjuvant durvalumab after cystectomy is FDA-approved for adults ineligible for or declining cisplatin-based chemotherapy. <a class="cite" href="https://www.fda.gov/drugs/cancer-approvals/fda-approves-durvalumab-enfortumab-vedotin-ejfv-muscle-invasive-bladder-cancer">[FDA 2026]</a>
- [November FDA decisions]({{ '/oncology-fda-decisions-to-watch-november-2026/' | relative_url }}) — HARMONi ivonescimab plus chemotherapy after EGFR TKI received an FDA complete response letter; the sponsor reports that PFS without statistically significant OS improvement did not establish a favourable benefit–risk profile. The adjuvant giredestrant decision remains pending.
- **More updates:** [ESMO week review]({{ '/esmo-2026-week-update/' | relative_url }}), [TNM ninth-edition lung staging cases]({{ '/lung-cancer-staging-puzzle/' | relative_url }}), [four phase 3 trials after osimertinib]({{ '/egfr-nsclc-after-osimertinib-2026-phase-3-options/' | relative_url }}) and [early-phase SITC late-breakers]({{ '/sitc-2026-late-breakers/' | relative_url }}).
- **Evidence reviews:** [testicular tumours]({{ '/testicular-tumors/' | relative_url }}) (staging, risk groups and treatment), [stage IV lung adenocarcinoma]({{ '/stage4-lung-adenocarcinoma/' | relative_url }}) and [initial myeloma treatment]({{ '/ndmm-frontline/' | relative_url }}); the lung and myeloma reviews include India / resource-limited context.
- [November oncology quiz]({{ '/november-2026-oncology-quiz/' | relative_url }}) — FDA news, post-osimertinib trials and SITC late-breakers.
- **India context:** FDA decisions do not establish CDSCO approval, Indian availability or price; check local regulatory status and access before prescribing.
