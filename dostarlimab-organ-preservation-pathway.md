<h2>How to read the figure</h2>
<ol>
<li><strong>Biopsy + test.</strong> About 5–10% of rectal cancers are MSI-H or dMMR. Universal MMR immunohistochemistry (or MSI testing) is the gate.</li>
<li><strong>PD-1 blockade.</strong> Label dose in the accelerated approval is dostarlimab 500 mg IV every 3 weeks for 9 cycles (about 6 months).</li>
<li><strong>Restage · decide.</strong> Clinical complete response can allow intensive watch-and-wait surveillance; residual disease needs salvage chemoradiation or surgery without delay.</li>
</ol>

<h2>Where the numbers come from</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>AZUR-1</strong> (pivotal multicentre phase 2, n=154): investigator-assessed 12-month sustained clinical complete response 68%; independent central review 47% (immature); 71% needed nothing beyond dostarlimab at the reported cut-off.</li>
<li><strong>MSK expanded phase 2</strong> (NEJM 2025): rectal cohort 49/49 clinical complete responses; 2-year recurrence-free survival 96%. Label cohort (n=50): cCR12 93%.</li>
</ul>
