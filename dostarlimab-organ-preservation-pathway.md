<h2>How to read the figure</h2>
<ol>
<li><strong>Biopsy + test.</strong> Assess tumour MMR by immunohistochemistry or MSI testing. The graphic’s 5–10% estimate refers broadly to rectal cancer; the FDA cites approximately 3% for locally advanced rectal cancer, the population relevant to this pathway. <a class="cite" href="https://www.fda.gov/news-events/press-announcements/fda-approves-immunotherapy-may-allow-certain-patients-rectal-cancer-avoid-chemotherapy-radiation-and">[FDA 2026]</a></li>
<li><strong>PD-1 blockade.</strong> The US label specifies dostarlimab 500 mg IV every 3 weeks for up to 9 cycles, stopping for progression or unacceptable toxicity. The accelerated approval covers adults with stage II/III MSI-H or dMMR locally advanced rectal cancer, determined by an FDA-authorized solid tumour test; long-term relapse risk remains unknown. <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/761174s015lbl.pdf">[FDA label 2026]</a></li>
<li><strong>Restage · decide.</strong> Clinical complete response may permit nonoperative surveillance after multidisciplinary assessment. Persistent or recurrent disease warrants prompt evaluation for standard treatment; a near-complete response requires careful assessment rather than automatic surgery. <a class="cite" href="https://www.fda.gov/drugs/cancer-approvals/fda-grants-accelerated-approval-dostarlimab-locally-advanced-rectal-cancer">[FDA 2026]</a></li>
</ol>

<h2>Where the numbers come from</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>AZUR-1:</strong> In this single-arm phase 2 trial, 154 previously untreated patients received dostarlimab. Sustained clinical complete response for 12 months (cCR12) was 68% among 144 investigator-evaluable patients and 47% among 116 evaluable by independent central review; 38 additional centrally assessed patients had a complete response but insufficient follow-up for cCR12. At the interim cut-off, 110/154 (71%) had received no further oncological treatment and had not progressed or relapsed; this does not establish that they will never need further treatment. <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/761174s015lbl.pdf">[FDA label 2026]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0923753426080701">[AZUR-1 congress abstract 2026]</a></li>
<li><strong>Investigator-led phase 2:</strong> All 49 rectal-cancer patients who completed treatment achieved a clinical complete response; in the 50-patient rectal cohort, 2-year recurrence-free survival was 96% (95% CI 90–100), with median recurrence follow-up of 30.2 months. <a class="cite" href="https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12661660/">[NEJM 2025]</a> The FDA’s assessment of the 50-patient cohort reported cCR12 of 93% (95% CI 82–99). <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/761174s015lbl.pdf">[FDA label 2026]</a></li>
</ul>
<p>Both studies were single-arm; comparisons between them are limited by differing assessments and follow-up. In AZUR-1, serious adverse reactions occurred in 20%, immune-mediated reactions in 23%, and adverse reactions led to permanent discontinuation in 3%. <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/761174s015lbl.pdf">[FDA label 2026]</a> India availability and regulatory status may differ from the US; verify current CDSCO status locally.</p>
