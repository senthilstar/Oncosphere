Three Defining Themes of ESMO 2026

  • 1. Engineered Antibodies Dominate: Half of all Presidential LBAs (6/12) evaluated ADCs or bispecifics/BiTEs — enfortumab vedotin, tarlatamab, sonesitatug vedotin, ivonescimab, risvutatug rezetecan, and sacituzumab tirumotecan (Life Science Daily).
  • 2. Higher Bar for Active Comparators: Krascendo 1 beat 1st-gen KRAS G12C inhibitors (sotorasib/adagrasib) and HARMONi-GI1 beat durvalumab + chemotherapy, moving beyond placebo or legacy chemo controls (ESMO).
  • 3. De-Escalation Requires Rigor: MOIO (LBA10) failed non-inferiority with an OS HR of 2.0 (p=0.0143) when spacing ICI to Q12W after 6 months, reinforcing that dose reduction needs the same Phase III proof as intensification (ESMO).
