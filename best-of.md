## Blogs & explainers
- **[NCI Cancer Currents blog](https://www.cancer.gov/news-events/cancer-currents-blog)** `general` — NCI's plain-language research news; reliable first read on new trials.
- **[Cancer Research UK — how cancer starts](https://www.cancerresearchuk.org/about-cancer/what-is-cancer/how-cancer-starts)** `patient explainer` — Clear explainer of genes, cells and how cancer develops.
- **[Cancer Research UK — immunotherapy](https://www.cancerresearchuk.org/about-cancer/treatment/immunotherapy)** `immunotherapy` — Patient-friendly guide to types and side effects of immunotherapy.
- **[Cancer Research UK — causes of cancer](https://www.cancerresearchuk.org/about-cancer/causes-of-cancer)** `prevention` — Evidence-graded guide to real vs myth risk factors.
- **[The Cancer Letter](https://www.cancerletter.com/)** `policy` — Investigative oncology journalism — FDA, NCI, drug pricing.

## Articles (landmark papers)
- **[KEYNOTE-024 — NEJM 2016](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27718847/)** `lung` — Pembrolizumab beat chemotherapy first-line in PD-L1 ≥50% NSCLC; launched IO monotherapy era.
- **[Le et al. — PD-1 blockade in MMR-deficient tumors, NEJM 2015](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26028255/)** `MSI · pan-tumour` — Mismatch-repair deficiency predicted response to PD-1 blockade across tumour types.
- **[PACIFIC — NEJM 2017](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28885881/)** `lung · stage III` — Durvalumab consolidation after chemoradiation in unresectable stage III NSCLC.
- **[DESTINY-Breast04 — NEJM 2022](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35665782/)** `breast · HER2-low` — T-DXd in HER2-low disease created a new treatable category.
- **[Cercek et al. — dostarlimab in dMMR rectal cancer, NEJM 2022](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35660797/)** `rectal · MSI` — PD-1 blockade alone in locally advanced dMMR rectal cancer; organ-sparing possibility.
- **[CheckMate 227 — NEJM 2019](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31562796/)** `lung` — Nivolumab + ipilimumab chemo-free option in advanced NSCLC.
- **[KRYSTAL-1 CRC — NEJM 2023](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36546659/)** `colorectal · KRAS G12C` — Adagrasib ± cetuximab in KRAS G12C colorectal cancer; EGFR co-blockade matters.

## Debates
- **[Mumbai breast screening trial — BMJ 2021](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33627312/)** `breast · screening` — 20-year results of clinical breast exam screening in Mumbai; central to the India screening debate.
- **[Lancet Oncology 2014 — cancer control in China, India, Russia](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24731404/)** `India · policy` — Commission-style analysis of access, cost and workforce gaps — still debated.
- **[PACIFIC vs CheckMate 227: chemo-free IO in lung?](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31562796/)** `lung · debate` — When is dual IO enough? Read alongside KEYNOTE-024 for the PD-L1 debate.
