# Essential oncology reading and listening

A concise selection of trial papers, trusted explainers, podcasts and perspectives on cancer care.

## Recent trial papers

### Lung cancer
- **[Krascendo 1 — NEJM 2026](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2605703)** `lung · KRAS G12C` — In previously treated advanced NSCLC, divarasib improved median PFS versus sotorasib or adagrasib: 9.8 vs 5.7 months; HR 0.58. <a class="cite" href="https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2605703">[NEJM 2026]</a>
- **[SAFFRON — NEJM 2026](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42929693/)** `lung · EGFR/MET` — After osimertinib progression in EGFR-mutant, MET-overexpressed or amplified advanced NSCLC, osimertinib plus savolitinib improved PFS and overall survival versus platinum–pemetrexed.
- **[DeLLphi-305 — NEJM 2026](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42929702/)** `SCLC` — Adding tarlatamab to durvalumab maintenance improved survival versus durvalumab alone in extensive-stage SCLC without progression after initial chemoimmunotherapy.
- **[SACHI — Lancet 2026](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41544643/)** `lung · EGFR/MET` — In China, savolitinib plus osimertinib improved PFS versus platinum–pemetrexed in EGFR-mutant, MET-amplified advanced NSCLC after EGFR inhibitor failure.
- **[SAVANNAH — phase II study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40461383/)** `lung · EGFR/MET` — Single-arm efficacy analysis of savolitinib plus osimertinib after first-line osimertinib progression in EGFR-mutant NSCLC with high MET expression or amplification.

### Other cancers and approvals
- **[ATOMIC — NEJM 2026](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41880612/)** `colon · dMMR` — Adding atezolizumab to adjuvant FOLFOX improved disease-free survival in resected stage III dMMR colon cancer.
- **[HER2CLIMB-05 — phase III study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41369677/)** `breast · HER2` — Adding tucatinib to trastuzumab and pertuzumab maintenance improved progression-free survival after induction without progression in HER2-positive metastatic breast cancer; overall survival data were immature.
- **[PSMAddition — Lancet 2026](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42561994/)** `prostate` — Lutetium Lu-177 PSMA-617 added to androgen deprivation plus an androgen receptor pathway inhibitor improved radiographic PFS in PSMA-positive metastatic hormone-sensitive prostate cancer.
- **[FDA oncology approval notifications](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/cancer-approvals/oncology-cancerhematologic-malignancies-approval-notifications)** `regulatory` — Official announcements with indications and links to supporting information; the list is not exhaustive.

### Evidence and interpretation
- **[KRYSTAL-1 — NEJM 2023](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36546659/)** `colorectal · KRAS G12C` — Nonrandomized cohorts showed activity of adagrasib alone and with cetuximab in heavily pretreated metastatic colorectal cancer; the cohorts were not randomized against each other.
- **[Clinical breast examination screening — BMJ 2021](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33627312/)** `breast · screening` — Mumbai cluster-randomized trial: clinical breast examination downstaged diagnosis; the overall breast cancer mortality reduction was not statistically significant.

- **[Cancer Research UK — how cancer starts](https://www.cancerresearchuk.org/about-cancer/what-is-cancer/how-cancer-start-grow-spread/how-cancer-starts)** `patient explainer` — Patient guide to genes, cell changes and cancer development.
- **[Cancer Research UK — targeted drugs and immunotherapy](https://www.cancerresearchuk.org/about-cancer/treatment/targeted-cancer-drugs-immunotherapy)** `immunotherapy` — Patient information on targeted drugs, immunotherapy and side effects.
- **[Cancer Research UK — causes of cancer](https://www.cancerresearchuk.org/about-cancer/causes-of-cancer)** `prevention` — Patient information on cancer risk factors and prevention.
- **[The Cancer Letter](https://cancerletter.com/)** `policy` — Independent reporting on cancer research, regulation and policy.

## Articles (landmark papers)
- **[KEYNOTE-024 — NEJM 2016](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27718847/)** `lung` — First-line pembrolizumab improved PFS versus chemotherapy in advanced NSCLC with PD-L1 tumour expression ≥50% and no sensitizing EGFR mutation or ALK translocation. <a class="cite" href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27718847/">[NEJM 2016]</a>
- **[Mismatch-repair deficiency and PD-1 blockade — NEJM 2015](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26028255/)** `dMMR · immunotherapy` — Pembrolizumab showed activity in mismatch-repair-deficient colorectal and non-colorectal tumours, unlike mismatch-repair-proficient colorectal cancer.
- **[PACIFIC — NEJM 2017](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28885881/)** `lung · stage III` — Durvalumab after concurrent platinum-based chemoradiation without progression improved progression-free survival in unresectable stage III NSCLC.
- **[DESTINY-Breast04 — NEJM 2022](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35665782/)** `breast · HER2-low` — Trastuzumab deruxtecan improved outcomes versus chemotherapy in previously treated HER2-low metastatic breast cancer.
- **[Dostarlimab in dMMR rectal cancer — NEJM 2022](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35660797/)** `rectal · dMMR` — Neoadjuvant dostarlimab produced clinical complete responses in a small dMMR locally advanced rectal cancer cohort, raising the possibility of organ preservation.
- **[CheckMate 227 — NEJM 2019](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31562796/)** `lung` — First-line nivolumab plus ipilimumab improved overall survival versus chemotherapy in advanced NSCLC with PD-L1 expression.

## Debates
- **[Clinical breast examination screening — BMJ 2021](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33627312/)** `breast · screening` — Mumbai trial: downstaging with clinical breast examination; no statistically significant overall breast cancer mortality reduction. The apparent benefit in older women was a post hoc subgroup finding.
- **[Cancer control in China, India and Russia — Lancet Oncology 2014](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24731404/)** `India · policy` — Commission on cancer control; Indian barriers include affordability, workforce and sociocultural factors.
- **[CheckMate 227 — NEJM 2019](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31562796/)** `lung · treatment choice` — First-line nivolumab plus ipilimumab versus chemotherapy in advanced NSCLC; consider PD-L1 expression and the trial population when weighing treatment choices.
