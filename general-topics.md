## Vaccination during chemotherapy {#vaccination}
- Inactivated vaccines (influenza, COVID-19, pneumococcal) — generally safe; ideally ≥2 weeks before chemo or between cycles.
- Live vaccines (MMR, varicella, oral typhoid) — avoid during chemo and for months after.
- Household contacts should be vaccinated. [IDSA guideline](https://academic.oup.com/cid/article/58/3/e44/336537)

## Febrile neutropenia at home {#febrile-neutropenia}
- Fever ≥38.3 °C once, or ≥38.0 °C for 1 h, with ANC <500 → emergency until proven otherwise.
- Low-risk (MASCC ≥21) adults may get oral antibiotics as outpatients after assessment.
- Keep a thermometer, the oncology helpline number, and go to hospital without delay. [ASCO/IDSA](https://ascopubs.org/doi/10.1200/JCO.18.00374)

## Onco-cardiology basics {#onco-cardiology}
- Anthracyclines, trastuzumab, ICIs (myocarditis), fluoropyrimidines (vasospasm), TKIs (hypertension, QT).
- Baseline ECG/echo for at-risk drugs; control BP, lipids, diabetes. [ESC 2022 cardio-oncology](https://academic.oup.com/eurheartj/article/43/41/4229/6673995)

## CMCHIS / PM-JAY cancer coverage {#cmchis-pmjay}
- PM-JAY: ₹5 lakh/family/year; oncology packages cover chemo, radiation, surgery at empanelled hospitals. [PM-JAY](https://pmjay.gov.in/)
- CMCHIS (Tamil Nadu): ₹5 lakh/family/year; many cancer procedures covered. [CMCHIS](https://www.cmchistn.com/)
- Carry ration/Aadhaar card; ask the hospital's Arogya Mitra desk.
