# General medical topics

## Vaccination during chemotherapy {#vaccination}
- Nonlive influenza, COVID-19 and pneumococcal vaccines: ideally vaccinate 2–4 weeks before treatment; they can also be given during chemotherapy, though responses may be reduced. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1200/JCO.24.00032">[ASCO 2024]</a>
- Live vaccines (MMR, varicella, oral typhoid): avoid during immunosuppressive treatment; timing after treatment depends on immune recovery and therapy. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1200/JCO.24.00032">[ASCO 2024]</a>
- Household contacts should keep their vaccinations up to date to help protect the patient. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1200/JCO.24.00032">[ASCO 2024]</a>

## Febrile neutropenia at home {#febrile-neutropenia}
- Fever during chemotherapy needs urgent assessment; do not wait for a blood count. Febrile neutropenia: oral temperature ≥38.3 °C once or ≥38.0 °C for 1 hour, with ANC <500/µL or expected to fall below 500/µL within 48 hours. <a class="cite" href="https://academic.oup.com/cid/article/52/4/e56/382256">[IDSA 2011]</a>
- Adults with low-risk febrile neutropenia (MASCC score ≥21) may be candidates for outpatient oral antibiotics after clinical assessment and observation; the score alone is insufficient. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1200/JCO.18.00374">[ASCO/IDSA 2018]</a>
- Keep a thermometer and the oncology helpline number handy; follow the team’s fever instructions and seek care promptly.

## Onco-cardiology basics {#onco-cardiology}
- Anthracyclines and trastuzumab: cardiac dysfunction; immune checkpoint inhibitors: myocarditis; fluoropyrimidines: coronary vasospasm; selected tyrosine kinase inhibitors: hypertension or QT prolongation. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1093/eurheartj/ehac244">[ESC 2022]</a>
- Assess cardiovascular risk before treatment; ECG or echocardiography may be needed depending on the treatment and patient. Manage blood pressure, lipids and diabetes. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1093/eurheartj/ehac244">[ESC 2022]</a>

## CMCHIS / PM-JAY cancer coverage {#cmchis-pmjay}
- PM-JAY: eligible families receive up to ₹5 lakh per year at empanelled hospitals; cancer packages include surgery, chemotherapy and radiotherapy, subject to pre-authorisation. <a class="cite" href="https://nha.gov.in/img/resources/Adhikar-Patra.pdf">[NHA]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://supportbeta.nha.gov.in/kb/faq.php?id=57">[NHA cancer FAQ]</a>
- Tamil Nadu’s CMCHIS: eligible families receive up to ₹5 lakh per year at empanelled hospitals; check the proposed cancer treatment against covered packages. <a class="cite" href="https://www.cmchistn.com/salient-features">[CMCHIS]</a>
- Ask the hospital help desk or Arogya Mitra about eligibility, required documents and coverage for the proposed treatment.
