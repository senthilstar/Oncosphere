## Why this matters

PSMAddition tested whether adding PSMA-directed radioligand treatment to androgen deprivation therapy (ADT) and an androgen receptor pathway inhibitor (ARPI) delays progression in metastatic hormone-sensitive prostate cancer. The peer-reviewed phase 3 report and FDA approval establish a new treatment option; they do not yet establish an overall survival advantage. <a class="cite" href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42561994/">[Lancet 2026]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://www.fda.gov/drugs/cancer-approvals/fda-approves-lutetium-lu-177-vipivotide-tetraxetan-androgen-receptor-pathway-inhibitor-therapy">[FDA 2026]</a>

The FDA describes the eligible disease as **metastatic androgen pathway modulation-naive or -sensitive (mAPMN/S)**, previously called metastatic hormone-sensitive prostate cancer (mHSPC). This population is distinct from metastatic castration-resistant disease. <a class="cite" href="https://www.fda.gov/drugs/cancer-approvals/fda-approves-lutetium-lu-177-vipivotide-tetraxetan-androgen-receptor-pathway-inhibitor-therapy">[FDA 2026]</a>

## How Lu-PSMA-617 works

Lu-PSMA-617 links a PSMA-binding ligand to a beta-emitting radionuclide. Radiation affects target-expressing cells and nearby tissue; normal-organ exposure remains relevant. <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/215833s037lbl.pdf">[FDA label 2026]</a>

PSMA PET identifies target expression, but a positive scan does not guarantee response at every disease site. Interpret uptake alongside conventional imaging and the clinical picture.

## PSMAddition design

This open-label, randomised phase 3 superiority study enrolled at **169 sites in 20 countries**. Metastases had to be visible on conventional imaging, with at least one PSMA-positive metastatic lesion on centrally reviewed gallium PSMA-11 PET. <a class="cite" href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42561994/">[Lancet 2026]</a>

Prior systemic treatment was restricted: neoadjuvant or adjuvant ADT was allowed, as was up to **45 days** of ADT, ARPI or both for metastatic disease before consent. A PET-positive lesion needed uptake above normal liver; patients whose liver metastases were all PET-negative were excluded. <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/215833s037lbl.pdf">[FDA label 2026]</a>

Patients were assigned **1:1** to ADT plus an investigator-selected ARPI, with or without intravenous Lu-PSMA-617 **7.4 GBq every 6 weeks for up to 6 cycles**. <a class="cite" href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42561994/">[Lancet 2026]</a> Randomisation was stratified by disease volume, age and previous or planned primary-tumour treatment; control-arm crossover was permitted after centrally confirmed radiographic progression. <a class="cite" href="https://s3.eu-central-1.amazonaws.com/m-anage.com.storage.esmo/static/esmo2025_abstracts/LBA6.html.pdf">[ESMO 2025]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42561994/">[Lancet 2026]</a>

The primary endpoint was centrally assessed radiographic progression-free survival (rPFS), including death; overall survival (OS), response, safety and patient-reported outcomes were secondary measures. Central imaging review reduces assessment bias, although open-label treatment may influence symptom reporting and subsequent care. The comparison tests addition to ADT and ARPI, not omission of either component. <a class="cite" href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42561994/">[Lancet 2026]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://s3.eu-central-1.amazonaws.com/m-anage.com.storage.esmo/static/esmo2025_abstracts/LBA6.html.pdf">[ESMO 2025]</a>

## Who was enrolled

Of **1,144** randomised participants, **572** were assigned to each arm; **50%** had de novo metastases, **68%** high-volume disease, and median age was **68 years**. <a class="cite" href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42561994/">[Lancet 2026]</a> The overall result is useful for counselling, but estimates in smaller subgroups are less precise.

## Efficacy: rPFS and immature OS

At the prespecified primary rPFS analysis, with a **13 January 2025** cutoff, progression or death occurred in **139/572 (24.3%) versus 172/572 (30.1%)**. <a class="cite" href="https://s3.eu-central-1.amazonaws.com/m-anage.com.storage.esmo/static/esmo2025_abstracts/LBA6.html.pdf">[ESMO 2025]</a> Median rPFS was not reached in either arm; the HR was **0.72 (95% CI 0.58–0.90; p=0.0021)**. <a class="cite" href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42561994/">[Lancet 2026]</a> This shows a difference in the rate of radiographic progression or death over follow-up; it does not specify the extra time gained by an individual.

At the same cutoff, interim OS included **85 versus 99 deaths**, with HR **0.84 (95% CI 0.63–1.13)**. <a class="cite" href="https://s3.eu-central-1.amazonaws.com/m-anage.com.storage.esmo/static/esmo2025_abstracts/LBA6.html.pdf">[ESMO 2025]</a> Among patients with measurable disease, objective response was **85.3% versus 80.8%**; response and survival answer different questions. <a class="cite" href="https://s3.eu-central-1.amazonaws.com/m-anage.com.storage.esmo/static/esmo2025_abstracts/LBA6.html.pdf">[ESMO 2025]</a>

The sponsor subsequently reported a **final rPFS analysis** at the **17 March 2026** cutoff: HR **0.67 (95% CI 0.55–0.82)**, with median rPFS not reached versus **48.5 months (95% CI 40.5–not estimable)**. <a class="cite" href="https://www.pluvicto-hcp.com/psma-positive-mhspc/efficacy">[Sponsor-reported 2026]</a> This later rPFS analysis was not controlled for type I error; the sponsor reports a second interim OS HR of **0.80 (95% CI 0.63–1.01)**, with median OS not reached in either arm. <a class="cite" href="https://www.pluvicto-hcp.com/psma-positive-mhspc/efficacy">[Sponsor-reported 2026]</a> The FDA label describes OS as immature, with **291 deaths** across both arms; the agency requires a final OS analysis. <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/215833s037lbl.pdf">[FDA label 2026]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/appletter/2026/215833Orig1s037ltr.pdf">[FDA 2026]</a>

These later HRs are sponsor-reported and have not been located in a later peer-reviewed trial report. Crossover and subsequent therapies complicate OS interpretation; the trial does not prove that starting radioligand treatment earlier improves survival.

## Toxicity and practical safety

In the treated population, grade **≥3 adverse events occurred in 286/564 (50.7%) versus 243/565 (43.0%)**, and serious adverse events in **180/564 (31.9%) versus 162/565 (28.7%)**. <a class="cite" href="https://s3.eu-central-1.amazonaws.com/m-anage.com.storage.esmo/static/esmo2025_abstracts/LBA6.html.pdf">[ESMO 2025]</a> Dry mouth was grade **1 in 41.0% versus 3.4%**, and grade **2 in 4.8% versus 0.4%**; no cases were serious, but symptoms may affect daily life. <a class="cite" href="https://s3.eu-central-1.amazonaws.com/m-anage.com.storage.esmo/static/esmo2025_abstracts/LBA6.html.pdf">[ESMO 2025]</a> Grade **≥3 cytopenias occurred in 14.4% versus 5.0%**. <a class="cite" href="https://s3.eu-central-1.amazonaws.com/m-anage.com.storage.esmo/static/esmo2025_abstracts/LBA6.html.pdf">[ESMO 2025]</a>

The reported time to worsening on quality-of-life measures did not establish a meaningful benefit; open-label reporting and follow-up warrant caution. <a class="cite" href="https://s3.eu-central-1.amazonaws.com/m-anage.com.storage.esmo/static/esmo2025_abstracts/LBA6.html.pdf">[ESMO 2025]</a> The label warns of radiation exposure, myelosuppression, renal and embryo-fetal toxicity, and infertility. Check blood counts and renal function, support hydration and salivary care, and follow the nuclear medicine team's radiation-safety instructions; manage delays or dose changes according to the applicable label. <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/215833s037lbl.pdf">[FDA label 2026]</a>

## The label

On **31 July 2026**, the FDA approved lutetium Lu 177 vipivotide tetraxetan with an ARPI for adults with PSMA-positive mAPMN/S prostate cancer. Selection uses Locametz or another approved PSMA PET product. <a class="cite" href="https://www.fda.gov/drugs/cancer-approvals/fda-approves-lutetium-lu-177-vipivotide-tetraxetan-androgen-receptor-pathway-inhibitor-therapy">[FDA 2026]</a> The US schedule is **7.4 GBq every 6 weeks for 6 doses**, or until progression or unacceptable toxicity. <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/215833s037lbl.pdf">[FDA label 2026]</a>

The trial allowed investigator-selected abiraterone, apalutamide, enzalutamide, darolutamide or another ARPI; patients also received medical castration or had undergone bilateral orchiectomy. <a class="cite" href="https://www.fda.gov/drugs/cancer-approvals/fda-approves-lutetium-lu-177-vipivotide-tetraxetan-androgen-receptor-pathway-inhibitor-therapy">[FDA 2026]</a> The US decision does not itself establish approval in another jurisdiction.

## What it changes in practice

For a patient with PSMA-positive metastatic disease, assess fitness, marrow and renal reserve, imaging eligibility and the practical burden of nuclear medicine treatment. Discuss delayed radiographic progression alongside toxicity and the unresolved OS question.

The published subgroup analysis found an rPFS HR of **0.72 (95% CI 0.56–0.92)** in high-volume and **0.73 (95% CI 0.42–1.27)** in low-volume disease; the latter estimate is imprecise and cannot establish equal benefit between subgroups. <a class="cite" href="https://ascopubs.org/doi/pdfdirect/10.1200/JCO.2026.44.16_suppl.5020">[JCO 2026]</a> De novo and recurrent presentations also need individual assessment. Docetaxel triplets and PARP inhibitor combinations are alternative intensification routes for selected patients. The [TALAPRO-3 deep dive]({{ '/talazoparib-enzalutamide-hrr-mhspc-talapro-3/' | relative_url }}) discusses an HRR-selected PARP strategy. These strategies were not directly compared in PSMAddition; fitness, biology, toxicity, access and patient preference matter.

Whether prior Lu-PSMA treatment changes later options at castration resistance, including retreatment, remains unresolved. Do not present early use as a proven OS gain.

## India and resource-limited settings

PSMA PET and Lu-177 therapy are available in India, but access varies. Travel, scheduling and the cost of the full treatment course and PET can determine feasibility. Discuss monitoring and supportive care before starting.

ADT plus an ARPI, including generic abiraterone, or docetaxel remain appropriate standards for selected patients. We did not verify CDSCO approval of Pluvicto for this indication or an Indian price.

## Open questions

- Will mature OS demonstrate a survival benefit, and how will crossover and later therapies affect its interpretation?
- Which patients with lower-volume or recurrent disease benefit most, and can imaging improve selection?
- How should radioligand therapy be sequenced with docetaxel and biomarker-directed combinations?
- Can retreatment help after earlier exposure, and how can access improve?

## Bottom line

For selected patients with PSMA-positive metastatic hormone-sensitive disease, adding Lu-PSMA-617 to ADT and an ARPI delays radiographic progression. **An OS benefit remains unproven**; balance the established rPFS result against toxicity, logistics and access. <a class="cite" href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42561994/">[Lancet 2026]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/215833s037lbl.pdf">[FDA label 2026]</a>
