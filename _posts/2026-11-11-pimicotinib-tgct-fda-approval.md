On 10 November 2026, the US Food and Drug Administration approved **pimicotinib** (Pimjetmi), an oral CSF-1 receptor inhibitor, for adults with symptomatic tenosynovial giant cell tumour (TGCT) when surgery may worsen functional limitation or cause severe morbidity. <a class="cite" href="https://www.fda.gov/drugs/cancer-approvals/fda-approves-pimicotinib-symptomatic-tenosynovial-giant-cell-tumor">[FDA 2026]</a> The recommended dose is **50 mg orally once daily**, with or without food; swallow the capsules whole. <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/220414s000lbl.pdf">[FDA label 2026]</a>

TGCT is a locally aggressive tumour of the joint lining or tendon sheath. Surgery remains central to care, but diffuse or recurrent disease can impair movement and repeated operations may add morbidity.

## MANEUVER in brief

In the international, double-blind, placebo-controlled phase 3 MANEUVER trial, 94 patients were randomised 2:1 to pimicotinib (63) or placebo (31), stratified by region. <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/220414s000lbl.pdf">[FDA label 2026]</a> Eligible patients had symptomatic disease for which surgery could cause dysfunction or serious complications and at least one measurable lesion of 2 cm or more. <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/220414s000lbl.pdf">[FDA label 2026]</a> The published trial required worst pain or stiffness of at least 4 on a 0–10 scale. <a class="cite" href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41796601/">[Lancet 2026]</a>

The FDA label reports overall response rate (ORR) at week 25 by blinded independent central review using RECIST v1.1. <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/220414s000lbl.pdf">[FDA label 2026]</a>

| Week 25 (BICR, RECIST v1.1) | Pimicotinib | Placebo |
|---|---|---|
| Overall response rate | **35/63, 56%** (95% CI 42–68) <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/220414s000lbl.pdf">[FDA label 2026]</a> | 1/31, 3.2% (95% CI 0.1–17) <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/220414s000lbl.pdf">[FDA label 2026]</a> |

- **Published primary analysis:** ORR was 34/63 (54%) versus 1/31 (3%); the absolute difference was 51 percentage points (95% CI 33–63). <a class="cite" href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41796601/">[Lancet 2026]</a> This is a separate analysis from the FDA label estimate. <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/220414s000lbl.pdf">[FDA label 2026]</a>
- **Duration of response:** in the FDA analysis, median duration was not reached; 32/35 responders (91%) had responses lasting at least 6 months and 27/35 (77%) at least 12 months. <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/220414s000lbl.pdf">[FDA label 2026]</a>
- **Function and symptoms:** at week 25, active joint range of motion, worst pain and physical function improved significantly versus placebo. <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/220414s000lbl.pdf">[FDA label 2026]</a>
- **Longer follow-up:** an ESMO 2026 poster reported a best overall RECIST response of 76.2% (95% CI 63.8–86.0) among the 63 patients originally assigned pimicotinib at the 1 March 2026 cutoff; 34 remained on treatment. <a class="cite" href="https://medical.emdserono.com/content/dam/web/healthcare/biopharma/web/USMI/congress-presentations/oncology/esmo--2026/gelderblom-esmo-2026-maneuver-2yr-efficacy-safety-poster-approved-26-oct-2026.pdf">[ESMO 2026]</a> This ongoing extension is open label after the placebo-controlled period, so the later response rate is not a randomised comparison. <a class="cite" href="https://medical.emdserono.com/content/dam/web/healthcare/biopharma/web/USMI/congress-presentations/oncology/esmo--2026/gelderblom-esmo-2026-maneuver-2yr-efficacy-safety-poster-approved-26-oct-2026.pdf">[ESMO 2026]</a>

## Safety

Common adverse reactions (>20%) included pruritus, rash, facial and periorbital oedema, fatigue, nausea, headache and peripheral oedema; common laboratory abnormalities (>30%) included elevations in creatine phosphokinase, lactate dehydrogenase, AST, lipase, cholesterol, amylase and ALT. <a class="cite" href="https://www.fda.gov/drugs/cancer-approvals/fda-approves-pimicotinib-symptomatic-tenosynovial-giant-cell-tumor">[FDA 2026]</a>

The label warns about hepatotoxicity, embryo-fetal toxicity and increased serum creatinine without an effect on renal function. <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/220414s000lbl.pdf">[FDA label 2026]</a> Avoid treatment in active liver or biliary disease; check AST, ALT, ALP, GGT and bilirubin before treatment, monthly for the first 3 months, then every 3 months during the first year and subsequently as clinically indicated. <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/220414s000lbl.pdf">[FDA label 2026]</a> Use a non-creatinine-based measure if renal function needs assessment, and review CYP3A interactions before prescribing. <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/220414s000lbl.pdf">[FDA label 2026]</a> The label advises effective contraception during treatment and for 2 weeks after the last dose for patients who can become pregnant and male patients with partners who can become pregnant. <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/220414s000lbl.pdf">[FDA label 2026]</a>

## Regulatory notes

The FDA review used Project Orbis and the Assessment Aid; pimicotinib had fast track and breakthrough therapy designations. <a class="cite" href="https://www.fda.gov/drugs/cancer-approvals/fda-approves-pimicotinib-symptomatic-tenosynovial-giant-cell-tumor">[FDA 2026]</a> The full US prescribing information is now available. <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/220414s000lbl.pdf">[FDA label 2026]</a>

## For readers in India

An FDA approval does not establish CDSCO approval, availability or reimbursement in India. For localised TGCT, discussion with an experienced orthopaedic oncology team remains central; systemic treatment is relevant when surgery risks substantial functional loss or morbidity. Liver monitoring and reproductive counselling should follow the applicable prescribing information. We will update this note if an Indian approval or access route is announced.
