On 10 November 2026 the US Food and Drug Administration **approved pimicotinib** (Pimjetmi, EMD Serono), an oral kinase inhibitor, for adults with **symptomatic tenosynovial giant cell tumour (TGCT)** for which surgical resection may cause worsening functional limitation or severe morbidity. The recommended dose is **50 mg orally once daily**, with or without food.

TGCT is a locally aggressive, usually non-metastasising tumour of the joint lining and tendon sheath. Surgery remains the mainstay, but diffuse or recurrent disease around large joints can leave patients with pain and stiffness, and repeated operations can make function worse. That is the group this label addresses.

## MANEUVER in brief
Efficacy was evaluated in MANEUVER ([NCT05804045](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05804045)), a double-blind, placebo-controlled, international trial. Patients with symptomatic TGCT, at least one measurable lesion of 2 cm or more, and disease where resection could cause dysfunction or serious complications were randomised 2:1 to pimicotinib 50 mg or placebo once daily, stratified by region (China vs non-China).

The major efficacy outcome was overall response rate by blinded independent central review per RECIST v1.1 at week 25:

| Week 25 (BICR, RECIST v1.1) | Pimicotinib | Placebo |
|---|---|---|
| Overall response rate | **56%** (95% CI 42–68) | 3.2% (95% CI 0.1–17) |

- **Duration of response:** median not reached; 32 responders (91%) had a response lasting at least 6 months and 27 (77%) at least 12 months.
- **Function and symptoms:** at week 25 the pimicotinib arm showed statistically significant improvements over placebo in active range of motion of the affected joint (goniometry), worst pain (BPI Worst Pain NRS) and physical functioning (PROMIS Physical Function T-score).

The FDA notice does not give the size of each arm, so we have not quoted one here.

## Safety
The most common adverse reactions (>20%) were pruritus, rash, face oedema, periorbital oedema, fatigue, nausea, headache and peripheral oedema. The most common laboratory abnormalities (>30%) were raised creatine phosphokinase, lactate dehydrogenase, AST, lipase, cholesterol, amylase and ALT.

The prescribing information carries warnings and precautions for **hepatotoxicity**, **embryo-fetal toxicity**, and **increased serum creatinine without an effect on renal function**. Liver tests deserve attention in practice; the full prescribing information will be posted on Drugs@FDA.

## Regulatory notes
Pimicotinib had fast track and breakthrough therapy designations. The review ran under Project Orbis with Australia’s TGA, Health Canada, Israel’s Ministry of Health, Swissmedic and the UK MHRA, with the EMA as an observer; those agencies may still be reviewing their applications. The FDA also used the Assessment Aid.

## For readers in India
An FDA approval does not mean CDSCO approval, availability or reimbursement here. For most patients with localised TGCT, surgery by an experienced orthopaedic oncology team is still the first conversation; systemic therapy is for disease where an operation is likely to cost more function than it saves. Baseline and periodic liver tests, and a pregnancy plan for patients of reproductive age, follow directly from the label’s warnings. We will update this note if an Indian approval or access route is announced.
