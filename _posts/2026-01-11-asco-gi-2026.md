## HERIZON-GEA-01 — HER2+ gastro-oesophageal adenocarcinoma (first line)
- Phase 3, n=914 (A 308 / B 304 / C 302). Arms: trastuzumab + chemo (A), zanidatamab + chemo (B), zanidatamab + tislelizumab + chemo (C); chemo = CAPOX or FP. Mandatory antidiarrhoeal prophylaxis. Median follow-up 25.9 mo. <a class="cite" href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42202319/">[NEJM 2026]</a>
- PFS: 8.1 (A) vs **12.4** (B; HR 0.65, 95% CI 0.52–0.81) vs **12.4 mo** (C; HR 0.63, 0.51–0.78); P<0.001 for both.
- OS (interim): 19.2 vs 24.4 (B; HR 0.80, 0.64–1.01, p=0.06, not significant) vs **26.4 mo** (C; HR 0.72, 0.57–0.90, p=0.004).
- Grade ≥3 AEs (published paper): 74.5% (A) / 73.8% (B) / 83.3% (C). Grade ≥3 diarrhoea 12.9% / 20.0% / 24.8%. <a class="cite" href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42202319/">[NEJM 2026]</a> (Earlier symposium figures of 60% / 59% / 72% are superseded by the full paper.)
- **FDA, 25 Aug 2026:** zanidatamab + tislelizumab + chemo approved for HER2 IHC 3+ or IHC 2+/ISH+ disease; zanidatamab + chemo (no PD-1) only for **IHC 3+**, because the arm B effect was mainly in IHC 3+ tumours (exploratory: PFS 14.2 vs 7.6 mo, HR 0.55). The zanidatamab label carries a boxed warning for diarrhoea and embryo-fetal toxicity. <a class="cite" href="https://www.fda.gov/drugs/resources-information-approved-drugs/fda-approves-zanidatamab-hrii-and-tislelizumab-jsgr-her2-positive-gastric-gastroesophageal-junction">[FDA 2026]</a>

## BREAKWATER — BRAF V600E mCRC (first line)
Cohort 3 (n=147): encorafenib + cetuximab + FOLFIRI vs FOLFIRI ± bevacizumab. ORR (BICR, primary endpoint) **64.4% vs 39.2%** (odds ratio 2.76, 95% CI 1.42–5.35; Abstract 13). <a class="cite" href="https://ascopubs.org/doi/10.1200/JCO.2026.44.2_suppl.13">[JCO 2026 Abstract 13]</a> Later analysis (cut-off 6 Jan 2026): PFS **15.2 vs 8.3 mo, HR 0.44** (95% CI 0.27–0.70); OS HR 0.56 (0.34–0.94; median not estimable vs 20.3 mo). Serious AEs 49.3% vs 44.1%. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1016/j.annonc.2026.04.017">[Ann Oncol 2026]</a> This extends the FOLFOX-based BREAKWATER result (PFS 12.8 vs 7.1 mo, HR 0.53; OS 30.3 vs 15.1 mo, HR 0.49). The 2024 accelerated approval became a **traditional FDA approval on 24 Feb 2026**, now covering encorafenib + cetuximab with any fluorouracil-based chemotherapy, FOLFIRI included. <a class="cite" href="https://www.fda.gov/drugs/resources-information-approved-drugs/fda-grants-traditional-approval-encorafenib-metastatic-colorectal-cancer-braf-v600e-mutation">[FDA 2026]</a>

## COMMIT — dMMR/MSI-H mCRC
n=102 (randomised phase 3 NRG-GI004/SWOG-S1610; chemo-bev arm closed after 20 patients because of KEYNOTE-177, leaving 41 vs 41). mFOLFOX6 + bevacizumab + atezolizumab vs atezolizumab alone: PFS HR **0.439** (95% CI 0.23–0.84; P=0.0103, primary analysis); updated (median follow-up 46 mo) median PFS **24.5 vs 5.3 mo** (HR 0.42); 24-month PFS 53.7% vs 31.6%. ORR 86.1% vs 46%; primary progression 2.8% vs 32.4%. More grade ≥3 AEs (34 vs 18 patients) and grade 5 events (4 vs 1). <a class="cite" href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42525921/">[JCO 2026]</a>

## Practical take-aways for the clinic
- **HER2-positive gastric/GEJ:** confirm HER2 by IHC (and ISH for 2+); zanidatamab-based regimens require proactive diarrhoea management. Pembrolizumab + trastuzumab + chemotherapy (KEYNOTE-811) remains an alternative standard for PD-L1 CPS ≥1 disease in many guidelines; HERIZON-GEA-01 is now published (NEJM 2026) and FDA-approved (Aug 2026). The PD-1-free zanidatamab regimen is labelled only for IHC 3+, and final OS for arm B is still awaited.
- **BRAF V600E colorectal:** test BRAF together with RAS and MMR at diagnosis. Both FOLFOX- and FOLFIRI-based encorafenib + cetuximab schedules now have randomised data, allowing a choice based on neuropathy risk and prior oxaliplatin.
- **dMMR colorectal:** immunotherapy first. KEYNOTE-177 established pembrolizumab over chemotherapy (PFS 16.5 vs 8.2 months, HR 0.60, 95% CI 0.45–0.80), <a class="cite" href="https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2017699">[NEJM 2020]</a> and nivolumab + ipilimumab is a further option. COMMIT asks whether adding chemotherapy helps — interesting, but toxicity was higher.

## Other themes from the symposium
- **Biomarker-first GI oncology:** HER2, MMR/MSI, BRAF, Claudin 18.2 and PD-L1 CPS now each change first-line therapy somewhere in the GI tract. Ordering them together on the diagnostic biopsy saves weeks.
- **Toxicity management as part of the regimen:** HERIZON-GEA-01 mandated antidiarrhoeal prophylaxis — a reminder that supportive care protocols are part of reproducing trial results.
- **Chemotherapy backbones still matter:** both BREAKWATER cohorts and COMMIT combined new agents with FOLFOX/FOLFIRI, the regimens most Indian centres already deliver well.

## Reading conference data
Symposium data are often presented before peer review; numbers can change at publication. Interim OS analyses (as in HERIZON-GEA-01) may not be final, and subgroup analyses are exploratory. Where full papers exist, the figures above are taken from them; as of 28 Oct 2026, all three headline trials (HERIZON-GEA-01, BREAKWATER Cohort 3, COMMIT) have full publications.
