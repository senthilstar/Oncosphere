The symposium took place in San Francisco on 8–10 January 2026; the trial summaries below incorporate later reports where available. <a class="cite" href="https://www.asco.org/about-asco/press-center/news-releases/glp-1s-exercise-colorectal-cancer-treatment-highlighted-2026-asco-gastrointestinal-cancers-symposium">[ASCO 2026]</a>

## HERIZON-GEA-01 — HER2+ gastro-oesophageal adenocarcinoma (first line)
- This phase 3 trial randomised 914 patients with previously untreated advanced disease to trastuzumab plus chemotherapy (A, n=308), zanidatamab plus chemotherapy (B, n=304), or zanidatamab, tislelizumab and chemotherapy (C, n=302); chemotherapy was CAPOX or fluorouracil/cisplatin. <a class="cite" href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42202319/">[NEJM 2026]</a>
- At 25.9 months' median follow-up, centrally assessed median PFS was 8.1 months in A, 12.4 in B (HR 0.65, 95% CI 0.52–0.81) and 12.4 in C (HR 0.63, 95% CI 0.51–0.78); both comparisons with A had P<0.001. <a class="cite" href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42202319/">[NEJM 2026]</a>
- At the later, second interim and final formal OS analysis, sponsor-reported median OS was 19.6 months in A, 22.9 in B (HR 0.76, 95% CI 0.62–0.93; P=0.0088) and 27.0 in C (HR 0.65, 95% CI 0.52–0.80), each compared with A. <a class="cite" href="https://investor.jazzpharma.com/news-releases/news-release-details/ziiherar-zanidatamab-hrii-combinations-demonstrate-sustained">[ESMO 2026, sponsor-reported]</a> The comparison of C with B did not reach statistical significance (HR 0.81, 95% CI 0.65–1.00; P=0.0502). <a class="cite" href="https://investor.jazzpharma.com/news-releases/news-release-details/ziiherar-zanidatamab-hrii-combinations-demonstrate-sustained">[ESMO 2026, sponsor-reported]</a>
- In the published analysis, grade ≥3 adverse events occurred in 74.5%, 73.8% and 83.3% in A, B and C; grade ≥3 diarrhoea occurred in 12.9%, 20.0% and 24.8%, respectively. <a class="cite" href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42202319/">[NEJM 2026]</a> In the zanidatamab arms, loperamide 4 mg twice daily was mandatory for the first 7 days of cycle 1. <a class="cite" href="https://www.beonemedinfo.com/CongressDocuments/Elimova_HERIZON-GEA-01_Diarrhea_Safety_ASCO_Poster_2026.pdf">[ASCO 2026]</a>
- **FDA, 25 August 2026:** for adults with locally advanced unresectable or metastatic gastric, GEJ or oesophageal adenocarcinoma, zanidatamab with tislelizumab and fluoropyrimidine/platinum chemotherapy is approved for HER2 IHC 3+ or IHC 2+/ISH-positive disease; zanidatamab with chemotherapy alone is restricted to IHC 3+. <a class="cite" href="https://www.fda.gov/drugs/cancer-approvals/fda-approves-zanidatamab-hrii-and-tislelizumab-jsgr-her2-positive-gastric-gastroesophageal-junction">[FDA 2026]</a> The zanidatamab label has boxed warnings for diarrhoea and embryo-fetal toxicity. <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/761416s003lbl.pdf">[FDA label 2026]</a>

## BREAKWATER — BRAF V600E mCRC (first line)
Cohort 3 randomised 147 patients to encorafenib plus cetuximab and FOLFIRI or FOLFIRI with or without bevacizumab. <a class="cite" href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42219860/">[Ann Oncol 2026]</a> Centrally assessed objective response, the primary endpoint, was 64.4% versus 39.2% (odds ratio 2.76, 95% CI 1.42–5.35). <a class="cite" href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42219860/">[Ann Oncol 2026]</a> At the 6 January 2026 cut-off, median PFS was 15.2 versus 8.3 months (HR 0.44, 95% CI 0.27–0.70); median OS was not estimable versus 20.3 months (HR 0.56, 95% CI 0.34–0.94). <a class="cite" href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42219860/">[Ann Oncol 2026]</a> Serious adverse events occurred in 49.3% versus 44.1%. <a class="cite" href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42219860/">[Ann Oncol 2026]</a>

In the separate mFOLFOX6 comparison, median PFS was 12.8 versus 7.1 months (HR 0.53), and median OS was 30.3 versus 15.1 months (HR 0.49), against standard chemotherapy with or without bevacizumab. <a class="cite" href="https://www.asco.org/abstracts-presentations/243633">[ASCO 2025]</a> On 24 February 2026, the FDA granted traditional approval to encorafenib with cetuximab and fluorouracil-based chemotherapy for adults with BRAF V600E-mutated metastatic colorectal cancer detected by an FDA-authorized test. <a class="cite" href="https://www.fda.gov/drugs/cancer-approvals/fda-grants-traditional-approval-encorafenib-metastatic-colorectal-cancer-braf-v600e-mutation">[FDA 2026]</a>

## COMMIT — dMMR/MSI-H mCRC
The phase 3 trial enrolled 102 patients; the chemotherapy–bevacizumab-only arm closed after 20 patients, leaving 41 each assigned to atezolizumab alone or mFOLFOX6, bevacizumab and atezolizumab. <a class="cite" href="https://ascopubs.org/doi/10.1200/JCO.2026.44.2_suppl.14">[JCO 2026 abstract]</a> At the updated analysis, median PFS favoured the combination: 24.5 versus 5.3 months (HR 0.42, 95% CI 0.22–0.80; P=0.0068). <a class="cite" href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42525921/">[JCO 2026]</a> Objective response was 86.1% versus 46%; grade ≥3 adverse events occurred in 34 versus 18 patients. <a class="cite" href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42525921/">[JCO 2026]</a>

<p class="opinion">The atezolizumab-only PFS appears low in comparison with other immunotherapy trials; cross-trial comparisons are unreliable. COMMIT alone does not establish that chemotherapy should be added for every patient with dMMR metastatic colorectal cancer.</p>

## Practical take-aways for the clinic
- **HER2-positive gastric/GEJ:** confirm HER2 by IHC and reflex ISH for IHC 2+; plan diarrhoea prophylaxis and prompt treatment with zanidatamab. <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/761416s003lbl.pdf">[FDA label 2026]</a> Pembrolizumab, trastuzumab and fluoropyrimidine/platinum chemotherapy is another FDA-approved first-line option for PD-L1 CPS ≥1 disease. <a class="cite" href="https://www.fda.gov/drugs/cancer-approvals/fda-approves-pembrolizumab-her2-positive-gastric-or-gastroesophageal-junction-adenocarcinoma">[FDA 2025]</a>
- **BRAF V600E colorectal:** test BRAF alongside RAS and MMR when metastatic disease is diagnosed. Randomised evidence supports encorafenib and cetuximab with either FOLFOX or FOLFIRI; neuropathy and prior oxaliplatin exposure can inform the backbone. <a class="cite" href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42219860/">[Ann Oncol 2026]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://www.asco.org/abstracts-presentations/243633">[ASCO 2025]</a>
- **dMMR colorectal:** immunotherapy is a first-line option. In KEYNOTE-177, pembrolizumab improved median PFS over chemotherapy, 16.5 versus 8.2 months (HR 0.60, 95% CI 0.45–0.80). <a class="cite" href="https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2017699">[NEJM 2020]</a> Nivolumab plus ipilimumab is another option; weigh the PFS signal from COMMIT against the added toxicity and the limited size of the comparison.

## Other themes from the symposium
- **Biomarker-led treatment:** HER2, MMR/MSI, BRAF, claudin 18.2 and PD-L1 can affect treatment selection in advanced GI cancers. Order relevant tests early enough to guide the initial decision.
- **Supportive care:** diarrhoea prevention and early treatment matter when using zanidatamab-based regimens. <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/761416s003lbl.pdf">[FDA label 2026]</a>
- **Backbone selection:** FOLFOX and FOLFIRI remain relevant when incorporating targeted therapy into colorectal cancer treatment.

## Reading conference data
Meeting results can change with longer follow-up and peer review. The HERIZON-GEA-01 survival update above is sponsor-reported from a later congress analysis; its PFS and toxicity figures come from the published primary analysis. Subgroup findings and comparisons across trials need particular caution.
