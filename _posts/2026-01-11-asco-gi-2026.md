## HERIZON-GEA-01 — HER2+ gastro-oesophageal adenocarcinoma (first line)
- Phase 3, n=914. Arms: trastuzumab + chemo (A), zanidatamab + chemo (B), zanidatamab + tislelizumab + chemo (C). Mandatory antidiarrhoeal prophylaxis.
- PFS: 8.1 (A) vs **12.4** (B; HR 0.65) vs **12.4 mo** (C; HR 0.63).
- OS (interim): 19.2 vs 24.4 (HR 0.80, p=0.056, not significant) vs **26.4 mo** (HR 0.72, p=0.0043).
- Grade ≥3 AEs: 60% / 59% / 72%. Diarrhoea the main toxicity.

## BREAKWATER — BRAF V600E mCRC (first line)
Cohort 3 (n=147): encorafenib + cetuximab + FOLFIRI vs FOLFIRI ± bevacizumab. ORR (BICR, primary endpoint) **64.4% vs 39.2%** (odds ratio 2.76, 95% CI 1.42–5.35; Abstract 13). <a class="cite" href="https://ascopubs.org/doi/10.1200/JCO.2026.44.2_suppl.13">[JCO 2026 Abstract 13]</a> Later analysis (cut-off 6 Jan 2026): PFS **15.2 vs 8.3 mo, HR 0.44** (95% CI 0.27–0.70); OS HR 0.56 (0.34–0.94). <a class="cite" href="https://www.annalsofoncology.org/article/S0923-7534(26)00179-1/fulltext">[Ann Oncol 2026]</a> This extends the FOLFOX-based BREAKWATER result that led to FDA accelerated approval.

## COMMIT — dMMR/MSI-H mCRC
n=102 (randomised phase 3; chemo-bev arm closed after KEYNOTE-177). mFOLFOX6 + bevacizumab + atezolizumab vs atezolizumab alone: median PFS **24.5 vs ~5 mo**; ORR 86.1% vs 46%. More grade ≥3 AEs (34 vs 18 patients) and grade 5 events (4 vs 1).
