At the ESMO Presidential Symposium II in Madrid on 25 October 2026, four phase 3 studies reported longer overall survival (OS) with an experimental treatment than with an active comparator. Gastric and biliary findings are from interim analyses and remain sponsor-reported. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2605703">[NEJM 2026]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2611335">[NEJM 2026]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://www.astrazeneca.com/media-centre/press-releases/2026/sonesitatug-vedotin-reduced-the-risk-of-death-by-42-pct-in-2nd-and-later-line-cldn182-positive-advanced-gastricgej-cancers-in-clarity-gastric01.html">[AstraZeneca 2026, sponsor-reported]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://markets.ft.markitdigital.com/data/announce/detail?dockey=600-202610251152BIZWIRE_USPRX____20261025_BW603151-1">[Summit 2026, sponsor-reported]</a>

<p class="note"><strong>Bottom line:</strong> The lung trials select for KRAS G12C mutation or MET alteration after osimertinib; the gastrointestinal trials study CLDN18.2-expressing gastro-oesophageal cancer and untreated advanced biliary cancer.</p>

## LBA5 — Krascendo 1 (divarasib)
- **Design:** In this open-label study, 338 patients with previously treated advanced KRAS G12C-mutant NSCLC were assigned to divarasib 400 mg daily (167) or a control KRAS inhibitor (171: sotorasib 960 mg daily in 156, adagrasib 600 mg twice daily in 15). Median follow-up was 14.3 months. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2605703">[NEJM 2026]</a>
- **PFS:** BICR-assessed median PFS was 9.8 versus 5.7 months; HR 0.58 (95% CI 0.44–0.76; P < .001). <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2605703">[NEJM 2026]</a>
- **OS:** At the first interim OS analysis, median survival was 17.7 versus 12.0 months; HR 0.56 (95% CI 0.41–0.78; P < .001). <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2605703">[NEJM 2026]</a>
- **Response:** In 328 response-evaluable patients, confirmed objective response rate (ORR) was 58.4% versus 32.3%; median response duration was 11.9 versus 6.9 months. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2605703">[NEJM 2026]</a>
- **Safety:** Grade 3–4 treatment-related adverse events affected 24.6% versus 34.5%; gastrointestinal toxicity remains relevant to treatment selection. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2605703">[NEJM 2026]</a>

## LBA6 — SAFFRON (osimertinib + savolitinib)
- **Eligibility and treatment:** Eligible EGFR-mutant NSCLC had progressed on osimertinib and showed MET IHC 3+ in at least 90% of tumour cells and/or at least 10 MET gene copies by FISH on post-progression biopsy. Of 1,746 screened, 338 were assigned to osimertinib 80 mg daily plus savolitinib 300 mg twice daily (171) or platinum–pemetrexed (167). <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2611335">[NEJM 2026]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1648257/000164825726000068/hcm-ex99d1.htm">[HUTCHMED 2026, sponsor-reported]</a>
- **PFS:** Median PFS by BICR was 7.6 versus 5.4 months; HR 0.55 (95% CI 0.42–0.72; P < .001). In the MET-overexpressed and MET-amplified subsets, respective HRs were 0.51 (95% CI 0.37–0.71) and 0.49 (95% CI 0.35–0.66), ; these subset analyses were not formally tested. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2611335">[NEJM 2026]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1648257/000164825726000068/hcm-ex99d1.htm">[HUTCHMED 2026, sponsor-reported]</a>
- **OS:** At the final OS analysis, median survival was 17.8 versus 12.9 months; HR 0.68 (95% CI 0.50–0.91; P = .010). <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2611335">[NEJM 2026]</a>
- **Response and safety:** ORR was 51% versus 27%, and on-treatment grade ≥3 adverse events affected 64% versus 63%. Fatal adverse events were reported in 12 combination recipients and none on chemotherapy; one was judged treatment-related, and nine had documented progression. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2611335">[NEJM 2026]</a>

## LBA7 — CLARITY-Gastric 01 (sonesitatug vedotin, CLDN18.2 ADC)
- **Eligibility:** Patients had previously treated advanced gastric, gastro-oesophageal junction or oesophageal adenocarcinoma with CLDN18.2 membranous staining of any intensity in at least 25% of tumour cells. The ITT groups contained 267 and 265 patients; the control was investigator-selected therapy. <a class="cite" href="https://www.astrazeneca.com/media-centre/press-releases/2026/sonesitatug-vedotin-reduced-the-risk-of-death-by-42-pct-in-2nd-and-later-line-cldn182-positive-advanced-gastricgej-cancers-in-clarity-gastric01.html">[AstraZeneca 2026, sponsor-reported]</a>
- **OS, third line or later:** At interim analysis the third-line-or-later dual primary OS endpoint was met: 10.0 versus 5.0 months; HR 0.47 (95% CI 0.36–0.62; P < .000001), among 355 patients. <a class="cite" href="https://www.astrazeneca.com/media-centre/press-releases/2026/sonesitatug-vedotin-reduced-the-risk-of-death-by-42-pct-in-2nd-and-later-line-cldn182-positive-advanced-gastricgej-cancers-in-clarity-gastric01.html">[AstraZeneca 2026, sponsor-reported]</a>
- **OS, ITT:** For this key secondary endpoint, median OS was 10.6 versus 6.4 months; HR 0.58 (95% CI 0.47–0.73; P = .000004). <a class="cite" href="https://www.astrazeneca.com/media-centre/press-releases/2026/sonesitatug-vedotin-reduced-the-risk-of-death-by-42-pct-in-2nd-and-later-line-cldn182-positive-advanced-gastricgej-cancers-in-clarity-gastric01.html">[AstraZeneca 2026, sponsor-reported]</a>
- **PFS, ITT:** For the other dual primary endpoint, BICR PFS was 4.2 versus 3.8 months; HR 0.77 (95% CI 0.62–0.96; P = .053193), and did not cross the prespecified significance boundary. <a class="cite" href="https://www.astrazeneca.com/media-centre/press-releases/2026/sonesitatug-vedotin-reduced-the-risk-of-death-by-42-pct-in-2nd-and-later-line-cldn182-positive-advanced-gastricgej-cancers-in-clarity-gastric01.html">[AstraZeneca 2026, sponsor-reported]</a>
- **Interpretation:** The OS HR was 0.59 (95% CI 0.45–0.78) for CLDN18.2 staining in ≥75% and 0.63 (95% CI 0.42–0.94) for ≥25% to <75% of cells. These exploratory estimates cannot establish benefit separately for each treatment line or tumour site. <a class="cite" href="https://www.astrazeneca.com/media-centre/press-releases/2026/sonesitatug-vedotin-reduced-the-risk-of-death-by-42-pct-in-2nd-and-later-line-cldn182-positive-advanced-gastricgej-cancers-in-clarity-gastric01.html">[AstraZeneca 2026, sponsor-reported]</a>
- **Safety:** Grade ≥3 treatment-related adverse events were reported in 36.4% versus 44.8%. <a class="cite" href="https://www.astrazeneca.com/media-centre/press-releases/2026/sonesitatug-vedotin-reduced-the-risk-of-death-by-42-pct-in-2nd-and-later-line-cldn182-positive-advanced-gastricgej-cancers-in-clarity-gastric01.html">[AstraZeneca 2026, sponsor-reported]</a>

## LBA8 — HARMONi-GI1 (ivonescimab, PD-1 × VEGF bispecific)
- **Design:** This double-blind study in China assigned 682 patients with untreated advanced biliary tract cancer to ivonescimab or durvalumab, both with gemcitabine–cisplatin and subsequent antibody maintenance. <a class="cite" href="https://markets.ft.markitdigital.com/data/announce/detail?dockey=600-202610251152BIZWIRE_USPRX____20261025_BW603151-1">[Summit 2026, sponsor-reported]</a>
- **OS:** At interim analysis, after median follow-up of 14.3 months, median OS was 14.9 versus 12.1 months; HR 0.75 (95% CI 0.61–0.92; one-sided P = .0031). Estimated OS at 12 months was 59.7% versus 50.7%. <a class="cite" href="https://markets.ft.markitdigital.com/data/announce/detail?dockey=600-202610251152BIZWIRE_USPRX____20261025_BW603151-1">[Summit 2026, sponsor-reported]</a>
- **PFS and response:** Median investigator-assessed PFS was 8.4 versus 5.8 months; HR 0.61 (95% CI 0.51–0.72). ORR was 53.8% versus 32.5%, with disease control in 91.5% versus 77.8%. <a class="cite" href="https://markets.ft.markitdigital.com/data/announce/detail?dockey=600-202610251152BIZWIRE_USPRX____20261025_BW603151-1">[Summit 2026, sponsor-reported]</a>
- **Safety:** Serious treatment-emergent adverse events were reported in 43.4% versus 43.7%; proteinuria, hypertension and bleeding were among the potentially VEGF-related events reported with ivonescimab. <a class="cite" href="https://markets.ft.markitdigital.com/data/announce/detail?dockey=600-202610251152BIZWIRE_USPRX____20261025_BW603151-1">[Summit 2026, sponsor-reported]</a>

## What is not yet known
- Full peer-reviewed reports and longer OS follow-up for CLARITY-Gastric01 and HARMONi-GI1 remain needed. The biliary findings require assessment in other populations.
- On 19 November 2026, the sponsor reported US priority review of the sonesitatug vedotin application for previously treated CLDN18.2-positive advanced gastric, junctional or oesophageal adenocarcinoma; review is not approval. <a class="cite" href="https://www.astrazeneca.com/media-centre/press-releases/2026/sonesitatug-vedotin-granted-priority-review-us-for-2nd-later-line-treatment-patients-with-cldn182-positive-advanced-gastric-gej-cancers.html">[AstraZeneca 2026, sponsor-reported]</a>
- India availability and indication-specific CDSCO approval require local verification before prescribing; these trial results alone do not establish approval.
