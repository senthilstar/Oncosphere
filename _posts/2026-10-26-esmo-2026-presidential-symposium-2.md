Presidential Symposium II, ESMO 2026, Madrid — Sunday 25 October. Four phase 3 late-breakers (LBA5–8). All showed an overall survival benefit. Data as presented.

<p class="note"><strong>Bottom line:</strong> every trial beat an active control, not placebo. Two are lung (KRAS G12C; EGFR-mutant with MET after osimertinib), two are GI (CLDN18.2 gastro-oesophageal; first-line biliary).</p>

| LBA | Trial | Comparison | PFS | OS |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5 | Krascendo 1 | Divarasib vs sotorasib/adagrasib, pretreated KRAS G12C NSCLC | 9.8 vs 5.7 mo (HR 0.58) | 17.7 vs 12.0 mo (HR 0.56) |
| 6 | SAFFRON | Osimertinib + savolitinib vs platinum–pemetrexed, post-osimertinib, MET-altered | 7.6 vs 5.4 mo (HR 0.55) | 17.8 vs 12.9 mo (HR 0.68) |
| 7 | CLARITY-Gastric 01 | Sonesitatug vedotin vs investigator's choice, CLDN18.2+ GEC, 2L+ | ITT not significant (HR 0.77) | 3L+: 10.0 vs 5.0 mo (HR 0.47) |
| 8 | HARMONi-GI1 | Ivonescimab + GemCis vs durvalumab + GemCis, 1L biliary | 8.4 vs 5.8 mo (HR 0.61) | 14.9 vs 12.1 mo (HR 0.75) |

## LBA5 — Krascendo 1 (divarasib)
- **Design:** open-label; n = 338. Divarasib 400 mg OD vs sotorasib (n = 156) or adagrasib (n = 15). 1–3 prior lines incl. platinum + PD-(L)1. No crossover.
- **PFS (BICR):** HR 0.58 (P < .0001); 12-month PFS 43.9% vs 24.0%.
- **OS (first interim):** HR 0.56 (P = .0005); 12-month OS 67.1% vs 49.2%.
- **ORR:** 58% vs 32%. DoR 11.9 vs 6.9 months.
- **Toxicity:** treatment-related grade 3–4 AEs *lower* (24.6% vs 34.5%) despite longer exposure. More GI upset (diarrhoea 66%, nausea 53%, mostly grade 1); less transaminitis.

## LBA6 — SAFFRON (osimertinib + savolitinib)
- **Eligibility:** EGFR-mutant NSCLC progressing on osimertinib; MET IHC 3+ in ≥ 90% cells and/or FISH ≥ 10 copies on a **post-progression biopsy**. Only 35% of those tested qualified.
- **PFS:** HR 0.55 (P < .001); 12-month PFS 36% vs 10%.
- **OS (53% maturity):** HR 0.68 (P = .010).
- **ORR:** 51% vs 27%.
- **Toxicity:** peripheral oedema 49%; fatal AEs 12 (7%) vs 0 — 1 treatment related, 9 with progression.

## LBA7 — CLARITY-Gastric 01 (sonesitatug vedotin, CLDN18.2 ADC)
- **Eligibility:** CLDN18.2 membranous staining ≥ 25% of tumour cells; ≥ 1 prior line. 2.2 mg/kg q3w (n = 267) vs investigator's choice (n = 265).
- **OS, 3L+ (dual primary):** 10.0 vs 5.0 months; HR 0.47. **OS, ITT:** 10.6 vs 6.4 months; HR 0.58.
- **PFS, ITT (dual primary):** not met (HR 0.77; P = .053).
- **Toxicity:** more nausea/vomiting; less grade ≥ 3 neutropenia (10.3% vs 25.4%).
- Benefit seen at both CLDN18.2 ≥ 75% and 25–74% expression.

## LBA8 — HARMONi-GI1 (ivonescimab, PD-1 × VEGF bispecific)
- **Design:** double-blind; n = 682, **all in China**. Ivonescimab 20 mg/kg vs durvalumab 1500 mg, both with gemcitabine–cisplatin, then maintenance.
- **OS (interim):** HR 0.75 (95% CI 0.61–0.92). 18-month OS 44.0% vs 32.5%.
- **ORR:** 53.8% vs 32.5%.
- **Toxicity:** grade 3–4 AEs 82.0% vs 72.3%; immune-related AEs similar.
- First phase 3 to beat the TOPAZ-1 regimen (durvalumab + GemCis).

## What is not yet known
- Full papers, final OS, and whether SAFFRON and HARMONi-GI1 results generalise beyond mainly Asian/Chinese populations.
- Regulatory status — divarasib, savolitinib, sonesitatug vedotin and ivonescimab are not approved for these indications in India.
