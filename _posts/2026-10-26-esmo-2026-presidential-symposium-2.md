Presidential Symposium II, ESMO 2026, Madrid — Sunday 25 October. Four phase 3 late-breakers (LBA5–8). All showed an overall survival benefit. Data as presented; Krascendo 1 and SAFFRON were published simultaneously in *NEJM*. Fact-checked against the papers and sponsor releases on 1 Nov 2026.

<p class="note"><strong>Bottom line:</strong> every trial beat an active control, not placebo. Two are lung (KRAS G12C; EGFR-mutant with MET after osimertinib), two are GI (CLDN18.2 gastro-oesophageal; first-line biliary).</p>

| LBA | Trial | Comparison | PFS | OS |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5 | Krascendo 1 | Divarasib vs sotorasib/adagrasib, pretreated KRAS G12C NSCLC | 9.8 vs 5.7 mo (HR 0.58) | 17.7 vs 12.0 mo (HR 0.56) |
| 6 | SAFFRON | Osimertinib + savolitinib vs platinum–pemetrexed, post-osimertinib, MET-altered | 7.6 vs 5.4 mo (HR 0.55) | 17.8 vs 12.9 mo (HR 0.68) |
| 7 | CLARITY-Gastric 01 | Sonesitatug vedotin vs investigator's choice, CLDN18.2+ GEC, 2L+ | ITT 4.2 vs 3.8 mo, not significant (HR 0.77) | 3L+: 10.0 vs 5.0 mo (HR 0.47) |
| 8 | HARMONi-GI1 | Ivonescimab + GemCis vs durvalumab + GemCis, 1L biliary | 8.4 vs 5.8 mo (HR 0.61) | 14.9 vs 12.1 mo (HR 0.75) |

## LBA5 — Krascendo 1 (divarasib)
- **Design:** open-label; n = 338 (167 vs 171). Divarasib 400 mg OD vs sotorasib 960 mg OD (n = 156) or adagrasib 600 mg BD (n = 15). 1–3 prior lines incl. platinum + PD-(L)1. No crossover. Median follow-up 14.3 months.
- **PFS (BICR):** 9.8 vs 5.7 mo; HR 0.58 (95% CI 0.44–0.76; P < .0001); 12-month PFS 43.9% vs 24.0%.
- **OS (first interim):** 17.7 vs 12.0 mo; HR 0.56 (95% CI 0.41–0.78; P = .0005); 12-month OS 67.1% vs 49.2%. Upper CI of divarasib median not yet estimable. Benefit seen regardless of PD-L1 or KEAP1 status (Roche).
- **ORR (response-evaluable, n = 328):** 58.4% vs 32.3%. DoR 11.9 vs 6.9 months.
- **Toxicity:** treatment-related grade 3–4 AEs *lower* (24.6% vs 34.5%) despite longer exposure (median 11.3 vs 5.0 months on treatment); serious TRAEs 7.8% vs 15.2%; discontinuation for TRAEs 4.8% vs 11.7%; no grade 5 TRAEs. More GI upset (diarrhoea 66%, nausea 53%, mostly grade 1); less transaminitis.

## LBA6 — SAFFRON (osimertinib + savolitinib)
- **Eligibility:** EGFR-mutant NSCLC progressing on osimertinib; MET IHC 3+ in ≥ 90% cells and/or FISH ≥ 10 copies on a **post-progression biopsy**. Only 35% of those tested qualified; 1,746 screened, 338 randomised (171 vs 167). Osimertinib 80 mg OD + savolitinib 300 mg BD.
- **PFS (BICR):** 7.6 vs 5.4 mo; HR 0.55 (95% CI 0.42–0.72; P < .001); 12-month PFS 36% vs 10%. MET-overexpressed subset HR 0.51 (0.37–0.71); MET-amplified subset HR 0.49 (0.35–0.66), not formally tested.
- **OS (final analysis, 53% maturity):** 17.8 vs 12.9 mo; HR 0.68 (95% CI 0.50–0.91; P = .010); 12-month OS 70% vs 53%.
- **ORR:** 51% vs 27%.
- **Toxicity:** grade ≥ 3 AEs 64% vs 63%; peripheral oedema 49%, anaemia 26%, diarrhoea 26%; fatal AEs 12 (7%) vs 0 — 1 treatment related, 9 with progression.

## LBA7 — CLARITY-Gastric 01 (sonesitatug vedotin, CLDN18.2 ADC)
- **Eligibility:** CLDN18.2 membranous staining ≥ 25% of tumour cells; ≥ 1 prior line. 2.2 mg/kg q3w (n = 267) vs investigator's choice (n = 265). Control: 2L ramucirumab + paclitaxel (or taxane alone); 3L+ irinotecan, trifluridine/tipiracil (outside China) or apatinib (China). Median follow-up 8.6 months (cut-off 31 May 2026).
- **OS, 3L+ (dual primary, n = 355):** 10.0 vs 5.0 months; HR 0.47 (95% CI 0.36–0.62; P < .000001); 12-month OS 38.1% vs 18.1%.
- **OS, ITT (key secondary):** 10.6 vs 6.4 months; HR 0.58 (95% CI 0.47–0.73; P = .000004); 12-month OS 42.6% vs 27.8%.
- **PFS, ITT (dual primary, BICR):** 4.2 vs 3.8 months; HR 0.77 (95% CI 0.62–0.96; P = .053) — not met. 3L+ PFS 4.1 vs 2.7 months (HR 0.56), not formally tested.
- **Caveats:** 2L subgroup OS HR 0.88 (0.60–1.30) and GEJ/oesophageal primary HR 1.08 (0.68–1.71) — the benefit is driven by 3L+ gastric primaries.
- **Toxicity:** grade ≥ 3 TRAEs 36.4% vs 44.8%; more nausea/vomiting; less grade ≥ 3 neutropenia (10.3% vs 25.4%).
- Benefit seen at both CLDN18.2 ≥ 75% (ITT OS HR 0.59) and 25–74% (HR 0.63) expression.

## LBA8 — HARMONi-GI1 (ivonescimab, PD-1 × VEGF bispecific)
- **Design:** double-blind; n = 682, **all in China**. Ivonescimab 20 mg/kg vs durvalumab 1500 mg, both with gemcitabine–cisplatin, then maintenance.
- **OS (interim; median follow-up 14.3 mo):** 14.9 vs 12.1 mo; HR 0.75 (95% CI 0.61–0.92; one-sided P = .0031). 12-month OS 59.7% vs 50.7%; 18-month OS 44.0% vs 32.5%. Subsequent therapy less often in the ivonescimab arm (31.8% vs 45.9%).
- **PFS (investigator):** 8.4 vs 5.8 mo; HR 0.61 (95% CI 0.51–0.72).
- **ORR:** 53.8% vs 32.5%; DCR 91.5% vs 77.8%. Age ≥ 65: OS HR 0.67; ECOG 1: OS HR 0.71.
- **Toxicity:** grade 3–4 TEAEs 82.0% vs 72.3% (longer exposure, 7.8 vs 5.7 mo); grade 3–4 neutropenia 54.6% vs 41.3%; serious AEs 43.4% vs 43.7%; grade ≥ 3 immune-related AEs 4.1% in both; VEGF effects — proteinuria ~32% vs 12%, hypertension 15.0% vs 2.4%, bleeding 9.7% vs 6.5% (grade ≥ 3 1.5% vs 1.8%).
- For context, TOPAZ-1 control-to-durvalumab gain was 11.5 to 12.8 months (HR 0.80).
- First phase 3 to beat the TOPAZ-1 regimen (durvalumab + GemCis).

## What is not yet known
- Full papers for CLARITY-Gastric01 and HARMONi-GI1, final OS, and whether SAFFRON and HARMONi-GI1 results generalise beyond mainly Asian/Chinese populations.
- Regulatory status — divarasib, savolitinib, sonesitatug vedotin and ivonescimab are not approved for these indications in India.
