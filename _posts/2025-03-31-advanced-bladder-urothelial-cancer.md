##### Insights

Explore cancer history, treatments, and latest research.

## Key first-line evidence
- **EV-302** (n=886): EV + pembrolizumab vs gemcitabine + platinum. PFS 12.5 vs 6.3 mo (HR 0.45); OS 31.5 vs 16.1 mo (HR 0.47); ORR 67.7% vs 44.4%. <a class="cite" href="https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2312117">[NEJM 2024]</a> Benefit regardless of cisplatin eligibility and PD-L1.
- **CheckMate-901** (cisplatin-eligible): nivolumab + gem-cis vs gem-cis. OS 21.7 vs 18.9 mo (HR 0.78); PFS 7.9 vs 7.6 mo (HR 0.72); CR 21.7% vs 11.8%. <a class="cite" href="https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2309863">[NEJM 2023]</a>
- **JAVELIN Bladder 100**: avelumab maintenance after response/stable disease on platinum. OS 21.4 vs 14.3 mo (HR 0.69). <a class="cite" href="https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2002788">[NEJM 2020]</a>

## Toxicity to anticipate with EV-pembro
Skin reactions (SJS/TEN rare but serious), peripheral neuropathy, hyperglycaemia, ocular surface toxicity, plus immune-related AEs. Check HbA1c and baseline neuropathy.

## Later lines
- FGFR2/3 alteration: erdafitinib (THOR: OS 12.1 vs 7.8 mo vs chemo; HR 0.64). <a class="cite" href="https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2308849">[NEJM 2023]</a>
- Sacituzumab govitecan: US accelerated indication was withdrawn in 2024 after the confirmatory TROPiCS-04 trial did not improve OS.
- HER2-expressing tumours: trastuzumab deruxtecan has a US tumour-agnostic accelerated approval (HER2 IHC 3+).

## Trial design notes
- **EV-302:** open-label phase 3; enfortumab vedotin 1.25 mg/kg on days 1 and 8 plus pembrolizumab 200 mg on day 1 of 3-week cycles, vs gemcitabine with cisplatin or carboplatin. Dual primary endpoints PFS and OS. <a class="cite" href="https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2312117">[NEJM 2024]</a> EV continues until progression; pembrolizumab is capped (up to 35 cycles).
- **CheckMate-901:** cisplatin-eligible patients only; nivolumab 360 mg with gem-cis for up to 6 cycles, then nivolumab maintenance for up to 2 years. <a class="cite" href="https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2309863">[NEJM 2023]</a> The OS gain is modest and the trial did not test carboplatin.
- **JAVELIN Bladder 100:** randomised only patients *without* progression after 4–6 cycles of platinum chemotherapy, so it selects a favourable group; avelumab was given every 2 weeks until progression. <a class="cite" href="https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2002788">[NEJM 2020]</a>
- **THOR (cohort 1):** FGFR3/2-altered tumours after anti-PD-(L)1 therapy; erdafitinib vs investigator's choice chemotherapy (docetaxel or vinflunine). Besides OS, PFS was 5.6 vs 2.7 months (HR 0.58) and ORR 45.6% vs 11.5%. <a class="cite" href="https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2308849">[NEJM 2023]</a>

## Cisplatin eligibility in practice
Commonly used criteria (Galsky consensus): patients are usually considered cisplatin-ineligible with ECOG PS ≥2, creatinine clearance <60 mL/min, grade ≥2 hearing loss or neuropathy, or NYHA class III heart failure. Split-dose cisplatin is used in borderline renal function but is not well validated. With EV-pembro, cisplatin eligibility matters less for first-line choice, but it still guides the fallback option.

## Monitoring checklist
- **EV:** blood sugar every cycle (hyperglycaemia can be severe even without diabetes), skin review in cycle 1–2, neuropathy grading, eye lubrication.
- **Pembrolizumab/nivolumab/avelumab:** TSH, LFTs, creatinine, cortisol if symptoms; educate on colitis and pneumonitis.
- **Erdafitinib:** serum phosphate (hyperphosphataemia is expected and drives dose titration), ophthalmology for central serous retinopathy, nail and mouth care.

## Upper tract and variant histology
Upper-tract urothelial carcinoma was included in the main trials and is treated similarly in the metastatic setting. Pure non-urothelial histologies (squamous, adenocarcinoma, small cell) were largely excluded; small-cell bladder cancer follows small-cell lung cancer–type regimens.
