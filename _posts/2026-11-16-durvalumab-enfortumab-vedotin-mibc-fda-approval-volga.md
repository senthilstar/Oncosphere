On **13 November 2026**, the FDA approved neoadjuvant **durvalumab plus enfortumab vedotin-ejfv**, followed by radical cystectomy and adjuvant durvalumab alone, for adults with **muscle-invasive bladder cancer (MIBC)** who cannot receive or have refused cisplatin-based chemotherapy. <a class="cite" href="https://www.fda.gov/drugs/cancer-approvals/fda-approves-durvalumab-enfortumab-vedotin-ejfv-muscle-invasive-bladder-cancer">[FDA 2026]</a>

## The regimen
- **Neoadjuvant:** for weight ≥30 kg, durvalumab 1,500 mg every 3 weeks plus enfortumab vedotin 1.25 mg/kg on days 1 and 8 of each cycle, for 3 cycles before surgery. <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/761069s059lbl.pdf">[FDA label 2026]</a>
- **Radical cystectomy.**
- **Adjuvant:** durvalumab 1,500 mg every 4 weeks for up to 9 cycles after surgery; for weight <30 kg, the label specifies durvalumab 20 mg/kg in both phases. <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/761069s059lbl.pdf">[FDA label 2026]</a>

## VOLGA in brief
VOLGA was an open-label, randomised phase 3 trial of cystectomy candidates ineligible for or declining cisplatin: 695 patients were allocated 1:1:1 to durvalumab plus EV (n = 233), durvalumab plus tremelimumab and EV (n = 233), or upfront cystectomy with observation or locally approved adjuvant treatment (n = 229). <a class="cite" href="https://www.nejm.org/doi/10.1056/NEJMoa2608323">[NEJM 2026]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://bladder.uroonco.uroweb.org/publication/perioperative-durvalumab-with-or-without-tremelimumab-plus-neoadjuvant-enfortumab-vedotin-in-muscle-invasive-bladder-cancer-results-from-the-phase-3-volga-trial/">[ESMO 2026]</a>

Randomisation was stratified by PD-L1 status, clinical stage and cisplatin eligibility or refusal; event-free survival (EFS) was assessed by blinded independent central review. <a class="cite" href="https://bladder.uroonco.uroweb.org/publication/perioperative-durvalumab-with-or-without-tremelimumab-plus-neoadjuvant-enfortumab-vedotin-in-muscle-invasive-bladder-cancer-results-from-the-phase-3-volga-trial/">[ESMO 2026]</a> At the prespecified interim analysis, EFS included recurrence after surgery, specified progression or failure to undergo surgery with residual disease, and death. <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/761069s059lbl.pdf">[FDA label 2026]</a>

| Durvalumab + EV vs cystectomy alone | Result |
|---|---|
| EFS HR | **0.51** (95% CI 0.38–0.67); p < 0.0001. <a class="cite" href="https://www.fda.gov/drugs/cancer-approvals/fda-approves-durvalumab-enfortumab-vedotin-ejfv-muscle-invasive-bladder-cancer">[FDA 2026]</a> |
| Median EFS | Not reached (95% CI 33.2–not reached) vs **12.1 months** (95% CI 7.6–27.0). <a class="cite" href="https://www.fda.gov/drugs/cancer-approvals/fda-approves-durvalumab-enfortumab-vedotin-ejfv-muscle-invasive-bladder-cancer">[FDA 2026]</a> |
| 24-month EFS | **63.5% vs 45.1%**, estimated. <a class="cite" href="https://www.nejm.org/doi/10.1056/NEJMoa2608323">[NEJM 2026]</a> |
| OS HR | **0.58** (95% CI 0.40–0.83); p = 0.0027; median overall survival (OS) was not reached in either group. <a class="cite" href="https://www.fda.gov/drugs/cancer-approvals/fda-approves-durvalumab-enfortumab-vedotin-ejfv-muscle-invasive-bladder-cancer">[FDA 2026]</a> |
| 24-month OS | **78.4% vs 69.4%**, estimated. <a class="cite" href="https://www.nejm.org/doi/10.1056/NEJMoa2608323">[NEJM 2026]</a> |
| Grade ≥3 adverse events, any cause | **71.1% vs 50.2%**. <a class="cite" href="https://www.nejm.org/doi/10.1056/NEJMoa2608323">[NEJM 2026]</a> |

- Median EFS follow-up was **22.9 months** at the **2 March 2026** data cutoff. <a class="cite" href="https://bladder.uroonco.uroweb.org/publication/perioperative-durvalumab-with-or-without-tremelimumab-plus-neoadjuvant-enfortumab-vedotin-in-muscle-invasive-bladder-cancer-results-from-the-phase-3-volga-trial/">[ESMO 2026]</a>
- The triplet arm also improved EFS versus control, but it is not the FDA-approved regimen described here. <a class="cite" href="https://www.nejm.org/doi/10.1056/NEJMoa2608323">[NEJM 2026]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://www.fda.gov/drugs/cancer-approvals/fda-approves-durvalumab-enfortumab-vedotin-ejfv-muscle-invasive-bladder-cancer">[FDA 2026]</a>

## Practice points
- This is an option for cystectomy candidates who cannot receive or decline cisplatin; the comparison was with upfront surgery, not another active perioperative regimen. <a class="cite" href="https://www.fda.gov/drugs/cancer-approvals/fda-approves-durvalumab-enfortumab-vedotin-ejfv-muscle-invasive-bladder-cancer">[FDA 2026]</a>
- Monitor for EV-associated skin reactions, neuropathy and hyperglycaemia, as well as immune-mediated adverse effects; manage toxicity promptly to protect the planned cystectomy. <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/761069s059lbl.pdf">[FDA label 2026]</a>
- The EV-containing indication is restricted to patients ineligible for or refusing cisplatin; the durvalumab label separately includes a cisplatin-based perioperative regimen. <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/761069s059lbl.pdf">[FDA label 2026]</a>
