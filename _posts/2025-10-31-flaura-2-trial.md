## Design
Phase 3, open-label; n=557; untreated advanced EGFR ex19del/L858R NSCLC. Osimertinib 80 mg + pemetrexed + cisplatin/carboplatin ×4, then osimertinib + pemetrexed maintenance vs osimertinib alone.

## Results
- **PFS** (primary, investigator-assessed, NEJM 2023): 25.5 vs 16.7 mo; HR 0.62 (95% CI 0.49–0.79; p<0.001); BICR HR 0.62 (29.4 vs 19.9 mo). 24-mo PFS 57% vs 41%. ORR 83% vs 76%; median DoR 24.0 vs 15.3 mo. <a class="cite" href="https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2306434">[NEJM 2023]</a>
- **OS** (final, ESMO 2025/NEJM): **47.5 vs 37.6 mo**; HR 0.77 (95% CI 0.61–0.96; p=0.02); 57% maturity; median OS follow-up ~51 mo. <a class="cite" href="https://www.nejm.org/doi/10.1056/NEJMoa2510308">[NEJM 2025]</a>
- OS at 24 mo: 79.7% vs 71.5%; 36 mo: 63.1% vs 50.9%; 48 mo: 49.1% vs 40.8% (exploratory). <a class="cite" href="https://www.astrazeneca.com/media-centre/press-releases/2025/tagrisso-plus-chemotherapy-demonstrated-a-median-overall-survival-of-nearly-four-years.html">[AZ]</a>
- Baseline CNS metastases (exploratory): median OS 40.9 vs 29.7 mo; HR 0.72 (0.52–0.99). <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1016/j.annonc.2025.09.091">[LBA77]</a>
- Grade ≥3 AEs (all causes): 70% vs 34% — mainly haematological, chemotherapy-related. AEs leading to osimertinib discontinuation: 12% vs 7%. <a class="cite" href="https://www.nejm.org/doi/10.1056/NEJMoa2510308">[NEJM 2025]</a>

## Where it fits (Oct 2026)
NCCN/ESMO list three preferred first-line options for common EGFR mutations: osimertinib, osimertinib + chemotherapy (FLAURA2) and amivantamab + lazertinib (MARIPOSA, also with OS benefit vs osimertinib). Choice depends on toxicity tolerance, logistics (IV vs oral), and disease features.

<p class="opinion">I favour FLAURA2 for fit patients with brain metastases or high tumour burden; osimertinib monotherapy remains entirely reasonable for elderly/frail patients or those prioritising fewer hospital visits.</p>

## Subgroups worth knowing
Exploratory final-OS subgroups (ESMO 2025 LBA77); HRs all consistent with the overall 0.77, CIs mostly cross 1 — hypothesis-generating. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1016/j.annonc.2025.09.091">[LBA77]</a>

- **CNS metastases:** the only subgroup whose CI excludes 1, with an ~11-month median OS gain — the main reason many oncologists favour the combination here.
- **L858R vs exon 19 deletion:** identical HRs (0.76); L858R does worse in absolute terms on either arm, a reasonable argument for intensification.
- **ctDNA / co-mutations (TP53):** poorer-prognosis biology still benefits, but small numbers.

## Toxicity in practice
Most extra toxicity came from chemotherapy (anaemia, neutropenia, thrombocytopenia, nausea) and was concentrated in the first 4 cycles. Pemetrexed maintenance requires folic acid and B12 premedication and regular renal checks. At final analysis, AEs led to osimertinib discontinuation in 12% vs 7%; the excess grade ≥3 AEs were described as reversible. <a class="cite" href="https://www.nejm.org/doi/10.1056/NEJMoa2510308">[NEJM 2025]</a>

## The competing option: MARIPOSA
Amivantamab + lazertinib improved PFS over osimertinib (23.7 vs 16.6 months, HR 0.70) <a class="cite" href="https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2403614">[NEJM 2024]</a> and, at final analysis (median follow-up 37.8 mo), overall survival: HR 0.75 (95% CI 0.61–0.92; p=0.005); 3-year OS 60% vs 51%; grade ≥3 AEs 80% vs 52%. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2503001">[NEJM 2025]</a> It requires IV infusions and carries high rates of rash, paronychia, infusion reactions and venous thromboembolism (prophylactic anticoagulation is recommended). No head-to-head trial compares it with FLAURA2.

## Practical sequencing
- Get **EGFR mutation subtype** right first; uncommon mutations (G719X, L861Q, S768I) and exon 20 insertions were excluded from FLAURA2.
- At progression on either strategy, re-biopsy (tissue or plasma) to look for MET amplification, histological transformation (small cell) or other resistance mechanisms.
- The protocol allowed cisplatin 75 mg/m² or carboplatin (AUC 5) for 4 cycles; carboplatin is the practical choice for older patients or those with renal/hearing concerns.
