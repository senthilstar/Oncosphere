The EMA's human medicines committee (**CHMP**) met on **9–12 November 2026** and recommended seven new medicines, two of them for **non-small cell lung cancer**. A CHMP positive opinion is a recommendation; the European Commission makes the final decision, usually within about two months.

## Aumseqa (aumolertinib)
- Third-generation, irreversible **EGFR TKI** (exon 19 deletion, L858R, T790M); 55 mg tablets.
- **Indications recommended:** first-line advanced NSCLC with EGFR exon 19 deletion or exon 21 L858R; and advanced **EGFR T790M**-positive NSCLC.
- **Basis:** PFS improvement over **gefitinib** first line; ORR and duration of response in T790M-positive disease.
- **Common side effects:** raised AST/ALT and CPK, hyponatraemia, low white cell and platelet counts, upper respiratory infections, rash.
- The comparator was a first-generation TKI, not osimertinib.

## Zybrivo (sintilimab)
- **PD-1** monoclonal antibody; 100 mg concentrate for infusion.
- **Indication recommended:** with **pemetrexed and platinum**, first-line non-squamous NSCLC **without EGFR or ALK** alterations — locally advanced and not a candidate for curative surgery or chemoradiation, or metastatic — regardless of PD-L1.
- **Basis:** PFS improvement vs chemotherapy alone in a randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial.
- Adds another PD-1 to a class where pembrolizumab-based chemo-immunotherapy is already standard.

## Other notes from the meeting
- Withdrawn application: **Qezzaqar (catequentinib)** for two soft tissue sarcoma subtypes.
- A **ruxolitinib** generic (myelofibrosis, polycythaemia vera, GvHD) also received a positive opinion.
