The EMA's human medicines committee (**CHMP**) recommended seven new medicines at its **9–12 November 2026** meeting, including aumolertinib and sintilimab for non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC). <a class="cite" href="https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/news/meeting-highlights-committee-medicinal-products-human-use-chmp-9-12-november-2026">[EMA 2026]</a> A positive opinion is a recommendation; the European Commission makes the marketing authorisation decision. Both new opinions were listed as pending Commission decisions in the meeting record. <a class="cite" href="https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/news/meeting-highlights-committee-medicinal-products-human-use-chmp-9-12-november-2026">[EMA 2026]</a>

## Aumseqa (aumolertinib)
- **Recommended use:** monotherapy for adults with advanced NSCLC, first line for EGFR exon 19 deletion or exon 21 L858R, or for EGFR T790M-positive disease. The irreversible EGFR inhibitor is supplied as 55 mg tablets. <a class="cite" href="https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/medicines/human/EPAR/aumseqa">[EMA 2026]</a>
- **Regulatory history:** the Commission granted a prior Aumseqa authorisation on 12 February 2026 and revoked it on 25 September 2026, after finding that the application had relied partly on another medicine's data that were protected at submission. The November opinion concerns a new application. <a class="cite" href="https://www.ema.europa.eu/ga/medicines/human/EPAR/aumseqa">[EMA 2026]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/medicines/human/EPAR/aumseqa">[EMA 2026]</a>
- **Evidence:** in the phase 3 AENEAS trial (429 patients), investigator-assessed median progression-free survival (PFS) was 19.3 months with aumolertinib versus 9.9 months with gefitinib; hazard ratio (HR) 0.46, 95% confidence interval (CI) 0.36–0.60. <a class="cite" href="https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9509093/">[AENEAS 2022]</a> The T790M indication rests on response rate and duration of response evidence, rather than that first-line comparison. <a class="cite" href="https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/medicines/human/EPAR/aumseqa">[EMA 2026]</a>
- **Safety:** EMA lists raised liver enzymes and creatine phosphokinase, hyponatraemia, reduced white cells and platelets, upper respiratory infections and rash among common adverse effects. <a class="cite" href="https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/medicines/human/EPAR/aumseqa">[EMA 2026]</a> AENEAS compared aumolertinib with a first-generation TKI; it does not establish equivalence to osimertinib. <a class="cite" href="https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9509093/">[AENEAS 2022]</a>
- **Newer combination evidence:** AENEAS2 compared aumolertinib plus pemetrexed–platinum with aumolertinib alone in untreated EGFR-sensitive advanced NSCLC. Independent review showed median PFS of 28.9 versus 18.9 months (HR 0.47, 95% CI 0.37–0.60); overall survival follow-up remains needed. This chemotherapy combination is not the CHMP-recommended monotherapy indication. <a class="cite" href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42296979/">[AENEAS2 2026]</a>

## Zybrivo (sintilimab)
- **Recommended use:** sintilimab, a PD-1 antibody, with pemetrexed and platinum for first-line treatment of adults with non-squamous NSCLC without EGFR or ALK genomic alterations, when locally advanced disease is unsuitable for curative surgery or chemoradiation, or when metastatic. The trial included patients regardless of PD-L1 expression; the proposed infusion concentrate contains 100 mg. <a class="cite" href="https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/medicines/human/EPAR/zybrivo">[EMA 2026]</a>
- **Evidence:** the randomised, double-blind ORIENT-11 trial compared sintilimab plus pemetrexed–platinum with placebo plus chemotherapy. In the final overall survival analysis of 397 patients, median survival was 24.2 versus 16.8 months (HR 0.65, 95% CI 0.50–0.85); 47% of the control group crossed over to sintilimab. <a class="cite" href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35917647/">[ORIENT-11 2022]</a> The comparator was chemotherapy alone, rather than an established pembrolizumab-based regimen. <a class="cite" href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35917647/">[ORIENT-11 2022]</a>
- **Safety:** common effects reported by EMA include cytopenias, nausea, reduced appetite, fatigue, elevated liver enzymes, fever, hypothyroidism and rash. <a class="cite" href="https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/medicines/human/EPAR/zybrivo">[EMA 2026]</a>
- **US context:** the sponsor reported an FDA complete response letter in March 2022 for sintilimab with chemotherapy in this setting; the application was not approved in its submitted form, and an additional multiregional study was recommended. <a class="cite" href="https://investor.lilly.com/node/46986/pdf">[Sponsor regulatory update 2022]</a>

## Other notes from the meeting
- The Qezzaqar (catequentinib) application for leiomyosarcoma and synovial sarcoma was withdrawn. <a class="cite" href="https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/news/meeting-highlights-committee-medicinal-products-human-use-chmp-9-12-november-2026">[EMA 2026]</a>
- Ruxolitinib Krka received a positive opinion for myelofibrosis and polycythaemia vera in adults, and graft-versus-host disease in adults and children. <a class="cite" href="https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/news/meeting-highlights-committee-medicinal-products-human-use-chmp-9-12-november-2026">[EMA 2026]</a>
