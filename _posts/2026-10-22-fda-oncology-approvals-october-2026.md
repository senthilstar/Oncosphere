A busy month for US approvals. Summarised from the FDA's oncology approval notifications:

| Date (2026) | Drug | Indication |
|---|---|---|
| 2 Oct | Pirtobrutinib (Jaypirca) | Previously untreated CLL/SLL with no known 17p deletion |
| 7 Oct | Tucatinib + trastuzumab + pertuzumab | Maintenance after induction in unresectable locally advanced or metastatic HER2-positive breast cancer |
| 8 Oct | Atezolizumab (Tecentriq / Tecentriq Hybreza) + fluoropyrimidine + oxaliplatin | Adjuvant treatment of Stage III dMMR colon cancer |
| 14 Oct | Nogapendekin alfa inbakicept (Anktiva) + BCG | BCG-unresponsive NMIBC — expanded to include high-grade papillary-only tumours |
| 16 Oct | Pembrolizumab (Keytruda / Keytruda Qlex) | Traditional approval for previously treated TMB-H (≥13 mut/Mb) solid tumours with no satisfactory alternatives |
| 19 Oct | Dostarlimab (Jemperli) | Accelerated approval for Stage II/III MSI-H/dMMR locally advanced rectal cancer |

## Why it matters
Two approvals (atezolizumab in colon cancer, dostarlimab in rectal cancer) reinforce **universal MMR/MSI testing** of colorectal cancers at diagnosis — something we can do with immunohistochemistry in most centres.

## Notes on each approval
- **Pirtobrutinib (first-line CLL/SLL):** a non-covalent BTK inhibitor, previously used after covalent BTK inhibitors. The first-line label applies to patients without 17p deletion; check the FDA label for the trial data supporting it.
- **Tucatinib maintenance:** adds an oral HER2 tyrosine-kinase inhibitor to HP maintenance after first-line induction — relevant given the frequency of brain metastases in HER2-positive disease.
- **Adjuvant atezolizumab in dMMR colon cancer:** the first immunotherapy approval in the curative colon setting, based on adding atezolizumab to FOLFOX-type chemotherapy in stage III dMMR disease.
- **Nogapendekin alfa + BCG:** an IL-15 superagonist for BCG-unresponsive non-muscle-invasive bladder cancer; the label now includes papillary-only high-grade disease.
- **Pembrolizumab TMB-H:** conversion from accelerated to traditional approval. TMB-H is uncommon and benefit varies by tumour type — it is a last-line, not a first-line, biomarker in most cancers.
- **Dostarlimab (dMMR rectal):** see the [separate post]({{ '/dostarlimab-msi-h-rectal-cancer/' | relative_url }}).

## How to use FDA news in India
FDA approval tells us an agency judged the benefit-risk acceptable in the US. It does not mean the drug is approved by CDSCO, affordable, or covered by government schemes. Many of these drugs have no Indian price yet — check current availability before discussing them with patients.

Detailed efficacy figures for each approval are not reproduced here; read the linked FDA notices and labels for trial names and endpoints.

<p class="note">These are US FDA decisions. Approval and availability in India (CDSCO) may differ.</p>
