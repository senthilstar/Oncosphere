A busy month for US approvals. Summarised from the FDA's oncology approval notifications:

| Date (2026) | Drug | Indication |
|---|---|---|
| 2 Oct | Pirtobrutinib (Jaypirca) | Previously untreated CLL/SLL with no known 17p deletion |
| 7 Oct | Tucatinib + trastuzumab + pertuzumab | Maintenance after induction in unresectable locally advanced or metastatic HER2-positive breast cancer |
| 8 Oct | Atezolizumab (Tecentriq / Tecentriq Hybreza) + fluoropyrimidine + oxaliplatin | Adjuvant treatment of Stage III dMMR colon cancer |
| 14 Oct | Nogapendekin alfa inbakicept (Anktiva) + BCG | BCG-unresponsive NMIBC — expanded to include high-grade papillary-only tumours |
| 16 Oct | Pembrolizumab (Keytruda / Keytruda Qlex) | Traditional approval for previously treated TMB-H (≥13 mut/Mb) solid tumours with no satisfactory alternatives |
| 19 Oct | Dostarlimab (Jemperli) | Accelerated approval for Stage II/III MSI-H/dMMR locally advanced rectal cancer |

## Why it matters
Two approvals (atezolizumab in colon cancer, dostarlimab in rectal cancer) reinforce **universal MMR/MSI testing** of colorectal cancers at diagnosis — something we can do with immunohistochemistry in most centres.

<p class="note">These are US FDA decisions. Approval and availability in India (CDSCO) may differ.</p>
