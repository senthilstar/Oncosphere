## Why it matters
The colon and rectal indications reinforce **universal MMR/MSI testing** at colorectal cancer diagnosis; immunohistochemistry can make this actionable in many centres.

## Notes on each approval
- **Pirtobrutinib (first-line CLL/SLL):** This non-covalent BTK inhibitor is now an option for previously untreated adults without known 17p deletion. In BRUIN CLL-313 (n=282), independent-review median PFS was not estimable with pirtobrutinib versus 33.5 months with bendamustine–rituximab; HR 0.20 (95% CI 0.11–0.37), with immature overall survival (OS). The dose is 200 mg orally once daily; monitor for infection, bleeding, cytopenias and arrhythmias. <a class="cite" href="https://www.fda.gov/drugs/cancer-approvals/fda-approves-pirtobrutinib-previously-untreated-chronic-lymphocytic-leukemia-or-small-lymphocytic">[FDA 2026]</a> **Caveat:** the comparator does not establish superiority over covalent BTK inhibitors or fixed-duration venetoclax regimens.
- **Tucatinib maintenance:** HER2CLIMB-05 enrolled 654 adults without progression after 4–8 cycles of trastuzumab, pertuzumab and a taxane; brain metastases were permitted and endocrine therapy could continue for hormone receptor-positive disease. Investigator-assessed median PFS was 24.9 versus 16.3 months, HR 0.64 (95% CI 0.51–0.80; p<0.0001); OS was immature. Tucatinib is given at 300 mg orally twice daily with trastuzumab and pertuzumab. The label carries a boxed hepatotoxicity warning; monitor diarrhoea and recognise that serum creatinine may rise without a change in renal function. <a class="cite" href="https://www.fda.gov/drugs/cancer-approvals/fda-approves-tucatinib-trastuzumab-and-pertuzumab-maintenance-treatment-her2-positive-breast-cancer">[FDA 2026]</a>

- **Adjuvant atezolizumab in dMMR colon cancer:** After complete resection, ATOMIC randomised 712 patients to mFOLFOX6 with or without atezolizumab; the experimental arm continued atezolizumab alone after 6 months of combination treatment, for 12 months total. At 40.9 months' median follow-up, estimated 3-year DFS was 86.3% versus 76.2% (HR 0.50, 95% CI 0.35–0.73); grade 3–4 adverse events occurred in 84.1% versus 71.9%. <a class="cite" href="https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2507874">[NEJM 2026]</a> The IV approval covers patients aged at least 2 years; the subcutaneous formulation is approved from age 12 years and weight 40 kg. <a class="cite" href="https://www.fda.gov/drugs/cancer-approvals/fda-approves-atezolizumab-combination-chemotherapy-stage-iii-mismatch-repair-deficient-colon-cancer">[FDA 2026]</a> The study enrolled stage III dMMR disease; its results should not be extrapolated to pMMR or stage II disease. <a class="cite" href="https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2507874">[NEJM 2026]</a>

- **Nogapendekin alfa + BCG:** The expanded label includes high-grade papillary-only BCG-unresponsive NMIBC. FDA extrapolated from the earlier CIS indication: in single-arm QUILT-3.032 cohort A (n=77), complete response was 62%, with responses lasting at least 12 and 24 months in 58% and 40% of responders, respectively. The intravesical dose is 400 mcg with BCG weekly for 6 induction weeks, followed by maintenance for up to 37 months. <a class="cite" href="https://www.fda.gov/drugs/cancer-approvals/fda-approves-expanded-indication-nogapendekin-alfa-inbakicept-pmln-bcg-unresponsive-non-muscle">[FDA 2026]</a> Separately, papillary-only cohort B (n=80) reported estimated DFS of 58.2%, 52.1% and 38.2% at 12, 24 and 36 months; at 36 months PFS was 83.1% and the cystectomy-free rate was 81.8%. <a class="cite" href="https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12708036/">[J Urol 2026]</a> The label warns that delaying cystectomy can lead to metastatic disease. <a class="cite" href="https://www.fda.gov/drugs/cancer-approvals/fda-approves-expanded-indication-nogapendekin-alfa-inbakicept-pmln-bcg-unresponsive-non-muscle">[FDA 2026]</a>
- **Pembrolizumab for TMB-H disease:** Traditional approval requires an FDA-authorized test showing ≥13 mutations/Mb in an unresectable or metastatic tumour that progressed after prior treatment, with no satisfactory alternative. The prior threshold was ≥10; efficacy supporting traditional approval was not confirmed at 10 to <13 mutations/Mb. In KEYNOTE-158, 220 patients met the ≥13 threshold (145 in cohort M, 75 in cohorts A–J); ORR was 36% (95% CI 30–43), and 58% of responses lasted at least 12 months. <a class="cite" href="https://www.fda.gov/drugs/cancer-approvals/fda-grants-traditional-approval-pembrolizumab-or-pembrolizumab-and-berahyaluronidase-alfa-pmph">[FDA 2026]</a> Reassess older reports against the current indication and consider tumour-specific treatment options.
- **Dostarlimab (dMMR/MSI-H rectal cancer):** Accelerated approval requires an FDA-authorized solid-tumour test; response assessment and close surveillance are essential when considering non-operative management. <a class="cite" href="https://www.fda.gov/drugs/cancer-approvals/fda-grants-accelerated-approval-dostarlimab-locally-advanced-rectal-cancer">[FDA 2026]</a> See the [separate post]({{ '/dostarlimab-msi-h-rectal-cancer/' | relative_url }}).
- **Talazoparib + enzalutamide (HRR-mutated mHSPC):** TALAPRO-3 randomised 599 patients receiving continuous androgen deprivation to enzalutamide with talazoparib or placebo. Eligible HRR alterations were identified in tissue or circulating tumour DNA: ATM, ATR, BRCA1/2, CDK12, CHEK2, FANCA, MLH1, MRE11A, NBN, PALB2 or RAD51C. Investigator-assessed median radiographic PFS was not reached versus 45.8 months (HR 0.48, 95% CI 0.36–0.65; p<0.0001); OS was immature. The FDA dose is talazoparib 0.5 mg once daily with enzalutamide, plus a GnRH analogue unless bilateral orchiectomy has been performed. <a class="cite" href="https://www.fda.gov/drugs/cancer-approvals/fda-approves-talazoparib-enzalutamide-metastatic-androgen-pathway-modulation-naive-or-sensitive">[FDA 2026]</a> In the trial, enzalutamide was given at 160 mg once daily; interim OS HR was 0.77 (95% CI 0.56–1.04), and grade ≥3 anaemia occurred in 51% on talazoparib. <a class="cite" href="https://www.nejm.org/doi/10.1056/NEJMoa2604126">[NEJM 2026]</a> Anaemia led to permanent talazoparib discontinuation in 5%. <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/217439s004lbl.pdf">[FDA label 2026]</a> Arrange germline and tumour/ctDNA HRR testing, monitor blood counts and plan for transfusion support. ## How to use FDA news in India
FDA approval reflects a US benefit–risk decision. Check CDSCO status, local availability and coverage before discussing access with patients.

Key efficacy figures above are taken from the FDA approval notices (and the cited trial publications); check the full prescribing information on Drugs@FDA before use.
