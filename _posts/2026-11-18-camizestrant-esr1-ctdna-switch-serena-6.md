## Why this matters

Serial blood testing can identify an emerging ESR1 mutation while scans still show disease control. SERENA-6 tested whether replacing the aromatase inhibitor (AI) with camizestrant at that point, while continuing the CDK4/6 inhibitor, delays progression. <a class="cite" href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42442380/">[Lancet Oncol 2026]</a>

The switch prolonged progression-free survival (PFS). An extended peer-reviewed analysis has since reported final second PFS (PFS2), but overall survival (OS) remains immature. The FDA granted accelerated approval on **4 September 2026**; the EU authorised treatment on **20 July 2026**. <a class="cite" href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42442380/">[Lancet Oncol 2026]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://www.fda.gov/drugs/cancer-approvals/fda-grants-accelerated-approval-camizestrant-cdk46-inhibitor-esr1-mutated-hr-positive-her2-negative">[FDA 2026]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/medicines/human/EPAR/etcamah">[EMA 2026]</a>

## The biology in brief

Oestrogen receptor signalling can sustain HR-positive breast cancer. During oestrogen deprivation, an acquired ESR1 mutation can allow signalling to continue; detecting it in circulating tumour DNA (ctDNA) may reveal resistance before imaging shows progression.

Camizestrant is an oral selective oestrogen receptor degrader and antagonist. The strategy changes the endocrine partner when resistance emerges while retaining CDK4/6 inhibition. A negative plasma result must be interpreted alongside assay performance and tumour DNA shedding. <a class="cite" href="https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/medicines/human/EPAR/etcamah">[EMA 2026]</a>

## SERENA-6 design

This randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial enrolled patients with ER-positive, HER2-negative advanced breast cancer receiving first-line AI plus CDK4/6 inhibition for at least **6 months**, without progression. Blood was tested for ESR1 mutations every **2–3 months**; **3,256** patients were tested and **548** had a positive result by screening closure. <a class="cite" href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42442380/">[Lancet Oncol 2026]</a>

Of those eligible without radiological progression, **315** were randomised: **157** switched to camizestrant and **158** continued anastrozole or letrozole, with the same CDK4/6 inhibitor maintained in both groups. <a class="cite" href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42442380/">[Lancet Oncol 2026]</a>

Investigator-assessed PFS from randomisation was the primary endpoint; blinded independent central review (BICR), PFS2, OS and patient-reported outcomes were also assessed. Pre/perimenopausal women and men received an LHRH agonist in the trial. <a class="cite" href="https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2502929">[NEJM 2025]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42442380/">[Lancet Oncol 2026]</a>

## Efficacy: a clear PFS signal, an incomplete survival answer

At the primary interim analysis, investigator-assessed median PFS was **16.0 versus 9.2 months**, HR **0.44 (95% CI 0.31–0.60)**; BICR yielded **19.3 versus 11.5 months**, HR **0.43 (95% CI 0.29–0.63)**. <a class="cite" href="https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2502929">[NEJM 2025]</a>

The extended analysis, with median follow-up of **23.5 months** and a **2 January 2026** cutoff, reported descriptive PFS and the final PFS2 analysis. Times start at randomisation after mutation detection, rather than at the start of first-line therapy. <a class="cite" href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42442380/">[Lancet Oncol 2026]</a>

| Measure | Camizestrant switch versus continued AI |
|---|---|
| Updated investigator PFS | 16.8 versus 9.2 months; HR 0.45 (95% CI 0.34–0.59). <a class="cite" href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42442380/">[Lancet Oncol 2026]</a> |
| Final PFS2 | 25.7 versus 19.1 months; HR 0.63 (95% CI 0.46–0.86). <a class="cite" href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42442380/">[Lancet Oncol 2026]</a> |
| Later OS, immature | HR 0.87 (95% CI 0.57–1.30). <a class="cite" href="https://health.ec.europa.eu/document/download/eb0c1717-e076-458e-a17c-1db044afe0fe_en?filename=hta_jca_mp_202414_camizestrant_summary-report_en.pdf">[EU assessment 2026]</a> |
| Interim global health status/QoL deterioration | 21.0 versus 6.4 months; HR 0.54 (95% CI 0.34–0.84). <a class="cite" href="https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2502929">[NEJM 2025]</a> |

PFS2 measures time from randomisation to progression after the first subsequent treatment or death. Although the final analysis met its statistical threshold, subsequent treatment was not standardised; FDA reviewers considered PFS2 inadequate to resolve the clinical value of early switching. <a class="cite" href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42442380/">[Lancet Oncol 2026]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://www.fda.gov/media/192245/download">[FDA reviewers 2026]</a>

The OS confidence interval includes no difference and clinically relevant effects in either direction. The QoL result favoured the switch, but missing data and methodological limitations constrain interpretation; FDA reviewers did not accept patient-reported outcomes as evidence of clinical benefit for this decision. <a class="cite" href="https://health.ec.europa.eu/document/download/eb0c1717-e076-458e-a17c-1db044afe0fe_en?filename=hta_jca_mp_202414_camizestrant_summary-report_en.pdf">[EU assessment 2026]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://www.fda.gov/media/192245/download">[FDA reviewers 2026]</a>

## Toxicity and practical safety

In the extended analysis, grade **3–4** neutropenia was reported in **27% versus 17%**, and decreased neutrophil count in **23% versus 19%**; these are separately reported adverse-event terms. Serious adverse events occurred in **15% versus 19%**. Continue blood-count surveillance and assess fever promptly. <a class="cite" href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42442380/">[Lancet Oncol 2026]</a>

At the later cutoff, grade **≥3** adverse events occurred in **64.5% versus 52.9%**. Exposure durations and prior AI exposure differ, so crude rates need context. <a class="cite" href="https://health.ec.europa.eu/document/download/eb0c1717-e076-458e-a17c-1db044afe0fe_en?filename=hta_jca_mp_202414_camizestrant_summary-report_en.pdf">[EU assessment 2026]</a>

At the original interim analysis, photopsia occurred in **20.0% versus 7.7%**, mostly grade **1**; no patient discontinued because of it. Bradycardia and sinus bradycardia occurred in **5.2%** and **2.6%**, respectively, in the camizestrant group. Discuss visual changes and assess dizziness, syncope or a slow pulse. <a class="cite" href="https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2502929">[NEJM 2025]</a>

The US label carries a boxed warning for arrhythmia risk with QTc-prolonging drugs, including ribociclib. Avoid other QTc-prolonging drugs where possible; obtain an ECG before treatment, weekly for the first **2 weeks**, and periodically thereafter as clinically indicated. Assess and correct electrolytes before and during treatment, monitor heart rate and QTc, and follow label instructions for dose interruption or discontinuation. The label also warns about bradycardia and embryo-fetal toxicity. <a class="cite" href="https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=c54223d3-f3c7-4186-afa0-429d84805826">[FDA label 2026]</a>

## The regulatory split

The FDA asked its oncology advisory committee whether switching at mutation detection, before radiographic progression, had demonstrated clinically meaningful benefit. On **30 Apr 2026**, the vote was **3 yes, 6 no**. Reviewers questioned the meaning of PFS measured from molecular detection, whether switching early improves long-term outcomes, and whether PFS2, patient reports or time without chemotherapy or antibody-drug conjugates could settle that question. They also weighed cardiac toxicity against uncertain benefit. <a class="cite" href="https://www.fda.gov/media/192244/download">[ODAC question]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://www.fda.gov/media/192245/download">[FDA reviewers]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://www.astrazeneca.com/media-centre/press-releases/2026/fda-odac-vote-on-camizestrant-breast-cancer.html">[AstraZeneca]</a>

The CHMP gave a positive opinion on **21 May 2026** and EU authorisation followed on **20 Jul 2026**. The FDA nevertheless granted accelerated approval on **4 Sep 2026**, based on PFS from mutation detection, with continued approval contingent on confirmatory evidence. Its postmarketing requirements include final OS from SERENA-6 and a new trial reporting time to strategy failure and OS. The letter specifies final reports in **12/2028** and **09/2032**, respectively. These different decisions reflect the weight placed on the available endpoint and residual uncertainty; they do not make the missing survival answer disappear. <a class="cite" href="https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/smop-initial/chmp-summary-positive-opinion-etcamah_en.pdf">[CHMP]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/medicines/human/EPAR/etcamah">[EMA]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://www.fda.gov/drugs/cancer-approvals/fda-grants-accelerated-approval-camizestrant-cdk46-inhibitor-esr1-mutated-hr-positive-her2-negative">[FDA]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/appletter/2026/220359Orig1s000ltr.pdf">[FDA letter]</a>

## Who fits the label

The FDA indication covers adults with HR-positive, HER2-negative locally advanced or metastatic breast cancer, with an ESR1 mutation detected by an FDA-authorized test during AI plus CDK4/6 inhibitor therapy. The authorised partners are abemaciclib, palbociclib and ribociclib. Guardant360 CDx is the FDA-approved blood-based companion diagnostic. Camizestrant is **75 mg orally once daily**, with or without food; continue the CDK4/6 inhibitor at the dose used when the mutation was detected. <a class="cite" href="https://www.fda.gov/drugs/cancer-approvals/fda-grants-accelerated-approval-camizestrant-cdk46-inhibitor-esr1-mutated-hr-positive-her2-negative">[FDA 2026]</a> The US label specifies at least **6 months** of prior AI plus CDK4/6 inhibition and plasma testing every **3 months** until a mutation is detected. <a class="cite" href="https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=c54223d3-f3c7-4186-afa0-429d84805826">[FDA label 2026]</a>

The EU wording specifies detection of ESR1 mutation **without disease progression during first-line endocrine therapy plus CDK4/6 inhibition**. It requires LHRH agonist or antagonist treatment for pre/perimenopausal women and men. Check the relevant local product information and assay requirements: trial eligibility and regional label wording are related but not interchangeable. <a class="cite" href="https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/medicines/human/EPAR/etcamah">[EMA]</a>

## What it changes in practice

For someone matching the trial population, a ctDNA surveillance pathway can identify an endocrine switch before radiological progression. The case for use rests on the PFS findings and a mutation-directed change that retains the existing CDK4/6 partner. Before testing, agree on how results will be reviewed and what treatment will follow a positive result. <a class="cite" href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42442380/">[Lancet Oncol 2026]</a>

Continuing the current regimen until radiological progression and switching endocrine therapy later is an alternative strategy. SERENA-6 did not directly compare early and later camizestrant switches, so it cannot establish which timing gives better long-term outcomes. Discuss this uncertainty alongside cardiac risk, access and patient preferences. <a class="cite" href="https://www.fda.gov/media/192245/download">[FDA reviewers]</a>

## India and resource-limited settings

In India and other resource-limited settings, repeated ctDNA testing requires an appropriate assay, timely results and an affordable route to treatment. Access to the drug, ECG monitoring and assessment of bradycardia and interactions also shapes feasibility.

Continuing AI plus CDK4/6 inhibition until clinical or radiological progression remains a reasonable standard when testing or treatment is unavailable, or when an informed patient prefers that approach. **We did not verify CDSCO approval or an Indian price** for camizestrant. <a class="cite" href="https://www.fda.gov/media/192245/download">[FDA reviewers]</a>

## Open questions

- Does an early molecular switch improve mature OS or time to strategy failure compared with a later switch?
- Which patients benefit most, and how should a negative or uncertain ctDNA result inform repeat testing?
- How should cardiac monitoring and drug interactions be managed in routine practice, especially with ribociclib?
- Can the testing and treatment pathway be delivered equitably where resources are constrained?

## Bottom line

SERENA-6 makes molecular progression an actionable treatment point for a defined population and shows a substantial PFS improvement. The FDA and EU authorisations provide routes to use it, but **OS is immature and the long-term advantage of switching before radiological progression is unproven**. Offer a balanced discussion of the potential delay in progression, toxicity, testing burden and the option to continue current treatment.
