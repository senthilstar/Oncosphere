## Why this matters

ROSELLA tested relacorilant added to weekly nab-paclitaxel in previously treated platinum-resistant ovarian cancer. The PFS difference was small, but the final overall survival (OS) analysis supports a clinically relevant benefit in the studied population. Treatment selection still depends on prior bevacizumab, marrow reserve, steroid needs and access. <a class="cite" href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41974149/">[Lancet 2026]</a>

## How relacorilant works

Relacorilant is a selective, reversible glucocorticoid receptor (GR) antagonist. GR signalling promotes anti-apoptotic proteins, and blockade increased chemotherapy sensitivity in xenograft models; this rationale does not establish single-agent activity in ovarian cancer. <a class="cite" href="https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/medicines/human/EPAR/lifyorli">[EMA]</a>

GR antagonism can also interfere with systemic steroid treatment. Review medications and prepare an acute illness plan before starting therapy. <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/220641s000lbl.pdf">[US label]</a>

## The ROSELLA trial design

ROSELLA (GOG-3073/ENGOT-ov72; NCT05257408), an open-label phase 3 trial, enrolled at **117 sites in 14 countries** across Australia, Europe, Latin America, North America and South Korea. <a class="cite" href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41974149/">[Lancet 2026]</a> Between **5 Jan 2023 and 8 Apr 2024**, **381 patients** were randomised **1:1** to the combination (**188**) or control (**193**). <a class="cite" href="https://discovery.ucl.ac.uk/id/eprint/10213945/1/ROSELLA_Final%20Accepted%20Manuscript%20Full_5_14_2025.pdf">[Lancet 2025]</a>

Eligible patients had epithelial ovarian, fallopian tube or primary peritoneal cancer of high-grade serous or endometrioid histology, or carcinosarcoma with **≥30% epithelial component**; platinum resistance meant progression **<6 months** after the last platinum dose. <a class="cite" href="https://discovery.ucl.ac.uk/id/eprint/10213945/1/ROSELLA_Final%20Accepted%20Manuscript%20Full_5_14_2025.pdf">[Lancet 2025]</a> Additionally, measurable disease by **RECIST 1.1**, **ECOG 0–1**, no more than **three prior lines**, and prior bevacizumab were required. <a class="cite" href="https://discovery.ucl.ac.uk/id/eprint/10213945/1/ROSELLA_Final%20Accepted%20Manuscript%20Full_5_14_2025.pdf">[Lancet 2025]</a>

- **Combination:** relacorilant **150 mg orally** on the day before, of and after each infusion, plus nab-paclitaxel **80 mg/m² IV on days 1, 8 and 15 of each 28-day cycle**. <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/220641s000lbl.pdf">[US label]</a>
- **Control:** nab-paclitaxel **100 mg/m²** on the same schedule. <a class="cite" href="https://discovery.ucl.ac.uk/id/eprint/10213945/1/ROSELLA_Final%20Accepted%20Manuscript%20Full_5_14_2025.pdf">[Lancet 2025]</a>

PFS by blinded independent central review (BICR) and OS were dual primary endpoints; OS was added after regulatory feedback. Blinded imaging review helps interpretation of PFS in this open-label study. <a class="cite" href="https://discovery.ucl.ac.uk/id/eprint/10213945/1/ROSELLA_Final%20Accepted%20Manuscript%20Full_5_14_2025.pdf">[Lancet 2025]</a>

## Efficacy: PFS, OS and response

Combination results are listed first throughout.

- **Primary PFS:** median **6.5 (95% CI 5.6–7.4) versus 5.5 (3.9–5.9) months**; HR **0.70**, **95% CI 0.54–0.91**, **p=0.0076**. <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/220641s000lbl.pdf">[US label]</a>
- **Interim OS:** median **15.97 (95% CI 13.47–not reached) versus 11.50 (10.02–13.57) months**; HR **0.69 (95% CI 0.52–0.92)**, **p=0.0121**, which did not meet the prespecified interim significance boundary. <a class="cite" href="https://discovery.ucl.ac.uk/id/eprint/10213945/1/ROSELLA_Final%20Accepted%20Manuscript%20Full_5_14_2025.pdf">[Lancet 2025]</a>
- **Final OS:** median follow-up **24.8 months (95% CI 23.6–25.7)**; median OS **16.0 (95% CI 13.0–18.3) versus 11.9 (10.0–13.8) months**, a difference of **4.1 months**; HR **0.65 (95% CI 0.51–0.83)**, **p=0.0004**. <a class="cite" href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41974149/">[Lancet 2026]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/220641s000lbl.pdf">[US label]</a>

Subsequent anticancer treatments were similarly distributed between groups. <a class="cite" href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41974149/">[Lancet 2026]</a>

Investigator-assessed ORR was **36.9% (69/187) versus 30.1% (58/193)**, with **nominal p=0.17**. <a class="cite" href="https://discovery.ucl.ac.uk/id/eprint/10213945/1/ROSELLA_Final%20Accepted%20Manuscript%20Full_5_14_2025.pdf">[Lancet 2025]</a> Clinical benefit at **24 weeks** was **51.1% versus 38.9%** (**nominal p=0.016**); CA-125 response was **43.4% versus 37.4%**. <a class="cite" href="https://discovery.ucl.ac.uk/id/eprint/10213945/1/ROSELLA_Final%20Accepted%20Manuscript%20Full_5_14_2025.pdf">[Lancet 2025]</a> These exploratory outcomes provide context for the primary endpoints. <a class="cite" href="https://discovery.ucl.ac.uk/id/eprint/10213945/1/ROSELLA_Final%20Accepted%20Manuscript%20Full_5_14_2025.pdf">[Lancet 2025]</a>

## Toxicity and the steroid problem

In the primary report safety population (**188 versus 190** treated), grade **≥3 AEs** occurred in **74.5% versus 59.5%**, and serious AEs in **35.1% versus 23.7%**. <a class="cite" href="https://discovery.ucl.ac.uk/id/eprint/10213945/1/ROSELLA_Final%20Accepted%20Manuscript%20Full_5_14_2025.pdf">[Lancet 2025]</a> Grade **≥3 neutropenia** was **43.6% versus 25.3%**, anaemia **18.1% versus 8.4%**, and fatigue **9.0% versus 1.6%**. <a class="cite" href="https://discovery.ucl.ac.uk/id/eprint/10213945/1/ROSELLA_Final%20Accepted%20Manuscript%20Full_5_14_2025.pdf">[Lancet 2025]</a> Serious febrile neutropenia occurred in **2.1% versus 0.5%**; growth-factor use was **43.6% versus 21.6%**. <a class="cite" href="https://discovery.ucl.ac.uk/id/eprint/10213945/1/ROSELLA_Final%20Accepted%20Manuscript%20Full_5_14_2025.pdf">[Lancet 2025]</a>

Median nab-paclitaxel exposure was **4.52 versus 3.48 months**, about **30% longer** with combination treatment. <a class="cite" href="https://discovery.ucl.ac.uk/id/eprint/10213945/1/ROSELLA_Final%20Accepted%20Manuscript%20Full_5_14_2025.pdf">[Lancet 2025]</a> Exposure-adjusted differences overlapped zero for serious AEs, including febrile neutropenia, and for neutropenia and anaemia. That context does not remove the observed burden. **Four deaths due to AEs** occurred, all in the combination arm: cardiac arrest, intestinal perforation, ischaemic stroke and septic shock. <a class="cite" href="https://www.thelancet.com/journals/lancet/article/PIIS0140-6736(25)01040-2/abstract">[Lancet 2025]</a>

For the combination, the label reports febrile neutropenia at **3.7%**; keep this separate from the primary paper's serious-event comparison. <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/220641s000lbl.pdf">[US label]</a> Check **CBC before every weekly treatment and as needed**. <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/220641s000lbl.pdf">[US label]</a> Adverse reactions led to relacorilant interruptions in **72%**, nab-paclitaxel dose reductions in **48%**, and permanent discontinuation of the combination in **9%**. <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/220641s000lbl.pdf">[US label]</a> The final OS report identified no new safety signals. <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/220641s000lbl.pdf">[US label]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41974149/">[Lancet 2026]</a>

**Adrenal insufficiency needs an explicit response plan.** It can develop at any time, with greater risk during infection, surgery or other stress. Serum cortisol is unreliable for assessing it during relacorilant treatment. Withhold relacorilant and give glucocorticoids for suspected adrenal insufficiency; high doses may be necessary. GR antagonism can persist for **6 days** after the last dose. <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/220641s000lbl.pdf">[US label]</a> Consider supplemental perioperative steroids if relacorilant was taken within **30 days** of surgery. <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/220641s000lbl.pdf">[US label]</a>

Concurrent systemic glucocorticoids for a lifesaving indication, such as transplant immunosuppression, are contraindicated. Steroid-treated conditions may worsen. Relacorilant is a strong **CYP3A inhibitor** and weak **CYP2C8 inducer**: avoid CYP3A substrates and certain CYP2C8 substrates unless otherwise recommended; avoid strong CYP3A inducers. Avoid use in moderate or severe hepatic impairment. Take capsules with food; discuss embryo-fetal toxicity. <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/220641s000lbl.pdf">[US label]</a>

## Who fits the label (FDA and EU)

**FDA approval, 25 Mar 2026:** Lifyorli with nab-paclitaxel for adults with platinum-resistant epithelial ovarian, fallopian tube or primary peritoneal cancer after **one to three systemic regimens**, including **at least one containing bevacizumab**. Patients needing chronic or frequent glucocorticoids were excluded from the trial. Use the combination's labelled chemotherapy dose. <a class="cite" href="https://www.fda.gov/drugs/cancer-approvals/fda-approves-relacorilant-nab-paclitaxel-platinum-resistant-epithelial-ovarian-fallopian-tube-or">[FDA approval]</a>

**EU, checked 21 Nov 2026:** CHMP gave a positive opinion on **17 Sep 2026** for adults with platinum-resistant **high-grade** epithelial disease at these sites after **one to three systemic regimens**. The wording has **no bevacizumab requirement**. The EMA page remains at the opinion stage; a November CHMP agenda lists the application in the decision phase after a revised opinion on **23 Oct 2026**. <a class="cite" href="https://www.ema.europa.eu/system/files/documents/agenda/draft-chmp-agenda-09-12-november-2026-en.pdf">[EMA 2026]</a> The European Commission decision was not found in the sources checked. An opinion must not be described as completed EU authorisation. <a class="cite" href="https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/medicines/human/EPAR/lifyorli">[EMA]</a>

## What it changes in practice

This is an option for bevacizumab-pretreated disease without biomarker selection; discuss benefit alongside the observed toxicity burden. <a class="cite" href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41974149/">[Lancet 2026]</a> In the final analysis, **44%** had received **three prior lines** and **61%** a PARP inhibitor. <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/220641s000lbl.pdf">[US label]</a> For FRα-high disease, mirvetuximab remains another targeted option; treatment choice requires an individual discussion. <a class="cite" href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41974149/">[Lancet 2026]</a>

A subsequent exploratory analysis reported OS favouring the combination across prior taxane exposure subgroups; subgroup findings should be interpreted cautiously. <a class="cite" href="https://ascopubs.org/doi/10.1200/JCO.2026.44.16_suppl.5503">[JCO 2026 abstract]</a>

ROSELLA used nab-paclitaxel as its control; it did not directly compare the combination with weekly paclitaxel, pegylated liposomal doxorubicin, topotecan or gemcitabine. Nab-paclitaxel doses differed between arms; pharmacokinetic data informed the lower combination dose because relacorilant inhibits its metabolism. <a class="cite" href="https://discovery.ucl.ac.uk/id/eprint/10213945/1/ROSELLA_Final%20Accepted%20Manuscript%20Full_5_14_2025.pdf">[Lancet 2025]</a>

## India and resource-limited settings

Plan steroid-free antiemetics and premedication where feasible. Nab-paclitaxel generally needs no hypersensitivity premedication; consider it after a previous reaction and check the intended regimen carefully. <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2019/021660s046lbl.pdf">[FDA 2019]</a> Patients should understand adrenal-insufficiency symptoms and carry a treatment explanation for emergency units far from the treating team.

Only **13%** of the trial population was Asian, and the participating regions (Australia, Europe, Latin America, North America and South Korea) did not include India. Local applicability and supportive-care delivery therefore need attention. <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/220641s000lbl.pdf">[US label]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://www.thelancet.com/journals/lancet/article/PIIS0140-6736(25)01040-2/abstract">[Lancet 2025]</a>

## Open questions

Comparison with other single agents, outcomes in bevacizumab-naive patients and fuller quality-of-life detail remain important. Whether the benefit carries over to other chemotherapy partners is not answered by ROSELLA. Real-world adrenal-insufficiency rates and Asian/Indian experience will help define practical use beyond trial care.

## Bottom line

ROSELLA showed a modest median PFS gain and a meaningful final OS gain. Relacorilant adds an oral, non-chemotherapy component to weekly chemotherapy, with substantial monitoring and steroid-management obligations. Discuss the survival evidence, prior-treatment fit and supportive-care demands together.
