## Why this matters

ROSELLA supports adding oral relacorilant to weekly nab-paclitaxel in bevacizumab-pretreated platinum-resistant ovarian cancer without biomarker selection. The absolute PFS improvement was modest; the final OS result makes the combination clinically consequential. It is a non-chemotherapy add-on, but treatment still involves weekly chemotherapy and substantial supportive care.

The useful question is where this regimen fits for an individual patient: prior bevacizumab, marrow reserve, glucocorticoid requirements and access all matter. Benefit should be discussed alongside the higher observed toxicity burden. <a class="cite" href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41974149/">[Lancet 2026]</a>

## How relacorilant works

Relacorilant selectively and reversibly antagonises the glucocorticoid receptor (GR). GR activation increases anti-apoptotic proteins; blocking that pathway enhanced chemotherapy sensitivity in xenograft models. This is a rationale for combining it with chemotherapy, rather than evidence that relacorilant alone controls this disease. <a class="cite" href="https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/medicines/human/EPAR/lifyorli">[EMA]</a>

GR blockade also creates a practical conflict with systemic steroids. Medication review and a plan for acute illness belong in treatment preparation, alongside the usual chemotherapy assessment.

## The ROSELLA trial design

ROSELLA, GOG-3073/ENGOT-ov72, was a randomised, open-label phase 3 study at **117 sites in 14 countries** across Australia, Europe, Latin America, North America and South Korea. From **5 Jan 2023 to 8 Apr 2024**, **381 patients** were randomised **1:1**, with **188 versus 193** assigned to combination or control.

Eligible disease was platinum-resistant epithelial ovarian, primary peritoneal or fallopian tube cancer: high-grade serous, endometrioid, or carcinosarcoma with **≥30% epithelial component**. Patients needed measurable disease by **RECIST 1.1**, **ECOG 0–1**, up to **three prior lines**, and prior bevacizumab.

- **Combination:** relacorilant **150 mg orally** the day before, day of and day after each infusion; nab-paclitaxel **80 mg/m² IV on days 1, 8 and 15 every 28 days**.
- **Control:** nab-paclitaxel **100 mg/m²** on the same schedule.

PFS by blinded independent central review (BICR) and OS were dual primary endpoints. OS was added as a primary endpoint after regulatory feedback; PFS had originally been the sole primary endpoint. Open-label treatment remains relevant to interpretation, although BICR supported the PFS assessment. <a class="cite" href="https://www.thelancet.com/journals/lancet/article/PIIS0140-6736(25)01040-2/abstract">[Lancet 2025]</a>

## Efficacy: PFS, OS and response

All comparisons below list combination before control.

- **Primary PFS:** median **6.5 versus 5.5 months**; HR **0.70**, **95% CI 0.54–0.91**, **p=0.0076**.
- **Interim OS:** median **16.0 versus 11.5 months**; HR **0.69 (0.52–0.92)**, **p=0.012**.
- **Final OS:** with median follow-up **24.8 months**, median **16.0 (13.0–18.3) versus 11.9 (10.0–13.8) months**, an absolute difference of **4.1 months**; HR **0.65 (95% CI 0.51–0.83)**, **p=0.0004**. Subsequent anticancer treatment was similar between arms. <a class="cite" href="https://www.thelancet.com/journals/lancet/article/PIIS0140-6736(25)01040-2/abstract">[Lancet 2025]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41974149/">[Lancet 2026]</a>

Investigator-assessed ORR was **36.9% versus 30.1%**, with **nominal p=0.17**: no statistically significant response-rate difference. Clinical benefit at **24 weeks** was **51.1% versus 38.9%** (**nominal p=0.016**); CA-125 response was **43.4% versus 37.4%**. These outcomes add context without replacing the primary endpoints. <a class="cite" href="https://www.thelancet.com/journals/lancet/article/PIIS0140-6736(25)01040-2/abstract">[Lancet 2025]</a>

## Toxicity and the steroid problem

In the primary report, grade **≥3 AEs** occurred in **74.5% versus 59.5%**, and serious AEs in **35.1% versus 23.7%**. Grade **≥3 neutropenia** was **43.6% versus 25.3%**, anaemia **18.1% versus 8.4%**, and fatigue **9.0% versus 1.6%**. Serious febrile neutropenia occurred in **2.1% versus 0.5%**; G-CSF use was **43.6% versus 21.6%**.

Median nab-paclitaxel exposure was **4.52 versus 3.48 months**, about **30% longer** with combination treatment. Exposure-adjusted differences overlapped zero for serious AEs, including febrile neutropenia, and for neutropenia and anaemia. That context does not remove the observed burden. **Four deaths due to AEs** occurred, all in the combination arm: cardiac arrest, intestinal perforation, ischaemic stroke and septic shock. <a class="cite" href="https://www.thelancet.com/journals/lancet/article/PIIS0140-6736(25)01040-2/abstract">[Lancet 2025]</a>

The label reports febrile neutropenia at **3.7%**; keep this separate from the primary paper's serious-event comparison. Check **CBC before every weekly dose**. Dose interruptions occurred in **72%**, nab-paclitaxel reductions in **48%**, and permanent discontinuation in **9%**. The final OS report identified no new safety signals. <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/220641s000lbl.pdf">[US label]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41974149/">[Lancet 2026]</a>

**Adrenal insufficiency needs an explicit response plan.** It can develop at any time, with greater risk during infection, surgery or other stress. Serum cortisol is unreliable for assessing it during relacorilant treatment. Treat suspected adrenal insufficiency with glucocorticoids; high doses may be necessary. GR antagonism can persist for **6 days** after the last dose. Consider supplemental perioperative steroids if relacorilant was taken within **30 days** of surgery.

Concurrent systemic glucocorticoids for a lifesaving indication, such as transplant immunosuppression, are contraindicated. Steroid-treated conditions may worsen. Relacorilant is a strong **CYP3A inhibitor** and weak **CYP2C8 inducer**: avoid CYP3A substrates unless recommended and avoid strong CYP3A inducers. Avoid use in moderate or severe hepatic impairment. Take capsules with food; discuss embryo-fetal toxicity. <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/220641s000lbl.pdf">[US label]</a>

## Who fits the label (FDA and EU)

**FDA approval, 25 Mar 2026:** Lifyorli with nab-paclitaxel for adults with platinum-resistant epithelial ovarian, fallopian tube or primary peritoneal cancer after **one to three systemic regimens**, including **at least one containing bevacizumab**. Patients needing chronic or frequent glucocorticoids were excluded from the trial. Use the combination's labelled chemotherapy dose. <a class="cite" href="https://www.fda.gov/drugs/cancer-approvals/fda-approves-relacorilant-nab-paclitaxel-platinum-resistant-epithelial-ovarian-fallopian-tube-or">[FDA approval]</a>

**EU, checked 16 Nov 2026:** CHMP gave a positive opinion on **17 Sep 2026** for adults with platinum-resistant **high-grade** epithelial disease at these sites after **one to three systemic regimens**. The wording has **no bevacizumab requirement**. The EMA page remains at the opinion stage; the European Commission decision is pending. An opinion must not be described as completed EU authorisation. <a class="cite" href="https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/medicines/human/EPAR/lifyorli">[EMA]</a>

## What it changes in practice

This is an option for bevacizumab-pretreated disease without biomarker selection. In the final analysis, **44%** had received **three prior lines** and **61%** a PARP inhibitor. For FRα-high disease, mirvetuximab remains another targeted option; treatment choice requires an individual discussion. <a class="cite" href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41974149/">[Lancet 2026]</a>

The comparator matters. Weekly paclitaxel, PLD, topotecan and gemcitabine are typical single agents worldwide; ROSELLA compared against nab-paclitaxel. Its chemotherapy doses also differed between arms. These results establish the tested regimen's benefit over its tested control, without ranking it against every alternative.

## India and resource-limited settings

Plan steroid-free antiemetics and premedication where feasible. In usual practice, nab-paclitaxel does not routinely require steroid premedication, unlike conventional paclitaxel; check the intended regimen carefully. Patients should understand adrenal-insufficiency symptoms and carry a treatment explanation for emergency units far from the treating team.

Only **13%** of the trial population was Asian, and the participating regions (Australia, Europe, Latin America, North America and South Korea) did not include India. Local applicability and supportive-care delivery therefore need attention. <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/220641s000lbl.pdf">[US label]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://www.thelancet.com/journals/lancet/article/PIIS0140-6736(25)01040-2/abstract">[Lancet 2025]</a>

## Open questions

Comparison with other single agents, outcomes in bevacizumab-naive patients and fuller quality-of-life detail remain important. Whether the benefit carries over to other chemotherapy partners is not answered by ROSELLA. Real-world adrenal-insufficiency rates and Asian/Indian experience will help define practical use beyond trial care.

## Bottom line

ROSELLA showed a modest median PFS gain and a meaningful final OS gain. Relacorilant adds an oral, non-chemotherapy component to weekly chemotherapy, with substantial monitoring and steroid-management obligations. Discuss the survival evidence, prior-treatment fit and supportive-care demands together.
