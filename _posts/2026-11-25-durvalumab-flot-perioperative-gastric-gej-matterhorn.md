## Why this matters

Resectable gastric and gastroesophageal junction (GEJ) adenocarcinoma can recur despite surgery and perioperative FLOT. MATTERHORN tested whether adding immune checkpoint blockade to that established backbone could improve outcomes. It is the first phase 3 perioperative immunotherapy trial in this disease to improve event-free survival (EFS) and subsequently overall survival (OS). The FDA approved the regimen on **25 November 2025**. European approval on **16 March 2026** was sponsor-reported; an Indian company announcement reporting CDSCO approval appeared in **February 2026**. <a class="cite" href="https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2503701">[NEJM 2025]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://www.thelancet.com/pdfs/journals/lancet/PIIS0140-6736(26)01254-7.pdf">[Lancet 2026]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://www.fda.gov/drugs/cancer-approvals/fda-approves-durvalumab-resectable-gastric-or-gastroesophageal-junction-adenocarcinoma">[FDA 2025]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://www.astrazeneca.com/media-centre/press-releases/2026/imfinzi-approved-in-eu-for-early-gastric-cancer.html">[Sponsor-reported 2026]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://www.expresspharma.in/cdsco-approves-durvalumab-with-flot-for-resectable-gastric-and-gej-cancer-in-india/">[Company announcement reported 2026]</a>

## Why add PD-L1 blockade around surgery

Durvalumab blocks PD-L1. Treatment before resection offers an opportunity to address occult micrometastases early, while tumour antigens remain in place to support immune priming. Continuing after surgery is intended to sustain immune activity when residual microscopic disease may remain. These are biological reasons for the schedule, not proof of the separate contribution of either phase.

## Trial design

MATTERHORN was a randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled phase 3 trial at **147 centres in 20 countries**. It enrolled previously untreated patients with resectable **stage II–IVA** gastric or GEJ adenocarcinoma who were fit for radical surgery. Randomisation was stratified by region, clinical nodal status and PD-L1. Of **948** patients, **474** entered each group; median age was **62** and **72%** were male. <a class="cite" href="https://www.thelancet.com/pdfs/journals/lancet/PIIS0140-6736(26)01254-7.pdf">[Lancet 2026]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2503701">[NEJM 2025]</a>

Durvalumab **1500 mg every 4 weeks** or placebo accompanied FLOT on days **1 and 15** of each **4-week cycle**. Patients received **2 cycles before** and **2 after surgery**, followed by durvalumab or placebo every **4 weeks for 10 further cycles**. FLOT combines fluorouracil, leucovorin, oxaliplatin and docetaxel. The primary endpoint was EFS by blinded independent central review (BICR); key secondary endpoints included OS and pathological complete response (pCR). <a class="cite" href="https://www.thelancet.com/pdfs/journals/lancet/PIIS0140-6736(26)01254-7.pdf">[Lancet 2026]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://www.fda.gov/drugs/cancer-approvals/fda-approves-durvalumab-resectable-gastric-or-gastroesophageal-junction-adenocarcinoma">[FDA 2025]</a>

## Results: EFS and pCR

At the primary analysis, estimated **2-year EFS was 67.4% versus 58.5%**, hazard ratio (HR) **0.71 (95% CI 0.58–0.86)**. Median EFS was not reached with durvalumab versus **32.8 months** with placebo, according to the FDA. The EMA reported EFS events in **167/474 versus 218/474**. The sponsor reported **1-year EFS of 78.2% versus 74.0%**. These are estimates across a population; they do not predict an individual's outcome. <a class="cite" href="https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2503701">[NEJM 2025]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://www.fda.gov/drugs/cancer-approvals/fda-approves-durvalumab-resectable-gastric-or-gastroesophageal-junction-adenocarcinoma">[FDA 2025]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/medicines/human/EPAR/imfinzi">[EMA]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://www.astrazeneca.com/media-centre/press-releases/2026/imfinzi-approved-in-eu-for-early-gastric-cancer.html">[Sponsor-reported 2026]</a>

pCR occurred in **19.2% versus 7.2%**, relative risk **2.69 (1.86–3.90)**. Pathological response describes the resection specimen; EFS also captures later clinical events. A response is encouraging, but it is not a guarantee of cure. <a class="cite" href="https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2503701">[NEJM 2025]</a>

## Results: overall survival

At the interim analysis, the piecewise OS HR was **0.99 (0.70–1.39)** through **12 months** and **0.67 (0.50–0.90)** thereafter. The interim **p=0.03** did not cross the prespecified **p<0.0001** boundary. The final analysis subsequently met its boundary: OS HR **0.78 (95% CI 0.63–0.96), p=0.021**, against **p<0.0499**. The EMA reported **160 versus 192 deaths**. <a class="cite" href="https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2503701">[NEJM 2025]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://www.thelancet.com/pdfs/journals/lancet/PIIS0140-6736(26)01254-7.pdf">[Lancet 2026]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/medicines/human/EPAR/imfinzi">[EMA]</a>

A secondary report gave final-analysis **2-year OS as 76% versus 70%** and **3-year OS as 69% versus 62%**; the latter estimate was also sponsor-reported. Later separation of the curves may reflect time needed for differences in recurrence to translate into deaths, but the trial cannot establish that explanation. The piecewise analysis is descriptive of the observed pattern, not a promise of delayed benefit for every patient. <a class="cite" href="https://news.gastro.org/issues/2026/october-2026/perioperative-durvalumab-improved-survival-in-phase-3-gastric-cancer-trial/">[Gastro.org 2026]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://www.astrazeneca.com/media-centre/press-releases/2026/imfinzi-approved-in-eu-for-early-gastric-cancer.html">[Sponsor-reported 2026]</a>

## PD-L1 and pathology

In an exploratory analysis, OS HR was **0.79 (0.41–1.50)** for PD-L1 tumour area positivity (TAP) **<1%** and **0.79 (0.63–0.99)** for TAP **≥1%**. The lower-expression subgroup was small, with a wide confidence interval compatible with considerable uncertainty. These estimates do not establish equivalent effects between subgroups. <a class="cite" href="https://www.annalsofoncology.org/article/S0923-7534(25)04866-5/fulltext">[Annals of Oncology 2025]</a>

Node-negative pathology (ypN0) was reported in **58.2% versus 44.8%**. Exploratory EFS HRs within pCR, major pathological response (MPR), ypN-negative and ypN-positive groups were **0.29 (0.08–0.96)**, **0.32 (0.15–0.68)**, **0.74 (0.46–1.18)** and **0.77 (0.58–1.02)**, respectively. These are post-randomisation groups: treatment can affect who enters them. They cannot preserve the original randomised comparison and are not a reason to select or discontinue treatment. <a class="cite" href="https://www.annalsofoncology.org/article/S0923-7534(25)04866-5/fulltext">[Annals of Oncology 2025]</a>

## Safety and surgery

Grade **3–4 adverse events** occurred in **71.6% versus 71.2%**. Delayed surgery was reported in **10.1% versus 10.8%**, and delayed adjuvant start in **2.3% versus 4.6%**. Deaths considered possibly treatment-related occurred in **6 versus 2** patients. Similar overall rates do not mean that the types or consequences of toxicity are identical. <a class="cite" href="https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2503701">[NEJM 2025]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://www.thelancet.com/pdfs/journals/lancet/PIIS0140-6736(26)01254-7.pdf">[Lancet 2026]</a>

Common adverse reactions in the FDA label include diarrhoea, nausea, peripheral neuropathy, fatigue, alopecia, decreased appetite, rash, abdominal pain, vomiting, musculoskeletal pain, pyrexia and stomatitis. Check thyroid and liver function and investigate new respiratory symptoms for pneumonitis. Diarrhoea may arise from FLOT or immune-mediated colitis; assess severity and competing causes promptly rather than assuming a single mechanism. Follow the label for interruption, immune toxicity management and resumption, coordinating decisions with the surgical team. <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/761069s060lbl.pdf">[FDA label 2026]</a>

## Why KEYNOTE-585 missed and MATTERHORN hit

KEYNOTE-585 tested pembrolizumab with a cisplatin–fluoropyrimidine backbone in its main cohort. EFS did not meet the prespecified significance boundary at the planned interim analysis. Its FLOT cohort was smaller and exploratory, without power for a definitive comparison. MATTERHORN used FLOT throughout and a different statistical design. These differences may help explain the different formal results, but neither identifies a single cause. Populations, treatment, follow-up and testing plans differ; cross-trial comparisons cannot rank the immunotherapies or establish superiority of a backbone. <a class="cite" href="https://ascopubs.org/doi/10.1200/JCO-25-00486">[JCO 2025]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13756502/">[KEYNOTE-585 exploratory analysis]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2503701">[NEJM 2025]</a>

## Regulatory status and dosing

The FDA indication is for adults with resectable gastric or GEJ adenocarcinoma: durvalumab with FLOT before and after surgery, then durvalumab alone. At **≥30 kg**, the recommended dose is **1500 mg every 4 weeks**; at **<30 kg**, it is **20 mg/kg**. The schedule allows up to **12 cycles after surgery**, including up to **2 with FLOT** and **10 alone**. The trial was **not designed to isolate the effect of durvalumab in each phase**. Consult the label for full dosing and modifications. <a class="cite" href="https://www.fda.gov/drugs/cancer-approvals/fda-approves-durvalumab-resectable-gastric-or-gastroesophageal-junction-adenocarcinoma">[FDA 2025]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/761069s060lbl.pdf">[FDA label 2026]</a>

The EU indication similarly covers adults with resectable gastric or GEJ adenocarcinoma receiving durvalumab with neoadjuvant and adjuvant FLOT, followed by adjuvant monotherapy. CDSCO approval of the same treatment sequence was reported through a company announcement. Use the applicable local label. <a class="cite" href="https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/medicines/human/EPAR/imfinzi">[EMA]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://www.expresspharma.in/cdsco-approves-durvalumab-with-flot-for-resectable-gastric-and-gej-cancer-in-india/">[Company announcement reported 2026]</a>

## What it changes in practice

For a fit patient with resectable **stage II–IVA** disease able to tolerate FLOT and radical surgery, discuss this perioperative option at a multidisciplinary meeting. Assess nutrition, performance status, comorbidity and surgical planning alongside immune toxicity and treatment access. The study does not resolve treatment for elderly or frail patients unable to receive FLOT. Postoperative recovery can limit adjuvant treatment; discuss the planned course and the possibility of interruption before starting.

MSI-high/dMMR disease is a distinct question. MATTERHORN was not built to define the best strategy for this small molecular group, and immunotherapy-first approaches without routine perioperative chemotherapy are under study. Test MMR/MSI status at diagnosis and discuss these patients individually rather than assuming the overall result settles their sequence.

For appropriate resectable GEJ adenocarcinoma, FLOT is often preferred over CROSS after ESOPEC: in oesophageal adenocarcinoma, OS HR was **0.70 (0.53–0.92)** and median OS **66 versus 37 months** with perioperative FLOT versus CROSS. Durvalumab plus FLOT therefore fits naturally into a FLOT-based pathway for eligible patients, while the populations and comparisons remain distinct. <a class="cite" href="https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2409408">[NEJM 2025]</a>

## India and resource-limited settings

CDSCO approval exists, but drug cost and approximately **1 year** of **4-weekly** infusions can make the full course difficult to access. Travel, laboratory monitoring and the ability to manage immune toxicity matter alongside the medicine itself. We did not verify an Indian price. <a class="cite" href="https://www.expresspharma.in/cdsco-approves-durvalumab-with-flot-for-resectable-gastric-and-gej-cancer-in-india/">[Company announcement reported 2026]</a>

FLOT requires reliable supportive care, clinical judgement about G-CSF availability and use, and timely surgery with quality D2 lymphadenectomy. Many Indian patients present beyond resectable stage, so staging and multidisciplinary assessment remain essential. Where durvalumab is unaffordable, well-delivered perioperative FLOT with quality D2 gastrectomy remains an evidence-based backbone. PD-L1 testing is not required for selection under the FDA label; local requirements may differ. <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/761069s060lbl.pdf">[FDA label 2026]</a>

## Open questions

- Which patients benefit from each treatment phase, and can the postoperative course be tailored safely?
- What is the best approach for MSI-high/dMMR disease and for patients unable to tolerate FLOT?
- How will longer follow-up, postoperative treatment completion and access affect outcomes in routine care?

## Bottom line

Adding perioperative durvalumab to FLOT improved EFS and final OS in resectable gastric and GEJ adenocarcinoma. The evidence supports a complete treatment strategy for selected patients; surgical quality, supportive care, immune toxicity and access remain essential to its delivery. <a class="cite" href="https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2503701">[NEJM 2025]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://www.thelancet.com/pdfs/journals/lancet/PIIS0140-6736(26)01254-7.pdf">[Lancet 2026]</a>
