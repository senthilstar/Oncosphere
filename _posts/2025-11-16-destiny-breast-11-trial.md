## Design
Phase 3, open-label, 147 sites in 18 countries; randomised 1:1:1. High-risk HER2+ early breast cancer (≥T3, node-positive or inflammatory). Arms: T-DXd ×8 (closed early), **T-DXd ×4 → THP ×4**, ddAC ×4 → THP ×4. Primary endpoint: pCR (ypT0/is ypN0).

## Results (data cut-off 12 Mar 2025)
- Randomised: T-DXd 286, T-DXd → THP 321, ddAC → THP 320 (927 in total). Median follow-up 24.2 vs 23.6 months. <a class="cite" href="https://www.astrazeneca.com/media-centre/press-releases/2025/enhertu-followed-by-thp-before-surgery-resulted-in-a-pcr-in-67-of-patients-in-db11.html">[AstraZeneca 2025]</a>
- pCR: **67.3% vs 56.3%** (Δ 11.2%, 95% CI 4.0–18.3; p=0.003). T-DXd alone 43.0%. <a class="cite" href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41130363/">[Ann Oncol 2026]</a>
- HR+: 61.4% (145/236) vs 52.3% (123/235), Δ 9.1% (95% CI 0.2–17.9). HR−: 83.1% (69/83) vs 67.1% (57/85), Δ 16.1% (95% CI 3.0–28.8). <a class="cite" href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41130363/">[Ann Oncol 2026]</a>
- RCB 0/I: 81.3% vs 69.1% (Δ 12.2%). <a class="cite" href="https://www.astrazeneca.com/media-centre/press-releases/2025/enhertu-followed-by-thp-before-surgery-resulted-in-a-pcr-in-67-of-patients-in-db11.html">[AstraZeneca 2025]</a>
- EFS: HR 0.56 (0.26–1.17), 4.5% maturity — not interpretable yet.
- Grade ≥3 AEs 37.5% vs 55.8% (T-DXd alone 22.6%); serious AEs 10.6% vs 20.2%; all-grade LV dysfunction 1.3% vs 6.1%; adjudicated drug-related ILD 4.4% vs 5.1% (T-DXd alone 4.9%). Treatment-related deaths: 1 (0.3%) vs 2 (0.6%). <a class="cite" href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41130363/">[Ann Oncol 2026]</a>

## Interpretation
- First anthracycline-free regimen to beat ddAC-THP for pCR in a phase 3 trial of high-risk disease.
- Caveats: open label; pCR is a surrogate; EFS/OS awaited; ILD needs vigilance.
- Adjuvant pathway has moved: pCR → complete HP; residual invasive disease → T-DM1 (KATHERINE) or, since May 2026 in the US, T-DXd (DESTINY-Breast05 — see the update below).

<p class="opinion">For high-risk patients with cardiac concerns, T-DXd → THP is attractive where affordable. I would wait for EFS before abandoning anthracycline regimens universally.</p>

## Who was enrolled
High-risk HER2-positive early breast cancer: T3 or larger, inflammatory, or node-positive disease (cT0–4, cN1–3). HER2 status was centrally confirmed. 927 patients were randomised across the three arms (286 / 321 / 320); enrolment to the T-DXd monotherapy arm was closed early on the recommendation of the Independent Data Monitoring Committee. <a class="cite" href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41130363/">[Ann Oncol 2025]</a>

## Regimens in detail
- **T-DXd → THP:** trastuzumab deruxtecan 5.4 mg/kg every 3 weeks ×4 (the FDA-labelled neoadjuvant dose), then paclitaxel + trastuzumab + pertuzumab ×4.
- **ddAC → THP:** dose-dense doxorubicin + cyclophosphamide every 2 weeks ×4, then THP ×4 (the standard high-risk control in many countries).
- After surgery, patients received standard adjuvant HER2-directed therapy per investigator (HP, or T-DM1 for residual disease).

## Why pCR matters (and its limits)
pCR after neoadjuvant therapy is strongly associated with better event-free survival at the patient level, but trial-level gains in pCR have not always translated into EFS gains. Residual disease also opens the door to post-neoadjuvant escalation: T-DM1 improved invasive disease-free survival over trastuzumab in KATHERINE (3-year IDFS 88.3% vs 77.0%; HR 0.50, 95% CI 0.39–0.64), <a class="cite" href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30516102/">[NEJM 2019]</a> and T-DXd improved it further over T-DM1 in DESTINY-Breast05 (HR 0.47). <a class="cite" href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41370739/">[NEJM 2026]</a> Long-term EFS from DESTINY-Breast11 is therefore essential.

## Safety in practice
- **ILD/pneumonitis:** baseline chest imaging, educate about cough and breathlessness, stop T-DXd for any grade ≥2 ILD.
- **Nausea:** T-DXd is moderately-to-highly emetogenic — use a three-drug antiemetic regimen.
- **Cardiac:** baseline and serial LVEF are still required because THP follows.
- **Alopecia and fatigue** occur with both regimens.

## Guideline context
Neoadjuvant anthracycline-free regimens such as TCHP were already acceptable alternatives: in TRAIN-2 (n=438), pCR was 67% with anthracyclines vs 68% without (p=0.95), with more grade ≥3 febrile neutropenia when anthracyclines were added (10% vs 1%). <a class="cite" href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30413379/">[Lancet Oncol 2018]</a> DESTINY-Breast11 adds a new option for the highest-risk patients; NCCN/ESMO adoption should be checked against the current guideline version.

**Regulatory and adjuvant update (verified 28 Oct 2026):**
- **FDA, 15 May 2026:** T-DXd followed by THP approved as neoadjuvant treatment for HER2-positive (IHC 3+ or ISH+) **stage II or III** breast cancer, at 5.4 mg/kg every 3 weeks ×4, then THP ×4. FDA noted that EFS and OS in DESTINY-Breast11 were not statistically controlled or powered, and that DESTINY-Breast05 provided supportive evidence. A post-marketing requirement asks for 3-year EFS and final OS (reports due 11/2027). <a class="cite" href="https://www.fda.gov/drugs/resources-information-approved-drugs/fda-approves-two-separate-indications-fam-trastuzumab-deruxtecan-nxki-her2-positive-early-stage">[FDA 2026]</a>
- **DESTINY-Breast05 (adjuvant, residual disease):** n=1635, T-DXd vs T-DM1, up to 14 cycles. 3-year IDFS **92.4% vs 83.7%** (HR 0.47, 95% CI 0.34–0.66; P<0.001). Adjudicated drug-related ILD **9.6% vs 1.6%**; 2 ILD deaths with T-DXd. Nausea 71.3% with T-DXd. <a class="cite" href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41370739/">[NEJM 2026]</a> FDA approved adjuvant T-DXd for residual invasive disease on the same day. <a class="cite" href="https://www.fda.gov/drugs/resources-information-approved-drugs/fda-approves-two-separate-indications-fam-trastuzumab-deruxtecan-nxki-her2-positive-early-stage">[FDA 2026]</a>
- The T-DXd label carries a boxed warning for ILD/pneumonitis. ILD risk was about 4–5% with 4 neoadjuvant cycles (DESTINY-Breast11) but 9.6% with up to 14 adjuvant cycles (DESTINY-Breast05), so take exposure into account when planning a T-DXd-containing sequence.
