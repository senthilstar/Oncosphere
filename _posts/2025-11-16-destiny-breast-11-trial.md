## Design
Phase 3, open-label, 147 sites. High-risk HER2+ early breast cancer (≥T3, node-positive or inflammatory). Arms: T-DXd ×8 (closed early), **T-DXd ×4 → THP ×4**, ddAC ×4 → THP ×4. Primary endpoint: pCR (ypT0/is ypN0).

## Results (data cut-off 12 Mar 2025)
- pCR: **67.3% vs 56.3%** (Δ 11.2%, 95% CI 4.0–18.3; p=0.003). T-DXd alone 43.0%. <a class="cite" href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41130363/">[Ann Oncol 2025]</a>
- HR+: 61.4% vs 52.3%. HR−: 83.1% vs 67.1%.
- RCB 0/I: 81.3% vs 69.1%.
- EFS: HR 0.56 (0.26–1.17), 4.5% maturity — not interpretable yet.
- Grade ≥3 AEs 37.5% vs 55.8%; serious AEs 10.6% vs 20.2%; LV dysfunction 1.3% vs 6.1% (Ann Oncol); adjudicated ILD 4.4% vs 5.1%.

## Interpretation
- First anthracycline-free regimen to beat ddAC-THP for pCR in a phase 3 trial of high-risk disease.
- Caveats: open label; pCR is a surrogate; EFS/OS awaited; ILD needs vigilance.
- Adjuvant pathway unchanged: residual disease → T-DM1 (KATHERINE); pCR → complete HP.

<p class="opinion">For high-risk patients with cardiac concerns, T-DXd → THP is attractive where affordable. I would wait for EFS before abandoning anthracycline regimens universally.</p>
