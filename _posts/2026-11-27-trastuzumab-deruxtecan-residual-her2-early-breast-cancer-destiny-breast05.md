## Why this matters

Residual invasive HER2-positive breast cancer after neoadjuvant HER2-directed therapy signals a substantial risk of relapse. Post-neoadjuvant trastuzumab emtansine (T-DM1) became the standard through KATHERINE. At final follow-up, its overall survival (OS) hazard ratio (HR) versus trastuzumab was **0.66 (95% CI 0.51–0.87)**; **7-year OS was 89.1% versus 84.4%**. That survival evidence matters when weighing a new escalation. <a class="cite" href="https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2406070">[NEJM 2025]</a>

DESTINY-Breast05 is the first head-to-head antibody–drug conjugate escalation trial in this setting. Trastuzumab deruxtecan (T-DXd) improved invasive disease-free survival (IDFS), but brings a different lung toxicity burden. The FDA approved adjuvant use on **15 May 2026**; the CHMP issued a positive opinion on **17 September 2026**, and European Commission approval on **27 October 2026** was sponsor-reported. <a class="cite" href="https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2514661">[NEJM 2026]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://www.fda.gov/drugs/resources-information-approved-drugs/fda-approves-two-separate-indications-fam-trastuzumab-deruxtecan-nxki-her2-positive-early-stage">[FDA 2026]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/medicines/human/variation/enhertu-0">[EMA 2026]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://www.daiichisankyo.com/files/news/pressrelease/pdf/202610/20261027_E.pdf">[Sponsor-reported 2026]</a>

## Trial design

This international, open-label, randomised phase **3** trial enrolled **1,635** patients with centrally confirmed HER2-positive residual invasive disease after neoadjuvant treatment. High risk meant positive nodes at surgery (ypN1–3) or inoperable disease at diagnosis (cT4 N0–3 M0 or cT1–3 N2–3 M0). Prior ILD or pneumonitis requiring steroids, ILD at screening and ECOG performance status above **1** were exclusions. Thus, a residual tumour alone did not establish trial eligibility. <a class="cite" href="https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2514661">[NEJM 2026]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=7e67e73e-ddf4-4e4d-8b50-09d7514910b6">[DailyMed label]</a>

Patients were assigned T-DXd **5.4 mg/kg** (818) or T-DM1 **3.6 mg/kg** (817), every **3 weeks** for up to **14 cycles**. Stratification used operability at presentation, post-treatment nodal status, hormone receptor (HR) status and single versus dual HER2-targeted neoadjuvant therapy. IDFS was primary; disease-free survival (DFS) was a key secondary endpoint, with OS, distant recurrence-free interval (DRFI), brain metastasis-free interval (BMFI) and safety also assessed. <a class="cite" href="https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2514661">[NEJM 2026]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=7e67e73e-ddf4-4e4d-8b50-09d7514910b6">[DailyMed label]</a>

Median age was **51**; **48%** were Asian, **71%** HR-positive, **52%** inoperable at presentation and **81%** ypN-positive. Dual HER2-directed neoadjuvant treatment had been given to **79%**, and **50%** had received an anthracycline. Radiotherapy could be concurrent or sequential. All had screening chest CT; patients receiving radiotherapy had regular chest CT during and after treatment. This surveillance context matters when applying the safety findings. <a class="cite" href="https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=7e67e73e-ddf4-4e4d-8b50-09d7514910b6">[DailyMed label]</a>

## Results: IDFS and DFS

At approximately **30 months** of median follow-up, IDFS events occurred in **51 (6.2%) versus 102 (12.5%)**. The HR was **0.47 (95% CI 0.34–0.66)** and estimated **3-year IDFS was 92.4% versus 83.7%**. DFS showed a similar pattern. These are time-to-event estimates, not a guarantee for an individual patient; further follow-up will clarify their durability. At the IDFS analysis, **47 patients (2.9%)** had died across both groups. <a class="cite" href="https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2514661">[NEJM 2026]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://www.fda.gov/drugs/resources-information-approved-drugs/fda-approves-two-separate-indications-fam-trastuzumab-deruxtecan-nxki-her2-positive-early-stage">[FDA 2026]</a>

## Distant, CNS and survival endpoints

DRFI favoured T-DXd. Fewer central nervous system recurrences were observed (**17 versus 26**), but the BMFI comparison was **not statistically significant**; its confidence interval includes no difference. OS was only **2.9% mature**. **An OS benefit has not yet been shown**, despite the encouraging point estimate. The established OS benefit of T-DM1 in KATHERINE remains important context, although that comparison was against trastuzumab, not T-DXd. <a class="cite" href="https://datasourcebydaiichisankyo.com/documents/d/datahub/sabcs-2025_t-dxd_loibl_oral-final">[SABCS 2025]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2406070">[NEJM 2025]</a>

## Subgroups

Exploratory IDFS HRs pointed in the same direction for HR-positive (**0.54**) and HR-negative (**0.37**), initially inoperable (**0.41**) and operable (**0.58**), and single (**0.43**) and dual (**0.48**) HER2-targeted neoadjuvant therapy. For ypN-positive disease the HR was **0.43**; for ypN0 it was **0.73 (0.33–1.59)**. The ypN0 group was small and its wide interval leaves considerable uncertainty. Subgroup estimates are exploratory and do not establish that effects differ between groups. <a class="cite" href="https://datasourcebydaiichisankyo.com/documents/d/datahub/sabcs-2025_t-dxd_loibl_oral-final">[SABCS 2025]</a>

## Safety: the ILD question

Adjudicated drug-related interstitial lung disease (ILD) occurred in **9.6% versus 1.6%**, mostly grade **1–2**. There were **2** T-DXd-associated ILD deaths; the presentation reported that management guidance had not been appropriately followed in those cases. Among patients who had radiotherapy, median time to ILD onset with T-DXd was approximately **123.5 days**, roughly **4 months**. New cough, dyspnoea or fever warrants prompt assessment; asymptomatic imaging changes also matter. <a class="cite" href="https://www.annalsofoncology.org/article/S0923-7534(25)04791-X/fulltext">[Annals of Oncology 2025]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://datasourcebydaiichisankyo.com/documents/d/datahub/sabcs-2025_t-dxd_loibl_oral-final">[SABCS 2025]</a>

The reported ILD incidence and severity did not materially change with concurrent versus sequential radiotherapy. Investigator-reported radiation pneumonitis among radiotherapy recipients was **31.4% versus 30.5%**, all grade **2** or less. Distinguishing radiation pneumonitis from drug ILD is clinically important and can require multidisciplinary review; similar group rates do not settle the cause in an individual. <a class="cite" href="https://datasourcebydaiichisankyo.com/documents/d/datahub/sabcs-2025_t-dxd_loibl_oral-final">[SABCS 2025]</a>

Grade **3 or higher** treatment-emergent adverse events were **50.6% versus 51.9%**. Nausea occurred in **71.3%** with T-DXd; the label calls for antiemetic premedication. T-DM1 more often featured liver enzyme elevations and decreased platelets. Completion of all **14 cycles** was **reported** as **72.3% versus 76.3%**, and discontinuation for treatment-emergent adverse events as **17.9% versus 12.7%**. Equal-looking overall severe event rates conceal different monitoring and supportive-care needs. <a class="cite" href="https://www.annalsofoncology.org/article/S0923-7534(25)04791-X/fulltext">[Annals of Oncology 2025]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2514661">[NEJM 2026]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=7e67e73e-ddf4-4e4d-8b50-09d7514910b6">[DailyMed label]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://www.onclive.com/view/t-dxd-gains-eu-approval-in-residual-her2-early-breast-cancer">[OncLive reported]</a>

## Label ILD rules in plain language

For suspected drug-related ILD, interrupt treatment and investigate, including assessment for radiation pneumonitis in the adjuvant setting. For asymptomatic grade **1** drug ILD, interrupt until grade **0**; if resolved within **28 days**, maintain the dose, and if later, reduce by one dose level. Consider steroids. For symptomatic grade **2 or higher** drug ILD, permanently discontinue and promptly start steroids. If only radiation pneumonitis is suspected, consider interruption at grade **2** and permanently discontinue at grade **3 or higher**. Consult the full label for evaluation, steroid treatment and other dose modifications. <a class="cite" href="https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=7e67e73e-ddf4-4e4d-8b50-09d7514910b6">[DailyMed label]</a>

## Indication vs trial population

The FDA indication covers HER2-positive residual invasive disease after neoadjuvant trastuzumab, with or without pertuzumab, and taxane-based treatment. The EU indication covers residual invasive disease after neoadjuvant taxane-based and HER2-targeted treatment. Neither indication is restricted to the trial's high-risk definition. Patients who were operable at diagnosis and ypN0 after treatment were not studied under that definition; the trial's ypN0 subgroup consisted of eligible patients who were inoperable at presentation. Discuss the uncertainty when considering treatment for lower-risk residual disease. <a class="cite" href="https://www.fda.gov/drugs/resources-information-approved-drugs/fda-approves-two-separate-indications-fam-trastuzumab-deruxtecan-nxki-her2-positive-early-stage">[FDA 2026]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/medicines/human/variation/enhertu-0">[EMA]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2514661">[NEJM 2026]</a>

## What changes in practice

For a fit patient matching the high-risk trial population, without a history of steroid-treated ILD, discuss T-DXd and its reduction in recurrence events alongside the need for lung surveillance and rapid access to assessment. Review baseline respiratory symptoms, imaging and capacity to deliver supportive care. A history of lung disease or higher ILD concern can make T-DM1 reasonable; so can lower-risk residual disease outside the studied population or limited access. The choice should reflect the patient's priorities and local label.

[DESTINY-Breast11]({{ '/destiny-breast-11-trial/' | relative_url }}) addresses a neoadjuvant T-DXd-containing approach; Breast05 does not establish the best post-neoadjuvant sequence after prior T-DXd. For HR-positive disease, endocrine therapy continues according to local guidelines. T-DXd, trastuzumab and T-DM1 are distinct products and must not be substituted for one another. <a class="cite" href="https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=7e67e73e-ddf4-4e4d-8b50-09d7514910b6">[DailyMed label]</a>

## India and resource-limited settings

Zydus launched the T-DM1 biosimilar Ujvira in **2021** and stated that its price was approximately **80%** lower than the innovator. Intas biosimilar approval was reported in **July 2026**. An Indian retrospective series included **101** patients across **7** centres, with only **19** in the adjuvant cohort: useful local experience, but too small and non-randomised to establish comparative efficacy. <a class="cite" href="https://www.zyduslife.com/public/pdf/pressrelease/Zydus-launches-Ujvira-Trastuzumab-Emtansine-a-breakthrough-in-Breast-Cancer-treatment-24-05-21.pdf">[Company announcement 2021]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://pharma.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/drug-approvals-and-launches/intas-gets-cdsco-nod-for-breast-cancer-drug-trastuzumab-emtansine/132551850">[Approval reported 2026]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1007/s10549-026-08114-0">[Breast Cancer Research and Treatment 2026]</a>

We could not verify CDSCO approval of T-DXd for the adjuvant early-breast indication; check current CDSCO status and the local label. Cost, the planned **14 cycles**, access to chest CT for ILD surveillance and pulmonology support all affect feasibility. Where T-DXd is unaffordable, completing **14 cycles** of T-DM1, including an available biosimilar, remains an evidence-based, survival-proven standard. The KATHERINE survival evidence is for T-DM1; local biosimilar use should follow applicable regulatory guidance. <a class="cite" href="https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2406070">[NEJM 2025]</a>

## Open questions

- Will longer follow-up show an OS benefit, and how durable is the IDFS difference?
- What is the balance of benefit and harm for operable, ypN0 residual disease outside trial eligibility?
- How should residual disease be treated after neoadjuvant T-DXd in the Breast11 approach?
- How will ILD detection and outcomes translate to routine care, and is the strategy cost-effective in low- and middle-income settings?

## Bottom line

T-DXd improved IDFS over T-DM1 in high-risk residual HER2-positive early breast cancer. OS remains immature, and the higher ILD risk demands a practical monitoring and response plan. Treatment choice also depends on trial eligibility, lung health and access. <a class="cite" href="https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2514661">[NEJM 2026]</a>
