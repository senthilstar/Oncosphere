The European Society for Medical Oncology (ESMO) Congress took place in **Madrid from 23 to 27 October 2026**. <a class="cite" href="https://dam.esmo.org/image/upload/v1791201382/ESMO-Congress-2026-Programme_cws0yw.pdf">[ESMO programme 2026]</a>

## Key sessions
- **Presidential Symposium I** — Saturday 24 October, 16:30–18:15 CEST (20:00–21:45 IST), Alicante Auditorium, Hall 6; presentations included melanoma, bladder cancer, small-cell lung cancer and high-risk prostate cancer. <a class="cite" href="https://dam.esmo.org/image/upload/v1791201382/ESMO-Congress-2026-Programme_cws0yw.pdf">[ESMO programme 2026]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://oncologypro.esmo.org/congress-resources/esmo-congress-2026">[ESMO abstracts 2026]</a>
- **Presidential Symposium II** — Sunday 25 October, 16:30–18:15 CET (21:00–22:45 IST), in the same auditorium; presentations covered non-small-cell lung, gastric and biliary-tract cancers. <a class="cite" href="https://dam.esmo.org/image/upload/v1791201382/ESMO-Congress-2026-Programme_cws0yw.pdf">[ESMO programme 2026]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://oncologypro.esmo.org/congress-resources/esmo-congress-2026">[ESMO abstracts 2026]</a>
- **Presidential Symposium III** — Monday 26 October, 16:30–18:15 CET (21:00–22:45 IST), in the same auditorium. <a class="cite" href="https://dam.esmo.org/image/upload/v1791201382/ESMO-Congress-2026-Programme_cws0yw.pdf">[ESMO programme 2026]</a>

Madrid changed from summer to standard time between the first and second sessions; the India conversions above account for that change. The programme also included artificial intelligence, toxicity of novel therapies and patient advocacy on access and equity, all relevant to oncology practice in India. <a class="cite" href="https://dam.esmo.org/image/upload/v1791201382/ESMO-Congress-2026-Programme_cws0yw.pdf">[ESMO programme 2026]</a>

## How to follow ESMO from India
- ESMO's OncologyPRO congress resource page provides open-access abstracts; presentation slides and recordings are available to ESMO members and congress delegates, subject to permission. <a class="cite" href="https://oncologypro.esmo.org/congress-resources/esmo-congress-2026">[ESMO resources 2026]</a>
- Read the full paper when available: an abstract or presentation may not contain the complete methods, safety data or follow-up needed for a treatment decision.

## How to judge a "practice-changing" headline
1. **Identify the endpoint.** Overall survival, progression-free survival, pathological complete response and response rate answer different questions; a surrogate benefit does not automatically establish longer survival.
2. **Check the comparator.** Is it an appropriate current standard for the patient and setting in your practice?
3. **Weigh absolute benefit against harm and cost.** Consider the duration of follow-up and the ESMO Magnitude of Clinical Benefit Scale where applicable. <a class="cite" href="https://www.esmo.org/guidelines/esmo-mcbs">[ESMO-MCBS]</a>
4. **Check the evidence's maturity.** Distinguish a peer-reviewed report from a congress presentation and an interim result from a final analysis.
5. **Consider delivery.** Biomarker testing, drug availability, supportive care and affordability may determine whether a result can be applied in India.

## What to read after the meeting
The linked summaries cover trial populations, efficacy and toxicity, with context for clinical decisions and access in India. Check the primary reports and current local regulatory status before changing treatment.
