## October 2026 oncology updates

### ATOMIC: ADJUVANT IO IN COLON CANCER

🟢 FDA approval: 8 October 2026.
Resected stage III dMMR colon cancer:
Atezolizumab + fluoropyrimidine/oxaliplatin.
📊 ATOMIC, n=712:

- 3-year DFS: 86.3% vs 76.2%
- HR 0.50; 95% CI 0.35–0.73
- Absolute DFS difference: 10.1 percentage points
Trial schedule:
6 months atezolizumab + mFOLFOX6 → 6 months atezolizumab alone.
⚠️ Grade 3–4 adverse events: 84.1% vs 71.9%.
🔎 Clinical implication: establish MMR/MSI status before the adjuvant discussion. This indication concerns stage III dMMR disease; extrapolation to pMMR or stage II disease requires separate evidence.

### HER2CLIMB-05: MAINTENANCE INTENSIFIES

🟢 FDA approval: 7 October 2026.
Tucatinib + trastuzumab + pertuzumab after induction for unresectable locally advanced/metastatic HER2+ breast cancer.
📊 Randomised trial, n=654:

- Median PFS: 24.9 vs 16.3 months
- HR 0.64; 95% CI 0.51–0.80
- OS immature
Eligibility matters:
No progression after 4–8 cycles of taxane + trastuzumab + pertuzumab; patients with or without brain metastases were eligible.
💊 Tucatinib: 300 mg twice daily.
Endocrine therapy was permitted for HR+ disease.
⚠️ Hepatotoxicity, diarrhoea and creatinine elevation require attention.
This trial tested maintenance after THP induction. It does not answer every contemporary first-line sequencing question.

### ESR1: MOLECULAR PROGRESSION BEFORE SCAN PROGRESSION

🟢 Earlier development: FDA accelerated approval of camizestrant + a CDK4/6 inhibitor on 4 September 2026.
SERENA-6:
ER+/HER2− advanced breast cancer, receiving first-line AI + CDK4/6 inhibition for ≥6 months, with emerging ctDNA ESR1 mutation and no radiological progression.
📊 n=315:

- Median PFS: 16.0 vs 9.2 months
- HR 0.44
- OS immature
Strategy:
Switch the AI to camizestrant while continuing the CDK4/6 inhibitor.
⚠️ QTc interactions and bradycardia matter.
The European Guardant360 CDx announcement concerns diagnostic access. The clinical question remains precise: when should molecular resistance trigger an endocrine switch?

### NRG-BR002: ABLATION DOES NOT AUTOMATICALLY MEAN BETTER SURVIVAL

🟡 Newly published randomised evidence in oligometastatic breast cancer.
125 eligible patients:
Systemic therapy ± ablation of all metastases, predominantly SBRT.
📊 Median PFS:

- No ablation: 23.0 months
- Ablation: 19.5 months
- HR 0.92; 95% CI 0.57–1.47
No significant OS improvement.
The phase III component did not proceed.
🔎 Routine metastasis-directed ablation to improve survival is unsupported by this trial.
Palliation remains a separate indication. Oligoprogression also requires its own evidence; it should not be conflated with this study population.

### PSMADDITION: RADIOLIGANDS MOVE EARLIER

🟢 Earlier approval: 31 July 2026.
177Lu-PSMA-617 + ARPI for PSMA-positive metastatic androgen pathway modulation-naïve/sensitive prostate cancer, historically termed mHSPC.
📊 PSMAddition:

- rPFS HR 0.72; 95% CI 0.58–0.90
- Median rPFS not reached in either arm
- OS immature
PSMA PET selection is required.
ADT remains foundational.
⚠️ Myelosuppression, renal toxicity, radiation precautions and treatment logistics matter.
🇮🇳 Practical appraisal must include PSMA PET access, nuclear-medicine capacity, cost and alternatives—not only the relative hazard reduction.
This is an earlier approval being discussed now, not a new October approval.

### RAMPP: CYTOREDUCTIVE PROSTATECTOMY

🟡 Randomised study, n=132:
Best systemic therapy ± radical prostatectomy in prostate cancer with 1–5 bone metastases, with/without nodal involvement.
📊 Five-year outcomes:

- Cancer-specific mortality: 13% vs 23%
- CSM HR 0.39; 95% CI 0.16–0.98
- OS: 81% vs 74%; difference not statistically significant
⚠️ Accrual stopped early.
Grade ≥III surgical complications: 14%.
The cancer-specific mortality signal deserves further study. It does not establish routine cytoreductive prostatectomy for contemporary metastatic practice.
Separate the evidence for surgery from the evidence for prostate radiotherapy.

### NANT: CHEMOTHERAPY-FREE NEOADJUVANT OVARIAN THERAPY?

🔵 Phase II, single-arm NANT:
Eight weeks of niraparib in initially unresectable HRD+ high-grade serous ovarian cancer.

- Treated: 67
- Response-evaluable: 48
- ORR: 30/48 = 62.5%
- Operated: 40
- R0 resection: 32/40 = 80%
⚠️ These are different denominators.
Grade ≥3 treatment-related toxicity was substantial.
Crucial counterpoint:
Randomised OPAL cohort C, n=36, reported ORR 31.6% with niraparib vs 70.6% with platinum–taxane and closed for futility.
NANT generates a research hypothesis. It does not justify replacing standard platinum–taxane neoadjuvant chemotherapy.

### FRα ADCS: THE OVARIAN PIPELINE EXPANDS

🔵 Sofetabart mipitecan, LY4170156:
An investigational FRα-targeting antibody–drug conjugate.
Its first-in-human programme evaluates safety, dose optimisation and preliminary efficacy.
The programme has also advanced to phase III FRAmework-01, including comparisons with chemotherapy or mirvetuximab in platinum-resistant disease.
Questions for the eventual dataset:

- Activity after prior mirvetuximab?
- Dependence on FRα expression?
- Payload-related toxicity?
- Durable responses across resistant subgroups?
Breakthrough Therapy designation supports development; it does not establish marketing approval.
Avoid importing mirvetuximab’s eligibility criteria or toxicity profile into a different ADC.

### EXCALIBER-RRMM: BIG PFS SIGNAL, REAL TOXICITY

🟡 Phase III topline update, 8 October:
Iberdomide + daratumumab + dexamethasone vs DVd.
📊 Confirmatory cohort, n=800:

- Median PFS: 42 vs 20 months
- HR 0.49; reported p<0.000001
Published primary MRD analysis:

- MRD-negative CR: 41% vs 21%
- Grade 3–4 neutropenia: 84% vs 11%
- Grade 3–4 infection: 39% vs 22%
🔎 Eligibility matters:
1–2 prior lines; anti-CD38-refractory and bortezomib-refractory disease were excluded.
A compelling efficacy signal with substantial supportive-care requirements. Do not extrapolate directly to daratumumab-refractory myeloma.

### FRAIL MYELOMA & THE “CURE” QUESTION

🟡 Updated MAIA frailty analysis:
D-Rd vs Rd in transplant-ineligible NDMM.
Frail subgroup:

- Median PFS: 52.2 vs 30.4 months
- HR 0.64
- OS HR 0.79; 95% CI 0.58–1.06
PFS benefit persists. The frail-subgroup OS difference was not statistically significant; frailty was assessed retrospectively.
🔵 Earlier CARTITUDE-1 follow-up:
32/97 patients remained progression-free for ≥5 years after cilta-cel, without maintenance or subsequent myeloma therapy.
Serial MRD and imaging confirmation was reported in a 12-patient subset.
Remarkable treatment-free remission deserves recognition. “Cure” requires more mature evidence and should not be inferred from selected long-term survivors.

### BISPECIFICS MOVE EARLIER

🟡 CELESTIMO, phase III:
Mosunetuzumab + lenalidomide vs rituximab + lenalidomide in R/R follicular lymphoma after ≥1 prior line.
The September sponsor announcement reported a positive PFS endpoint. Numerical effect size and detailed safety are needed before ranking its clinical value.
🔵 First-line DLBCL phase II, n=41:
Glofitamab + pola-R-CHP.

- Best CR: 95%
- 24-month PFS: 95%
- CRS: 4 patients, all grade 1
- No reported ICANS
Glofitamab started in cycle 3 after initial chemotherapy.
Promising schedule and activity—but a small single-arm cohort cannot establish superiority over standard first-line therapy.

### ctDNA MOVES FROM PROGNOSIS TO TREATMENT SELECTION

🟢 Earlier development: 15 May 2026.
FDA approval of adjuvant atezolizumab after cystectomy for MIBC with ctDNA molecular residual disease, identified by an FDA-authorised test.
📊 IMvigor011, ctDNA-positive randomised population, n=250:

- Median DFS: 9.9 vs 4.8 months
- DFS HR 0.64
- Median OS: 32.8 vs 21.1 months
- OS HR 0.59
Signatera CDx was approved for patient selection.
🔎 The evidence concerns serially detected postoperative ctDNA-positive disease.
It should not be extended automatically to ctDNA-negative patients or to every commercially available MRD assay.
Biomarker selection can define who receives treatment—not merely who has higher recurrence risk.

### MULTICANCER SCREENING: A MAJOR REALITY CHECK

🟡 NHS-Galleri full results are available—not merely a Quick Take.
Randomised population: 142,250.
After three screening rounds:

- Primary endpoint, stage III/IV incidence across 12 prespecified cancers:
IRR 1.03; 95% CI 0.92–1.14; p=0.63

- Stage IV secondary endpoint:
IRR 0.86; 95% CI 0.74–1.00
The primary endpoint was not met.
A possible stage IV signal requires follow-up; a mortality benefit has not been established by these findings.
🔵 Natera’s 9 October MCED announcement:
FDA Breakthrough Device designation.
Designation ≠ approval.
Test performance ≠ demonstrated screening benefit.

## HER2 breast cancer — 2026

### 🚀 DESTINY-BREAST11 — ADCS ENTER NEOADJUVANT THERAPY

May 2026: a new preoperative option.
📊 DESTINY-Breast11
High-risk early HER2+ breast cancer.
Phase III | n=927.
Key comparison:
🔹 T-DXd ×4 cycles → THP ×4 cycles.
🔹 Dose-dense AC ×4 → THP ×4.
Pathologic complete response:
🔥 67.3% vs 56.3%.
Absolute improvement: 11 percentage points.
p=0.003.
T-DXd dose: 5.4 mg/kg IV every 3 weeks ×4 before THP.
📅 15 May 2026:
FDA approved T-DXd → THP for neoadjuvant stage II–III HER2+ disease.
What is NOT proven?
⚠️ Mature EFS/OS superiority is not yet established.
⚠️ Comparison was against ddAC→THP, not directly against standard TCHP.
⚠️ Greater pCR does not automatically mean greater cure probability for every individual.
⚠️ T-DXd introduces ILD/pneumonitis risk.
🎯 Practical implication:
Discuss T-DXd→THP as an approved neoadjuvant option in eligible stage II–III disease where available.
Do not claim TCHP is obsolete.
A new regimen has arrived. The optimal patient remains a clinical question.

### ⚔️ NON-pCR: T-DM1 OR T-DXd?

Residual invasive HER2+ cancer after neoadjuvant treatment is a therapeutic decision point.
Not all residual disease is equal.
📊 KATHERINE
n=1,486 | Residual invasive HER2+ disease.
T-DM1 3.6 mg/kg every 3 weeks ×14 vs trastuzumab.
At 8.4-year median follow-up:

- 7-year IDFS: 80.8% vs 67.1%.
- 7-year OS: 89.1% vs 84.4%.
- OS HR 0.66.
T-DM1 established post-neoadjuvant escalation with proven survival benefit.
THEN CAME:
📊 DESTINY-Breast05
n=1,635 | High-risk residual invasive HER2+ disease.
T-DXd 5.4 mg/kg vs T-DM1 3.6 mg/kg, every 3 weeks, up to 14 cycles.
🔥 3-year IDFS: 92.4% vs 83.7%.
🔥 HR 0.47; p<0.0001.
📅 15 May 2026: FDA approved adjuvant T-DXd for residual invasive HER2+ disease after neoadjuvant trastuzumab ± pertuzumab and taxane-based therapy.
How to choose?
✅ High-risk residual disease, adequate pulmonary reserve, access to T-DXd → strongly consider T-DXd.
✅ Limited residual burden, substantial ILD risk or affordability concerns → T-DM1 remains a robust option.
⚠️ DESTINY-Breast05 enrolled a high-risk population. FDA indication is broader than the precise trial population.
⚠️ T-DXd OS data remain immature.
🎯 Non-pCR now has two evidence-based ADC options.
The decision is efficacy versus toxicity, not simply newest versus oldest.

### 🌍 FIRST-LINE HER2+ METASTATIC BREAST CANCER — 2026

For over a decade, THP dominated first line.
Now there are TWO major evidence-based approaches.
📊 CLEOPATRA
Docetaxel + trastuzumab + pertuzumab (THP).
Median OS: 57.1 vs 40.8 months.
HR 0.69 against docetaxel–trastuzumab.
💊 Docetaxel usually ≥6 cycles if tolerated, followed by HP maintenance until progression.
📊 DESTINY-Breast09
T-DXd + pertuzumab vs THP.
n=1,157 across 3 randomised arms.
🔥 Median PFS: 40.7 vs 26.9 months.
🔥 HR 0.56; p<0.0001.
🔥 ORR: 87% vs 81%.
OS immature at primary PFS analysis.
📅 FDA first-line approval: 15 December 2025.
📘 ESMO 2026:
Both THP and T-DXd + pertuzumab are recommended [I,A].
CHOOSING 1L
🔹 T-DXd + P:
Major PFS benefit; consider when access and pulmonary safety permit.
🔹 THP:
Mature OS data, established maintenance pathways and an ADC-free first-line approach.
⚠️ T-DXd requires vigilance for ILD. It is not a short, fixed six-cycle induction like docetaxel.
⚠️ There is no head-to-head proof that 1L T-DXd→later HER2 therapy improves OS versus THP→2L T-DXd.
🎯 ESMO recommendation ≠ a mandate to abandon THP.
Choose the full therapeutic sequence, not only the longest first-line PFS.

### 💎 HER2+ MAINTENANCE — PATINA VS HER2CLIMB-05

After 4–8 cycles of taxane + HP, no progression.
What next?
It is no longer simply indefinite trastuzumab–pertuzumab.
📊 PATINA
HR+/HER2+ metastatic breast cancer after induction therapy.
HP + endocrine therapy ± palbociclib.
🔥 Median PFS: 44.3 vs 29.1 months.
🔥 HR 0.75; p=0.02.
💊 Palbociclib 125 mg daily, days 1–21 every 28 days, with appropriate dose modifications.
⚠️ Grade 3/4 neutropenia is common.
📊 HER2CLIMB-05
n=654 | HER2+ MBC, any HR status.
After 4–8 cycles of taxane + HP, without progression:
HP + tucatinib vs HP + placebo.
🔥 Median maintenance-phase PFS:
24.9 vs 16.3 months.
🔥 HR 0.64; p<0.0001.
💊 Tucatinib 300 mg twice daily continuously.
📅 7 October 2026:
FDA approved tucatinib + HP maintenance after induction treatment.
PRACTICAL SELECTION
✅ HR+/HER2+:
HP + endocrine therapy remains a foundation. Palbociclib-based or tucatinib-based escalation can be discussed according to evidence, availability and toxicity.
✅ HR−/HER2+:
HP ± tucatinib is relevant; PATINA does not apply.
⚠️ NEVER directly compare the 44.3-month PATINA PFS against 24.9-month HER2CLIMB-05 PFS.
Different populations. Different designs. Different risk profiles.
🎯 The next frontier is personalised maintenance, not necessarily indefinite chemotherapy.

### ⚔️ SECOND-LINE HER2+ MBC — WHAT AFTER FIRST-LINE?

Sequencing must now be based on prior ADC exposure.
SCENARIO A:
Progression after first-line THP.
📊 DESTINY-Breast03
T-DXd vs T-DM1.

- Median PFS: 29.0 vs 7.2 months.
- HR 0.30.
- Median OS: 52.6 vs 42.7 months.
- OS HR 0.73.
- ORR: 78.9% vs 36.9%.
🎯 T-DXd 5.4 mg/kg q3w is preferred when available and appropriate.
SCENARIO B:
Progression after first-line T-DXd + pertuzumab.
⚠️ No adequately validated phase III standard sequence specifically after first-line T-DXd.
Options according to prior exposure, CNS disease and availability:
🔹 Tucatinib + trastuzumab + capecitabine.
🔹 T-DM1 if not previously received.
🔹 Trastuzumab with an alternative chemotherapy partner.
🔹 Neratinib–capecitabine or lapatinib–capecitabine in selected circumstances.
🔹 Clinical trial whenever available.
SCENARIO C:
Early metastatic relapse after adjuvant T-DM1/T-DXd.
Do NOT mechanically apply a trastuzumab-naive metastatic sequence.
Reassess treatment-free interval, cardiac/pulmonary reserve, prior toxicities and CNS involvement.
🎯 The 2026 problem:
We have excellent drugs.
But the optimal sequence after early ADC exposure remains incompletely defined.
Drug availability is not the same as sequencing evidence.

### 🧠 HER2+ BRAIN METASTASES — A DIFFERENT SEQUENCING PROBLEM

HER2+ breast cancer has a substantial propensity for CNS relapse.
But every brain metastasis does NOT require a new systemic regimen.
SCENARIO 1:
Isolated intracranial progression, extracranial disease controlled.
✅ SRS or surgery where appropriate.
✅ Continue effective systemic HER2 therapy if extracranial control persists.
SCENARIO 2:
Multiple/progressive brain lesions requiring systemic CNS-active treatment.
📊 HER2CLIMB
Tucatinib + trastuzumab + capecitabine.
Among patients with brain metastases:
🔥 1-year CNS-PFS: 40.2% vs 0%.
🔥 Intracranial ORR in measurable active disease: 47.3% vs 20.0%.
🔥 Median OS: 18.1 vs 12.0 months in the brain-metastasis subgroup analysis.
💊 Tucatinib 300 mg twice daily.
💊 Capecitabine 1,000 mg/m2 twice daily, days 1–14 q21d.
💊 Trastuzumab q3w.
📊 DESTINY-Breast12
T-DXd demonstrated substantial intracranial activity in HER2+ MBC with baseline brain metastases.
ESMO 2026:

- T-DXd is an important preferred systemic option after prior taxane–trastuzumab.
- Tucatinib triplet is particularly relevant following T-DXd progression.
⚠️ Symptomatic mass effect or neurological deterioration requires urgent CNS assessment and local intervention when indicated.
⚠️ Concurrent SRS and ADC administration requires caution; treatment timing should be coordinated.
🎯 Think in two compartments:
The brain may progress while the body remains controlled.
Treat the compartment that is failing.

### 🇮🇳 HER2 BREAST CANCER — 2026 CLINICAL RULEBOOK

EARLY DISEASE
✅ pT1aN0: individualise chemotherapy–trastuzumab; omission reasonable in selected low-risk patients.
✅ pT1b/cN0: consider/adopt weekly paclitaxel–trastuzumab according to risk.
✅ cT2 or N+: prefer neoadjuvant HER2-directed combination therapy.
✅ pCR: complete planned anti-HER2 treatment.
✅ Residual invasive disease: T-DM1 or T-DXd according to evidence, risk, approval and access.
METASTATIC DISEASE
✅ 1L: THP or T-DXd + pertuzumab.
✅ Following taxane–trastuzumab: T-DXd preferred if not previously exposed.
✅ Brain metastases: consider T-DXd and/or tucatinib triplet according to line, CNS status and prior drugs.
✅ HR+/HER2-low/ultralow after endocrine progression: assess T-DXd eligibility.
SAFETY RULES
🫀 Check LVEF before and during relevant HER2 therapies; interrupt or modify per agent-specific criteria.
🫁 T-DXd:

- Suspected ILD → interrupt, investigate promptly.
- Confirmed grade 1 → hold until resolution, adjust as indicated.
- Grade ≥2 → permanently discontinue and treat appropriately.
🩸 T-DM1: monitor platelets and transaminases.
💊 Tucatinib: monitor liver enzymes, diarrhoea and drug interactions.
💊 Neratinib: prevent/manage diarrhoea aggressively.
INDIA / LMIC PRIORITIES
🔹 Do not compromise curative therapy without exploring quality-assured biosimilars.
🔹 Prioritise effective, affordable adjuvant trastuzumab for eligible patients.
🔹 Avoid escalating tiny low-risk tumours simply because new drugs exist.
🔹 Reassess HER2 on metastatic tissue when feasible.
🔹 Distinguish trial efficacy from local approval, affordability and realistic treatment completion.
🎯 FINAL TAKEAWAY
Test precisely.
Stage correctly.
Select by risk.
Sequence by prior exposure.
HER2 care is no longer one drug for one receptor.
It is a treatment pathway spanning diagnosis, cure, recurrence and resistance.
