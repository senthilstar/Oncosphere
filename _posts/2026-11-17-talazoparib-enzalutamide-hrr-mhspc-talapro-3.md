## Why this matters

TALAPRO-3 tests whether adding a PARP inhibitor to enzalutamide can delay progression in metastatic prostate cancer that remains sensitive to androgen pathway treatment and carries an HRR alteration. The FDA approved talazoparib plus enzalutamide on **26 Oct 2026**. <a class="cite" href="https://www.fda.gov/drugs/cancer-approvals/fda-approves-talazoparib-enzalutamide-metastatic-androgen-pathway-modulation-naive-or-sensitive">[FDA 2026]</a> The regulatory term, metastatic androgen pathway modulation-naive or -sensitive disease, maps to the familiar mHSPC/mCSPC setting. ADT remains the backbone. <a class="cite" href="https://www.fda.gov/drugs/cancer-approvals/fda-approves-talazoparib-enzalutamide-metastatic-androgen-pathway-modulation-naive-or-sensitive">[FDA]</a>

The advance is a substantial delay in radiographic progression or death against an active enzalutamide control. It is also a commitment to additional marrow toxicity and supportive care. **An overall-survival benefit has not yet been established in this setting.** Testing, patient preference and the ability to manage anaemia should shape the decision together. <a class="cite" href="https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2604126">[NEJM 2026]</a>

## The biology in brief

PARP participates in the response to DNA damage. Talazoparib blocks PARP and traps it on DNA, creating a particular vulnerability when homologous recombination repair is impaired. Enzalutamide blocks androgen-receptor signalling. Combining these approaches targets androgen dependence alongside a DNA-repair vulnerability, rather than waiting for castration resistance.

HRR alterations are biologically diverse. A BRCA alteration and a mutation in another repair-associated gene need not confer the same sensitivity. A panel-positive result establishes eligibility only when its interpretation fits the authorised test and indication; it does not promise identical benefit across genes. The exploratory subgroup results should inform that conversation, without being treated as definitive gene-specific trials. <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/217439s004lbl.pdf">[US label]</a>

## TALAPRO-3 design and population

The randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial enrolled **599 patients**, allocated **1:1** to talazoparib plus enzalutamide or placebo plus enzalutamide, with continuous ADT. <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/217439s004lbl.pdf">[FDA label 2026]</a> The assigned groups contained **300 versus 299** patients.  <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/217439s004lbl.pdf">[FDA label 2026]</a> Eligibility required HRR-mutated metastatic hormone-sensitive disease and **ECOG 0–1**.  <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/217439s004lbl.pdf">[FDA label 2026]</a> ADT could have started up to **3 months** before randomisation, with or without an approved androgen receptor pathway inhibitor; prior docetaxel was excluded. <a class="cite" href="https://www.fda.gov/drugs/cancer-approvals/fda-approves-talazoparib-enzalutamide-metastatic-androgen-pathway-modulation-naive-or-sensitive">[FDA]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/217439s004lbl.pdf">[US label]</a>

Stratification accounted for de novo versus relapsed metastases, high versus low disease volume, and BRCA versus non-BRCA mutations. The primary endpoint was investigator-assessed rPFS using **RECIST 1.1** and **PCWG3** bone criteria; OS was an alpha-protected key secondary endpoint. <a class="cite" href="https://ascopubs.org/doi/abs/10.1200/JCO.2026.44.17_suppl.LBA5007">[JCO 2026]</a> Median follow-up was **37.6 / 37.7 months**, with a data cutoff of **18 Feb 2026**. <a class="cite" href="https://ascopubs.org/doi/abs/10.1200/JCO.2026.44.17_suppl.LBA5007">[ASCO 2026]</a>

At baseline, **73%** had de novo metastases, **66%** had high-volume disease and **51%** had both.  <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/217439s004lbl.pdf">[FDA label 2026]</a> Median age was **70 years**; **39%** were Asian.  <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/217439s004lbl.pdf">[FDA label 2026]</a> Prior ARPI exposure in the metastatic hormone-sensitive setting was recorded in **13%**; the whole cohort was therefore not ARPI-naive. Prior exposure still requires careful review against the actual eligibility and label. <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/217439s004lbl.pdf">[US label]</a>

## Efficacy: progression benefit, survival still pending

All comparisons below give combination before control. Median rPFS was **not reached versus 45.8 months**, HR **0.48 (95% CI 0.36–0.65)**, **p<0.0001**.  <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/217439s004lbl.pdf">[FDA label 2026]</a> At **3 years**, estimated rPFS was **77% versus 56%**. <a class="cite" href="https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2604126">[NEJM 2026]</a> A median not reached is not a predicted duration of control; follow-up has not yet located that median. <a class="cite" href="https://www.fda.gov/drugs/cancer-approvals/fda-approves-talazoparib-enzalutamide-metastatic-androgen-pathway-modulation-naive-or-sensitive">[FDA]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2604126">[NEJM 2026]</a>

## Toxicity: anaemia and marrow reserve

Marrow toxicity, particularly anaemia, is a major practical constraint. Grade **≥3 clinical anaemia was 51% versus 3%**; **5%** stopped talazoparib for anaemia.  <a class="cite" href="https://www.pfizer.com/news/press-release/press-release-detail/pfizers-talzenna-plus-xtandi-fda-approved-earlier">[Sponsor 2026]</a> Separately, the label's laboratory table reports haemoglobin decrease in **88% versus 46%**, with grade **3** decrease in **49% versus 3.4%**.  <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/217439s004lbl.pdf">[FDA label 2026]</a> Clinical adverse events and laboratory abnormalities are different reporting categories; these figures should not be interchanged. <a class="cite" href="https://www.pfizer.com/news/press-release/press-release-detail/pfizers-talzenna-plus-xtandi-fda-approved-earlier">[Pfizer]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/217439s004lbl.pdf">[US label]</a>

In the label safety population, talazoparib interruption occurred in **69%**, reduction in **60%**, and permanent discontinuation in **19%**.  <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/217439s004lbl.pdf">[FDA label 2026]</a> Neutrophil and platelet decreases also matter. The NEJM report recorded serious adverse events in **42% versus 32%**, and **two treatment-related deaths** in the combination group. <a class="cite" href="https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2604126">[NEJM 2026]</a> Oral administration does not remove the need for close monitoring. <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/217439s004lbl.pdf">[US label]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2604126">[NEJM 2026]</a>

Check CBC and renal function before treatment, assess existing cytopenias and reversible causes of anaemia, and monitor CBC monthly during treatment, with closer assessment when counts fall or symptoms develop.  <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/217439s004lbl.pdf">[FDA label 2026]</a> Arrange a clear route for urgent review and transfusion before starting. For clinically important cytopenias, interrupt treatment, investigate and support recovery; resume with label-directed dose modification when appropriate. Avoid simply treating fatigue as an expected nuisance.

For haemoglobin **<8 g/dL**, hold talazoparib until **≥9 g/dL**, then resume at a reduced dose. For platelets **<50,000/µL**, hold until **≥75,000/µL**; for neutrophils **<1,000/µL**, hold until **≥1,500/µL**; resume at a reduced dose after recovery. <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/217439s004lbl.pdf">[FDA label 2026]</a>

Talazoparib is **0.5 mg orally once daily**, with enzalutamide **160 mg once daily** and ongoing ADT, until progression or unacceptable toxicity.  <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/217439s004lbl.pdf">[FDA label 2026]</a> In moderate renal impairment, **CLcr 30–59**, the talazoparib dose is **0.35 mg daily** (CLcr in mL/min).  <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/217439s004lbl.pdf">[FDA label 2026]</a> For severe renal impairment, **CLcr 15–29 mL/min**, talazoparib is **0.25 mg once daily**. <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/217439s004lbl.pdf">[FDA label 2026]</a> Renal impairment and treatment toxicity each require their own dose assessment. The label warns about **MDS/AML**, myelosuppression and embryo-fetal toxicity. Persistent or unexplained cytopenias warrant treatment review and haematology assessment rather than repeated empirical transfusions alone. <a class="cite" href="https://www.fda.gov/drugs/cancer-approvals/fda-approves-talazoparib-enzalutamide-metastatic-androgen-pathway-modulation-naive-or-sensitive">[FDA]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/217439s004lbl.pdf">[US label]</a>

## How it compares: TALAPRO-2 and AMPLITUDE

TALAPRO-2 tested the same drug pair in **mCRPC**, a different disease state. In its **399-patient HRRm cohort**, BICR rPFS was NR versus **13.8 months**, HR **0.45 (0.33–0.61)**.  <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/217439s004lbl.pdf">[FDA label 2026]</a> OS was **45.1 versus 31.1 months**, HR **0.62 (0.48–0.81)**, **p=0.0005**.  <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/217439s004lbl.pdf">[FDA label 2026]</a> In the earlier primary analysis, measurable-disease ORR was **67% (49/73) versus 40% (26/65)**, and grade **≥3 adverse events were 68% versus 40%**. <a class="cite" href="https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10803259/">[Nature Medicine 2024]</a> That established survival result cannot be transferred to TALAPRO-3's immature hormone-sensitive OS analysis. <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/217439s004lbl.pdf">[US label]</a>

Its exploratory BRCAm rPFS HR was **0.20 (0.11–0.36)**; the non-BRCA HRRm estimate was **0.74 (0.50–1.09)**. The molecular subsets have different estimates and wide intervals; results from this castration-resistant cohort do not settle the hormone-sensitive non-BRCA question. <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/217439s004lbl.pdf">[US label]</a>

AMPLITUDE studied niraparib with abiraterone/prednisone in HRR-mutated mCSPC. Among **696 patients**, **56%** had BRCA alterations, **78%** high-volume disease and **16%** prior docetaxel. <a class="cite" href="https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12705445/">[Nature Medicine 2025]</a> BRCA-subgroup median rPFS was **NR versus 26.0 months**, HR **0.52 (95% CI 0.37–0.72)**; overall median was **NR versus 29.5 months**, HR **0.63 (95% CI 0.49–0.80)**. <a class="cite" href="https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12705445/">[Nature Medicine 2025]</a> OS remained immature, HR **0.79 (0.59–1.04)**. <a class="cite" href="https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12705445/">[Nature Medicine 2025]</a> Among patients with measurable disease, ORR was **72% (76/106) versus 74% (81/110)**. <a class="cite" href="https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12705445/">[Nature Medicine 2025]</a> Grade **3–4** adverse events occurred in **75% versus 59%**; **25% versus 3.7%** needed transfusion for anaemia. <a class="cite" href="https://www.nature.com/articles/s41591-025-03961-8">[AMPLITUDE]</a>

**Cross-trial comparisons cannot establish superiority.** Control arms differ: enzalutamide versus abiraterone/prednisone. Gene panels, BRCA proportions, disease burden, prior treatment and endpoint assessment also differ. AMPLITUDE’s panel included genes absent from TALAPRO-3 and omitted others. Neither the hazard ratios nor control medians establish which PARP combination is superior. <a class="cite" href="https://www.nature.com/articles/s41591-025-03961-8">[AMPLITUDE]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12705445/">[Nature Medicine 2025]</a>

The FDA authorised niraparib plus abiraterone and prednisone for **BRCA2-mutated** mCSPC on **12 Dec 2025**; its eligibility is narrower than the overall AMPLITUDE trial population. <a class="cite" href="https://www.fda.gov/drugs/cancer-approvals/fda-approves-niraparib-and-abiraterone-acetate-plus-prednisone-brca2-mutated-metastatic-castration">[FDA 2025]</a>

## Who fits the FDA label

The US indication covers adults with metastatic androgen pathway modulation-naive or -sensitive prostate cancer with HRR mutations detected by an **FDA-authorized test**. FoundationOne CDx and FoundationOne Liquid CDx are the named companion diagnostics. Testing can use tumour tissue NGS or ctDNA; confirm that the assay and reported alteration support the intended indication. Continue a GnRH analogue unless the patient has undergone bilateral orchiectomy. <a class="cite" href="https://www.fda.gov/drugs/cancer-approvals/fda-approves-talazoparib-enzalutamide-metastatic-androgen-pathway-modulation-naive-or-sensitive">[FDA]</a>

The **12 genes** in the US label are **ATM, ATR, BRCA1, BRCA2, CDK12, CHEK2, FANCA, MLH1, MRE11A, NBN, PALB2 and RAD51C**.  <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/217439s004lbl.pdf">[FDA label 2026]</a> A negative liquid result may need reconsideration if tumour DNA shedding or assay adequacy is uncertain. Tumour profiling and inherited-risk testing answer related but different questions; a potentially hereditary finding needs germline evaluation and genetic counselling. <a class="cite" href="https://www.fda.gov/drugs/cancer-approvals/fda-approves-talazoparib-enzalutamide-metastatic-androgen-pathway-modulation-naive-or-sensitive">[FDA]</a>

## What it changes in practice

Order HRR testing at diagnosis of metastatic disease, while starting ADT and planning intensification. Do not let a delayed molecular report become a reason to leave symptomatic disease untreated. Discuss germline testing, counselling and implications for relatives early; cascade testing should follow an appropriately confirmed inherited finding.

For BRCA-mutated disease, the evidence gives a particularly strong rationale for discussing early PARP inhibition. For non-BRCA disease, explain the favourable pooled exploratory result and its gene-level uncertainty. Include baseline marrow reserve, renal function, comorbidity, patient priorities and monitoring access in shared decisions. TALAPRO-3 excluded prior docetaxel, leaving its applicability after a docetaxel-containing triplet unresolved. <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/217439s004lbl.pdf">[US label]</a>

## India and resource-limited settings

In India and other resource-limited settings, tissue access, assay turnaround, testing costs and counselling capacity can determine whether this approach is practical. Coordinate tumour profiling and germline evaluation to avoid unnecessary duplication, while preserving their distinct purposes. Confirmed inherited variants can also guide family cascade testing through counselling services.

Where PARP inhibitors are unaffordable or unavailable, **enzalutamide with ongoing ADT remains a strong standard**. Safe delivery also depends on reliable CBC monitoring, transfusion services and transport for urgent assessment. Discuss the full care burden before committing to treatment.

FDA approval says nothing about CDSCO approval or Indian price. **We did not verify Indian availability.** Check local regulatory status and supply before prescribing; these sources do not establish an Indian access pathway.

## Open questions

The full TALAPRO-3 publication and subsequent FDA approval are incorporated here; a search through 21 Nov 2026 did not identify a later primary OS analysis. <a class="cite" href="https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2604126">[NEJM 2026]</a> Mature OS is the central unanswered endpoint. <a class="cite" href="https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2604126">[NEJM 2026]</a> Other gaps include gene-specific benefit outside BRCA, outcomes after docetaxel-containing intensification, optimal sequencing at progression, and how sustained marrow toxicity affects treatment delivery in routine care. Comparative evidence between PARP combinations is also needed; separate positive trials do not settle that choice.

## Bottom line

TALAPRO-3 supports talazoparib plus enzalutamide and ADT for appropriately tested HRR-mutated hormone-sensitive metastatic prostate cancer. Radiographic progression was delayed, with the clearest molecular signal in BRCA disease; survival benefit remains unproven. Pair early molecular testing with a practical plan for blood-count monitoring and transfusion support.

<p class="note">For clinician education; individual treatment requires the current prescribing information, clinical judgement and local regulatory checks. US FDA approval does not establish CDSCO approval, Indian availability or affordability.</p>
