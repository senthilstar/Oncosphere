## Why this matters

TALAPRO-3 moves PARP inhibition earlier in metastatic prostate cancer: into disease still sensitive to androgen pathway modulation, with an HRR mutation identified before treatment selection. The FDA approved talazoparib plus enzalutamide on **26 Oct 2026**. The regulatory term, metastatic androgen pathway modulation-naive or -sensitive disease, maps to the familiar mHSPC/mCSPC setting. ADT remains the backbone. <a class="cite" href="https://www.fda.gov/drugs/resources-information-approved-drugs/fda-approves-talazoparib-enzalutamide-metastatic-androgen-pathway-modulation-naive-or-sensitive">[FDA]</a>

The advance is a substantial delay in radiographic progression or death against an active enzalutamide control. It is also a commitment to additional marrow toxicity and supportive care. **An overall-survival benefit has not yet been established in this setting.** Testing, patient preference and the ability to manage anaemia should shape the decision together. <a class="cite" href="https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2604126">[NEJM 2026]</a>

## The biology in brief

PARP helps cells respond to DNA damage. Talazoparib inhibits PARP activity and traps PARP on DNA; impaired homologous recombination repair can make tumour cells particularly vulnerable. Enzalutamide suppresses androgen-receptor signalling. Combining these approaches targets androgen dependence alongside a DNA-repair vulnerability, rather than waiting for castration resistance.

HRR is a pathway, not a uniform biomarker. BRCA alterations and alterations in other repair-associated genes need not predict the same sensitivity. A panel-positive result establishes eligibility only when its interpretation fits the authorised test and indication; it does not promise identical benefit across genes. The exploratory subgroup results should inform that conversation, without being treated as definitive gene-specific trials. <a class="cite" href="https://www.pfizermedical.com/talzenna">[US label]</a>

## TALAPRO-3 design and population

The randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial enrolled **599 patients**, allocated **1:1** to talazoparib plus enzalutamide or placebo plus enzalutamide, with continuous ADT. The assigned groups contained **300 versus 299** patients. Eligibility required HRR-mutated metastatic hormone-sensitive disease and **ECOG 0–1**. ADT could have started up to **3 months** before randomisation; prior docetaxel was excluded. <a class="cite" href="https://www.fda.gov/drugs/resources-information-approved-drugs/fda-approves-talazoparib-enzalutamide-metastatic-androgen-pathway-modulation-naive-or-sensitive">[FDA]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://www.pfizermedical.com/talzenna">[US label]</a>

Stratification accounted for de novo versus relapsed metastases, high versus low disease volume, and BRCA versus non-BRCA mutations. The primary endpoint was investigator-assessed rPFS using **RECIST 1.1** and **PCWG3** bone criteria; OS was an alpha-protected key secondary endpoint. Median follow-up was **37.6 / 37.7 months**, with a data cutoff of **18 Feb 2026**. <a class="cite" href="https://www.asco.org/abstracts-presentations/258180/abstract">[ASCO 2026]</a>

This was predominantly newly presenting, substantial-burden disease: **73%** had de novo metastases, **66%** high-volume disease and **51%** both. Median age was **70 years**; **39%** were Asian. The label records prior ARPI exposure in the mHSPC setting in **13%**, so describing the entire population as completely ARPI-naive would be inaccurate. Prior exposure still requires careful review against the actual eligibility and label. <a class="cite" href="https://www.pfizermedical.com/talzenna">[US label]</a>

## Efficacy: progression benefit, survival still pending

All comparisons below give combination before control. Median rPFS was **not reached versus 45.8 months**, HR **0.48 (95% CI 0.36–0.65)**, **p<0.0001** in the FDA account. At **3 years**, rPFS was **77% versus 56%** in the NEJM report. A median not reached is not a predicted duration of control; follow-up has not yet located that median. <a class="cite" href="https://www.fda.gov/drugs/resources-information-approved-drugs/fda-approves-talazoparib-enzalutamide-metastatic-androgen-pathway-modulation-naive-or-sensitive">[FDA]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2604126">[NEJM 2026]</a>

The BRCAm subgroup contained **104 versus 103** patients; the non-BRCA HRRm subgroup **196 versus 196**. Both exploratory rPFS estimates favoured the combination. The stronger BRCA estimate is biologically coherent, but subgroup point estimates alone cannot prove a difference in treatment effect. Non-BRCA results pool heterogeneous genes and should not be read as equally persuasive evidence for each alteration. <a class="cite" href="https://www.pfizermedical.com/talzenna">[US label]</a>

Interim OS was **78% versus 72% at 3 years**, HR **0.77 (95% CI 0.56–1.04)**. The interval includes no effect, and the result was **not statistically significant**. At the rPFS analysis, deaths had occurred in **28%** overall. This is immature survival evidence: counsel about demonstrated progression delay, while awaiting mature OS rather than presenting survival improvement as established. <a class="cite" href="https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2604126">[NEJM 2026]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://www.pfizermedical.com/talzenna">[US label]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://www.asco.org/abstracts-presentations/258180/abstract">[ASCO 2026]</a>

## Toxicity: anaemia and marrow reserve

Anaemia is central to feasibility. Grade **≥3 clinical anaemia was 51% versus 3%**; **5%** stopped talazoparib for anaemia. Separately, the label's laboratory table reports haemoglobin decrease in **88% versus 46%**, with grade **3** decrease in **49% versus 3.4%**. Clinical adverse events and laboratory abnormalities are different reporting categories; these figures should not be interchanged. <a class="cite" href="https://www.pfizer.com/news/press-release/press-release-detail/pfizers-talzenna-plus-xtandi-fda-approved-earlier">[Pfizer]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://www.pfizermedical.com/talzenna">[US label]</a>

In the label safety population, talazoparib interruption occurred in **69%**, reduction in **60%**, and permanent discontinuation in **19%**. Neutrophil and platelet decreases also matter. The NEJM report recorded serious adverse events in **42% versus 32%**, and **two treatment-related deaths**. This is oral therapy with substantial monitoring needs. <a class="cite" href="https://www.pfizermedical.com/talzenna">[US label]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2604126">[NEJM 2026]</a>

Obtain a baseline CBC and renal function, review pre-existing cytopenias and reversible causes of anaemia, and repeat CBC regularly during treatment, with closer assessment when counts fall or symptoms develop. Arrange a clear route for urgent review and transfusion before starting. For clinically important cytopenias, interrupt treatment, investigate and support recovery; resume with label-directed dose modification when appropriate. Avoid simply treating fatigue as an expected nuisance.

Talazoparib is **0.5 mg orally daily** with enzalutamide. In moderate renal impairment, **CLcr 30–59**, the combination dose is **0.35 mg daily**. Renal adjustment and toxicity-driven reduction answer different problems; reassess both. The label warns about **MDS/AML**, myelosuppression and embryo-fetal toxicity. Persistent or unexplained cytopenias warrant treatment review and haematology assessment rather than repeated empirical transfusions alone. <a class="cite" href="https://www.fda.gov/drugs/resources-information-approved-drugs/fda-approves-talazoparib-enzalutamide-metastatic-androgen-pathway-modulation-naive-or-sensitive">[FDA]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://www.pfizermedical.com/talzenna">[US label]</a>

## How it compares: TALAPRO-2 and AMPLITUDE

TALAPRO-2 tested the same drug pair in **mCRPC**, a different disease state. In its **399-patient HRRm cohort**, BICR rPFS was NR versus **13.8 months**, HR **0.45 (0.33–0.61)**. OS was **45.1 versus 31.1 months**, HR **0.62 (0.48–0.81)**, **p=0.0005**. That established survival result cannot be transferred to TALAPRO-3's immature hormone-sensitive OS analysis. <a class="cite" href="https://www.pfizermedical.com/talzenna">[US label]</a>

Its exploratory BRCAm rPFS HR was **0.20 (0.11–0.36)**; the non-BRCA HRRm estimate was **0.74 (0.50–1.09)**. This reinforces the need to distinguish broad HRR eligibility from strength of evidence within molecular subsets. It does not negate TALAPRO-3's non-BRCA result. <a class="cite" href="https://www.pfizermedical.com/talzenna">[US label]</a>

AMPLITUDE studied niraparib with abiraterone/prednisone in HRR-mutated mCSPC. Among **696 patients**, **56%** had BRCA alterations, **78%** high-volume disease and **16%** prior docetaxel. rPFS HR was **0.52 (0.37–0.72)** in the BRCA subgroup and **0.63 (0.49–0.80)** overall. OS remained immature, HR **0.79 (0.59–1.04)**. Grade **3–4** adverse events occurred in **75% versus 59%**; **25%** needed transfusion. <a class="cite" href="https://www.nature.com/articles/s41591-025-03961-8">[AMPLITUDE]</a>

**Cross-trial comparisons are unreliable.** Control arms differ: enzalutamide versus abiraterone/prednisone. Gene panels, BRCA proportions, disease burden, prior treatment and endpoint assessment also differ. AMPLITUDE's panel included genes absent from TALAPRO-3 and omitted others. Neither the hazard ratios nor control medians establish which PARP combination is superior. <a class="cite" href="https://www.nature.com/articles/s41591-025-03961-8">[AMPLITUDE]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://www.asco.org/abstracts-presentations/243837">[ASCO AMPLITUDE]</a>

## Who fits the FDA label

The US indication covers adults with metastatic androgen pathway modulation-naive or -sensitive prostate cancer with HRR mutations detected by an **FDA-authorized test**. FoundationOne CDx and FoundationOne Liquid CDx are the named companion diagnostics. Testing can use tumour tissue NGS or ctDNA; confirm that the assay and reported alteration support the intended indication. Continue a GnRH analogue unless the patient has undergone bilateral orchiectomy. <a class="cite" href="https://www.fda.gov/drugs/resources-information-approved-drugs/fda-approves-talazoparib-enzalutamide-metastatic-androgen-pathway-modulation-naive-or-sensitive">[FDA]</a>

The **12 genes** are **ATM, ATR, BRCA1, BRCA2, CDK12, CHEK2, FANCA, MLH1, MRE11A, NBN, PALB2 and RAD51C**. A negative liquid result may need reconsideration if tumour DNA shedding or assay adequacy is uncertain. Tumour profiling and inherited-risk testing answer related but different questions; a potentially hereditary finding needs germline evaluation and genetic counselling. <a class="cite" href="https://www.fda.gov/drugs/resources-information-approved-drugs/fda-approves-talazoparib-enzalutamide-metastatic-androgen-pathway-modulation-naive-or-sensitive">[FDA]</a>

## What it changes in practice

Order HRR testing at diagnosis of metastatic disease, while starting ADT and planning intensification. Do not let a delayed molecular report become a reason to leave symptomatic disease untreated. Discuss germline testing, counselling and implications for relatives early; cascade testing should follow an appropriately confirmed inherited finding.

For BRCA-mutated disease, the evidence gives a particularly strong rationale for discussing early PARP inhibition. For non-BRCA disease, explain the favourable pooled exploratory result and its gene-level uncertainty. Include baseline marrow reserve, renal function, comorbidity, patient priorities and monitoring access in shared decisions. TALAPRO-3 excluded prior docetaxel, leaving its applicability after a docetaxel-containing triplet unresolved. <a class="cite" href="https://www.pfizermedical.com/talzenna">[US label]</a>

## India and resource-limited settings

Testing cost, tissue access, assay turnaround and counselling capacity can determine whether this strategy is usable. Coordinate tumour profiling and germline evaluation to avoid unnecessary duplication, while preserving their distinct purposes. Confirmed inherited variants can also guide family cascade testing through counselling services.

Where PARP inhibitors are unaffordable or unavailable, **enzalutamide with ongoing ADT remains a strong standard**. Drug access alone is insufficient: reliable CBC surveillance, transfusion capacity and transport for urgent care are part of delivering the combination safely. Discuss the full care burden before committing to treatment.

FDA approval says nothing about CDSCO approval or Indian price. **We did not verify Indian availability.** Check local regulatory status and supply before prescribing; these sources do not establish an Indian access pathway.

## Open questions

Mature OS is the central unanswered endpoint. Other gaps include gene-specific benefit outside BRCA, outcomes after docetaxel-containing intensification, optimal sequencing at progression, and how sustained marrow toxicity affects treatment delivery in routine care. Comparative evidence between PARP combinations is also needed; separate positive trials do not settle that choice.

## Bottom line

TALAPRO-3 supports talazoparib plus enzalutamide and ADT for appropriately tested HRR-mutated hormone-sensitive metastatic prostate cancer. The rPFS result is compelling, BRCA evidence is strongest, and interim OS remains immature and not significant. Pair early molecular testing with an equally concrete marrow-monitoring and transfusion plan.
