On 5 November 2026, the US FDA granted **accelerated approval** to subcutaneous amivantamab and hyaluronidase-lpuj (Rybrevant Faspro) alone for adults with recurrent or metastatic head and neck squamous cell carcinoma (HNSCC) progressing on or after platinum-based chemotherapy and an anti-PD-(L)1 antibody. <a class="cite" href="https://www.fda.gov/drugs/cancer-approvals/fda-grants-accelerated-approval-amivantamab-and-hyaluronidase-lpuj-recurrent-or-metastatic-head-and">[FDA 2026]</a> The indication excludes HPV-associated oropharyngeal squamous cell carcinoma, assessed by p16 immunohistochemistry. <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/761433s002lbl.pdf">[FDA label 2026]</a>

Approval is based on response rate and duration; continued approval may depend on confirmation of clinical benefit. <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/761433s002lbl.pdf">[FDA label 2026]</a>

## How to read the figure
The graphic shows blinded central review results for the same 102-patient OrigAMI-4 efficacy population: overall response rate (ORR) 42% (95% CI 32–52%), including complete responses in 15% and partial responses in 28%; rounding accounts for the apparent difference in the sum. <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/761433s002lbl.pdf">[FDA label 2026]</a> Among responders, 56% had a response lasting at least 6 months; the observed duration range was 1.4+ to 19.7+ months, with a plus sign denoting an ongoing response. <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/761433s002lbl.pdf">[FDA label 2026]</a> The bar depicts the response rate and confidence interval, not a randomised comparison.

## OrigAMI-4 in brief
OrigAMI-4 was an open-label, multicentre, multi-cohort phase 1b/2 study; its primary efficacy population comprised 102 patients previously treated with platinum chemotherapy and an anti-PD-(L)1 antibody, excluding p16-positive oropharyngeal cancer and prior anti-EGFR therapy. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1200/JCO-26-01042">[JCO 2026]</a> Prior platinum and immunotherapy could have been given together or separately; treatment with curative intent qualified if progression occurred within 6 months of the last dose. <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/761433s002lbl.pdf">[FDA label 2026]</a>

Responses were confirmed by blinded independent central review using RECIST version 1.1. <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/761433s002lbl.pdf">[FDA label 2026]</a>

- **ORR:** 42% (95% CI 32–52%); complete response 15%, partial response 28%. <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/761433s002lbl.pdf">[FDA label 2026]</a>
- **Duration of response:** 56% of responders had a response lasting at least 6 months; median duration was not reached (95% CI 6.9 months to not reached) in the published analysis. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1200/JCO-26-01042">[JCO 2026]</a>
- **Time-to-event outcomes:** at median follow-up of 11.8 months, median progression-free survival was 6.8 months (95% CI 5.2–8.3) and median overall survival was 12.5 months (95% CI 10.2–16.8); these are single-arm estimates. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1200/JCO-26-01042">[JCO 2026]</a>

The US label permits a weight-based schedule every 3 weeks after initial weekly doses: below 80 kg, amivantamab 1,600 mg with hyaluronidase 20,000 units on week 1 day 1, then 2,400 mg with 30,000 units on weeks 2 and 3 and every 3 weeks from week 4; at 80 kg or above, the corresponding doses are 2,240 mg with 28,000 units, then 3,360 mg with 42,000 units. <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/761433s002lbl.pdf">[FDA label 2026]</a> The label also provides an every-4-week schedule; consult it for administration, premedication and dose modifications. <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/761433s002lbl.pdf">[FDA label 2026]</a>

Amivantamab targets EGFR and MET; hyaluronidase enables subcutaneous administration. The FDA reviewed the application through Project Orbis. <a class="cite" href="https://www.fda.gov/drugs/cancer-approvals/fda-grants-accelerated-approval-amivantamab-and-hyaluronidase-lpuj-recurrent-or-metastatic-head-and">[FDA 2026]</a>

## Safety in OrigAMI-4
In the 102-patient HNSCC safety population, common adverse reactions included rash (79%), fatigue (39%), nail toxicity (35%), stomatitis (32%) and oedema (28%). <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/761433s002lbl.pdf">[FDA label 2026]</a>

Serious adverse reactions occurred in 39%; events in at least 2% included pneumonia (8%), haemorrhage (4.9%), dysphagia and pneumothorax (2.9% each), and anaemia, dyspnoea, fatigue, increased hepatic enzymes and ILD/pneumonitis (2% each). <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/761433s002lbl.pdf">[FDA label 2026]</a> Fatal adverse reactions occurred in 6%: sudden death in 2%, and myocardial ischaemia, tumour haemorrhage, aspiration pneumonia and ILD/pneumonitis in one patient each (1% each). <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/761433s002lbl.pdf">[FDA label 2026]</a> Adverse reactions led to permanent discontinuation in 17%, interruption in 51% and dose reduction in 23%. <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/761433s002lbl.pdf">[FDA label 2026]</a>

The label warns about hypersensitivity and administration-related reactions, ILD/pneumonitis, skin and ocular toxicity, and embryo-fetal toxicity; it contraindicates use in patients with known hypersensitivity to hyaluronidase or any excipient. <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/761433s002lbl.pdf">[FDA label 2026]</a> Premedication is required, and patients should be monitored for 2–4 hours after the first injection. <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/761433s002lbl.pdf">[FDA label 2026]</a>

## What still needs confirming
[OrigAMI-5](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07276399) is a randomised phase 3 trial comparing first-line amivantamab, pembrolizumab and carboplatin with pembrolizumab, platinum and fluorouracil. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1200/JCO.2026.44.16_suppl.TPS6127">[JCO 2026]</a> The FDA lists its completion as a postmarketing requirement, with an original projected completion date of 31 May 2029. <a class="cite" href="https://www.fda.gov/drugs/cancer-approvals/ongoing-cancer-accelerated-approvals">[FDA 2026]</a> The confirmatory trial is in an earlier treatment setting than the approved later-line indication.

## For readers in India
An FDA accelerated approval does not establish CDSCO approval, local availability or reimbursement. Confirm p16 or HPV status for an oropharyngeal primary: the US indication excludes HPV-associated disease. <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/761433s002lbl.pdf">[FDA label 2026]</a> Later-line options after platinum and immunotherapy remain limited in many Indian centres. If the product becomes available, monitor for rash, ILD/pneumonitis and administration-related reactions as directed in the label. We will update this note if an Indian approval or access route is announced.
