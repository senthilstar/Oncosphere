On **16 November 2026** the FDA approved **giredestrant (Wilserdly)**, an oral selective estrogen receptor antagonist/degrader, **with everolimus** for adults with **ER+, HER2−, ESR1-mutated** locally advanced or metastatic breast cancer whose disease recurred during/after adjuvant endocrine therapy or progressed on ≥1 endocrine regimen for metastatic disease. <a class="cite" href="https://www.fda.gov/drugs/cancer-approvals/fda-approves-giredestrant-combination-everolimus-er-positive-her2-negative-esr1-mutated-locally">[FDA 2026]</a>

- **Companion diagnostic:** FoundationOne Liquid CDx (ctDNA), limited to specified ESR1 ligand-binding-domain mutations. <a class="cite" href="https://www.fda.gov/drugs/cancer-approvals/fda-approves-giredestrant-combination-everolimus-er-positive-her2-negative-esr1-mutated-locally">[FDA 2026]</a>
- **Review:** Project Orbis (with Health Canada and Israel MoH); approved **4 weeks ahead** of the goal date. <a class="cite" href="https://www.fda.gov/drugs/cancer-approvals/fda-approves-giredestrant-combination-everolimus-er-positive-her2-negative-esr1-mutated-locally">[FDA 2026]</a>
- The separate **adjuvant** giredestrant application (lidERA) is a different decision; see our [November FDA decisions note]({{ '/oncology-fda-decisions-to-watch-november-2026/' | relative_url }}).

## Why it matters
- Everolimus + exemestane has long been a post-CDK4/6 option; evERA asks whether swapping the endocrine partner for an oral SERD adds benefit when ESR1 is mutated. <a class="cite" href="https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2602457">[NEJM 2026]</a>
- Adds an **mTOR-inhibitor partner** to the ESR1-mutated toolkit. Other recent ESR1-directed FDA approvals use oral endocrine agents alone or with a CDK4/6 inhibitor (imlunestrant ± abemaciclib, vepdegestrant, camizestrant + CDK4/6 inhibitor). <a class="cite" href="https://www.fda.gov/drugs/resources-information-approved-drugs/oncology-cancer-hematologic-malignancies-approval-notifications">[FDA list 2026]</a>

## evERA Breast Cancer — design
- Phase 3, open-label, randomised 1:1; **n = 373** (giredestrant + everolimus 183; standard endocrine therapy + everolimus 190). <a class="cite" href="https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2602457">[NEJM 2026]</a>
- Population: ER+/HER2− advanced disease, progression/recurrence **after a CDK4/6 inhibitor + endocrine therapy**. <a class="cite" href="https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2602457">[NEJM 2026]</a>
- Arms: giredestrant **30 mg OD** vs physician's choice of **exemestane, fulvestrant or tamoxifen**; both with everolimus **10 mg OD**. GnRH agonist for pre/perimenopausal women and for men (unless orchiectomy). <a class="cite" href="https://www.fda.gov/drugs/cancer-approvals/fda-approves-giredestrant-combination-everolimus-er-positive-her2-negative-esr1-mutated-locally">[FDA 2026]</a>
- Primary endpoint: **investigator-assessed PFS** (RECIST 1.1), tested hierarchically — ESR1-mutated first (n = 207, ctDNA central testing), then overall population. <a class="cite" href="https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2602457">[NEJM 2026]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://www.fda.gov/drugs/cancer-approvals/fda-approves-giredestrant-combination-everolimus-er-positive-her2-negative-esr1-mutated-locally">[FDA 2026]</a>

## Results

| Endpoint | Giredestrant + everolimus | Standard ET + everolimus | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| Median PFS, **ESR1-mutated** (n = 207) | **10.0 mo** (95% CI 8.1–12.9) | 5.5 mo (95% CI 3.8–5.6) | **HR 0.38** (0.27–0.54); p < 0.001 |
| Median PFS, overall (n = 373) | 8.8 mo | 5.5 mo | HR 0.56 (0.44–0.71); p < 0.001 |
| ORR, ESR1-mutated | 27% (18–37) | 14% (8–22) | — |
| Overall survival | Immature | Immature | — |

Sources: ESR1-mutated PFS CIs and ORR <a class="cite" href="https://www.fda.gov/drugs/cancer-approvals/fda-approves-giredestrant-combination-everolimus-er-positive-her2-negative-esr1-mutated-locally">[FDA 2026]</a>; overall-population PFS <a class="cite" href="https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2602457">[NEJM 2026]</a>.

- Benefit was concentrated in ESR1-mutated tumours; the **FDA indication is ESR1-mutated only**, despite a positive overall-population test. <a class="cite" href="https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2602457">[NEJM 2026]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://www.fda.gov/drugs/cancer-approvals/fda-approves-giredestrant-combination-everolimus-er-positive-her2-negative-esr1-mutated-locally">[FDA 2026]</a>
- Caveats: open-label; investigator-assessed primary endpoint; control arm included tamoxifen and fulvestrant as partners (exemestane + everolimus is the familiar standard).

## Safety
- Any AE: 98.9% vs 96.8%. Most common: **stomatitis 47.3% vs 48.9%**, diarrhoea 26.9% vs 22.6%, anaemia 23.6% vs 21.0%. <a class="cite" href="https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2602457">[NEJM 2026]</a>
- Label warnings: **bradycardia** and embryo-fetal toxicity. Common adverse reactions (≥20%) also include rash, fatigue, musculoskeletal pain, **pneumonitis** and nausea. <a class="cite" href="https://www.fda.gov/drugs/cancer-approvals/fda-approves-giredestrant-combination-everolimus-er-positive-her2-negative-esr1-mutated-locally">[FDA 2026]</a>
- Toxicity burden is largely everolimus-driven → dexamethasone mouthwash prophylaxis, glucose/lipid checks and early pneumonitis assessment remain essential.

## Practice points
- **Test ESR1 on ctDNA at progression** on endocrine therapy/CDK4/6 inhibitor; ESR1 mutations are usually acquired under aromatase-inhibitor pressure, so an archival primary-tumour result can miss them.
- **Where it fits:** ESR1-mutated, post-CDK4/6 inhibitor, patient fit for everolimus. Alternatives: oral SERD monotherapy, oral SERD + CDK4/6 inhibitor, or PI3K/AKT-pathway agents when PIK3CA/AKT1/PTEN alterations are present. No head-to-head data between these options.
- **Not for:** ESR1 wild-type disease (outside the label), or patients unable to tolerate everolimus.
- Dose: giredestrant 30 mg orally once daily, with or without food, plus everolimus per its label. <a class="cite" href="https://www.fda.gov/drugs/cancer-approvals/fda-approves-giredestrant-combination-everolimus-er-positive-her2-negative-esr1-mutated-locally">[FDA 2026]</a>
