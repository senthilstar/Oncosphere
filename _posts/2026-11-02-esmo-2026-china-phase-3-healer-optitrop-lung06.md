Two phase 3 trials from ESMO 2026 (Madrid) that did not feature in the Presidential Symposia but went straight into NEJM. Both were run entirely in China, both challenge a long-standing first-line standard, and both report PFS at a prespecified interim analysis.

## HEALER: anbenitamab + HB1801 vs THP, first-line HER2+ advanced breast cancer

**Design**
- Phase 3, open-label, multicentre, China; 1:1.
- Anbenitamab (KN026, HER2 biparatopic bispecific antibody) + HB1801 (albumin-bound docetaxel) vs docetaxel + trastuzumab + pertuzumab (THP).
- n = 885 (445 vs 440).
- Primary endpoint: PFS by blinded independent central review (BICR). ORR secondary.

**Efficacy (interim; 401 events; median follow-up 21.0 mo)**
- 24-mo PFS: **58.5% vs 34.4%**.
- HR for progression or death **0.46** (95% CI 0.37–0.56; P < 0.001).
- ORR: 87.6% vs 73.4%.
- OS: not reported as mature.

**Safety**
- Grade ≥3 AEs: 45.9% vs 34.4%.
- Grade ≥3 neutropenia (commonest): 13.1% vs 8.4%.
- Fatal AEs: 2 patients in each arm.

**Read**
- Large PFS gain over THP, the CLEOPATRA-era standard.
- Two variables changed at once (bispecific in place of trastuzumab + pertuzumab, and albumin-bound in place of conventional docetaxel), so the effect cannot be attributed to the antibody alone.
- No comparison with T-DXd + pertuzumab (DESTINY-Breast09).
- Wait for OS and for data outside China before changing practice.

## OptiTROP-Lung06: sac-TMT + pembrolizumab vs chemo + pembrolizumab, PD-L1 <1% non-squamous NSCLC

**Design**
- Phase 3, open-label, 81 centres in China; 1:1.
- Untreated locally advanced/metastatic non-squamous NSCLC, PD-L1 TPS <1% (central), no EGFR/ALK or other targetable alterations, ECOG 0–1.
- Sacituzumab tirumotecan (sac-TMT, TROP2 ADC) 4 mg/kg q2w + pembrolizumab 400 mg q6w vs pemetrexed + platinum + pembrolizumab, then pemetrexed maintenance.
- n = 455 (229 vs 226). Primary endpoint: PFS by BICR. OS secondary.

**Efficacy (interim; data cut-off 9 Jun 2026; median follow-up 9.7 mo)**
- Median PFS: **15.0 vs 5.7 mo**; HR **0.49** (95% CI 0.36–0.65; P < 0.001).
- 12-mo PFS: 51.1% vs 22.8%.
- ORR: 50.2% vs 47.3%. Median DoR: 13.6 vs 6.8 mo.
- PFS benefit held for TROP2-low (HR 0.49) and TROP2-high (HR 0.59) tumours.
- OS immature, not formally tested: HR 0.58 (95% CI 0.38–0.88); 12-mo OS 77.6% vs 66.4%.

**Safety**
- Grade ≥3 AEs: 47.6% vs 60.6%.
- Fatal AEs: 2.2% vs 6.2%.
- Dose reductions for AEs: 4.8% vs 19.9%.
- Stomatitis common with sac-TMT, mostly grade 1–2.

**Read**
- First phase 3 ADC + IO regimen to beat chemo-IO in driver-negative, PD-L1-negative non-squamous NSCLC, the subgroup that gains least from pembrolizumab.
- Response rates were similar; the gain is in durability of response.
- Short follow-up; OS signal is encouraging but descriptive.
- No TROP2 selection needed on these data.

## Relevance for India and other LMICs

- **Access:** neither regimen is approved by FDA, EMA or CDSCO as of 2 Nov 2026. Both are China-developed (Kelun-Biotech for sac-TMT; JMT-Bio for anbenitamab), which may mean faster and cheaper regional access than Western ADCs, but nothing is confirmed for India.
- **Cost reality:** in most Indian public-sector settings, first-line HER2+ MBC is still trastuzumab (often biosimilar) + taxane, with pertuzumab access limited. HEALER's comparator (THP) is already beyond what many patients receive.
- **Population fit:** all-China enrolment; Asian pharmacogenomics and toxicity profiles may be closer to Indian patients than Western-majority trials, but this is an assumption, not data.
- **Biomarkers:** OptiTROP-Lung06 required central PD-L1 testing and exclusion of EGFR/ALK and other targetable drivers. Broad NGS remains unavailable for many Indian patients, a precondition before any of this applies.
- **Toxicity monitoring:** ADC-related stomatitis and haematological toxicity need nursing and day-care capacity; plan before adoption.

*For clinicians. Not medical advice; regimens discussed are not yet approved in India.*
