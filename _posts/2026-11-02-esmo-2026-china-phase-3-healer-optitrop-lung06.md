Two randomised trials conducted in China and presented at ESMO examined initial treatment for advanced cancer. Both reported improved progression-free survival (PFS) at a prespecified interim analysis; an overall survival (OS) benefit has not been established. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2610345">[NEJM 2026]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2611280">[NEJM 2026]</a>

## HEALER: anbenitamab + HB1801 vs THP, first-line HER2+ advanced breast cancer

**Design**
- In this open-label, multicentre Chinese phase 3 trial, 885 patients were randomised 1:1 to anbenitamab plus HB1801 (445) or docetaxel, trastuzumab and pertuzumab (THP; 440). <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2610345">[NEJM 2026]</a>
- Anbenitamab is a HER2-directed bispecific antibody; HB1801 is albumin-bound docetaxel. The primary endpoint was PFS by blinded independent central review (BICR); objective response was a secondary endpoint. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2610345">[NEJM 2026]</a>

**Efficacy**
- At the planned interim analysis, after 401 progression or death events and 21.0 months of median follow-up, estimated 24-month PFS was **58.5% vs 34.4%**, respectively (HR **0.46**, 95% CI 0.37–0.56; P < 0.001). <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2610345">[NEJM 2026]</a>
- Objective response rate (ORR) was 87.6% vs 73.4%. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2610345">[NEJM 2026]</a>
- Mature OS results were not available in the interim report. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2610345">[NEJM 2026]</a>

**Safety**
- Grade ≥3 adverse events occurred in 45.9% vs 34.4%; grade ≥3 neutropenia occurred in 13.1% vs 8.4%. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2610345">[NEJM 2026]</a>
- Adverse events led to death in 2 patients in each group. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2610345">[NEJM 2026]</a>

**Read**
- PFS favoured anbenitamab plus HB1801, while severe adverse events were more frequent overall.
- The arms differed in both HER2-directed therapy and docetaxel formulation, so the separate effect of the antibody cannot be isolated.
- The trial did not compare this regimen with trastuzumab deruxtecan plus pertuzumab. The FDA has approved that combination for first-line treatment of adults with unresectable or metastatic HER2-positive breast cancer, defined as IHC 3+ or ISH-positive by an FDA-approved test. <a class="cite" href="https://www.fda.gov/drugs/drug-approvals-and-databases/fda-approves-fam-trastuzumab-deruxtecan-nxki-pertuzumab-unresectable-or-metastatic-her2-positive">[FDA 2025]</a>
- Longer OS follow-up and evidence beyond the trial population are needed to define the regimen's place in care.

## OptiTROP-Lung06: sac-TMT + pembrolizumab vs chemo + pembrolizumab, PD-L1 <1% non-squamous NSCLC

**Design**
- This open-label phase 3 trial in China randomised 455 patients 1:1 to sacituzumab tirumotecan (sac-TMT) plus pembrolizumab (229) or platinum–pemetrexed plus pembrolizumab (226). <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2611280">[NEJM 2026]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://newsfile.futunn.com/public/NN-PersistNoticeAttachment/7781/20261025/12382371-0.PDF">[Sponsor 2026]</a>
- Participants had untreated locally advanced or metastatic non-squamous NSCLC, PD-L1 tumour proportion score <1% and no targetable genomic alterations. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2611280">[NEJM 2026]</a>
- Sac-TMT is a TROP2-directed antibody–drug conjugate. The primary endpoint was BICR PFS; OS was secondary. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2611280">[NEJM 2026]</a>

**Efficacy**
- At the planned interim analysis, with a 9 June 2026 data cut-off and 9.7 months of median follow-up, median PFS was **15.0 vs 5.7 months** (HR **0.49**, 95% CI 0.36–0.65; P < 0.001). <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2611280">[NEJM 2026]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://newsfile.futunn.com/public/NN-PersistNoticeAttachment/7781/20261025/12382371-0.PDF">[Sponsor 2026]</a>
- ORR was 50.2% vs 47.3%; median response duration was 13.6 vs 6.8 months. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2611280">[NEJM 2026]</a>
- OS data were immature: HR for death 0.58 (95% CI 0.38–0.88), with median OS not reached in either arm. This interim estimate does not establish an OS benefit. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2611280">[NEJM 2026]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://newsfile.futunn.com/public/NN-PersistNoticeAttachment/7781/20261025/12382371-0.PDF">[Sponsor 2026]</a>

**Safety**
- Grade ≥3 treatment-emergent adverse events occurred in 47.6% vs 60.6%. Stomatitis was common with sac-TMT plus pembrolizumab and predominantly grade 1–2. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2611280">[NEJM 2026]</a>

**Read**
- Response rates were similar, but response duration and PFS favoured the sac-TMT arm.
- Follow-up is short and the OS estimate remains immature. The study did not select by TROP2 expression; subgroup findings cannot establish that expression has no bearing on benefit.

## Relevance for India and other LMICs

- **Access:** check CDSCO approval for each specific regimen and local availability before use. The sponsor reports Chinese approval of sac-TMT for previously treated EGFR-mutant lung cancer; that is a different indication and does not establish approval of the combination discussed here. <a class="cite" href="https://cdsco.gov.in/opencms/opencms/en/Approval_new/Approved-New-Drugs">[CDSCO]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://newsfile.futunn.com/public/NN-PersistNoticeAttachment/7781/20261025/12382371-0.PDF">[Sponsor 2026]</a>
- **Cost reality:** pertuzumab and newer antibody–drug conjugates may be difficult to access in Indian public-sector practice; discuss local availability and affordability.
- **Population fit:** both trials enrolled in China. Apply the findings to Indian patients cautiously; toxicity may differ across populations.
- **Biomarkers:** the lung study required PD-L1 testing and exclusion of targetable alterations. Molecular testing access matters when applying these results.
- **Toxicity monitoring:** plan for stomatitis and haematological toxicity, including supportive care and day-care capacity.

*For clinicians. Not medical advice; check current approval of the regimens in India before use.*
