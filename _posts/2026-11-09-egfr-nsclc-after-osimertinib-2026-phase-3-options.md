Progression on osimertinib is the most common decision point in EGFR-mutant NSCLC. For years the answer was platinum–pemetrexed. In 2026 four randomised phase 3 trials have reported against that same comparator, and the FDA acts on one of them (ivonescimab, HARMONi) on **14 November**. This note lines them up. Cross-trial comparison is hazardous: populations, biomarker selection and follow-up differ.

## The four trials at a glance

| Trial | Experimental arm | Selection | n | PFS, months (HR) | OS so far |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| SAFFRON (global; NEJM) | Osimertinib + savolitinib (MET TKI) | MET overexpression and/or amplification after osimertinib | — | 7.6 vs 5.4 (0.55) | 17.8 vs 12.9 mo, HR 0.68 (p=0.01) |
| HERON-Lung01 (China; Lancet) | Ruzaltatug rezetecan (HER3 ADC) | Ex19del/L858R after any EGFR TKI | 498 | 8.3 vs 5.4 (0.47) | Immature, HR 0.70 (0.43–1.14) |
| PANKU-Lung01 (China; Lancet) | Izalontamab brengitecan (EGFR×HER3 bispecific ADC) | After third-generation EGFR TKI, chemo-naive | 432 | 11.1 vs 5.7 (0.40) | Immature; 12-mo OS 83.0% vs 72.9% |
| HARMONi (global) | Ivonescimab (PD-1×VEGF) + platinum–pemetrexed | After third-generation EGFR TKI | — | Significant (see our [November FDA note]({{ '/oncology-fda-decisions-to-watch-november-2026/' | relative_url }})) | Primary HR 0.79 (0.62–1.01), not significant |

All comparators: platinum + pemetrexed, then pemetrexed maintenance (HARMONi: placebo + platinum doublet). "—" means we have not verified the exact number for this note.

## SAFFRON — the biomarker-driven option
- Osimertinib continued plus savolitinib, in tumours with MET overexpression, amplification or both.
- PFS HR 0.55 (0.42–0.72); 12-month PFS 36% vs 10%. ORR 51% vs 27%.
- **Only trial of the four with a statistically significant OS gain:** HR 0.68 (0.50–0.91).
- Cost of the gain: peripheral oedema 49%; serious AEs 41% vs 28%; fatal AEs 7% vs 0%.
- Needs a tissue MET test (IHC and/or amplification) at progression. Already covered in depth in our [ESMO 2026 Presidential Symposium II note]({{ '/esmo-2026-presidential-symposium-2/' | relative_url }}).

## HERON-Lung01 — HER3 ADC (ruzaltatug rezetecan, SHR-A2009)
- 62 sites in China; ages 18–75; 8.0 mg/kg IV every 3 weeks.
- BICR PFS 8.3 vs 5.4 months, HR 0.47 (0.36–0.60), at a median follow-up of only 7.7 months.
- ORR 60.0% vs 34.3%; median DoR 8.5 vs 4.4 months.
- Grade ≥3 TRAEs 41% vs 39% (mainly neutropenia); discontinuation for TRAEs 1% in each arm; ILD 2.4% (one grade 3).
- OS immature (HR 0.70, CI crosses 1) and will be diluted by 19.4% crossover.

## PANKU-Lung01 — EGFR×HER3 bispecific ADC (izalontamab brengitecan, iza-bren)
- 73 sites in China; 2.5 mg/kg IV on days 1 and 8 every 3 weeks.
- BICR PFS 11.1 vs 5.7 months, HR 0.40 (0.31–0.51); 12-month PFS 46.5% vs 14.4%.
- Larger effect with exon 19 deletion (HR 0.31) than L858R (HR 0.59).
- ORR 60.2% vs 34.5%. Interim OS: median not reached vs 19.4 months.
- **More toxic:** grade ≥3 TRAEs 79% vs 62% (mostly haematological), and day 1 + day 8 dosing doubles infusion visits.
- Primary analysis was modified ITT (treated patients only).

## What to watch on 14 November
FDA must decide whether a PFS win plus a non-significant primary OS (HARMONi) is enough for ivonescimab. That decision will signal how regulators may treat the two Chinese ADC trials, which so far also rest on PFS with immature OS.

## Practical reading today
- Re-biopsy at progression still matters: MET status selects for SAFFRON-type therapy; transformation to small-cell histology changes everything.
- Where none of the new agents is accessible, platinum–pemetrexed remains a valid standard. Amivantamab + carboplatin–pemetrexed (MARIPOSA-2; FDA Sep 2024) and datopotamab deruxtecan (FDA accelerated approval Jun 2025) are the other approved routes in the US.
- PFS gains of 3–5 months with immature OS should be weighed against toxicity and visit burden.
