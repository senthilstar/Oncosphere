Presidential Symposium I, ESMO 2026, Madrid — Saturday 24 October. Four late-breaking abstracts (LBA1–4), all positive phase 3. Data as presented; not yet peer-reviewed in full.

<p class="note"><strong>Bottom line:</strong> OS gains at interim for VOLGA (cisplatin-ineligible MIBC) and DeLLphi-305 (ES-SCLC maintenance). INTerpath-001 and DASL-HiCaP met recurrence/metastasis end points; OS not yet significant.</p>

| LBA | Trial | Setting | Primary result | OS |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | INTerpath-001 | Adjuvant, resected stage IIB–IV melanoma | RFS HR 0.58 | HR 0.77, NS (immature) |
| 2 | VOLGA | MIBC, cisplatin-ineligible/refused | EFS HR 0.51 (durva + EV) | HR 0.58, P = .0027 |
| 3 | DeLLphi-305 | ES-SCLC, 1L maintenance | OS HR 0.42 | Primary end point met |
| 4 | DASL-HiCaP | Very high-risk localised prostate, with RT + ADT | MFS HR 0.42 | HR 0.88, NS |

## LBA1 — INTerpath-001 (intismeran autogene + pembrolizumab)
- **Design:** double-blind; n = 1137, 2:1. Intismeran (individualised mRNA neoantigen therapy) 1 mg IM q3w × 9 vs placebo; both + pembrolizumab 400 mg q6w × 9.
- **RFS:** HR 0.58 (95% CI 0.45–0.75; P < .001). 24-month RFS 82.2% vs 70.0%. Median follow-up 24.6 months.
- **Timing of benefit:** no separation in first 6 months (HR 0.99); thereafter HR 0.42.
- **DMFS:** HR 0.62 (P = .002). **OS:** immature, HR 0.77 (95% CI 0.45–1.31), not significant.
- **Toxicity:** grade 3–5 AEs 23.6% vs 20.3%. Pyrexia 52.2% vs 8.2%, injection-site pain 51.0% vs 9.5% — mostly low grade, transient.

## LBA2 — VOLGA (perioperative durvalumab ± tremelimumab + neoadjuvant enfortumab vedotin)
- **Design:** n = 695, 1:1:1. Neoadjuvant durvalumab + EV × 3 → cystectomy → adjuvant durvalumab × 9 (± tremelimumab) vs upfront cystectomy.
- **Durva + EV vs control:** EFS HR 0.51 (P < .0001); 24-month EFS 64% vs 45%. OS HR 0.58 (P = .0027); 24-month OS 78% vs 69%.
- **pCR:** 45% vs 10% (local review).
- **ctDNA (exploratory, n = 112):** 60% cleared before surgery; 24-month EFS 81% if cleared vs 55% if persistent.
- **Triplet (+ tremelimumab):** EFS HR 0.61; OS not significant (HR 0.77) — more toxicity (grade 3–4 AEs 70%).
- **Toxicity, doublet:** grade 3–4 AEs 64% vs 44%; discontinuation 21%.

## LBA3 — DeLLphi-305 (tarlatamab + durvalumab maintenance)
- **Population:** ES-SCLC without progression after 1L platinum–etoposide–durvalumab. n = 563, open-label.
- **OS (primary, interim):** median 21.1 vs 12.7 months; HR 0.42 (95% CI 0.32–0.54; P < .001). 12-month OS 79% vs 53%.
- **PFS:** 7.4 vs 1.9 months (HR 0.34). ORR 26.4% vs 4.1%.
- **Subgroups:** brain metastases HR 0.49; liver metastases HR 0.41.
- **Toxicity:** CRS 49.6% (one grade 3); ICANS 1.1%. Treatment-related grade ≥ 3 AEs 20.9% vs 10.8%. No treatment-related deaths with the combination.

## LBA4 — DASL-HiCaP / ANZUP1801 (darolutamide + ADT + RT)
- **Design:** placebo-controlled; n = 1106. Darolutamide 600 mg BD × 96 weeks vs placebo; all had 96 weeks ADT + RT (85% primary, 15% salvage). 71–74% Gleason 9–10; 29% pelvic nodes.
- **MFS by conventional imaging:** HR 0.42 (95% CI 0.27–0.66; P = .0001). 4-year MFS 95% vs 88%.
- **Secondary:** PSA-PFS HR 0.47; time to castration resistance HR 0.22.
- **OS:** HR 0.88, not significant (21 vs 24 deaths).
- **Toxicity:** similar rates of flushing and fatigue between arms.

## What is not yet known
- Full peer-reviewed papers and final OS for INTerpath-001 and DASL-HiCaP.
- Regulatory decisions — none of these combinations is approved for these indications yet.
- Cost-effectiveness; individualised mRNA therapy needs tumour sequencing and bespoke manufacture.
