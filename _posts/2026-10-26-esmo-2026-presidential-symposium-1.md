Presidential Symposium I, ESMO 2026, Madrid — Saturday 24 October. Four late-breaking abstracts (LBA1–4), all positive phase 3. VOLGA and DeLLphi-305 were published simultaneously in the *NEJM*; INTerpath-001 has been accepted by the *NEJM*; DASL-HiCaP is abstract-only so far. Figures below were re-checked on 31 Oct 2026 against the NEJM abstracts and sponsor press releases (cited inline).

<p class="note"><strong>Bottom line:</strong> OS gains at interim for VOLGA (cisplatin-ineligible MIBC) and DeLLphi-305 (ES-SCLC maintenance). INTerpath-001 and DASL-HiCaP met recurrence/metastasis end points; OS not yet significant.</p>

| LBA | Trial | Setting | Primary result | OS |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | INTerpath-001 | Adjuvant, resected stage IIB–IV melanoma | RFS HR 0.58 | HR 0.77, NS (immature) |
| 2 | VOLGA | MIBC, cisplatin-ineligible/refused | EFS HR 0.51 (durva + EV) | HR 0.58, P = .0027 |
| 3 | DeLLphi-305 | ES-SCLC, 1L maintenance | OS HR 0.42 | Primary end point met |
| 4 | DASL-HiCaP | Very high-risk localised prostate, with RT + ADT | MFS HR 0.42 | HR 0.88, NS |

## LBA1 — INTerpath-001 (intismeran autogene + pembrolizumab)
- **Design:** double-blind; n = 1137, 2:1. Intismeran (individualised mRNA neoantigen therapy) 1 mg IM q3w × 9 vs placebo; both + pembrolizumab 400 mg q6w × 9.
- **RFS:** HR 0.58 (95% CI 0.45–0.75; P < .001). 24-month RFS 82.2% (95% CI 79.3–84.8) vs 70.0% (65.0–74.5); median RFS not reached in either arm. Median follow-up 24.6 months. <a class="cite" href="https://www.merck.com/news/merck-and-moderna-will-present-results-from-landmark-phase-3-interpath-001-trial-for-intismeran-autogene-plus-keytruda-pembrolizumab-at-esmo-2026-presidential-symposium/">[Merck]</a>
- **Timing of benefit:** no separation in first 6 months (HR 0.99; 95% CI 0.66–1.49); after the 6-month immunisation course, prespecified piecewise HR 0.42 (0.30–0.58).
- **mITT sensitivity analysis** (excluding those who never received intismeran/placebo): RFS HR 0.51 (0.39–0.67); DMFS HR 0.55 (0.40–0.77). <a class="cite" href="https://www.merck.com/news/merck-and-moderna-will-present-results-from-landmark-phase-3-interpath-001-trial-for-intismeran-autogene-plus-keytruda-pembrolizumab-at-esmo-2026-presidential-symposium/">[Merck]</a>
- **DMFS:** HR 0.62 (95% CI 0.45–0.85; P = .002); 24-month DMFS 88.5% vs 80.2%. <a class="cite" href="https://www.merck.com/news/merck-and-moderna-will-present-results-from-landmark-phase-3-interpath-001-trial-for-intismeran-autogene-plus-keytruda-pembrolizumab-at-esmo-2026-presidential-symposium/">[Merck]</a> **OS:** immature, HR 0.77 (95% CI 0.45–1.31; P = .33), not significant. <a class="cite" href="https://www.targetedonc.com/view/esmo-2026-presidential-symposium-i-late-breaking-recap">[Targeted Oncology]</a>
- **Toxicity:** grade 3–5 AEs (all causes) 23.6% vs 20.3%; **treatment-related** grade 3–5 AEs 14.2% vs 13.4%. Pyrexia 52.2% vs 8.2%, injection-site pain 51.0% vs 9.5%, fatigue 38.5% — mostly low grade, transient. <a class="cite" href="https://www.merck.com/news/merck-and-moderna-will-present-results-from-landmark-phase-3-interpath-001-trial-for-intismeran-autogene-plus-keytruda-pembrolizumab-at-esmo-2026-presidential-symposium/">[Merck]</a>

## LBA2 — VOLGA (perioperative durvalumab ± tremelimumab + neoadjuvant enfortumab vedotin)
- **Design:** n = 695, 1:1:1. Neoadjuvant durvalumab + EV × 3 → cystectomy → adjuvant durvalumab × 9 (± tremelimumab) vs upfront cystectomy.
- **Durva + EV vs control:** EFS HR 0.51 (95% CI 0.38–0.67; P < .001); median EFS not reached vs 12.1 months; 24-month EFS 63.5% vs 45.1%. OS HR 0.58 (0.40–0.83; P = .003); 24-month OS 78.4% vs 69.4%; deaths 49 (21%) vs 74 (32%). Data cut-off 2 March 2026. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2608323">[NEJM]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://www.astrazeneca.com/media-centre/press-releases/2026/perioperative-imfinzi-plus-neoadjuvant-enfortumab-vedotin-reduced-risk-of-recurrence-by-49-and-risk-of-death-by-42-in-muscle-invasive-bladder-cancer.html">[AZ]</a>
- **pCR:** 45% vs 10% (local review).
- **ctDNA (exploratory, n = 112):** 60% cleared before surgery; 24-month EFS 81% if cleared vs 55% if persistent.
- **Triplet (+ tremelimumab):** 24-month EFS 60.1%; EFS HR 0.61 (0.47–0.80; P < .001); OS not significant (HR 0.77; 0.54–1.07). More toxicity: grade ≥ 3 AEs 79.5% (treatment-related 44.5%). <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2608323">[NEJM]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://www.targetedonc.com/view/esmo-2026-presidential-symposium-i-late-breaking-recap">[Targeted Oncology]</a>
- **Toxicity, doublet:** grade ≥ 3 AEs (any cause) 71.1% vs 50.2% with surgery alone; treatment-related grade ≥ 3 30.7% (NEJM). AstraZeneca's press release instead quotes grade 3–4 AEs (64% vs 44%); quote the NEJM grade ≥ 3 figures when comparing trials. Discontinuation for AEs 21%; immune-mediated AEs 33% vs 3%. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2608323">[NEJM]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://www.astrazeneca.com/media-centre/press-releases/2026/perioperative-imfinzi-plus-neoadjuvant-enfortumab-vedotin-reduced-risk-of-recurrence-by-49-and-risk-of-death-by-42-in-muscle-invasive-bladder-cancer.html">[AZ]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://www.targetedonc.com/view/esmo-2026-presidential-symposium-i-late-breaking-recap">[Targeted Oncology]</a>
- **Regulatory:** FDA granted Priority Review (September 2026) for perioperative durvalumab + neoadjuvant EV in cisplatin-ineligible/refusing MIBC. <a class="cite" href="https://www.astrazeneca.com/media-centre/press-releases/2026/perioperative-imfinzi-plus-neoadjuvant-enfortumab-vedotin-reduced-risk-of-recurrence-by-49-and-risk-of-death-by-42-in-muscle-invasive-bladder-cancer.html">[AZ]</a>

## LBA3 — DeLLphi-305 (tarlatamab + durvalumab maintenance)
- **Population:** ES-SCLC without progression after 1L platinum–etoposide–durvalumab. n = 563, open-label.
- **OS (primary, interim; cut-off 7 July 2026; median follow-up ~16 months):** median 21.1 (95% CI 20.8–NE) vs 12.7 months (11.2–14.8); HR 0.42 (95% CI 0.32–0.54; P < .001). 12-month OS 79% vs 53%. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2609482">[NEJM]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://www.amgen.com/newsroom/press-releases/2026/10/imdelltra-in-combination-with-imfinzi-reduced-risk-of-death-by-an-unprecedented-58-in-firstline-extensive-stage-small-cell-lung-cancer">[Amgen]</a>
- **PFS:** 7.4 (5.6–9.1) vs 1.9 months (1.9–2.0); HR 0.34 (0.28–0.42). ORR 26.4% vs 4.1%. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2609482">[NEJM]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://www.amgen.com/newsroom/press-releases/2026/10/imdelltra-in-combination-with-imfinzi-reduced-risk-of-death-by-an-unprecedented-58-in-firstline-extensive-stage-small-cell-lung-cancer">[Amgen]</a>
- **Subgroups:** brain metastases HR 0.49; liver metastases HR 0.41.
- **Toxicity:** grade ≥ 3 AEs (any cause) 46.8% vs 40.6%; treatment-related grade ≥ 3 20.9% vs 10.8% (commonest: neutropenia, decreased appetite, asthenia). CRS 49.6% (one grade 3); ICANS 1.1%. No treatment-related deaths with the combination. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2609482">[NEJM]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://www.amgen.com/newsroom/press-releases/2026/10/imdelltra-in-combination-with-imfinzi-reduced-risk-of-death-by-an-unprecedented-58-in-firstline-extensive-stage-small-cell-lung-cancer">[Amgen]</a>

## LBA4 — DASL-HiCaP / ANZUP1801 (darolutamide + ADT + RT)
- **Design:** placebo-controlled; n = 1106. Darolutamide 600 mg BD × 96 weeks vs placebo; all had 96 weeks ADT + RT (85% primary, 15% salvage). 71–74% Gleason 9–10; 29% pelvic nodes.
- **MFS by conventional imaging (interim, after 87 events; median follow-up 3.8 years):** HR 0.42 (95% CI 0.27–0.66; P = .0001); 27 vs 63 events. 4-year MFS 95% vs 88%. <a class="cite" href="https://www.bayer.com/en-us/united-states/news-and-stories/2026/metastasis-nubeqa">[Bayer]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://www.targetedonc.com/view/esmo-2026-presidential-symposium-i-late-breaking-recap">[Targeted Oncology]</a>
- **Secondary:** PSA-PFS HR 0.47 (0.33–0.67), 4-year 91% vs 83%; prostate cancer-specific survival HR 0.58 (0.17–1.97); time to castration resistance HR 0.22. <a class="cite" href="https://www.bayer.com/en-us/united-states/news-and-stories/2026/metastasis-nubeqa">[Bayer]</a>
- **OS:** HR 0.88 (95% CI 0.49–1.59), not significant; 4-year OS 97% vs 95% (21 vs 24 deaths). <a class="cite" href="https://www.bayer.com/en-us/united-states/news-and-stories/2026/metastasis-nubeqa">[Bayer]</a>
- **Toxicity:** any grade ≥ 3 AE 43% vs 36%. Grade ≥ 2 AEs of special interest: fatigue/asthenia 14% vs 10%, hypertension 7% vs 9%, fractures 2% vs 3%, cardiac events 3% vs 3%. <a class="cite" href="https://www.bayer.com/en-us/united-states/news-and-stories/2026/metastasis-nubeqa">[Bayer]</a>

## What is not yet known
- Full peer-reviewed papers for INTerpath-001 (accepted by NEJM) and DASL-HiCaP, and final OS for both (DASL-HiCaP: 130 MFS events planned for the final analysis).
- Regulatory decisions — none of these combinations is approved for these indications yet.
- Cost-effectiveness; individualised mRNA therapy needs tumour sequencing and bespoke manufacture.
