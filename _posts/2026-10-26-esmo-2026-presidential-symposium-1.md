## LBA1 — INTerpath-001 (intismeran autogene + pembrolizumab)
- **Design:** After complete resection of stage IIB–IV cutaneous melanoma, 1,137 patients entered a double-blind trial: randomisation was 2:1 to intramuscular intismeran 1 mg every 3 weeks for up to 9 doses or placebo, with pembrolizumab 400 mg every 6 weeks for up to 9 cycles in both arms. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2609762">[NEJM 2026]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://docs.publicnow.com/viewDoc?filename=178244%5CEXT%5CBE30DCA6A1EBAD5B10C57D354064ABFE8165B757_379E2CD359D32405C0CA6688BA53E37FF1CB86A9.PDF">[ESMO 2026 presentation]</a>
- **RFS:** After median follow-up of 24.6 months, the RFS HR was 0.58 (95% CI 0.45–0.75; P < .001); 24-month RFS was 82.2% (95% CI 79.3–84.8) versus 70.0% (65.0–74.5), with median RFS not reached in either group. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2609762">[NEJM 2026]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://docs.publicnow.com/viewDoc?filename=178244%5CEXT%5CBE30DCA6A1EBAD5B10C57D354064ABFE8165B757_379E2CD359D32405C0CA6688BA53E37FF1CB86A9.PDF">[ESMO 2026 presentation]</a>
- **Timing:** The prespecified time-specific RFS HRs were 0.99 (95% CI 0.66–1.49) before 6 months and 0.42 (0.30–0.58) thereafter; these time-specific estimates do not establish an individual patient's timing of benefit. <a class="cite" href="https://docs.publicnow.com/viewDoc?filename=178244%5CEXT%5CBE30DCA6A1EBAD5B10C57D354064ABFE8165B757_379E2CD359D32405C0CA6688BA53E37FF1CB86A9.PDF">[ESMO 2026 presentation]</a>
- **Sensitivity analysis:** In a modified intention-to-treat sensitivity analysis excluding patients who did not receive their assigned intismeran or placebo, the RFS HR was 0.51 (95% CI 0.39–0.67) and distant metastasis-free survival (DMFS) HR was 0.55 (0.40–0.77). <a class="cite" href="https://docs.publicnow.com/viewDoc?filename=178244%5CEXT%5CBE30DCA6A1EBAD5B10C57D354064ABFE8165B757_379E2CD359D32405C0CA6688BA53E37FF1CB86A9.PDF">[ESMO 2026 presentation]</a>
- **DMFS and OS:** For DMFS, the HR was 0.62 (95% CI 0.45–0.85; P = .002), with 24-month estimates of 88.5% versus 80.2%; OS was immature (HR 0.77; 95% CI 0.45–1.31; P = .33). <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2609762">[NEJM 2026]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://docs.publicnow.com/viewDoc?filename=178244%5CEXT%5CBE30DCA6A1EBAD5B10C57D354064ABFE8165B757_379E2CD359D32405C0CA6688BA53E37FF1CB86A9.PDF">[ESMO 2026 presentation]</a>
- **Toxicity:** Grade 3–5 adverse events (AEs) from any cause occurred in 23.6% versus 20.3%, and treatment-related grade 3–5 AEs in 14.2% versus 13.4%; fever and injection-site pain were more frequent with intismeran. <a class="cite" href="https://docs.publicnow.com/viewDoc?filename=178244%5CEXT%5CBE30DCA6A1EBAD5B10C57D354064ABFE8165B757_379E2CD359D32405C0CA6688BA53E37FF1CB86A9.PDF">[ESMO 2026 presentation]</a>

## LBA2 — VOLGA (perioperative durvalumab ± tremelimumab + neoadjuvant enfortumab vedotin)
- **Design:** The open-label study assigned 695 cystectomy candidates ineligible for or declining cisplatin-based chemotherapy, 1:1:1, to neoadjuvant durvalumab plus enfortumab vedotin (EV), the same regimen with tremelimumab, or radical cystectomy without neoadjuvant treatment. After surgery, the experimental arms continued immunotherapy; controls underwent observation or locally approved adjuvant treatment. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2608323">[NEJM 2026]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://www.fda.gov/drugs/cancer-approvals/fda-approves-durvalumab-enfortumab-vedotin-ejfv-muscle-invasive-bladder-cancer">[FDA 2026]</a>
- **Doublet versus control:** At the 2 March 2026 cutoff, the doublet EFS HR was 0.51 (95% CI 0.38–0.67; P < .001); median EFS was not reached versus 12.1 months and 24-month EFS was 63.5% versus 45.1%. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2608323">[NEJM 2026]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://bladder.uroonco.uroweb.org/publication/perioperative-durvalumab-with-or-without-tremelimumab-plus-neoadjuvant-enfortumab-vedotin-in-muscle-invasive-bladder-cancer-results-from-the-phase-3-volga-trial/">[ESMO 2026 abstract]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://www.fda.gov/drugs/cancer-approvals/fda-approves-durvalumab-enfortumab-vedotin-ejfv-muscle-invasive-bladder-cancer">[FDA 2026]</a>
- **OS:** HR was 0.58 (95% CI 0.40–0.83; P = .003), with 24-month OS 78.4% versus 69.4%. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2608323">[NEJM 2026]</a>
- **Pathological complete response:** By local review, pathological complete response occurred in 45% on the doublet and 10% in the control arm. <a class="cite" href="https://bladder.uroonco.uroweb.org/publication/perioperative-durvalumab-with-or-without-tremelimumab-plus-neoadjuvant-enfortumab-vedotin-in-muscle-invasive-bladder-cancer-results-from-the-phase-3-volga-trial/">[ESMO 2026 abstract]</a>
- **Triplet versus control:** The triplet EFS HR was 0.61 (95% CI 0.47–0.80; P < .001), with 24-month EFS 60.1%; OS did not meet significance at this analysis. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2608323">[NEJM 2026]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://bladder.uroonco.uroweb.org/publication/perioperative-durvalumab-with-or-without-tremelimumab-plus-neoadjuvant-enfortumab-vedotin-in-muscle-invasive-bladder-cancer-results-from-the-phase-3-volga-trial/">[ESMO 2026 abstract]</a>
- **Toxicity:** Grade ≥3 AEs from any cause occurred in 71.1% with the doublet, 79.5% with the triplet and 50.2% with control; treatment-related grade ≥3 AEs occurred in 30.7% and 44.5% in the respective experimental arms. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2608323">[NEJM 2026]</a>
- **Regulatory:** On 13 November 2026, the FDA approved neoadjuvant durvalumab plus EV followed by adjuvant durvalumab after radical cystectomy for adults with MIBC ineligible for or declining cisplatin-based chemotherapy. <a class="cite" href="https://www.fda.gov/drugs/cancer-approvals/fda-approves-durvalumab-enfortumab-vedotin-ejfv-muscle-invasive-bladder-cancer">[FDA 2026]</a>

## LBA3 — DeLLphi-305 (tarlatamab + durvalumab maintenance)
- **Population:** In this open-label trial, 563 patients with extensive-stage SCLC without progression after initial chemoimmunotherapy were assigned to maintenance tarlatamab plus durvalumab or durvalumab alone; survival was measured from maintenance randomisation. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2609482">[NEJM 2026]</a>
- **OS:** At the interim cutoff of 7 July 2026, median follow-up was 15.9 versus 16.1 months; median OS was 21.1 months (95% CI 20.8–not estimable) versus 12.7 months (11.2–14.8), HR 0.42 (95% CI 0.32–0.54; P < .001). <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2609482">[NEJM 2026]</a>
- **PFS:** Progression-free survival medians were 7.4 months (95% CI 5.6–9.1) versus 1.9 months (1.9–2.0), HR 0.34 (95% CI 0.28–0.42; P < .001). <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2609482">[NEJM 2026]</a>
- **Toxicity:** Grade ≥3 AEs from any cause occurred in 46.8% versus 40.6%; cytokine release syndrome (CRS), predominantly low grade, occurred in nearly half of patients receiving the combination. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2609482">[NEJM 2026]</a>
- **Practice implication:** Plan for CRS and neurological toxicity monitoring when using tarlatamab. The regulatory status of tarlatamab after progression cannot be extrapolated to this maintenance combination.

## LBA4 — DASL-HiCaP / ANZUP1801 (darolutamide + ADT + RT)
- **Design:** The placebo-controlled trial enrolled 1,106 patients with very high-risk localised disease or biochemical persistence or recurrence within 1 year after prostatectomy. Darolutamide 600 mg twice daily or placebo was given for 96 weeks alongside androgen deprivation therapy (ADT) and locoregional radiotherapy (RT). <a class="cite" href="https://prostate.uroonco.uroweb.org/publication/darolutamide-with-androgen-deprivation-therapy-and-definitive-or-salvage-locoregional-radiation-therapyfor-localised-very-high-risk-prostate-cancer-interim-analysis-of-an-international-placebo-cont/">[ESMO 2026 abstract]</a>
- **Population:** On CT, pelvic nodes were involved in 29%, Gleason score was 9–10 in 73%, and 15% had undergone prostatectomy. <a class="cite" href="https://prostate.uroonco.uroweb.org/publication/darolutamide-with-androgen-deprivation-therapy-and-definitive-or-salvage-locoregional-radiation-therapyfor-localised-very-high-risk-prostate-cancer-interim-analysis-of-an-international-placebo-cont/">[ESMO 2026 abstract]</a>
- **MFS:** At the interim analysis, median follow-up was 3.8 years; conventional-imaging MFS HR was 0.42 (95% CI 0.27–0.66; P = .0001), with 27 versus 63 events and 4-year MFS 95% versus 88%. <a class="cite" href="https://prostate.uroonco.uroweb.org/publication/darolutamide-with-androgen-deprivation-therapy-and-definitive-or-salvage-locoregional-radiation-therapyfor-localised-very-high-risk-prostate-cancer-interim-analysis-of-an-international-placebo-cont/">[ESMO 2026 abstract]</a>
- **Secondary outcomes:** The PSA progression-free survival HR was 0.47 (95% CI 0.33–0.67), with 4-year estimates of 91% versus 83%; prostate cancer-specific survival HR was 0.58 (0.17–1.97). <a class="cite" href="https://prostate.uroonco.uroweb.org/publication/darolutamide-with-androgen-deprivation-therapy-and-definitive-or-salvage-locoregional-radiation-therapyfor-localised-very-high-risk-prostate-cancer-interim-analysis-of-an-international-placebo-cont/">[ESMO 2026 abstract]</a>
- **OS:** The OS HR was 0.88 (95% CI 0.49–1.59), with 21 versus 24 deaths and 4-year OS 97% versus 95%; a survival benefit has not been established. <a class="cite" href="https://prostate.uroonco.uroweb.org/publication/darolutamide-with-androgen-deprivation-therapy-and-definitive-or-salvage-locoregional-radiation-therapyfor-localised-very-high-risk-prostate-cancer-interim-analysis-of-an-international-placebo-cont/">[ESMO 2026 abstract]</a>
- **Toxicity:** An AE of grade ≥3 occurred in 43% versus 36%; grade ≥2 fatigue/asthenia occurred in 14% versus 10%, hypertension 7% versus 9%, fractures 2% versus 3%, and cardiac events 3% versus 3%. <a class="cite" href="https://prostate.uroonco.uroweb.org/publication/darolutamide-with-androgen-deprivation-therapy-and-definitive-or-salvage-locoregional-radiation-therapyfor-localised-very-high-risk-prostate-cancer-interim-analysis-of-an-international-placebo-cont/">[ESMO 2026 abstract]</a>

## What is not yet known
- Longer follow-up is needed to establish OS in INTerpath-001 and DASL-HiCaP and the durability of the observed benefits. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2609762">[NEJM 2026]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://prostate.uroonco.uroweb.org/publication/darolutamide-with-androgen-deprivation-therapy-and-definitive-or-salvage-locoregional-radiation-therapyfor-localised-very-high-risk-prostate-cancer-interim-analysis-of-an-international-placebo-cont/">[ESMO 2026 abstract]</a>
- A full peer-reviewed DASL-HiCaP report and regulatory decisions on the other regimens remain awaited. FDA approval of the VOLGA doublet does not establish its approval or availability in India. <a class="cite" href="https://prostate.uroonco.uroweb.org/publication/darolutamide-with-androgen-deprivation-therapy-and-definitive-or-salvage-locoregional-radiation-therapyfor-localised-very-high-risk-prostate-cancer-interim-analysis-of-an-international-placebo-cont/">[ESMO 2026 abstract]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://www.fda.gov/drugs/cancer-approvals/fda-approves-durvalumab-enfortumab-vedotin-ejfv-muscle-invasive-bladder-cancer">[FDA 2026]</a>
- Individualised mRNA therapy requires tumour sequencing and patient-specific manufacture; its cost-effectiveness and equitable access remain uncertain.
