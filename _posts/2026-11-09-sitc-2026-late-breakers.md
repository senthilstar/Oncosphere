The Society for Immunotherapy of Cancer meeting and pre-conference programmes took place in Phoenix on 4–8 November 2026. <a class="cite" href="https://sitcancer.org/2026/schedule/sitc-2026-schedule">[SITC 2026]</a> These small, uncontrolled studies are hypothesis-generating; the uveal and lung results are sponsor-reported meeting data.

## 1. Amsoki-cel: TIL therapy without high-dose IL-2 (melanoma)
**What:** Amsokigene autoleucel (amsoki-cel, formerly OBX-115) is a tumour-infiltrating lymphocyte (TIL) product engineered with acetazolamide-regulated membrane-bound IL-15. Its regimen includes lymphodepletion without post-infusion IL-2; tumour tissue can be obtained by core needle biopsy. <a class="cite" href="https://obsidiantx.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/SITC-2026_FIH-Agni-N37_2026-10-22_FINAL.pdf">[SITC 2026]</a>

**Who:** Patients had advanced melanoma resistant to checkpoint inhibitors; 11/15 (73%) in the recommended-dose efficacy set had previously received both anti-PD-1 and anti-CTLA-4 therapy. <a class="cite" href="https://obsidiantx.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/SITC-2026_FIH-Agni-N37_2026-10-22_FINAL.pdf">[SITC 2026]</a>

**Results (Agni-01, recommended-dose efficacy set):**
- Confirmed objective response rate (ORR) was 67% (10/15; two complete and eight partial responses). <a class="cite" href="https://obsidiantx.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/SITC-2026_FIH-Agni-N37_2026-10-22_FINAL.pdf">[SITC 2026]</a> Median progression-free survival (PFS) was 8.3 months (95% CI 3.9–not estimable); median duration of response (DoR) was not reached at 14.3 months’ median follow-up. <a class="cite" href="https://obsidiantx.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/SITC-2026_FIH-Agni-N37_2026-10-22_FINAL.pdf">[SITC 2026]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://ir.obsidiantx.com/node/7211/pdf">[Sponsor report 2026]</a>
- At the 12-month post-infusion landmark, 5/15 patients (33%) remained in response. <a class="cite" href="https://obsidiantx.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/SITC-2026_FIH-Agni-N37_2026-10-22_FINAL.pdf">[SITC 2026]</a> This denominator is all treated patients; it is not the 12-month DoR estimate among responders. <a class="cite" href="https://obsidiantx.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/SITC-2026_FIH-Agni-N37_2026-10-22_FINAL.pdf">[SITC 2026]</a>
- Across the pooled safety population (n=37), one patient had grade 3 cytokine release syndrome (CRS); no treatment-related deaths, immune effector cell-associated neurotoxicity syndrome (ICANS), intensive care transfers or dose-limiting toxicities were reported. <a class="cite" href="https://obsidiantx.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/SITC-2026_FIH-Agni-N37_2026-10-22_FINAL.pdf">[SITC 2026]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://ir.obsidiantx.com/node/7211/pdf">[Sponsor report 2026]</a>
- The earlier *Nature Medicine* analysis used a shorter follow-up; a separate registration-enabling cohort is ongoing, with topline results expected by the end of 2027 according to the sponsor. <a class="cite" href="https://www.nature.com/articles/s41591-026-04743-6">[Nature Medicine 2026]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://ir.obsidiantx.com/node/7211/pdf">[Sponsor report 2026]</a>

**Why it matters:** Omitting post-infusion IL-2 may lessen treatment burden, although lymphodepletion and monitoring remain necessary. The small single-arm series cannot establish comparative efficacy or safety versus lifileucel.

## 2. Two T-cell engagers together: brenetafusp + tebentafusp (uveal melanoma)
**What:** Tebentafusp targets gp100 peptide–HLA and CD3; brenetafusp targets PRAME peptide–HLA and CD3. Both require HLA-A\*02:01; the US label indicates tebentafusp for HLA-A\*02:01-positive adults with unresectable or metastatic uveal melanoma. <a class="cite" href="https://www.immunocore.com/investors/news/press-releases/detail/139/immunocore-presents-promising-data-from-phase-12-trial-cohort-evaluating-brenetafusp-plus-kimmtrak-tebentafusp-tebn-in-metastatic-uveal-melanoma">[Sponsor report 2026]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://dailymed-beta.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?audience=professional&setid=24a49f57-d2fc-4ffe-9eb1-fe0460c6b067">[US label 2026]</a>

**Results:** At the June 2026 cutoff, 26 patients had received the combination; efficacy below is for 17 evaluable patients treated first line. <a class="cite" href="https://www.immunocore.com/investors/news/press-releases/detail/139/immunocore-presents-promising-data-from-phase-12-trial-cohort-evaluating-brenetafusp-plus-kimmtrak-tebentafusp-tebn-in-metastatic-uveal-melanoma">[Sponsor report 2026]</a>
- ORR was 29%, median DoR 24.1 months and disease control rate (DCR) 71%; median PFS was 8.1 months (95% CI 1.9–30.4). <a class="cite" href="https://www.marketscreener.com/news/immunocore-phase-1-evaluation-of-brenetafusp-alone-and-in-combination-with-tebentafusp-in-metastat-ce785fd2d08bf722">[SITC presentation 2026]</a>
- Estimated overall survival (OS) was 88% at one year and 70% at two years. <a class="cite" href="https://www.marketscreener.com/news/immunocore-phase-1-evaluation-of-brenetafusp-alone-and-in-combination-with-tebentafusp-in-metastat-ce785fd2d08bf722">[SITC presentation 2026]</a> Median OS was not reached at 29 months’ median follow-up. <a class="cite" href="https://www.immunocore.com/investors/news/press-releases/detail/139/immunocore-presents-promising-data-from-phase-12-trial-cohort-evaluating-brenetafusp-plus-kimmtrak-tebentafusp-tebn-in-metastatic-uveal-melanoma">[Sponsor report 2026]</a>
- Circulating tumour DNA (ctDNA) fell by at least 68% by week 9 in 8/9 evaluable patients and cleared in 4/9; this small biomarker subset cannot establish a survival benefit. <a class="cite" href="https://www.marketscreener.com/news/immunocore-phase-1-evaluation-of-brenetafusp-alone-and-in-combination-with-tebentafusp-in-metastat-ce785fd2d08bf722">[SITC presentation 2026]</a>
- In all 26 combination recipients, treatment-related CRS occurred in 77%, with no grade 3–4 CRS; grade 3–4 treatment-related adverse events occurred in 38%, most often transient lymphocyte decrease, and none led to discontinuation or death. <a class="cite" href="https://www.marketscreener.com/news/immunocore-phase-1-evaluation-of-brenetafusp-alone-and-in-combination-with-tebentafusp-in-metastat-ce785fd2d08bf722">[SITC presentation 2026]</a>
- Among 16 evaluable patients receiving brenetafusp alone after prior treatment, ORR was 19% and median OS 20.3 months. <a class="cite" href="https://www.marketscreener.com/news/immunocore-phase-1-evaluation-of-brenetafusp-alone-and-in-combination-with-tebentafusp-in-metastat-ce785fd2d08bf722">[SITC presentation 2026]</a>

**Next:** The planned randomised phase 2/3 DUET-UVEAL trial will compare the combination with tebentafusp alone. Historical comparisons cannot show whether adding brenetafusp improves survival. <a class="cite" href="https://www.immunocore.com/investors/news/press-releases/detail/139/immunocore-presents-promising-data-from-phase-12-trial-cohort-evaluating-brenetafusp-plus-kimmtrak-tebentafusp-tebn-in-metastatic-uveal-melanoma">[Sponsor report 2026]</a>

## 3. Cesalatamig: PD-1 × VEGF bispecific alone after immunotherapy (NSCLC)
**What:** Cesalatamig (AI-081) is an investigational PD-1 × VEGF bispecific antibody. <a class="cite" href="https://oncoc4.com/en/news-resources/news/oncoc4-reports-encouraging-phase-12-data-for-cesalatamig-monotherapy-in-previously-treated-non-small-cell-lung-cancer-at-sitc-2026/">[Sponsor report 2026]</a>

**Who:** In the pooled BiPAVE-001 studies, the safety population comprised 103 patients with NSCLC; median prior treatment was two lines, and 42% had received at least three. <a class="cite" href="https://oncoc4.com/en/news-resources/news/oncoc4-reports-encouraging-phase-12-data-for-cesalatamig-monotherapy-in-previously-treated-non-small-cell-lung-cancer-at-sitc-2026/">[Sponsor report 2026]</a>

**Results:** At the 31 July 2026 cutoff, 55 patients with PD-(L)1-resistant or refractory NSCLC were efficacy-evaluable; responses were investigator-assessed. <a class="cite" href="https://oncoc4.com/en/news-resources/news/oncoc4-reports-encouraging-phase-12-data-for-cesalatamig-monotherapy-in-previously-treated-non-small-cell-lung-cancer-at-sitc-2026/">[Sponsor report 2026]</a>

| Dose (every 3 weeks) | n | ORR | DCR |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 mg/kg | 10 | 10% | 40% <a class="cite" href="https://oncoc4.com/en/news-resources/news/oncoc4-reports-encouraging-phase-12-data-for-cesalatamig-monotherapy-in-previously-treated-non-small-cell-lung-cancer-at-sitc-2026/">[Sponsor report 2026]</a> |
| 3 mg/kg | 24 | 21% | 67% <a class="cite" href="https://oncoc4.com/en/news-resources/news/oncoc4-reports-encouraging-phase-12-data-for-cesalatamig-monotherapy-in-previously-treated-non-small-cell-lung-cancer-at-sitc-2026/">[Sponsor report 2026]</a> |
| 10 mg/kg | 21 | 33% | 90% <a class="cite" href="https://oncoc4.com/en/news-resources/news/oncoc4-reports-encouraging-phase-12-data-for-cesalatamig-monotherapy-in-previously-treated-non-small-cell-lung-cancer-at-sitc-2026/">[Sponsor report 2026]</a> |

- Responses occurred across PD-L1 expression levels; the sponsor selected 10 mg/kg every three weeks for further development and a planned pivotal study. FDA Fast Track designation applies to NSCLC progressing after PD-(L)1 therapy and platinum chemotherapy; designation is not approval. <a class="cite" href="https://oncoc4.com/en/news-resources/news/oncoc4-reports-encouraging-phase-12-data-for-cesalatamig-monotherapy-in-previously-treated-non-small-cell-lung-cancer-at-sitc-2026/">[Sponsor report 2026]</a>
- The apparent dose pattern is exploratory because these cohorts are small, open-label and non-randomised. The sponsor report does not provide a numerical adverse-event breakdown, limiting safety interpretation.

**Context:** After immunotherapy and chemotherapy, docetaxel-based treatment remains an option. For another PD-1 × VEGF antibody, the sponsor reported an FDA complete response letter for ivonescimab plus platinum chemotherapy after EGFR inhibitor therapy; the application was not approved in its present form. <a class="cite" href="https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1599298/000159929826000092/ivonescimabcrlscenariopr.htm">[Sponsor regulatory update 2026]</a> See also [our post-osimertinib note]({{ '/egfr-nsclc-after-osimertinib-2026-phase-3-options/' | relative_url }}).

## Bottom line
- Engineered TIL without post-infusion IL-2, combined T-cell engagers and PD-1 × VEGF bispecific antibodies remain investigational approaches.
- None of these uncontrolled findings establishes a new standard. Prospective comparison is needed to assess efficacy and safety.
