The Society for Immunotherapy of Cancer met in Phoenix on 4–8 November 2026 (41st Annual Meeting, ~1,200 abstracts). Most of the meeting is translational. Three clinical late-breakers stand out for practising oncologists. All are phase 1 or 1/2, single-arm and small. Figures below are from company releases and the meeting abstracts.

## 1. Amsoki-cel: TIL therapy without high-dose IL-2 (melanoma)
**What:** amsokigene autoleucel (formerly OBX-115), an engineered TIL product carrying drug-regulatable membrane-bound IL-15, so no IL-2 is given after infusion. Low-dose lymphodepletion only; tumour can be harvested by core needle biopsy.

**Who:** advanced melanoma progressing after immune checkpoint inhibitors; 93% had prior ipilimumab + nivolumab.

**Results (Agni-01, recommended dose, n=15; cutoff 25 Sep 2026):**
- ORR 67% (2 CR, 8 PR); median PFS 8.3 months; median DoR not reached at 14.3 months' follow-up.
- 33% (5/15) still in response 12 months after a single infusion.
- Pooled safety (n=37): no DLTs, treatment-related deaths, ICANS or ICU transfers; one grade 3 CRS.
- Published in *Nature Medicine* on 3 Nov 2026. Registration-enabling cohort topline expected by end-2027.

**Why it matters:** the IL-2 phase is what makes current TIL therapy (lifileucel) an ICU-level commitment. Removing it could widen who can receive TIL.

## 2. Two T-cell engagers together: brenetafusp + tebentafusp (uveal melanoma)
**What:** tebentafusp (gp100 × CD3, current standard for HLA-A\*02:01-positive metastatic uveal melanoma) plus brenetafusp (PRAME × CD3). Both need HLA-A\*02:01, so one blood test selects for either.

**Results (first-line combination, n=17 evaluable; June 2026 cutoff):**
- ORR 29%; median DoR 24.1 months; DCR 71%; median PFS 8.1 months.
- OS 88% at 1 year and **70% at 2 years**; median OS not reached at 29 months' median follow-up.
- ctDNA: 8/9 evaluable had a molecular response; 4/9 cleared ctDNA.
- CRS in 77%, no grade 3/4 CRS; grade 3–4 TRAEs 38% (mostly transient lymphopenia); no discontinuations or deaths from TRAEs.
- Brenetafusp alone, second line or later (n=16): ORR 19%, median OS 20.3 months.

**Next:** randomised phase 2/3 DUET-UVEAL vs tebentafusp alone. As with tebentafusp, RECIST response understates benefit; OS and ctDNA are the more meaningful signals.

## 3. Cesalatamig: PD-1 × VEGF bispecific alone after immunotherapy (NSCLC)
**What:** cesalatamig (AI-081), a tetravalent, Fc-silenced PD-1 × VEGF bispecific, the same class as ivonescimab.

**Who:** NSCLC resistant or refractory to PD-(L)1 therapy; median 2 prior lines (42% had ≥3). Safety population n=103 (BiPAVE-001 US and China).

**Results (n=55 evaluable, investigator-assessed):**

| Dose (every 3 weeks) | n | ORR | DCR |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 mg/kg | 10 | 10% | 40% |
| 3 mg/kg | 24 | 21% | 67% |
| 10 mg/kg | 21 | 33% | 90% |

- Clear dose–response; responses across PD-L1 levels. 10 mg/kg chosen for a planned pivotal study. FDA Fast Track (Sep 2026).
- Caveat: investigator-assessed, unblinded, small dose cohorts.

**Context:** after IO and chemotherapy, docetaxel-based therapy still gives modest results. The PD-1 × VEGF class is also awaiting an FDA decision for ivonescimab in EGFR-mutant NSCLC on 14 Nov (see [our post-osimertinib note]({{ '/egfr-nsclc-after-osimertinib-2026-phase-3-options/' | relative_url }})).

## Bottom line
- Engineered TIL without IL-2, dual T-cell engagers and PD-1 × VEGF bispecifics are the three themes to follow into 2027.
- None should change treatment now; each needs randomised data.
