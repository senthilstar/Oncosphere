**Recurrent or metastatic (R/M) squamous cell carcinoma of the oral cavity, oropharynx, larynx and hypopharynx — first-line management**

## 1️⃣ Initial Assessment — Is Cure Still Possible?

- **Confirm:** squamous histology by biopsy of the recurrence or the most accessible metastasis; a new lung nodule may be a second primary (lung SCC), not a metastasis.
- **Not this pathway:** nasopharyngeal carcinoma, sinonasal tumours, salivary gland cancers and cutaneous SCC have separate evidence and regimens.
- **Stage the recurrence:** contrast CT or MRI of head and neck + chest CT, or PET-CT; endoscopy for the airway.
- 🔁 **Before labelling "palliative":** tumour-board review for salvage surgery, re-irradiation or SBRT for oligometastases → any curative option outranks first-line systemic therapy. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1016/j.annonc.2020.07.011">[Ann Oncol 2020]</a>
- **Treat first what kills first:**
  - 🩸 Bleeding from tumour or herald bleed → airway and vascular plan first (embolisation, radiotherapy, tracheostomy).
  - 🫁 Stridor → secure the airway before cycle 1.
  - 🍽️ Dysphagia, weight loss → feeding tube early; nutrition determines tolerance.
  - 😣 Pain → opioids and nerve-pain agents from day 1.
- → Goal: longer survival with preserved swallowing, speech and airway.

**🔑 Key insights**

- 1️⃣ 🎯 Every recurrence first goes to a multidisciplinary board.
- 2️⃣ ⚠️ Airway, bleeding and nutrition can kill before the cancer progresses.

## 2️⃣ Biomarkers + Eligibility — Four Checks Before Choosing a Regimen

- 🧬 **PD-L1 combined positive score (CPS), 22C3 assay:** the selection biomarker for pembrolizumab alone; FDA approval of single-agent pembrolizumab is restricted to CPS ≥1. <a class="cite" href="https://web.archive.org/web/20190928072631/https://www.fda.gov/drugs/resources-information-approved-drugs/fda-approves-pembrolizumab-first-line-treatment-head-and-neck-squamous-cell-carcinoma">[FDA 2019]</a> In KEYNOTE-048, 85% had CPS ≥1 and 43% CPS ≥20. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(19)32591-7">[Lancet 2019]</a>
- 🦠 **p16 / HPV (oropharynx):** prognostic, a KEYNOTE-048 stratification factor; HPV-associated oropharyngeal cancer is excluded from the amivantamab approval (part 1️⃣1️⃣). <a class="cite" href="https://www.fda.gov/drugs/cancer-approvals/fda-grants-accelerated-approval-amivantamab-and-hyaluronidase-lpuj-recurrent-or-metastatic-head-and">[FDA 2026]</a>
- ⏱️ **Time since platinum given with curative intent:** progression within 6 months of platinum = platinum-refractory → the CheckMate 141 / KEYNOTE-040 population, not a platinum rechallenge candidate. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1602252">[NEJM 2016]</a>
- 🛡️ **Immunotherapy and platinum fitness:** ECOG 0–1, creatinine clearance, hearing, neuropathy, autoimmune disease, prior cetuximab or immunotherapy.
- 🧪 **Baseline:** thyroid function (most HNSCC patients had neck radiotherapy), cortisol, glucose, magnesium (cetuximab), albumin.
- → No EGFR expression or copy-number test selects cetuximab. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1093/annonc/mdr367">[Ann Oncol 2012]</a>

**🔑 Key insights**

- 1️⃣ 🧬 Ask for CPS, not tumour proportion score — the decision is made on CPS.
- 2️⃣ ⏱️ Recurrence within 6 months of chemoradiation changes the whole sequence.

## 3️⃣ Preferred First Line — Choose by CPS and Tumour Tempo

| Situation | Preferred | Alternatives |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CPS ≥20, low burden, no urgent symptoms | Pembrolizumab alone | Pembrolizumab + platinum + 5-FU |
| CPS ≥1, bulky or symptomatic, needs shrinkage | Pembrolizumab + platinum + 5-FU | Pembrolizumab + carboplatin + paclitaxel |
| CPS 1–19, fit | Pembrolizumab + platinum + 5-FU | Pembrolizumab alone (lower benefit) |
| CPS <1 | Cetuximab + platinum (EXTREME or TPEx) | Pembrolizumab + platinum + 5-FU |
| Immunotherapy contraindicated | EXTREME or TPEx | Weekly paclitaxel + cetuximab |
| Full-dose immunotherapy not accessible (India/LMIC) | Low-dose nivolumab + oral metronomic chemotherapy | Paclitaxel–carboplatin + oral metronomic chemotherapy; methotrexate + celecoxib |

- → PD-1 benefit rises with CPS; no immunotherapy option has beaten EXTREME in CPS <1. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1200/JCO.21.02198">[JCO 2022]</a>

**🔑 Key insights**

- 1️⃣ 🧬 CPS decides "pembrolizumab alone or with chemotherapy".
- 2️⃣ 🏃 Tumour tempo decides whether chemotherapy is added.

## 4️⃣ Pembrolizumab Alone — CPS ≥1, Best at CPS ≥20

**📊 KEYNOTE-048: Pembrolizumab vs Cetuximab + Platinum + 5-FU (EXTREME) ✨**

- **Study design:** phase 3, open-label, 1:1:1; n=882 (301 pembrolizumab, 281 pembrolizumab–chemotherapy, 300 EXTREME); untreated R/M HNSCC; PD-L1 not required for entry. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(19)32591-7">[Lancet 2019]</a>
- 💊 Pembrolizumab 200 mg every 3 weeks for up to 24 months. <a class="cite" href="https://web.archive.org/web/20190928072631/https://www.fda.gov/drugs/resources-information-approved-drugs/fda-approves-pembrolizumab-first-line-treatment-head-and-neck-squamous-cell-carcinoma">[FDA 2019]</a>
- **Key outcomes (pembrolizumab alone vs EXTREME):**
  - 🫀 CPS ≥20: OS 14.9 vs 10.7 months (HR 0.61; 95% CI 0.45–0.83). <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(19)32591-7">[Lancet 2019]</a>
  - 🫀 CPS ≥1: OS 12.3 vs 10.3 months (HR 0.78; 95% CI 0.64–0.96). <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(19)32591-7">[Lancet 2019]</a>
  - 🫀 Total population: 11.6 vs 10.7 months (HR 0.85; 95% CI 0.71–1.03) → non-inferior only. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(19)32591-7">[Lancet 2019]</a>
  - ⏱️ PFS not improved; CPS ≥1 median PFS 3.2 vs 5.0 months. <a class="cite" href="https://www.merck.com/news/fda-approves-two-new-indications-for-mercks-keytruda-pembrolizumab/">[Keytruda label data 2019]</a>
  - 📈 4-year update: OS HR 0.61 (95% CI 0.46–0.81) at CPS ≥20 and 0.74 (0.61–0.89) at CPS ≥1. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1200/JCO.21.02508">[JCO 2023]</a>
- ⚠️ **CPS <1 (n=128):** OS 7.9 vs 11.3 months (HR 1.51; 95% CI 0.96–2.37) → avoid pembrolizumab alone. CPS 1–19: 10.8 vs 10.1 months (HR 0.86; 0.66–1.12). <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1200/JCO.21.02198">[JCO 2022]</a>
- ⚠️ Grade ≥3 all-cause AEs 55% vs 83%. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(19)32591-7">[Lancet 2019]</a>

**🔑 Key insights**

- 1️⃣ 🎯 Ideal: CPS ≥20, slow disease, no airway threat.
- 2️⃣ ⚠️ Median PFS is shorter than with EXTREME → not for rapidly progressive, symptomatic disease.
- 3️⃣ 📈 Benefit is a survival tail, not a better median PFS.

## 5️⃣ Pembrolizumab + Platinum + 5-FU — The All-Comer Combination

**📊 KEYNOTE-048: Pembrolizumab + Chemotherapy vs EXTREME ✨**

- 💊 Cisplatin 100 mg/m² or carboplatin AUC 5 day 1 + 5-FU 1,000 mg/m²/day days 1–4, every 3 weeks × up to 6 cycles (same backbone as EXTREME), with pembrolizumab 200 mg every 3 weeks. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa0802656">[NEJM 2008]</a>
- **Key outcomes:**
  - 🫀 Total population: OS 13.0 vs 10.7 months (HR 0.77; 95% CI 0.63–0.93). <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(19)32591-7">[Lancet 2019]</a>
  - 🫀 CPS ≥20: 14.7 vs 11.0 months (HR 0.60; 95% CI 0.45–0.82); CPS ≥1: 13.6 vs 10.4 months (HR 0.65; 95% CI 0.53–0.80). <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(19)32591-7">[Lancet 2019]</a>
  - 📈 4-year update: total-population OS HR 0.71 (95% CI 0.59–0.85). <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1200/JCO.21.02508">[JCO 2023]</a>
  - ⚠️ CPS <1: 11.3 vs 10.7 months (HR 1.21; 95% CI 0.76–1.94) → no clear gain; CPS 1–19: 12.7 vs 9.9 months (HR 0.71; 95% CI 0.54–0.94). <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1200/JCO.21.02198">[JCO 2022]</a>
- ⚠️ Grade ≥3 all-cause AEs 85% vs 83%; AE-related deaths 12% vs 10%. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(19)32591-7">[Lancet 2019]</a>

**📊 KEYNOTE-B10: Pembrolizumab + Carboplatin + Paclitaxel (single arm) ✨**

- n=101; carboplatin AUC 5 + paclitaxel 175 mg/m² day 1 or 100 mg/m² days 1 and 8, every 3 weeks × ≤6 cycles; ORR 49% (complete response 7%); grade 3–5 treatment-related AEs 75%. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1200/JCO.23.02625">[JCO 2024]</a>
- → Practical substitute when infusional 5-FU is not feasible (no pump, mucositis risk); phase 2 evidence only.

**🔑 Key insights**

- 1️⃣ ✅ Only pembrolizumab regimen with an OS gain in the total population.
- 2️⃣ 🏃 Choose it when the tumour is bulky, symptomatic or near the airway.

## 6️⃣ Cetuximab Regimens — CPS <1 or Immunotherapy Not Possible

**📊 EXTREME: Cetuximab + Platinum + 5-FU vs Platinum + 5-FU ✨**

- n=442; cetuximab 400 mg/m² loading, then 250 mg/m² weekly; maintenance cetuximab after 6 cycles. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa0802656">[NEJM 2008]</a>
- 🫀 OS 10.1 vs 7.4 months (HR 0.80; 95% CI 0.64–0.99); PFS 5.6 vs 3.3 months (HR 0.54); ORR 36% vs 20%. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa0802656">[NEJM 2008]</a>
- ⚠️ Grade 3 skin reactions 9%; grade 3–4 infusion reactions 3%; sepsis 9 vs 1 patients. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa0802656">[NEJM 2008]</a>

**📊 TPExtreme: Docetaxel + Cisplatin + Cetuximab (TPEx) vs EXTREME ✨**

- n=541; docetaxel 75 mg/m² + cisplatin 75 mg/m² + weekly cetuximab, 4 cycles every 21 days with G-CSF, then cetuximab 500 mg/m² every 2 weeks. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1016/S1470-2045(20)30755-5">[Lancet Oncol 2021]</a>
- 🫀 OS 14.5 vs 13.4 months (HR 0.89; 95% CI 0.74–1.08) → not superior. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1016/S1470-2045(20)30755-5">[Lancet Oncol 2021]</a>
- ⚠️ Grade ≥3 AEs 81% vs 93% → TPEx is the better-tolerated cetuximab backbone (4 cycles, no 5-FU infusion). <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1016/S1470-2045(20)30755-5">[Lancet Oncol 2021]</a>

**🔑 Key insights**

- 1️⃣ 🧬 CPS <1 → cetuximab–platinum remains the reference.
- 2️⃣ 💊 TPEx: same survival as EXTREME, fewer severe toxicities.

## 7️⃣ What Failed in First Line — Do Not Substitute Other Immunotherapy Doublets

**📊 CheckMate 651: Nivolumab + Ipilimumab vs EXTREME ✨**

- n=947; OS all randomised 13.9 vs 13.5 months (HR 0.95; 97.9% CI 0.80–1.13); CPS ≥20 17.6 vs 14.6 months (HR 0.78; 97.51% CI 0.59–1.03) → both primary endpoints missed. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1200/JCO.22.00332">[JCO 2023]</a>
- ⚠️ Grade 3–4 treatment-related AEs 28.2% vs 70.7%; median duration of response at CPS ≥20 32.6 vs 7.0 months. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1200/JCO.22.00332">[JCO 2023]</a>

**📊 KESTREL: Durvalumab ± Tremelimumab vs EXTREME ✨**

- PD-L1-high OS 10.9 (durvalumab) vs 10.9 months (HR 0.96; 95% CI 0.69–1.32); durvalumab–tremelimumab HR 1.05 (95% CI 0.80–1.39) → not superior. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1016/j.annonc.2022.12.008">[Ann Oncol 2023]</a>
- ⏱️ Median PFS 2.8 vs 5.4 months; grade 3–4 TRAEs 8.9% / 19.1% vs 53.1%. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1016/j.annonc.2022.12.008">[Ann Oncol 2023]</a>

**🔑 Key insights**

- 1️⃣ ❌ Dual checkpoint blockade has no first-line role in HNSCC.
- 2️⃣ 📌 Among full-dose checkpoint inhibitors, only pembrolizumab (KEYNOTE-048) has beaten EXTREME on OS in first line.

## 8️⃣ Unfit, Platinum-Ineligible or No Access — Indian Randomised Evidence

**📊 Low-dose nivolumab + triple oral metronomic chemotherapy (TMC) vs TMC ✨**

- n=151; palliative R/M HNSCC, ECOG 0–1; TMC = methotrexate 9 mg/m² weekly + celecoxib 200 mg twice daily + erlotinib 150 mg daily; nivolumab 20 mg flat every 3 weeks. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1200/JCO.22.01015">[JCO 2023]</a>
- 🫀 1-year OS 43.4% vs 16.3% (HR 0.545; 95% CI 0.362–0.820); median OS 10.1 vs 6.7 months; grade ≥3 AEs 46.1% vs 50%. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1200/JCO.22.01015">[JCO 2023]</a>

**📊 Paclitaxel–carboplatin ± triple metronomic therapy (METRO PLUS) ✨**

- n=238; platinum-sensitive, unresectable HNSCC; adding erlotinib, celecoxib and methotrexate: OS 10 vs 5 months (HR 0.54; 95% CI 0.41–0.72); PFS 6 vs 2 months (HR 0.38; 95% CI 0.28–0.50); no rise in grade ≥3 toxicity. <a class="cite" href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42308452/">[JCO Glob Oncol 2026]</a>
- ⚠️ Single-centre trial with a weak control arm (5-month median) → confirm before replacing EXTREME-type regimens.

**📊 Methotrexate + celecoxib vs IV cisplatin ✨**

- n=422; methotrexate 15 mg/m² weekly + celecoxib 200 mg twice daily vs cisplatin 75 mg/m² every 3 weeks; OS 7.5 vs 6.1 months (HR 0.773; 95% CI 0.615–0.97); grade ≥3 AEs 19% vs 30%. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1016/S2214-109X(20)30275-8">[Lancet Glob Health 2020]</a>

**📊 Weekly paclitaxel + cetuximab (phase 2, platinum-unfit) ✨**

- n=46; paclitaxel 80 mg/m² + cetuximab weekly; ORR 54% (complete response 22%); PFS 4.2, OS 8.1 months; grade 3–4 acneiform rash 24%. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1093/annonc/mdr367">[Ann Oncol 2012]</a>

**🔑 Key insights**

- 1️⃣ 💰 Low-dose nivolumab + metronomic therapy is a randomised, low-cost alternative when full-dose pembrolizumab is unaffordable.
- 2️⃣ 💊 Oral metronomic chemotherapy is non-inferior to cisplatin and gentler.
- 3️⃣ 🧓 ECOG 3–4 → best supportive care; chemotherapy rarely helps.

## 9️⃣ Emerging First-Line Options — Bispecific EGFR Antibodies

**📊 LiGeR-HN1: Petosemtamab + Pembrolizumab vs Pembrolizumab (CPS ≥1) ✨**

- Phase 3, open-label, 1:1; first interim analysis: OS HR 0.50 (95% CI 0.37–0.67); ORR odds ratio 3.17 (95% CI 2.06–4.89); benefit regardless of HPV status; no new safety signals. <a class="cite" href="https://ir.genmab.com/news-releases/news-release-details/genmab-announces-statistically-significant-clinically-meaningful/">[Genmab 2026]</a>
- 📌 Topline press release only (28 Oct 2026); medians and toxicity not yet published; not approved anywhere as of 27 Nov 2026. <a class="cite" href="https://ir.genmab.com/news-releases/news-release-details/genmab-announces-statistically-significant-clinically-meaningful/">[Genmab 2026]</a>
- 🔬 Amivantamab + carboplatin + pembrolizumab is in a first-line phase 3 (OrigAMI-5). <a class="cite" href="https://www.fda.gov/drugs/cancer-approvals/fda-grants-accelerated-approval-amivantamab-and-hyaluronidase-lpuj-recurrent-or-metastatic-head-and">[FDA 2026]</a>

**🔑 Key insights**

- 1️⃣ 🔭 EGFR bispecific + PD-1 is likely to become the next CPS ≥1 standard → watch full data and approvals.
- 2️⃣ 🧪 Offer trial enrolment where available.

## 🔟 Toxicity Decisions — Airway, Skin, Thyroid and Mucosa

- 🦋 **Hypothyroidism:** most common pembrolizumab adverse event after platinum (13%) and frequent after neck radiotherapy → TSH every 6 weeks. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(18)31999-8">[Lancet 2019]</a>
- 🧴 **Cetuximab rash:** acneiform rash correlated with response, not survival; doxycycline prophylaxis, emollients, sun protection. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1093/annonc/mdr367">[Ann Oncol 2012]</a>
- 💉 **Infusion reactions:** premedicate cetuximab; grade 3–4 in 3% in EXTREME. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa0802656">[NEJM 2008]</a>
- 👄 **5-FU mucositis + dysphagia:** feeding tube, dose reduction, or switch to TPEx or carboplatin–paclitaxel.
- 🧂 **Hypomagnesaemia (cetuximab, cisplatin):** check magnesium every cycle.
- 🫁 **Pneumonitis vs aspiration:** common aspiration in post-treatment HNSCC → swallow assessment before labelling immune pneumonitis.
- 🩸 **Pseudoprogression with oedema around the airway:** a rapidly enlarging neck mass needs airway review before "continuing through".

**🔑 Key insights**

- 1️⃣ ⚠️ The airway and swallowing are monitored at every visit.
- 2️⃣ 🦋 Thyroid failure is expected, not exceptional.

## 1️⃣1️⃣ Progression — Sequence by What Was Given First

- 🔁 **After pembrolizumab alone:** platinum-based chemotherapy ± cetuximab; KEYNOTE-048 next-line taxane outcomes were maintained after pembrolizumab. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1200/JCO.21.02508">[JCO 2023]</a>
- 🔁 **After pembrolizumab + chemotherapy:** cetuximab ± taxane, methotrexate, docetaxel or afatinib; amivantamab SC if HPV-unrelated and available.
- 🔁 **After EXTREME/TPEx (immunotherapy-naive):** nivolumab or pembrolizumab.

**📊 Second-Line Trials ✨**

- 💊 Nivolumab (CheckMate 141, platinum-refractory): OS 7.5 vs 5.1 months (HR 0.70; 97.73% CI 0.51–0.96); ORR 13.3% vs 5.8%; grade 3–4 TRAEs 13.1% vs 35.1%. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1602252">[NEJM 2016]</a> 2-year OS 16.9% vs 6.0% (HR 0.68; 95% CI 0.54–0.86). <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1016/j.oraloncology.2018.04.008">[Oral Oncol 2018]</a>
- 💊 Pembrolizumab (KEYNOTE-040): OS 8.4 vs 6.9 months (HR 0.80; 95% CI 0.65–0.98); grade ≥3 TRAEs 13% vs 36%. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(18)31999-8">[Lancet 2019]</a>
- 💊 Amivantamab SC (OrigAMI-4, after platinum + anti-PD-(L)1, HPV-unrelated): n=102; ORR 42% (95% CI 32–52%); 56% of responses ≥6 months; FDA accelerated approval 5 Nov 2026. <a class="cite" href="https://www.fda.gov/drugs/cancer-approvals/fda-grants-accelerated-approval-amivantamab-and-hyaluronidase-lpuj-recurrent-or-metastatic-head-and">[FDA 2026]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1200/JCO-26-01042">[JCO 2026]</a>
- 💊 Afatinib (LUX-H&N1) vs methotrexate: PFS 2.6 vs 1.7 months (HR 0.80; 95% CI 0.65–0.98). <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1016/S1470-2045(15)70124-5">[Lancet Oncol 2015]</a>
- 💰 Ultra-low-dose nivolumab 20 mg every 2 weeks vs docetaxel/paclitaxel (DELII, 52% head and neck): OS 5.88 vs 4.70 months (HR 0.80; 95% CI 0.66–0.97); grade ≥3 TRAEs 42.5% vs 60.8%. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1200/JCO-25-01546">[JCO 2026]</a>
- 💊 Triple metronomic therapy after platinum vs physician's choice: OS 5 vs 3.1 months (HR 0.63; 95% CI 0.47–0.83). <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1200/GO-25-00032">[JCO Glob Oncol 2025]</a>
- ❌ No OS gain: durvalumab ± tremelimumab (EAGLE, HR 0.88 and 1.04), <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1016/j.annonc.2020.04.001">[Ann Oncol 2020]</a> monalizumab + cetuximab (INTERLINK-1, HR 1.00), <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1158/1078-0432.CCR-25-0073">[Clin Cancer Res 2025]</a> lenvatinib + pembrolizumab (LEAP-009, HR 1.48). <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ejca.2026.116824">[Eur J Cancer 2026]</a>

**🔑 Key insights**

- 1️⃣ 🔁 Immunotherapy once, early — rechallenge has minimal data.
- 2️⃣ 🎯 Post-immunotherapy, HPV-unrelated → amivantamab is the first new approved option in a decade (US).

## 1️⃣2️⃣ Response Assessment, India Access and the First-Line Rulebook

- 📷 CT/MRI head–neck + chest every 9–12 weeks; clinical review of airway, bleeding and swallowing at every cycle.
- ⏱️ Pembrolizumab up to 24 months; cetuximab maintenance until progression. <a class="cite" href="https://web.archive.org/web/20190928072631/https://www.fda.gov/drugs/resources-information-approved-drugs/fda-approves-pembrolizumab-first-line-treatment-head-and-neck-squamous-cell-carcinoma">[FDA 2019]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa0802656">[NEJM 2008]</a>
- 📈 Long-term survivors exist with pembrolizumab: Japanese KEYNOTE-048 cohort 5-year OS 35.7% vs 12.5% at CPS ≥20 (small subgroup, n=67 overall). <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1007/s10147-024-02632-x">[Int J Clin Oncol 2024]</a>
- 🤝 Speech–swallow therapy, dental care, nutrition and tracheostomy care alongside oncology.
- 🕊️ Early palliative care: pain, bleeding plan, fungating wounds, end-of-life airway decisions.
- 🇮🇳 Check current CDSCO indications and supply for pembrolizumab, nivolumab, cetuximab and amivantamab; an FDA approval does not establish Indian availability. Pan-Asian guidance adapts ESMO recommendations to regional resources. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1016/j.esmoop.2021.100309">[ESMO Open 2021]</a>

**🔑 Key insights — first-line rulebook**

- 1️⃣ 🔪 Salvage surgery or re-irradiation first, if curative.
- 2️⃣ 🧬 Test CPS (22C3); CPS ≥20 + slow disease → pembrolizumab alone.
- 3️⃣ 🏃 Bulky or symptomatic CPS ≥1 → pembrolizumab + platinum + 5-FU (or carboplatin–paclitaxel).
- 4️⃣ ⚠️ CPS <1 → cetuximab–platinum (EXTREME or TPEx).
- 5️⃣ 💰 No access → low-dose nivolumab + metronomic therapy; metronomic methotrexate–celecoxib for platinum-unfit.
- 6️⃣ 🔁 Second line: immunotherapy if not used; after immunotherapy → cetuximab-based, taxane, afatinib or amivantamab (HPV-unrelated).
- 7️⃣ 🔭 Watch EGFR bispecifics (petosemtamab, amivantamab) for first line.
