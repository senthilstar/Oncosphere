**Metastatic urothelial carcinoma — first-line management**

## 1️⃣ Initial Assessment — Identify Decisions That Change Treatment

- Confirm the dominant urothelial component; document squamous/glandular differentiation and micropapillary, plasmacytoid, sarcomatoid or neuroendocrine features. Pure squamous, adenocarcinoma and small-cell cancers need separate pathways.
- Nonregional nodes → M1a; visceral/bone spread → M1b. T4b M0 is locally advanced disease → assess separately for possible consolidation.
- **Record:**
  - ECOG PS, frailty, nutrition and liver, lung, bone and nodal burden.
  - Pain, haematuria, obstruction, urinary infection and bleeding severity.
  - Perioperative platinum, immunotherapy or EV; dates, response and persistent toxicity.
- CT chest/abdomen/pelvis; brain MRI for neurological symptoms and brain staging for small-cell disease. Skeletal imaging directed by symptoms or suspicious lesions.
- Baseline CBC, creatinine/CrCl, electrolytes including magnesium, liver tests, albumin, glucose/HbA1c and thyroid function. Examine neuropathy; assess hearing and cardiac function before cisplatin.
- Obstructive AKI → stent/nephrostomy when indicated → repeat renal assessment. Treat infection, dehydration and uncontrolled haemorrhage before systemic therapy.
- Distinguish potentially reversible cancer-related poor PS from fixed frailty. Send molecular tests early; urgent symptom-threatening disease need not await complete NGS results.

**🔑 Key insights**

- 1️⃣ Confirm histology and relieve reversible obstruction → reassess performance and renal function.
- 2️⃣ 💰 Match the pathway and monitoring to locally sustainable access.

## 2️⃣ Fitness + Biomarkers — Assess Cisplatin and EV Separately

**Cisplatin ineligibility — consensus criteria**

- Any one criterion supports ineligibility: ECOG PS 2; CrCl <60 mL/min; grade ≥2 hearing loss; grade ≥2 peripheral neuropathy; NYHA class III heart failure. <a class="cite" href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21555688/">[J Clin Oncol 2011]</a>
- Borderline estimated clearance → consider measured clearance/GFR before excluding cisplatin. Assess carboplatin using marrow/renal reserve, PS and comorbidity; GFR <30 mL/min, ECOG PS >2 or PS 2 with GFR <60 mL/min generally precludes platinum → reassess after reversible problems resolve. <a class="cite" href="https://uroweb.org/guidelines/muscle-invasive-and-metastatic-bladder-cancer/chapter/metastatic-disease">[EAU guideline]</a>

**EV + checkpoint-inhibitor suitability**

- Cisplatin ineligibility alone does not exclude EV–pembrolizumab. Trial eligibility broadly included ECOG 0–2, GFR ≥30 mL/min and adequate organ function. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2312117">[NEJM 2024]</a>
- Baseline neuropathy, uncontrolled diabetes and serious dermatological disease can limit EV; transplant, active autoimmune disease and previous severe immune toxicity require individual ICI assessment.
- 🧬 Susceptible FGFR3 mutations/fusions → erdafitinib pathway; HER2 IHC → evaluate ADC options. Broad NGS and clinically relevant MSI/MMR testing may find additional targets.
- FGFR protein staining does not establish an actionable mutation/fusion. Routine Nectin-4 testing is unnecessary before EV.
- PD-L1 testing is unnecessary for EV–pembrolizumab, GC–nivolumab or avelumab maintenance; it may determine eligibility for first-line ICI monotherapy under a regional label.

**🔑 Key insights**

- 1️⃣ Separate cisplatin eligibility from EV and immunotherapy suitability; order actionable tests early.
- 2️⃣ 💰 Match the pathway and monitoring to locally sustainable access.

## 3️⃣ Preferred First Line — Use EV + Pembrolizumab When Suitable

**💊 Regimen**

- EV 1.25 mg/kg IV, maximum 125 mg/dose, days 1 and 8 every 21 days; pembrolizumab 200 mg IV every 3 weeks or 400 mg every 6 weeks. <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/761137s037lbl.pdf">[FDA 2026]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/125514s191lbl.pdf">[FDA 2026]</a>
- Pembrolizumab was capped at 35 three-weekly cycles; EV continued until progression/unacceptable toxicity without the same fixed cap. Selection does not require PD-L1 positivity or cisplatin eligibility. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2312117">[NEJM 2024]</a>

**📊 EV-302 / KEYNOTE-A39: EV + Pembrolizumab ✨💉**

- **Study design:** phase 3, open-label; n=886; untreated locally advanced/metastatic urothelial cancer; comparator gemcitabine–cisplatin/carboplatin. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2312117">[NEJM 2024]</a>
- **Primary analysis:**
  - 📈 OS 31.5 vs 16.1 months (HR 0.47; 95% CI 0.38–0.58). <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2312117">[NEJM 2024]</a>
  - ⏱️ PFS 12.5 vs 6.3 months (HR 0.45; 95% CI 0.38–0.54). <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2312117">[NEJM 2024]</a>
  - 🎯 ORR 67.7% vs 44.4%; CR 29.1% vs 12.5%. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2312117">[NEJM 2024]</a>
- 🫀 **2026 conference follow-up:** OS 33.6 vs 15.9 months (HR 0.53; 95% CI 0.45–0.63; updated: 33.6 supersedes earlier 33.8 months). <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1200/JCO.2026.44.16_suppl.4507">[J Clin Oncol 2026 abstract]</a>
- ⏱️ Published follow-up PFS 12.5 vs 6.3 months (HR 0.48; 95% CI 0.41–0.57). <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1016/j.annonc.2025.05.536">[Ann Oncol 2025]</a>
- ⚠️ Primary-analysis grade ≥3 treatment-related AEs 55.9% vs 69.5%. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2312117">[N Engl J Med 2024]</a>; <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1016/j.annonc.2025.05.536">[Ann Oncol 2025]</a>

**Toxicity-driven adaptation**

- Check skin, glucose and neuropathy before each EV administration. Glucose >250 mg/dL → withhold until ≤250 mg/dL. <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/761137s037lbl.pdf">[FDA 2026]</a>
- Grade 2 neuropathy → hold until ≤grade 1; recurrent grade 2 → resume at a reduced dose. Grade ≥3 → permanently stop EV. EV dose levels step down from 1.25 to 1.0, 0.75 and 0.5 mg/kg under prescribing guidance. <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/761137s037lbl.pdf">[FDA 2026]</a>
- Suspected SJS/TEN → immediately withhold and assess urgently; confirmed SJS/TEN → permanent discontinuation.
- New dyspnoea/hypoxia → investigate infection, embolism, progression and pneumonitis promptly.
- Stopping EV for toxicity may allow pembrolizumab continuation after recovery and review; a planned elective early EV stop has limited supporting evidence.

**🔑 Key insights**

- 1️⃣ EV–pembrolizumab is preferred when deliverable; monitor skin, glucose and nerves.
- 2️⃣ 💰 Match the pathway and monitoring to locally sustainable access.

## 4️⃣ When EV Is Unsuitable or Unavailable — Choose a Coherent Platinum Pathway

**📊 CheckMate 901: GC + Nivolumab**

- **Study design:** phase 3, open-label; n=608 in the cisplatin-eligible comparison; nivolumab + gemcitabine–cisplatin versus GC alone in untreated advanced disease. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2309863">[NEJM 2023]</a>
- Gemcitabine 1,000 mg/m² IV days 1, 8 + cisplatin 70 mg/m² IV day 1 + nivolumab 360 mg IV day 1, every 21 days, up to 6 cycles. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2309863">[NEJM 2023]</a>
- Then nivolumab 240 mg IV every 2 weeks or 480 mg every 4 weeks, until progression/toxicity, maximum 2 years from the first nivolumab dose. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2309863">[NEJM 2023]</a>
- 📈 OS 21.7 vs 18.9 months (HR 0.78; 95% CI 0.63–0.96); PFS 7.9 vs 7.6 months (HR 0.72; 95% CI 0.59–0.88). <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2309863">[NEJM 2023]</a>
- 🎯 ORR 57.6% vs 43.1%. <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/125554s135lbl.pdf">[FDA 2026]</a>
- ⚠️ Grade ≥3 treatment-related AEs 61.8% vs 51.7%. A small median PFS difference can coexist with separation elsewhere in the survival curves. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2309863">[N Engl J Med 2023]</a>
- The randomised backbone was cisplatin–gemcitabine; planning carboplatin from the outset does not reproduce this evidence.

**Platinum → avelumab, or chemotherapy alone**

- Cisplatin-fit → GC for 4–6 cycles → avelumab for CR/PR/SD. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2002788">[NEJM 2020]</a>
- Cisplatin-unfit but carboplatin-fit → gemcitabine 1,000 mg/m² IV days 1, 8 + carboplatin AUC 4.5 IV day 1 every 21 days → avelumab for nonprogressing disease. Calculate dose by renal function; adjust for marrow reserve and institutional protocol. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1200/JCO.2011.37.3571">[J Clin Oncol 2012]</a>
- ICI unsuitable → platinum–gemcitabine alone. Generic GC is a deliverable resource-limited option.
- Cisplatin requires hydration, magnesium replacement and antiemetic prophylaxis. Check CBC before day 8 gemcitabine; delay/omit per protocol for cytopenias. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2309863">[NEJM 2023]</a>
- Nivolumab continuation and avelumab switch maintenance are separate strategies; routine switching between them lacks evidence.

**🔑 Key insights**

- 1️⃣ Choose cisplatin–nivolumab or platinum followed by maintenance as distinct pathways.
- 2️⃣ 💰 Match the pathway and monitoring to locally sustainable access.

## 5️⃣ Maintenance + Combination-Unfit Disease — Select the Correct Population

**📊 JAVELIN Bladder 100: Avelumab Switch Maintenance**

- **Study design:** phase 3; n=700; no progression after 4–6 first-line platinum–gemcitabine cycles; avelumab + supportive care versus supportive care alone. CR, PR and SD qualified, including after carboplatin. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1200/JCO.22.01792">[J Clin Oncol 2023]</a>
- Trial start: 4–10 weeks after the final chemotherapy dose. Trial dosing was 10 mg/kg IV every 2 weeks; current fixed dosing is 800 mg IV every 2 weeks until progression/toxicity, with antihistamine/paracetamol premedication for the first 4 infusions and subsequently as needed. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1200/JCO.22.01792">[J Clin Oncol 2023]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/761049s026lbl.pdf">[FDA 2026]</a>
- Initial OS 21.4 vs 14.3 months (HR 0.69); updated OS 23.8 vs 15.0 months (HR 0.76; 95% CI 0.63–0.91). <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1200/JCO.22.01792">[J Clin Oncol 2023]</a>
- ⚠️ Updated grade ≥3 treatment-related AEs with avelumab: 19.5%. PD-L1 positivity is not required. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1200/JCO.22.01792">[J Clin Oncol 2023]</a>
- The survival clock begins at maintenance randomisation after chemotherapy → do not compare these medians directly with upfront EV-302 medians. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1200/JCO.22.01792">[J Clin Oncol 2023]</a>
- Arrange funding and the transition appointment during induction. Progression during chemotherapy → change treatment; maintenance eligibility has been lost.

**📊 KEYNOTE-052: Pembrolizumab Alone for Selected Unfit Patients**

- **Study design:** single-arm phase 2; n=370; cisplatin-ineligible advanced disease; pembrolizumab 200 mg IV every 3 weeks, up to 24 months. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1200/JCO.19.01213">[J Clin Oncol 2020]</a>
- Long-term analysis: ORR 28.6%; CR 8.9%; median OS 11.3 months; response duration among responders 30.1 months. Durable responses occur, but most patients do not respond. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1200/JCO.19.01213">[J Clin Oncol 2020]</a>
- Use only under the applicable label: European eligibility includes cisplatin-ineligible CPS ≥10 disease; US first-line eligibility requires unsuitability for any platinum, regardless of PD-L1. Verify current regional prescribing guidance. <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/125514s191lbl.pdf">[FDA 2026]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/product-information/keytruda-epar-product-information_en.pdf">[EMA 2026]</a>
- Organ-threatening progression → weigh response probability and speed carefully. Severe irreversible frailty → prioritise symptom control.

**🔑 Key insights**

- 1️⃣ Start maintenance only without progression after initial platinum; plan the transition early.
- 2️⃣ 💰 Match the pathway and monitoring to locally sustainable access.

## 6️⃣ Progression — Sequence by Prior Drug Exposure

- **After EV–pembrolizumab:** platinum-naïve and fit → GC or gemcitabine–carboplatin; susceptible FGFR3 alteration → consider erdafitinib; subsequent biomarker-directed treatment, taxane or trial.
- Prospective evidence for following EV–pembrolizumab platinum is limited. Clinical inference: routine avelumab after this second-line platinum course is unsupported by JAVELIN's first-line maintenance design.
- **After platinum + avelumab/nivolumab:** EV if unused; erdafitinib for eligible FGFR3 disease; selected HER2-directed therapy; clinical trial.

**📊 EV-301: EV After Platinum + ICI**

- **Study design:** phase 3; n=608; EV versus docetaxel, paclitaxel or vinflunine after platinum and PD-(L)1 treatment. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2035807">[NEJM 2021]</a>
- EV monotherapy: 1.25 mg/kg IV, maximum 125 mg, days 1, 8, 15 every 28 days until progression/toxicity. This differs from the combination's days 1, 8 every 21 days. <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/761137s037lbl.pdf">[FDA 2026]</a>
- 📈 OS 12.88 vs 8.97 months (HR 0.70; 95% CI 0.56–0.89); PFS 5.55 vs 3.71 months (HR 0.62; 95% CI 0.51–0.75). <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2035807">[NEJM 2021]</a>
- 🎯 Investigator-assessed ORR 40.6% vs 17.9%. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2035807">[NEJM 2021]</a>
- ⚠️ Grade ≥3 treatment-related AEs 51.4% vs 49.8%. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2035807">[N Engl J Med 2021]</a>

**📊 KEYNOTE-045: Pembrolizumab After Platinum, If ICI-Naïve**

- **Study design:** phase 3; n=542; pembrolizumab 200 mg IV every 3 weeks versus paclitaxel/docetaxel/vinflunine. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1613683">[NEJM 2017]</a>
- 📈 OS 10.3 vs 7.4 months (HR 0.73; 95% CI 0.59–0.91); ORR 21.1% vs 11.4%; PFS 2.1 vs 3.3 months (HR 0.98; 95% CI 0.81–1.19). <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1613683">[N Engl J Med 2017]</a>

- ⚠️ Grade ≥3 treatment-related AEs 15.0% vs 49.4%. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1613683">[NEJM 2017]</a>

**📊 TROPiCS-04 — Remove an Unsupported Routine Step**

- Phase 3; n=711; sacituzumab govitecan versus chemotherapy after platinum/ICI. OS 10.3 vs 9.0 months (HR 0.86; 95% CI 0.73–1.02; P=0.087) → primary endpoint not met. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1016/j.annonc.2025.01.011">[Ann Oncol 2025]</a>
- The US urothelial indication was withdrawn on 22 November 2024; do not list it as a routine approved bladder-cancer step. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1016/j.annonc.2025.01.011">[Ann Oncol 2025]</a>
- Prior perioperative EV/ICI and progression on a class weaken the rationale for immediate recycling; record exposure at every transition.

**🔑 Key insights**

- 1️⃣ Prior drug exposure and residual toxicity determine the next line; a trial remains valuable.
- 2️⃣ 💰 Match the pathway and monitoring to locally sustainable access.

## 7️⃣ FGFR3-Altered Disease — Select and Monitor Erdafitinib

**📊 THOR Cohort 1: Erdafitinib versus Chemotherapy 🧬**

- **Study design:** phase 3; n=266; selected FGFR-altered advanced disease after systemic treatment including PD-(L)1 therapy; comparator docetaxel/vinflunine. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2308849">[NEJM 2023]</a>
- 📈 OS 12.1 vs 7.8 months (HR 0.64; 95% CI 0.47–0.88); PFS 5.6 vs 2.7 months (HR 0.58; 95% CI 0.44–0.78). <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2308849">[NEJM 2023]</a>
- FDA-confirmed ORR 35.3% vs 8.5%; distinguish this regulatory assessment from investigator-assessed response estimates. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1158/1078-0432.CCR-26-1154">[Clin Cancer Res 2026]</a> Grade 3–4 treatment-related AEs 45.9% vs 46.4%. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2308849">[N Engl J Med 2023]</a>
- Select a susceptible FGFR3 mutation/fusion with a validated assay; the current US indication specifies FGFR3, so older FGFR2/3 descriptions should not automatically determine eligibility. <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2025/212018s011lbl.pdf">[FDA 2025]</a>
- Start 8 mg orally daily; check phosphate/tolerability on days 14–21 → increase to 9 mg only when prescribing criteria are satisfied. Monitor phosphate and use dietary changes, binders or dose holds as indicated. <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2025/212018s011lbl.pdf">[FDA 2025]</a>
- Watch stomatitis, diarrhoea, nails, hand–foot toxicity and vision. Ophthalmic examinations monthly for 4 months, then every 3 months and urgently for visual symptoms; suspected central serous retinopathy/retinal pigment epithelial detachment → hold and obtain review. <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2025/212018s011lbl.pdf">[FDA 2025]</a>
- US guidance discourages use before PD-(L)1 therapy when ICI is suitable. FGFR3 alteration does not prove individual ICI resistance. <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2025/212018s011lbl.pdf">[FDA 2025]</a>
- No randomised EV-versus-erdafitinib comparison; neuropathy, retinal disease, oral adherence and access guide selection. THOR largely predates routine EV–pembrolizumab, limiting certainty after that sequence.

**🔑 Key insights**

- 1️⃣ A susceptible FGFR3 alteration and eye monitoring are essential to erdafitinib selection.
- 2️⃣ 💰 Match the pathway and monitoring to locally sustainable access.

## 8️⃣ HER2 Expression — Distinguish Three Treatment Settings

**📊 DESTINY-PanTumor02: Trastuzumab Deruxtecan (T-DXd)**

- **Study design:** single-arm phase 2 basket study; bladder cohort n=41; previously treated HER2-expressing disease; T-DXd 5.4 mg/kg IV every 3 weeks. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1200/JCO.23.02005">[J Clin Oncol 2024]</a>
- Primary bladder-cohort ORR 39.0%; central IHC 3+ ORR 56.3%, IHC 2+ ORR 35.0%. These are cohort response data, not a demonstrated randomised bladder-specific survival benefit. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1200/JCO.23.02005">[J Clin Oncol 2024]</a>
- The 2026 final-analysis conference abstract reported investigator-assessed bladder ORR 41.5% (17/41), median PFS 7.0 months and OS 12.8 months; central IHC 2+ ORR 40.0%. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1200/JCO.2026.44.7_suppl.734">[J Clin Oncol 2026 abstract]</a>
- US tumour-agnostic eligibility: unresectable/metastatic IHC 3+ solid tumour after prior systemic therapy with no satisfactory alternatives; IHC 2+ alone does not qualify. <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/761139s041s043lbl.pdf">[FDA 2026]</a>
- New cough/dyspnoea → urgent ILD evaluation; grade ≥2 drug-related ILD → permanently discontinue according to prescribing guidance. <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/761139s041s043lbl.pdf">[FDA 2026]</a>

**📊 RC48-C005/C009: Disitamab Vedotin After Treatment**

- **Study design:** pooled single-arm phase 2 studies; n=107; HER2 IHC 2+/3+ previously treated urothelial cancer; disitamab vedotin 2 mg/kg IV every 2 weeks. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1200/JCO.22.02912">[J Clin Oncol 2024]</a>
- 🎯 ORR 50.5%; median PFS 5.9 months; OS 14.2 months. Regional access/indication needs checking. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1200/JCO.22.02912">[J Clin Oncol 2024]</a>

**📊 RC48-C016: Disitamab Vedotin + Toripalimab First Line**

- **Study design:** phase 3; n=484; HER2 IHC 1+/2+/3+ untreated advanced disease; predominantly Chinese recruitment; comparator gemcitabine–cisplatin/carboplatin. Disitamab vedotin 2 mg/kg IV + toripalimab 3 mg/kg IV every 2 weeks until progression/toxicity. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2511648">[NEJM 2025]</a>
- ⏱️ PFS 13.1 vs 6.5 months (HR 0.36; 95% CI 0.28–0.46); interim OS 31.5 vs 16.9 months (HR 0.54; 95% CI 0.41–0.73); ORR 76.1% vs 50.2%. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2511648">[NEJM 2025]</a>
- ⚠️ Grade ≥3 treatment-related AEs 55.1% vs 86.9%. No direct EV–pembrolizumab comparison. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2511648">[N Engl J Med 2025]</a>
- Disitamab and EV both carry MMAE → prior neuropathy may limit either; HER2 targeting after EV is plausible, but the optimal ADC sequence remains uncertain. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2511648">[NEJM 2025]</a>

**🔑 Key insights**

- 1️⃣ HER2 expression thresholds and prior treatment define different treatment contexts.
- 2️⃣ 💰 Match the pathway and monitoring to locally sustainable access.

## 9️⃣ Historical Chemotherapy — Reserve Six Approaches for Selected Patients

- Historical response estimates do not establish activity after EV–ICI; match evidence level to the present clinical setting.
- **ddMVAC / HD-MVAC:** methotrexate, vinblastine, doxorubicin and cisplatin every 14 days with G-CSF; intensive cisplatin treatment for fit patients with reliable supportive care. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ejca.2005.08.032">[Eur J Cancer 2006]</a>
  - **📊 EORTC 30924:** randomised phase 3 versus classic MVAC; CR 21% vs 9%; long-term 5-year survival 21.8% vs 13.5%. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ejca.2005.08.032">[Eur J Cancer 2006]</a>
- **Split-dose GC:** cisplatin 35 mg/m² IV days 1 and 8 with gemcitabine on a 21-day cycle is an adapted schedule. Consider borderline renal function only with adequate overall fitness; small studies do not prove equivalent efficacy or renal safety. <a class="cite" href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15292922/">[Br J Cancer 2004]</a>
- **PCG:** paclitaxel–cisplatin–gemcitabine.
  - **📊 EORTC 30987:** phase 3 versus GC; ORR 55.5% vs 43.6%; OS 15.8 vs 12.7 months (P=0.075) → response advantage without meeting the primary OS endpoint. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1200/JCO.2011.38.6979">[J Clin Oncol 2012]</a>
- **M-CAVI:** methotrexate–carboplatin–vinblastine.
  - **📊 EORTC 30986:** randomised cisplatin-unfit population; OS 8.1 vs 9.3 months versus gemcitabine–carboplatin; severe acute toxicity 21.2% vs 9.3% → generally favour the more practical gemcitabine–carboplatin backbone. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1200/JCO.2011.37.3571">[J Clin Oncol 2012]</a>
- **GEMOX:** gemcitabine–oxaliplatin.
  - **📊 COACH:** randomised phase 2 versus gemcitabine–carboplatin; ORR 55.0% vs 48.7%; PFS 4.4 vs 5.5 months; OS 11.0 vs 9.1 months. Less severe marrow toxicity, but any-grade neuropathy 60% vs 8% → problematic after neurotoxic ADCs. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ejca.2019.08.034">[Eur J Cancer 2019]</a>
- **Platinum rechallenge:** consider a prior responder with a meaningful interval and recovered organ reserve; avoid immediate reuse after progression on platinum.
  - **📊 RISC retrospective cohort:** disease control 57.4% vs 44.8% and OS 7.9 vs 5.5 months versus non-platinum chemotherapy; substantial selection bias precludes a causal claim. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1002/onco.13925">[Oncologist 2021]</a>

**🔑 Key insights**

- 1️⃣ Older chemotherapy evidence has limited applicability after modern ADC exposure.
- 2️⃣ 💰 Match the pathway and monitoring to locally sustainable access.

## 🔟 Salvage + Regional Regimens — Weigh Six Further Options Against Toxicity

- **Gemcitabine–paclitaxel:** platinum-free doublet only if marrow reserve and neuropathy permit.
  - **📊 AUO AB20/99:** phase 3, short versus prolonged treatment; ORR 37.5% vs 41.5%, OS 7.8 vs 8.0 months. Prolongation added severe anaemia without improving survival. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1093/annonc/mdq398">[Ann Oncol 2011]</a>
- **Vinflunine:** single-agent regional option; constipation, neutropenia and renal dose adjustment matter.
  - 📈 **📊 Phase 3 versus supportive care:** OS 6.9 vs 4.6 months; primary ITT P=0.287. Favourable adjusted/eligible-population analyses must not replace the nonsignificant primary result. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1200/JCO.2008.20.5534">[J Clin Oncol 2009]</a>
- **Paclitaxel / nab-paclitaxel:** generic paclitaxel is a practical salvage drug if neuropathy allows.
  - **📊 CCTG BL.12:** randomised phase 2, nab-paclitaxel versus paclitaxel; PFS 3.4 vs 3.0 months, OS 7.5 vs 8.8 months, ORR 22% vs 25% → no efficacy advantage for nab-paclitaxel. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1001/jamaoncol.2020.3927">[JAMA Oncol 2020]</a>
- **Pemetrexed:** older single-arm phase 2 reported ORR 27.7%, time to progression 2.9 months and OS 9.6 months; inconsistent results across studies limit routine use. Give folate/B12; no pemetrexed dose is recommended at CrCl <45 mL/min. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1200/JCO.2005.03.6699">[J Clin Oncol 2006]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2024/210661s000lbl.pdf">[FDA 2024]</a>
- **TIN / PIN:** paclitaxel–ifosfamide–nedaplatin; regional specialist salvage.
  - **📊 SUOC phase 2:** n=45, ORR 40%; grade 3–4 neutropenia 95.6% → major supportive-care burden. Ifosfamide adds renal/neurotoxicity. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1349-7006.2011.01909.x">[Cancer Sci 2011]</a>
- **Docetaxel–ifosfamide:** small phase 2, 20 response-evaluable patients; ORR 25%, CR 20%, with CRs in nodal-only recurrence. Exploratory evidence does not justify routine modern use. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1097/00005392-200101000-00017">[J Urol 2001]</a>
- ⚠️ Persistent neuropathy limits taxane/MMAE choices; EV requires a hold for grade 2 neuropathy and discontinuation for grade ≥3. Choose by prior exposure, organ reserve and realistic benefit. <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/761137s037lbl.pdf">[FDA 2026]</a>

**🔑 Key insights**

- 1️⃣ Balance modest salvage evidence against marrow reserve and neuropathy.
- 2️⃣ 💰 Match the pathway and monitoring to locally sustainable access.

## 1️⃣1️⃣ Variant Histology — Change the Pathway When the Dominant Component Changes

- **Urothelial cancer with divergent differentiation:** generally use the urothelial pathway; uncommon variants are underrepresented in trials → expert pathology if dominance is unclear. Plasmacytoid disease can progress diffusely in the peritoneum with few measurable lesions.
- **Small-cell / high-grade neuroendocrine:** platinum–etoposide; carboplatin if cisplatin-unfit. Include brain staging. Adding small-cell-lung-style ICI is extrapolation with limited bladder-specific evidence.
- **Pure squamous:** consider platinum-based therapy and trials. Ifosfamide–paclitaxel–cisplatin (ITP/TIP) produced ORR 35% (7/20) in a prospective mixed non-transitional-cell cohort → not a pure bladder-SCC response estimate. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1016/j.urology.2006.10.029">[Urology 2007]</a>
- **Pure adenocarcinoma / urachal:** exclude colorectal or another primary. FOLFOX/CAPOX or other fluoropyrimidine regimens may be appropriate; comparative evidence is sparse. GEM-FLP combines gemcitabine, 5-FU, leucovorin and cisplatin in specialist practice.

**📊 ULTIMA / KCSG GU20-03: Modified FOLFIRINOX for Urachal Cancer**

- **Study design:** single-arm phase 2; n=21; recurrent/metastatic urachal cancer. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.4143/crt.2024.1231">[Cancer Res Treat 2026]</a>
- Oxaliplatin 85 mg/m², irinotecan 150 mg/m², leucovorin 400 mg/m² IV, then 5-FU 2,400 mg/m² IV over 46 hours, every 14 days, with prophylactic growth-factor support. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.4143/crt.2024.1231">[Cancer Res Treat 2026]</a>
- 🎯 ORR 61.9%; median PFS 9.3 months; OS 19.7 months. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.4143/crt.2024.1231">[Cancer Res Treat 2026]</a>
- Expanded 2026 conference analysis: n=35; ORR 57.1%; PFS 9.5 months; OS 25.5 months; grade 3 thrombocytopenia 8.6%, neutropenia 5.7% and anaemia 5.7%; no grade 4 events reported. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1200/JCO.2026.44.7_suppl.758">[J Clin Oncol 2026 abstract]</a>
- Urachal-specific activity does not justify automatic use in conventional urothelial cancer or severe frailty.

**🔑 Key insights**

- 1️⃣ Dominant non-urothelial histology may require a different systemic regimen.
- 2️⃣ 💰 Match the pathway and monitoring to locally sustainable access.

## 1️⃣2️⃣ Palliation, Follow-up + India Practice — Preserve Function and Sustainable Access

**Local symptoms and consolidation**

- Haematuria with clot retention → catheter drainage, irrigation and urgent urological review; consider TURBT/fulguration, haemostatic RT or embolisation.
- **📊 MRC BA09:** randomised palliative bladder RT, 21 Gy/3 fractions versus 35 Gy/10 fractions; symptom improvement in 68% of assessed patients without a significant schedule difference. Choose by PS, prognosis, prior pelvic RT and travel burden. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1016/S0360-3016(00)00430-2">[IJROBP 2000]</a>
- Obstruction → stent/nephrostomy according to infection, recovery potential, symptoms and goals; drainage may restore systemic-treatment eligibility.
- Bone metastases → analgesia, focal RT and fracture/spinal-stability assessment. Denosumab/zoledronic acid when indicated, with dental review, calcium/vitamin D and renal/calcium monitoring.
- Selected oligometastatic responders → multidisciplinary SBRT/metastasectomy discussion; predominantly retrospective evidence carries selection bias. Routine cystectomy in widely disseminated disease lacks support.

**Follow-up and access**

- Symptoms/PS each visit; initial response imaging around 8–12 weeks, tailored to treatment and clinical status, or earlier with deterioration; EV-302 assessed imaging every 9 weeks. <a class="cite" href="https://www.cda-amc.ca/sites/default/files/DRR/2024/PC0353-Padcev_FINAL_Rec.pdf">[CDA-AMC 2024]</a> EV → skin, nerves, glucose; platinum → CBC, renal function, magnesium; ICI → thyroid and immune symptoms.
- 🇮🇳 Correct obstruction/infection, establish histology and deliver an evidence-based regimen that can be sustained. Generic gemcitabine/platinum and generic taxanes are lower-cost options; ADC/ICI pathways generally occupy a much higher cost tier.
- Verify current CDSCO indication, procurement and coverage; regional or US approval does not establish Indian availability. Review PM-JAY/CMCHIS eligibility and covered medicines before induction.
- Fund maintenance during induction; include drainage, laboratory testing, travel and supportive care in the budget. If broad NGS is unaffordable, prioritise focused FGFR3 testing and HER2 IHC when they can unlock an accessible treatment.
- Integrate palliative care from diagnosis. Repeated serious toxicity, irreversible functional decline or no meaningful active option → supportive care aligned with patient priorities.

**🔑 Key insights**

- 1️⃣ Cisplatin fitness and EV–ICI fitness are separate decisions; relieve obstruction before applying a permanent unfit label.
- 2️⃣ Choose EV–pembrolizumab, GC–nivolumab or platinum → avelumab as a complete pathway; avoid unsupported mixing of maintenance strategies.
- 3️⃣ Sequence by prior classes, FGFR3/HER2 findings and residual toxicity; prospective following EV–ICI evidence remains limited.
- 4️⃣ 💰 A funded generic platinum pathway with timely maintenance planning may be more deliverable than an interrupted expensive regimen; account for the entire course in India.
