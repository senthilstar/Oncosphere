## Before cycle 1 — confirm and stabilise

- Establish the dominant histology on adequate tissue. Urothelial carcinoma with divergent differentiation needs careful review; pure squamous, pure adenocarcinoma and small-cell disease belong to separate algorithms.
- Map disease with chest and abdominal/pelvic imaging. Record haematuria, pain, visceral burden and any neurological or skeletal symptoms that need directed investigation.
- Separate reversible illness from persistent frailty. Drain an obstructed collecting system when appropriate; treat urinary infection, dehydration and uncontrolled bleeding before judging treatment fitness.
- Record renal function, hearing, neuropathy, cardiac reserve, glucose, nutritional status and daily function. Cisplatin fitness and fitness for enfortumab vedotin (EV) are different assessments.
- Request FGFR3 and HER2 testing early; retain material for broader profiling if feasible. Urgent symptom-threatening disease should not wait for a comprehensive sequencing report.
- Reconstruct prior perioperative exposure, response and residual toxicity. A previously used drug class cannot automatically be treated as a fresh metastatic option.

## Choosing first line — commit to a pathway

| Situation | Preferred approach | Alternatives / qualification |
|---|---|---|
| Suitable for EV and checkpoint blockade | EV + pembrolizumab | Review skin, nerve and metabolic risks before prescribing |
| EV unsuitable; cisplatin eligible | Gemcitabine–cisplatin + nivolumab, then nivolumab | Platinum chemotherapy followed by avelumab if disease has not progressed |
| Cisplatin unsuitable; carboplatin feasible | Gemcitabine–carboplatin, then avelumab for nonprogressing disease | EV + pembrolizumab remains an option if independently suitable |
| Checkpoint blockade contraindicated | Platinum–gemcitabine | Adapt to organ reserve and goals |
| Irreversible severe frailty | Symptom-directed care | Reassess only if a reversible cause of deterioration emerges |

As of November 2026, regional access matters for disitamab vedotin with toripalimab. Its evidence in HER2-expressing disease should not be interpreted as a direct comparison with EV plus pembrolizumab; confirm the applicable local indication.

## The evidence — keep the populations separate

- **EV-302:** EV plus pembrolizumab versus platinum chemotherapy: median PFS 12.5 versus 6.3 months; HR 0.45. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2312117">[N Engl J Med 2024]</a> Median OS 31.5 versus 16.1 months; HR 0.47. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2312117">[N Engl J Med 2024]</a>
- **EV-302 update:** Updated median OS 33.8 versus 15.9 months; HR 0.51. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1016/j.annonc.2025.05.536">[Ann Oncol 2025]</a>
- **CheckMate 901:** Cisplatin-eligible population: nivolumab plus gemcitabine–cisplatin achieved median OS 21.7 versus 18.9 months; HR 0.78. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2309863">[N Engl J Med 2023]</a>
- **JAVELIN Bladder 100:** Avelumab after nonprogressing platinum induction: median OS 21.4 versus 14.3 months; HR 0.69. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2002788">[N Engl J Med 2020]</a>
- **RC48-C016:** Disitamab vedotin–toripalimab versus platinum chemotherapy: PFS 13.1 versus 6.5 months; HR 0.36. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2511648">[N Engl J Med 2025]</a> Interim OS 31.5 versus 16.9 months; HR 0.54. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2511648">[N Engl J Med 2025]</a> Mainly Chinese recruitment; no EV-based comparator.

## Pitfalls / pearls

- **EV surveillance:** ask about new rash, painful skin, altered sensation and weakness at each treatment review. Check glucose; uncontrolled metabolic disease changes safety. Severe skin reactions require immediate interruption and urgent assessment.
- **Breathlessness:** investigate infection, embolism, progression and treatment-related pneumonitis. Do not attribute a new respiratory symptom to cancer without assessment.
- **Platinum delivery:** arrange hydration, antiemetics, renal and electrolyte monitoring. Obstruction-related renal impairment may improve after drainage; repeat the fitness assessment rather than preserving an outdated label.
- **Maintenance eligibility:** response or stable disease after induction can qualify for avelumab; progression during chemotherapy does not. The maintenance survival clock begins after chemotherapy, so its median cannot be compared directly with an upfront regimen.
- **Avoid mixing protocols:** nivolumab continuation and avelumab switch maintenance are distinct strategies. Routine substitution between them has no established rationale.
- **Local symptoms still matter:** haemostatic radiation, urological procedures and focal treatment for painful bone disease may be needed alongside systemic therapy.

<p class="opinion">Opinion: secure the maintenance appointment and funding pathway while chemotherapy is being delivered. A theoretically appropriate maintenance drug has little value if the transition is lost to referral or access delays.</p>

## After first line — preserve options

Review the reason for stopping: progression, toxicity and unaffordability require different responses. After EV plus pembrolizumab, platinum treatment may be considered in a fit, platinum-naive patient, but prospective sequencing evidence is limited. Do not transplant the avelumab maintenance results into this later-line sequence.

After a platinum/checkpoint pathway, consider EV if it has not been used and neuropathy permits. Revisit FGFR3-directed treatment eligibility and the precise HER2 assay result; expression, amplification and a genomic alteration are not interchangeable findings. Choose subsequent treatment using prior exposure, organ reserve and available trials. Keep pain control, urinary drainage and nutrition active throughout.
