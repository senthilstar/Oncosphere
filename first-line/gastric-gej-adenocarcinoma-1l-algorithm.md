## Clinical Synthesis & Mandatory Reflex Panel

Before initiating first-line systemic therapy for unresectable/metastatic gastric or gastroesophageal junction (GEJ) adenocarcinoma, four biomarkers must be tested simultaneously: HER2 (IHC/ISH), PD-L1 CPS, MMR/MSI, and CLDN18.2 (IHC 43-14A; positive = ≥75% tumor cells with moderate-to-strong membranous staining).

- dMMR / MSI-H (any HER2/CLDN18.2): Checkpoint inhibitor–based therapy (nivolumab + chemotherapy, pembrolizumab + chemotherapy, or nivolumab + ipilimumab) yields durable survival tails; avoid chemotherapy alone.
- HER2-Positive (IHC 3+ or IHC 2+/ISH+):
  - PD-L1 CPS ≥1: Trastuzumab + pembrolizumab + fluoropyrimidine/platinum (KEYNOTE-811).
  - PD-L1 CPS <1: Trastuzumab + fluoropyrimidine/platinum (ToGA); pembrolizumab showed no OS benefit in CPS <1 (HR 1.10).
- HER2-Negative / CLDN18.2-Positive:
  - PD-L1 CPS <5 (or <1): Zolbetuximab + mFOLFOX6 or CAPOX (SPOTLIGHT / GLOW). Prophylactic antiemetics (NK1 antagonist + 5-HT3 antagonist + dexamethasone) and slow infusion rates are critical in cycles 1–2.
  - Dual Positive (CLDN18.2+ and PD-L1 CPS ≥5): Weigh higher ORR/durable tail of IO + chemo against strong mOS in SPOTLIGHT (18.2 mo); no head-to-head trial exists.
- HER2-Negative / CLDN18.2-Negative:
  - PD-L1 CPS ≥5 (Nivo) or ≥1 (Pembro): Nivolumab + FOLFOX/CAPOX (CheckMate 649) or Pembrolizumab + chemo (KEYNOTE-859). Benefit is minimal in CPS <1.
- 2L+ Sequencing: Paclitaxel + ramucirumab (RAINBOW; mOS 9.6 vs 7.4 mo, HR 0.81) remains standard in HER2-negative disease. In HER2+, re-biopsy to confirm retained HER2 expression before trastuzumab deruxtecan (T-DXd) (DESTINY-Gastric01/02; ORR 42%–51%, mOS 12.1–12.5 mo; monitor for ILD).
