**Advanced medullary thyroid cancer — first-line management**

## 1️⃣ Initial Assessment — Establish Whether Structural Disease Needs Treatment

- Confirm medullary thyroid carcinoma (MTC) → cytology, calcitonin/CEA correlation and neuroendocrine immunohistochemistry; use calcitonin needle washout if cytology is inconclusive. This is a C-cell malignancy; radioiodine is ineffective and routine TSH suppression is not an anticancer strategy.
- **Record:**
  - Neck symptoms, airway/swallowing compromise, pain, diarrhoea, weight loss and performance status.
  - Prior thyroid/neck surgery, radiation, systemic agents, response and residual toxicity.
  - Structural tumour burden, measurable growth, bone/liver/lung disease and threatened organs.
- Neck ultrasound and cross-sectional staging tailored to burden; CT chest, liver-directed CT/MRI and skeletal imaging when indicated. Suspected spinal cord compression or airway compromise → urgent local assessment.
- Serial calcitonin and CEA using consistent assays; calculate both doubling times from multiple measurements. Doubling time <6 months → adverse prognosis; >24 months → more favourable course. Biochemical progression alone does not mandate systemic treatment.
- Rising CEA disproportionate to calcitonin or unexpectedly rapid structural growth → reconsider differentiation and biopsy when it changes management.

## 2️⃣ RET and MEN2 Assessment — Test Inherited and Tumour Risk Separately

- 🧬 Offer germline RET testing and genetic counselling to every patient, including apparently sporadic MTC. A pathogenic germline result → MEN2 evaluation and cascade testing of relatives.
- Germline-negative advanced disease → tumour RET mutation testing before systemic selection; tissue NGS preferred when feasible; focused testing may be sufficient when broad NGS is unaffordable.
- Plasma testing can help when tissue is unavailable; a negative result in low-shedding disease does not exclude a tumour mutation → seek tissue testing when feasible.
- RET mutation in MTC differs from a RET fusion in another thyroid histology; neither RET protein staining nor a variant of uncertain significance establishes matched-drug eligibility.
- **MEN2 safety:** evaluate pheochromocytoma with plasma free or urine fractionated metanephrines and assess calcium/PTH for hyperparathyroidism as appropriate. Pheochromocytoma → endocrine preparation and treatment before thyroid/other elective surgery.
- Relatives carrying a pathogenic variant require genotype-informed endocrine surveillance and preventive surgical counselling; do not infer family risk from a somatic-only result.

## 3️⃣ Progressive RET-mutant Disease — Prefer Selpercatinib When Suitable

**Regimen 💊**

- Adult/adolescent weight ≥50 kg: selpercatinib 160 mg orally twice daily; <50 kg: 120 mg twice daily under the relevant weight/age label. Continue until progression or unacceptable toxicity; no fixed maintenance cap.
- Review CYP3A interactions, acid suppression, hepatic impairment and formulation-specific administration before prescribing.

**📊 LIBRETTO-531: Selpercatinib versus Cabozantinib/Vandetanib ✨**

- **Study design:** phase 3, randomized 2:1, open-label; n=291; kinase-inhibitor-naive advanced RET-mutant MTC with documented progression within the preceding 14 months. Comparator was investigator-selected cabozantinib 140 mg daily or vandetanib 300 mg daily; crossover permitted after progression. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2309719">[NEJM 2023]</a>
- **Prespecified interim analysis:** median PFS not reached vs 16.8 months; HR 0.28 (95% CI 0.16–0.48). Twelve-month PFS 86.8% vs 65.7%. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2309719">[NEJM 2023]</a>
- 🎯 **Response and treatment persistence:** ORR 69.4% vs 38.8%; complete response 11.9% vs 4.1%; treatment-failure-free survival HR 0.25 (95% CI 0.15–0.42). <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2309719">[NEJM 2023]</a>
- ⚠️ Grade ≥3 AEs 52.8% vs 76.3%; dose reductions 38.9% vs 77.3%; discontinuation for AEs 4.7% vs 26.8%. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2309719">[NEJM 2023]</a>
- 📊 Later analysis → PFS HR 0.202 (95% CI 0.128–0.320); ORR 82.4% vs 43.9%; OS HR 0.275 (95% CI 0.124–0.608), with 26 deaths and immature OS. <a class="cite" href="https://www.pmda.go.jp/drugs/2026/P20260701001/530471000_30300AMX00448_B100_1.pdf">[PMDA 2026]</a>
- The trial establishes a first-line preference when systemic therapy is needed; it does not justify treating an asymptomatic mutation carrier or indolent biochemical-only recurrence.

## 4️⃣ Selective RET Inhibition Unavailable — Choose an Appropriate Multikinase Drug

- RET-negative disease requiring treatment → cabozantinib or vandetanib according to safety and access. RET-mutant disease with no sustainable selective-inhibitor access → these remain active alternatives.
- Cabozantinib → consider diarrhoea, mucositis, hand–foot syndrome, weight loss, hypertension, bleeding/fistula risk and upcoming surgery.
- Vandetanib → consider QT prolongation, arrhythmia, renal function, diarrhoea, rash/photosensitivity and interacting drugs; reliable ECG/electrolyte follow-up is essential.
- Active major bleeding, threatened perforation/fistula or poorly healed surgery → reassess antiangiogenic treatment and prioritise local/supportive measures.
- No randomized cabozantinib-versus-vandetanib trial identifies a universal winner. EXAM required recent progression; ZETA enrolled a different population → their median PFS values cannot rank drugs.

## 5️⃣ Indolent Disease or Frailty — Observe with Explicit Treatment Triggers

- Stable, low-volume, asymptomatic structural disease → surveillance rather than immediate chronic drug exposure. Biochemical-only persistence → surveillance and localisation as clinically indicated.
- Serial markers, examination and imaging at intervals matched to growth; commonly reassess within 3–6 months initially, extending only when stable.
- Start systemic treatment for meaningful radiographic progression, symptoms not adequately controlled locally or impending organ compromise. Document the specific trigger and expected benefit.
- A threatening single lesion → surgery, radiation or ablation may defer systemic treatment when other disease is indolent.
- Frailty → correct dehydration, malnutrition, thyroid/calcium disturbance and secretory diarrhoea before deciding treatment is impossible. Irreversible poor function → prioritise comfort and realistic goals.
- There is no validated switch-maintenance pathway; useful targeted treatment is generally continued with toxicity adaptation.

## 6️⃣ Progression — Distinguish Oligoprogression, Resistance and Intolerance

- Confirm structural progression and adherence; marker rise alone does not prove treatment failure. Review interactions and whether tolerated dosing was adequate.
- Isolated progression with control elsewhere → local ablation/radiation/surgery and continued effective systemic therapy may be reasonable after multidisciplinary review.
- Widespread progression on selpercatinib → tissue/plasma profiling when it can enable a resistance-directed trial; consider cabozantinib or vandetanib if safe and unused.
- After multikinase therapy, RET-mutant and selective-inhibitor-naive → selpercatinib where available; LIBRETTO-001 supports activity after prior multikinase therapy. [Long-term study](https://doi.org/10.1200/JCO.23.02503).
- Intolerance → hold, treat toxicity and resume at the appropriate reduced dose when safe. A tolerability-driven switch differs from a switch for molecular resistance.
- After selective RET resistance, empiric switching to another first-generation RET inhibitor may not overcome shared resistance; prioritise a trial rather than promising benefit.

**📊 LIBRETTO-001: Selpercatinib after or before multikinase treatment ✨**

- Phase 1/2, single-arm RET-mutant MTC → cabozantinib/vandetanib-naive cohort ORR 82.5%, complete response 23.8%, median PFS not reached at 42.4 months median follow-up; 3-year PFS 75.2%. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1200/JCO.23.02503">[J Clin Oncol 2024]</a>
- Previously treated cohort → ORR 77.6%, complete response 12.5%; median PFS 41.4 months, median response duration 45.3 months. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1200/JCO.23.02503">[J Clin Oncol 2024]</a>

## 7️⃣ Pralsetinib Evidence — Separate ARROW Activity from Current Indications

**📊 ARROW: Pralsetinib in RET-altered Thyroid Cancer**

- **Study design:** phase 1/2, open-label, multi-cohort; n=142 RET-altered thyroid cancer patients in the published safety population; pralsetinib 400 mg orally once daily fasting. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1016/S2213-8587(21)00120-0">[Lancet Diabetes Endocrinol 2021]</a>
- **Initial measurable-MTC efficacy cohorts:** treatment-naive ORR 71% (15/21; 95% CI 48–89); prior cabozantinib/vandetanib ORR 60% (33/55; 95% CI 46–73). <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1016/S2213-8587(21)00120-0">[Lancet Diabetes Endocrinol 2021]</a>
- ⚠️ Grade ≥3 treatment-related hypertension 17%, neutropenia 13%, lymphopenia 12% and anaemia 10% across the thyroid safety population; pneumonitis is a serious risk.. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1016/S2213-8587(21)00120-0">[Lancet Diabetes Endocrinol 2021]</a>
- 📊 Updated analysis → treatment-naive ORR 77.4%, complete response 6.5%, median PFS not reached; prior cabozantinib/vandetanib ORR 55.7%, median PFS and response duration both 25.8 months. <a class="cite" href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38009200/">[Thyroid 2024]</a>
- 📊 Final conference analysis → naive ORR 79.0%, complete response 11.3%, median PFS 55.3 months; previously treated ORR 56.7%, median PFS 24.9 months and response duration 21.7 months (updated: J Clin Oncol 2026 supersedes earlier 77.4%, 6.5%, not reached; 55.7%, 25.8 and 25.8 months). <a class="cite" href="https://ascopubs.org/doi/10.1200/JCO.2026.44.16_suppl.6028">[J Clin Oncol 2026]</a>
- Single-arm response data do not establish superiority over selpercatinib or a survival advantage.
- The US accelerated MTC indication was withdrawn in 2023 after confirmatory-study feasibility problems; do not present historical approval as current. <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/appletter/2023/213721Orig1s014ltr.pdf">[FDA 2023]</a>
- Any use requires verification of the local indication, availability and governance; it is not an automatic approved substitute in India.

## 8️⃣ Cabozantinib Evidence — Use EXAM's MTC Formulation and Dose Context

**📊 EXAM: Cabozantinib versus Placebo**

- **Study design:** phase 3, double-blind; n=330; progressive metastatic MTC, including previously treated patients; cabozantinib 140 mg orally daily as capsules versus placebo. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1200/JCO.2012.48.4659">[J Clin Oncol 2013]</a>
- ⏱️ **Primary analysis:** PFS 11.2 vs 4.0 months; HR 0.28 (95% CI 0.19–0.40); ORR 28% vs 0%. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1200/JCO.2012.48.4659">[J Clin Oncol 2013]</a>
- ⚠️ Grade 3–4 AEs 69% vs 33%; diarrhoea, hand–foot syndrome, fatigue, hypertension and hypocalcaemia require proactive monitoring. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1200/JCO.2012.48.4659">[J Clin Oncol 2013]</a>
- **Final OS:** 26.6 vs 21.1 months; HR 0.85 (95% CI 0.64–1.12), without significant overall survival benefit. RET M918T subgroup OS 44.3 vs 18.9 months → exploratory, without multiplicity adjustment. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1093/annonc/mdx479">[Ann Oncol 2017]</a>
- Capsule dose reductions: 140 → 100 → 60 mg daily, using toxicity-specific hold/restart rules. Take fasting according to product instructions.
- ⚠️ MTC capsules and cabozantinib tablets used in RCC are not interchangeable milligram-for-milligram. Verify the exact product; do not substitute an accessible generic tablet into the EXAM schedule without a specialist prescribing decision.

## 9️⃣ Vandetanib Evidence — Balance PFS Benefit Against QT Risk

**📊 ZETA: Vandetanib versus Placebo**

- **Study design:** phase 3, double-blind; n=331; unresectable locally advanced/metastatic MTC; vandetanib 300 mg orally daily versus placebo; radiographic progression before entry was not mandatory. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1200/JCO.2011.35.5040">[J Clin Oncol 2012]</a>
- ⏱️ **Primary analysis:** PFS HR 0.46 (95% CI 0.31–0.69); placebo median 19.3 months; ORR 45% vs 13%. Vandetanib median was not reached; 30.5 months was a model-predicted median, not an observed median. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1200/JCO.2011.35.5040">[J Clin Oncol 2012]</a>
- ⚠️ Grade ≥3 diarrhoea 11%, hypertension 9% and QT prolongation 8% with vandetanib. Crossover and immature OS limit survival interpretation. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1200/JCO.2011.35.5040">[J Clin Oncol 2012]</a>
- Baseline ECG, potassium, magnesium, calcium and TSH; repeat ECG/electrolytes early, after dose/interaction changes and periodically under the product protocol.
- QTcF >500 ms → interrupt, correct contributors and use label-directed resumption only after recovery. Avoid congenital long-QT syndrome and relevant interacting drugs.
- Dose reductions commonly 300 → 200 → 100 mg daily; renal impairment may require a lower starting dose. Long half-life → toxicity can persist after interruption.

## 🔟 Symptoms and Regional Therapy — Treat the Lesion Causing Harm

- Resectable locoregional disease → total thyroidectomy and central compartment dissection; therapeutic lateral dissection when indicated. Metastatic disease → individualise surgery to symptoms, neck burden and morbidity.
- Recurrent neck disease → expert surgery only when meaningful control is feasible without disproportionate morbidity; unresectable painful/compressive disease → radiation evaluation.
- Bone metastases → analgesia, stabilisation, radiation and selected bone-directed therapy after renal, dental and calcium assessment. Cord compression → urgent specialist management.
- Selected liver-dominant symptomatic disease → embolisation or ablation in an experienced centre; discuss liver reserve, burden and procedural risks.
- Secretory diarrhoea → hydration, electrolytes, nutrition and antidiarrhoeals; distinguish tumour secretion from drug toxicity or infection. Selected refractory cases may need specialist antisecretory treatment.
- Selected progressive, strongly SSTR-avid disease → consider investigational or specialist 177Lu-DOTATATE with adequate marrow and renal reserve; evidence remains limited.
- 🧬 FAP-targeted radionuclide therapy remains investigational → trial preferred. A small mixed 177Lu/225Ac series reported radiographic ORR 33% and disease control 80% among 15 evaluable patients. <a class="cite" href="https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s13550-026-01474-0">[EJNMMI Res 2026]</a>
- Persistent pain, cachexia, swallowing difficulty or psychosocial distress → early palliative and supportive care alongside tumour treatment.

## 1️⃣1️⃣ Toxicity and Special Situations — Adapt Before Harm Becomes Irreversible

- Selpercatinib → monitor BP, ALT/AST, bilirubin, ECG/electrolytes when QT risk applies, thyroid function and respiratory symptoms. Hypertension, liver injury, oedema, diarrhoea and electrolyte abnormalities → monitor; rare pneumonitis, bleeding, hypersensitivity and chylous effusions/ascites require prompt assessment.
- Adult ≥50 kg selpercatinib reductions commonly 160 → 120 → 80 → 40 mg twice daily; use weight-specific tables and toxicity-specific restart rules. Severe hepatic impairment/interactions require separate adjustments.
- Major surgery → label-directed pre/postoperative holds for antiangiogenic/RET therapy; restart only with adequate healing. Review prior neck radiation, invasive tumour and fistula risk.
- MEN2-associated adrenal disease → coordinate endocrine treatment; do not attribute episodic severe hypertension automatically to the anticancer drug.
- Maintain euthyroidism with replacement; assess postoperative hypoparathyroidism and correct calcium/magnesium. Pregnancy potential → discuss contraception and fetal risk.
- Off-label salvage → lenvatinib phase 2 ORR 36% (95% CI 24–49), all partial responses; sorafenib, sunitinib and pazopanib have limited small-study evidence and no established sequence. <a class="cite" href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26311725/">[Clin Cancer Res 2016]</a>
- Cytotoxic salvage after targeted options or trials → dacarbazine with fluorouracil, or cyclophosphamide/vincristine/dacarbazine (CVD); historical regimens included doxorubicin or streptozocin. Responses are modest and inconsistent; no preferred regimen or proven survival benefit. RET negativity alone does not justify chemotherapy.
- A small CVD series reported partial responses in 2/7; a separate series reported a partial response in 11%. <a class="cite" href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8293411/">[Cancer 1994]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21667440/">[Thyroid 2011]</a>
- For cytotoxic therapy, assess performance status and toxicity before treatment.

## 1️⃣2️⃣ Follow-up and India Practice — Build a Sustainable Long-term Plan

- Clinical review, BP, weight, stool frequency, nutrition and drug adherence at each visit; repeat imaging typically every 8–12 weeks on a new systemic agent, then individualise.
- Follow calcitonin/CEA alongside imaging; biochemical response does not replace structural assessment. Record why treatment started so later benefit can be judged against symptoms and growth.
- 🇮🇳 Verify current CDSCO approval, exact formulation and reliable supply before prescribing. Selective RET inhibitors may involve substantial sustained expenditure; confirm assistance, insurance and state-scheme coverage rather than assuming access from overseas approval.
- Quality-assured generic multikinase agents may lower acquisition cost, but formulation, ECG/laboratory monitoring and toxicity support determine feasibility. Biosimilars do not substitute for these small-molecule drugs.

**🔑 Key insights**

- 1️⃣ Treat progressive/symptomatic structural disease or organ threat; a rising marker alone is insufficient.
- 2️⃣ Germline RET testing serves family care; tumour RET testing selects systemic therapy.
- 3️⃣ Selpercatinib has randomized first-line evidence against established multikinase drugs; ARROW response data have a different evidentiary and regulatory context.
- 4️⃣ 💰 Prioritise focused actionable testing and funded monitoring. Include the whole treatment course, travel and management of toxicity in the affordability discussion.
