# First-line treatment principles by cancer

Confirm histology, disease extent, fitness and treatment-changing biomarkers.  
Match therapy to tumour biology, clinical urgency, safety and sustainable access.

- [Urothelial / bladder cancer]({{ '/first-line/urothelial-bladder-cancer/' | relative_url }}) — Assess histology, enfortumab vedotin suitability and platinum fitness; plan biomarkers and maintenance early.
- [Renal cell carcinoma]({{ '/first-line/renal-cell-carcinoma/' | relative_url }}) — For advanced clear-cell RCC, choose an immune doublet or immune–TKI combination by risk, tumour tempo, comorbidity and access.
- [Endometrial carcinoma]({{ '/first-line/endometrial-carcinoma/' | relative_url }}) — Assess local salvage and MMR status; use molecular classification to guide systemic treatment for advanced or recurrent disease.
- [Medullary thyroid cancer]({{ '/first-line/medullary-thyroid-cancer/' | relative_url }}) — Decide whether structural disease needs treatment; use RET status, safety and access to select therapy.
- [Gallbladder / biliary cancer]({{ '/first-line/gallbladder-biliary-cancer/' | relative_url }}) — Drain obstruction and control infection; select a gemcitabine–cisplatin pathway and test for actionable alterations, including HER2.
- [Locally advanced pancreatic cancer]({{ '/first-line/locally-advanced-pancreatic-cancer/' | relative_url }}) — Confirm anatomy and exclude metastases; choose induction by fitness and repeatedly reassess for surgery.
- [Chronic lymphocytic leukaemia]({{ '/first-line/chronic-lymphocytic-leukaemia/' | relative_url }}) — Treat active CLL; test TP53 and IGHV, then choose continuous BTK inhibition or finite venetoclax-based therapy.
- [HER2-positive metastatic breast cancer]({{ '/first-line/her2-positive-metastatic-breast-cancer/' | relative_url }}) — Confirm metastatic HER2 status; select induction by prior treatment, pulmonary safety and access, and plan maintenance and sequencing.
- [Cervical cancer (recurrent / metastatic)]({{ '/first-line/cervical-cancer/' | relative_url }}) — Assess curability, PD-L1 CPS, prior immunotherapy and bevacizumab safety; plan sustainable access.
- [Metastatic colorectal cancer]({{ '/first-line/metastatic-colorectal-cancer/' | relative_url }}) — Assess resection potential; test MMR, RAS, BRAF and HER2, then match therapy to biology, sidedness and fitness.
- [Advanced gastric / GEJ adenocarcinoma]({{ '/first-line/gastric-gej-adenocarcinoma-1l-algorithm/' | relative_url }}) — Assess HER2, PD-L1 CPS, MMR/MSI and CLDN18.2 up front; match first-line therapy to the biomarker profile.
- [Unresectable hepatocellular carcinoma]({{ '/first-line/unresectable-hcc-systemic-therapy-guide/' | relative_url }}) — Choose an IO-based first-line regimen by Child-Pugh class, variceal bleeding risk and the need for rapid shrinkage.
- [Metastatic hormone-sensitive prostate cancer]({{ '/first-line/mhspc-doublet-vs-triplet-therapy/' | relative_url }}) — Intensify by CHAARTED volume, presentation timing and chemotherapy fitness — triplet or ARPI doublet, never ADT alone.
