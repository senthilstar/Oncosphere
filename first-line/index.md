# First-line treatment principles by cancer

Test first: confirm histology, extent, fitness and the biomarkers that change treatment.  
Then match therapy to tumour biology, clinical urgency, safety and sustainable access.

- [Urothelial / bladder cancer]({{ '/first-line/urothelial-bladder-cancer/' | relative_url }}) — Choose initial treatment by histology, EV suitability and platinum fitness; arrange biomarkers and maintenance early.
- [Renal cell carcinoma]({{ '/first-line/renal-cell-carcinoma/' | relative_url }}) — For advanced clear-cell RCC, match immune combinations to risk, tumour tempo, comorbidity and sustainable access.
- [Endometrial carcinoma]({{ '/first-line/endometrial-carcinoma/' | relative_url }}) — Use molecular classification, especially MMR, to choose systemic therapy for advanced or recurrent endometrial cancer.
- [Medullary thyroid cancer]({{ '/first-line/medullary-thyroid-cancer/' | relative_url }}) — First decide whether medullary thyroid cancer needs a drug; then use RET status and safety to select treatment.
- [Gallbladder / biliary cancer]({{ '/first-line/gallbladder-biliary-cancer/' | relative_url }}) — Control biliary sepsis, select a gemcitabine–cisplatin pathway and profile gallbladder cancer with HER2 in mind.
- [Locally advanced pancreatic cancer]({{ '/first-line/locally-advanced-pancreatic-cancer/' | relative_url }}) — Use expert anatomical staging, fitness-led induction and repeated surgical reassessment for unresectable pancreatic cancer.
- [Chronic lymphocytic leukaemia]({{ '/first-line/chronic-lymphocytic-leukaemia/' | relative_url }}) — Treat active CLL, test TP53 and IGHV, then choose continuous BTK inhibition or a finite venetoclax-based strategy.
- [HER2-positive metastatic breast cancer]({{ '/first-line/her2-positive-metastatic-breast-cancer/' | relative_url }}) — Confirm metastatic HER2 status, choose an induction pathway and plan maintenance, pulmonary safety and later sequencing.
