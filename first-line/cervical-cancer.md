**Recurrent / metastatic cervical cancer — first-line management**

## 1️⃣ Initial Assessment — Is Curative Local Treatment Possible?

- Confirm recurrence histologically when feasible → distinguish squamous, adenocarcinoma, adenosquamous and neuroendocrine disease.
- Examine pelvis; CT chest/abdomen/pelvis → define extent; pelvic MRI for local anatomy; PET-CT when a curative local approach is plausible. Brain imaging for neurological symptoms.
- Isolated pelvic recurrence → assess surgery, definitive chemoradiation if radiation-naïve, or specialist reirradiation after prior radiation. Limited distant relapse → multidisciplinary local-treatment review.
- **Record:**
  - ECOG performance status, nutrition, pain, bleeding, discharge, infection and obstruction.
  - Prior radiation fields and dose, brachytherapy, platinum and checkpoint inhibitor exposure, dates, response and residual toxicity.
- CBC, renal and liver function, electrolytes and albumin; thyroid function before immunotherapy. Hydronephrosis with kidney injury → consider drainage and reassess platinum fitness.

## 2️⃣ Fitness and Biomarkers — Can Immunotherapy and Bevacizumab Be Given Safely?

- 🧬 Test PD-L1 combined positive score (CPS) using a validated assay such as 22C3 → CPS includes stained tumour and relevant immune cells relative to viable tumour cells; it is distinct from tumour proportion score. <a class="cite" href="https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10722390/">[JAMA Oncol 2024]</a>
- CPS ≥1 → supports pembrolizumab with chemotherapy under the US label; benefit in the small CPS <1 subgroup remains uncertain; check the locally applicable indication. <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/125514s191lbl.pdf">[FDA 2026]</a>
- Assess renal function, hearing, neuropathy and hydration capacity → select cisplatin or carboplatin. Prior radiosensitising cisplatin alone does not exclude either.
- ⚠️ Screen for fistula, perforation, major bleeding, unhealed wounds, recent surgery, hypertension, proteinuria and thrombosis → avoid or defer bevacizumab as appropriate; prior pelvic radiation, organ invasion and instrumentation raise concern for fistula.
- Autoimmune disease, transplant or prior serious immune toxicity → individualise checkpoint blockade. Consider HER2, MSI/MMR and broader profiling for subsequent treatment when accessible. <a class="cite" href="https://www.fda.gov/drugs/cancer-approvals/fda-grants-accelerated-approval-fam-trastuzumab-deruxtecan-nxki-unresectable-or-metastatic-her2">[FDA 2024]</a>

## 3️⃣ Preferred First Line — Add Pembrolizumab for Eligible CPS-Positive Disease

**💊 Regimen**

- Pembrolizumab 200 mg IV every 3 weeks, up to 35 cycles in the trial; the US label also permits 400 mg every 6 weeks. <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/125514s191lbl.pdf">[FDA 2026]</a>
- Paclitaxel 175 mg/m² plus cisplatin 50 mg/m² or carboplatin AUC 5 IV on day 1 every 3 weeks; add bevacizumab 15 mg/kg every 3 weeks if safe; chemotherapy was generally given for 6 cycles, with extension permitted for benefit. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1001/jamaoncol.2026.3547">[JAMA Oncol 2026]</a>
- After induction → continue pembrolizumab ± bevacizumab until progression, unacceptable toxicity or the applicable treatment limit.

**📊 KEYNOTE-826: Pembrolizumab versus Placebo with Chemotherapy ± Bevacizumab ✨**

- **Study design:** phase 3; n=617; previously untreated persistent, recurrent or metastatic disease unsuitable for cure; prior radiosensitising chemotherapy permitted; placebo comparator; bevacizumab use stratified; in the CPS ≥1 population, 63% received bevacizumab. <a class="cite" href="https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10722390/">[JAMA Oncol 2024]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/125514s191lbl.pdf">[FDA 2026]</a>
- **CPS ≥1 (n=548), exploratory long-term follow-up:** OS 28.6 vs 16.5 months (HR 0.62; 95% CI 0.50–0.76); PFS 10.5 vs 8.2 months (HR 0.58; 95% CI 0.48–0.71). <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1001/jamaoncol.2026.3547">[JAMA Oncol 2026]</a>
- Earlier final analysis, CPS ≥1: OS HR 0.60 (95% CI 0.49–0.74); PFS HR 0.58 (95% CI 0.47–0.71). <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1200/JCO.23.00914">[J Clin Oncol 2023]</a>
- CPS ≥10 (n=317), earlier final analysis: OS 29.6 vs 17.4 months (HR 0.58; 95% CI 0.44–0.78). <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1200/JCO.23.00914">[J Clin Oncol 2023]</a>
- 🎯 CPS ≥1 ORR 68% vs 50% at the earlier assessment; ⚠️ grade ≥3 adverse events 82.4% vs 75.4% at final analysis. <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/125514s191lbl.pdf">[FDA 2026]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1200/JCO.23.00914">[J Clin Oncol 2023]</a>
- All-comer long-term OS 26.4 vs 16.8 months (HR 0.64; 95% CI 0.53–0.78); PFS 10.4 vs 8.2 months (HR 0.61; 95% CI 0.51–0.74). <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1001/jamaoncol.2026.3547">[JAMA Oncol 2026]</a>

## 4️⃣ When the Preferred Regimen Is Unsuitable — Choose an Accessible Backbone

**📊 KEYNOTE-826: Exploratory Bevacizumab Subgroups ✨**

- Pembrolizumab versus placebo within each subgroup; bevacizumab was not randomised → between-subgroup comparisons cannot establish its added effect.
- CPS ≥1 with bevacizumab: PFS 15.3 vs 10.3 months (HR 0.56; 95% CI 0.43–0.73); OS 43.9 vs 23.0 months (HR 0.60; 95% CI 0.45–0.79); 24-month OS 62.3% vs 48.0%. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1016/j.annonc.2024.10.002">[Ann Oncol 2025]</a>
- CPS ≥1 without bevacizumab: PFS 7.0 vs 6.0 months (HR 0.61; 95% CI 0.44–0.85); OS 17.5 vs 11.9 months (HR 0.61; 95% CI 0.44–0.85). <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1016/j.annonc.2024.10.002">[Ann Oncol 2025]</a>
- All-comers with bevacizumab: PFS 15.2 vs 10.2 months (HR 0.57; 95% CI 0.45–0.73); OS 37.6 vs 22.5 months (HR 0.61; 95% CI 0.47–0.80). <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1016/j.annonc.2024.10.002">[Ann Oncol 2025]</a>
- All-comers without bevacizumab: PFS 6.3 vs 6.2 months (HR 0.69; 95% CI 0.50–0.94); OS 17.1 vs 12.6 months (HR 0.67; 95% CI 0.49–0.91). <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1016/j.annonc.2024.10.002">[Ann Oncol 2025]</a>
- ⚠️ With bevacizumab, grade ≥3 treatment-related adverse events 74.0% vs 66.8%. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1016/j.annonc.2024.10.002">[Ann Oncol 2025]</a>
- CPS-positive disease with bevacizumab unsafe → pembrolizumab plus platinum–paclitaxel remains a studied option.
- CPS <1 or checkpoint blockade unavailable → platinum–paclitaxel ± bevacizumab, guided by risk; chemotherapy alone if both additions are unsuitable. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1309748">[NEJM 2014]</a>

**📊 BEATcc: Atezolizumab + Bevacizumab + Chemotherapy versus Bevacizumab + Chemotherapy ✨**

- **Study design:** phase 3; n=410; measurable previously untreated disease, without PD-L1 selection; atezolizumab 1,200 mg added to paclitaxel 175 mg/m², cisplatin 50 mg/m² or carboplatin AUC 5, and bevacizumab 15 mg/kg IV on day 1 every 3 weeks. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(23)02405-4">[Lancet 2024]</a>
- ⏱️ PFS 13.7 vs 10.4 months (HR 0.62; 95% CI 0.49–0.78); interim OS 32.1 vs 22.8 months (HR 0.68; 95% CI 0.52–0.88); ORR 84% vs 72%. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(23)02405-4">[Lancet 2024]</a>
- ⚠️ Grade ≥3 adverse events 79% vs 75%. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(23)02405-4">[Lancet 2024]</a> Bevacizumab was mandatory → evidence does not establish an atezolizumab–doublet regimen without it; no direct comparison establishes superiority over pembrolizumab quadruplet; check local authorisation and access. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(23)02405-4">[Lancet 2024]</a>

## 5️⃣ After Induction — Maintain Benefit and Adapt to Fitness

- 🔁 Stop or reduce paclitaxel for troublesome neuropathy; monitor CBC, renal function and neuropathy before cytotoxic treatment. Premedicate for paclitaxel hypersensitivity.
- Bevacizumab → monitor blood pressure, urine protein, bleeding, abdominal pain and urinary or faecal vaginal leakage; suspected fistula or perforation → interrupt and investigate urgently; confirmed fistula generally requires permanent discontinuation.
- Withhold bevacizumab for proteinuria ≥2 g per 24 hours until below that threshold; discontinue for nephrotic syndrome. Hold for at least 28 days before elective surgery and after major surgery until adequate healing. <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2022/125085s340lbl.pdf">[FDA 2022]</a>
- Checkpoint blockade → monitor thyroid function and symptoms; new diarrhoea, hepatitis, dyspnoea or adrenal symptoms → grade, interrupt and treat per immune-toxicity guidance; severe toxicity may require permanent discontinuation.
- Pembrolizumab trial cap does not automatically apply to atezolizumab or bevacizumab; use the relevant protocol and prescribing guidance.
- Severe frailty or organ dysfunction → consider selected single-agent chemotherapy or symptom-directed care; reassess goals and likely burden with the patient.
- 💰 Agree on access to maintenance and monitoring before starting; do not extend cytotoxic chemotherapy solely because maintenance is unaffordable.

## 6️⃣ At Progression — Sequence by Prior Drug Exposure

**📊 innovaTV 301: Tisotumab Vedotin versus Investigator-Choice Chemotherapy 🔁**

- **Study design:** phase 3; n=502; recurrent or metastatic disease after chemotherapy, with or without prior bevacizumab or anti-PD-(L)1 therapy; tisotumab vedotin 2 mg/kg IV every 3 weeks versus single-agent chemotherapy. <a class="cite" href="https://www.fda.gov/drugs/cancer-approvals/fda-approves-tisotumab-vedotin-tftv-recurrent-or-metastatic-cervical-cancer">[FDA 2024]</a>
- 🫀 OS 11.5 vs 9.5 months (HR 0.70; 95% CI 0.54–0.89); PFS 4.2 vs 2.9 months (HR 0.67; 95% CI 0.54–0.82); ORR 17.8% vs 5.2%. <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2024/761208s007lbl.pdf">[FDA 2024]</a>
- ⚠️ Grade ≥3 adverse events 52.0% vs 62.3%; arrange eye examinations, prescribed drops and cooling, and monitor neuropathy and bleeding. New visual symptoms → interrupt and arrange urgent eye review. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2313811">[NEJM 2024]</a>
- Eye care: ophthalmic examination before treatment and each of the first 9 cycles, then as clinically indicated; prescribed steroid and vasoconstrictor drops, cooling during infusion, lubricants and contact-lens precautions are essential. <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2024/761208s007lbl.pdf">[FDA 2024]</a>
- After checkpoint inhibitor progression → consider tisotumab vedotin, biomarker-matched treatment, chemotherapy or a trial; switching routinely to another checkpoint antibody lacks established benefit.
- 🇮🇳 If the antibody–drug conjugate or its eye-care pathway is inaccessible → discuss single-agent chemotherapy, a trial or supportive care according to fitness and goals.

## 7️⃣ ICI-Naïve Relapse — Consider Checkpoint Blockade After Platinum

**📊 EMPOWER-Cervical 1: Cemiplimab versus Chemotherapy ✨**

- **Study design:** phase 3; n=608; progression after platinum; cemiplimab 350 mg IV every 3 weeks versus investigator-choice single-agent chemotherapy, regardless of PD-L1. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2112187">[NEJM 2022]</a>
- 🫀 Final OS 11.7 vs 8.5 months (HR 0.67; 95% CI 0.56–0.80). <a class="cite" href="https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0959804924017532">[Eur J Cancer 2025]</a>
- 🫀 Earlier OS 12.0 vs 8.5 months (HR 0.69; 95% CI 0.56–0.84). <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2112187">[NEJM 2022]</a>
- ⏱️ Earlier PFS 2.8 vs 2.9 months (HR 0.75; 95% CI 0.63–0.89); ORR 16.4% vs 6.3%; grade ≥3 adverse events 45.0% vs 53.4%. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2112187">[NEJM 2022]</a>
- Final-analysis ORR 17.1% vs 6.3%. <a class="cite" href="https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0959804924017532">[Eur J Cancer 2025]</a>
- Trial evidence principally concerns checkpoint-inhibitor-naïve patients → check local indication and access. For CPS ≥1 disease, pembrolizumab monotherapy after chemotherapy is also a US-labelled option in an appropriate untreated-with-ICI patient. <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/125514s191lbl.pdf">[FDA 2026]</a>

## 8️⃣ Actionable Biomarkers — Match Later Treatment to the Result

- 🧬 HER2 IHC 3+ after systemic therapy → consider trastuzumab deruxtecan where accessible and no satisfactory alternative exists; the US tumour-agnostic indication is accelerated approval. Monitor for interstitial lung disease and promptly investigate respiratory symptoms. <a class="cite" href="https://www.fda.gov/drugs/cancer-approvals/fda-grants-accelerated-approval-fam-trastuzumab-deruxtecan-nxki-unresectable-or-metastatic-her2">[FDA 2024]</a>
- MSI-high/dMMR or tumour mutational burden-high disease → assess tumour-agnostic immunotherapy eligibility, prior checkpoint exposure and the applicable label; evidence for rechallenge after immune progression is limited. <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/125514s191lbl.pdf">[FDA 2026]</a>
- Rare actionable alterations → consider a matched trial; testing should not delay urgent symptom control.

## 9️⃣ When Immunotherapy Is Unavailable — Weigh Bevacizumab Against Fistula Risk

**📊 GOG-240: Chemotherapy + Bevacizumab versus Chemotherapy ✨**

- **Study design:** phase 3; n=452; cisplatin–paclitaxel or topotecan–paclitaxel, randomised with or without bevacizumab 15 mg/kg every 3 weeks. <a class="cite" href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28756902/">[Lancet 2017]</a>
- 🫀 Interim OS 17.0 vs 13.3 months (HR 0.71; 98% CI 0.54–0.95). <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1309748">[NEJM 2014]</a>
- 🫀 Final OS 16.8 vs 13.3 months (HR 0.77; 95% CI 0.62–0.95). <a class="cite" href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28756902/">[Lancet 2017]</a>
- ⏱️ Earlier PFS 8.2 vs 5.9 months (HR 0.67; 95% CI 0.54–0.82); ORR 48% vs 36%. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1309748">[NEJM 2014]</a>
- ⚠️ Any-grade fistula 15% vs 1%; grade 3 fistula 6% vs <1%; all patients with fistula had prior pelvic irradiation. <a class="cite" href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28756902/">[Lancet 2017]</a>
- Topotecan–paclitaxel did not improve survival over cisplatin–paclitaxel → reserve for selected circumstances. <a class="cite" href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36898292/">[Gynecol Oncol 2023]</a>
- 💰 A quality-assured bevacizumab biosimilar with a platinum doublet may be practical when immunotherapy is inaccessible, provided toxicity can be monitored.

## 🔟 After Chemoradiation or Immunotherapy — Reconstruct Prior Exposure

- Prior radiosensitising cisplatin → review renal recovery, hearing, neuropathy and treatment-free interval before metastatic platinum selection.
- KEYNOTE-A18 studied pembrolizumab with definitive chemoradiation for high-risk locally advanced disease; it informs prior exposure, not metastatic treatment selection. <a class="cite" href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38521086/">[Lancet 2024]</a>
- Prior pembrolizumab with definitive chemoradiation → document completion date and immune toxicity; relapse during or soon after treatment favours a different drug class or a trial.
- A long treatment-free interval may justify an individual rechallenge discussion → prospective evidence is limited; avoid assuming the metastatic first-line trial answers this question.
- Prior pelvic radiation → review cumulative dose, tissue necrosis, bowel/bladder involvement and instrumentation before bevacizumab, surgery or reirradiation.

## 1️⃣1️⃣ Variant Histology and Special Populations — Adapt the Pathway

- High-grade neuroendocrine carcinoma → specialist pathology review; platinum–etoposide is a conventional backbone. Common-histology trial results cannot automatically be extrapolated.
- Unusual adenocarcinoma → confirm cervical origin and consider molecular profiling; distinguish endometrial and gastrointestinal primaries.
- HIV → coordinate antiretrovirals, infection prevention and interaction checks; controlled infection alone need not preclude cancer treatment.
- Pregnancy, transplant, severe autoimmune disease or major organ failure → multidisciplinary individualisation; trial populations may not represent these patients.
- Cadonilimab plus chemotherapy ± bevacizumab improved outcomes in a Chinese phase 3 trial; local authorisation and availability determine whether this is a practical option. <a class="cite" href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39426385/">[Lancet 2024]</a>

## 1️⃣2️⃣ Palliation and Follow-up — Build a Sustainable India-Aware Plan

- 🩸 Bleeding → stabilise, transfuse if indicated and consider haemostatic radiation or embolisation; account for prior radiation. Obstruction → consider stent or nephrostomy in line with goals.
- Treat pain, discharge, infection, fistula-related skin injury and nutritional decline; integrate palliative, sexual and psychosocial support early.
- Review symptoms and performance status each visit; KEYNOTE-826 imaged every 9 weeks through week 54, then every 12 weeks; individualise and image sooner for deterioration. <a class="cite" href="https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10722390/">[JAMA Oncol 2024]</a> At progression → record prior drugs and residual eye, nerve and renal toxicity. <a class="cite" href="https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10722390/">[JAMA Oncol 2024]</a>
- 🇮🇳 Check current CDSCO indication, procurement and coverage; plan for CPS testing, infusions, eye care, travel, toxicity management and maintenance.

**🔑 Key insights**

- 1️⃣ CPS-positive disease → pembrolizumab plus platinum–paclitaxel; add bevacizumab only when benefits and risks support it.
- 2️⃣ BEATcc supports the bevacizumab-containing atezolizumab quadruplet without PD-L1 selection; local access and safety govern use. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(23)02405-4">[Lancet 2024]</a>
- 3️⃣ After progression → prior checkpoint exposure, eye and nerve toxicity, biomarkers and access determine the next treatment.
- 4️⃣ 💰 A deliverable platinum regimen ± quality-assured bevacizumab is a practical alternative when immunotherapy cannot be funded.
