## Before cycle 1 — classify before combining

- Define the setting: unresectable advanced disease, residual tumour or recurrence requiring systemic treatment. Completely resected disease needs a separate adjuvant assessment; these pathways should not be merged.
- Review histology, including serous, clear-cell, carcinosarcoma and mixed components. Record disease sites, bleeding, pain, bowel symptoms and whether a complete surgical clearance remains plausible.
- Request MMR immunohistochemistry, with MLH1 promoter methylation when indicated by the loss pattern. Arrange hereditary-risk evaluation using the combined pathology and clinical findings.
- Build the molecular classification with POLE and p53 assessment: POLE-mutated, dMMR, p53-abnormal or NSMP. Resolve discordant results rather than selecting whichever label appears most treatment-favourable.
- Obtain ER/PR when an endocrine approach is possible. Assess HER2 in serous disease and carcinosarcoma; biomarker interpretation must follow the relevant tumour and assay context.
- Document marrow, renal and hepatic reserve, baseline neuropathy, autoimmune conditions, transplant history and medication burden. PD-L1 is not a prerequisite for routine chemo-immunotherapy selection.

## Choosing first line — MMR is the branch point

| Situation | Preferred approach | Alternatives / qualification |
|---|---|---|
| dMMR disease needing systemic control | Carboplatin–paclitaxel + pembrolizumab or dostarlimab | Use the chosen regimen’s continuation plan |
| pMMR disease requiring chemotherapy | Discuss carboplatin–paclitaxel + checkpoint blockade | Smaller benefit requires an explicit toxicity/access discussion |
| Checkpoint blockade unsuitable or inaccessible | Carboplatin–paclitaxel | Supportive measures and dose adaptation remain essential |
| Low-grade, receptor-positive, indolent disease | Endocrine treatment may be appropriate | Avoid relying on slow control when organs are threatened |
| HER2-positive serous disease | Consider a trastuzumab-containing pathway | Do not automatically stack HER2 and immune treatment |
| Plausible complete resection | Multidisciplinary surgical assessment | Systemic induction and later reassessment if initially unresectable |

As of November 2026, check the exact local label before selecting a checkpoint or PARP-containing pathway. Evidence from a trial is not permission to exchange maintenance components across regimens, and an overseas indication does not establish Indian approval.

## The evidence — molecular groups matter

- **GOG-209:** Carboplatin–paclitaxel established noninferiority against the more complex paclitaxel–doxorubicin–cisplatin comparator. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1200/JCO.20.01076">[J Clin Oncol 2020]</a>
- **NRG-GY018:** Pembrolizumab with chemotherapy: dMMR PFS at 12 months 74% versus 38%; HR 0.30. <a class="cite" href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36972022/">[N Engl J Med 2023]</a> In pMMR disease, median PFS 13.1 versus 8.7 months; HR 0.54. <a class="cite" href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36972022/">[N Engl J Med 2023]</a>
- **RUBY:** Dostarlimab with chemotherapy: dMMR PFS at 24 months 61.4% versus 15.7%; HR 0.28. <a class="cite" href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36972026/">[N Engl J Med 2023]</a>
- **RUBY survival update:** Overall OS HR 0.69; dMMR HR 0.32. <a class="cite" href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38866180/">[Ann Oncol 2024]</a> The pMMR estimate was 0.79 with a 95% CI of 0.60–1.04, leaving subgroup OS uncertainty. <a class="cite" href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38866180/">[Ann Oncol 2024]</a>
- **DUO-E:** Overall PFS HR 0.71 for durvalumab and 0.55 for durvalumab–olaparib strategies, each compared with the control pathway. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1200/JCO.23.02132">[J Clin Oncol 2024]</a> These comparisons do not isolate a universal incremental PARP effect.

## Pitfalls / pearls

- **MMR loss is actionable information:** establish the result before committing to a long treatment pathway. Methylation testing can clarify hereditary evaluation; it should not be confused with a new checkpoint eligibility test.
- **Subgroup discipline:** an overall survival benefit across the trial population does not prove the same survival benefit within every molecular subgroup. Explain pMMR uncertainty without dismissing its progression-control benefit.
- **Maintenance discipline:** in DUO-E, olaparib belongs to the maintenance strategy. Its results do not justify adding a PARP inhibitor indiscriminately during induction or after any checkpoint regimen.
- **Neuropathy and frailty:** assess walking, falls and daily tasks before paclitaxel. Dose adaptation should protect function and the ability to continue useful treatment.
- **Immune surveillance:** new diarrhoea, breathlessness, jaundice or profound fatigue warrants assessment for infection, endocrine disturbance and immune injury. Provide a route for urgent contact between visits.
- **Local control:** radiation may relieve bleeding or pain while systemic treatment is being organised. Reassess surgery when response makes complete clearance realistic and morbidity acceptable.

<p class="opinion">Opinion: when resources are constrained, obtaining a dependable MMR result is more valuable than choosing a more elaborate regimen without molecular context. Use the result to explain both the expected benefit and the uncertainties.</p>

## After first line — keep the original context

Document prior checkpoint exposure and why it ended before selecting further therapy. A patient progressing during checkpoint treatment differs from a patient who stopped after a planned course or because of toxicity. Revisit ER/PR and HER2 results, disease tempo and the need for another biopsy when the clinical pattern changes.

Endocrine treatment, chemotherapy, matched therapy or a trial may be appropriate depending on prior exposure and molecular findings. Avoid presenting evidence from checkpoint-naive relapse as a proven sequence after frontline chemo-immunotherapy. Reassess local treatment for an isolated symptomatic or potentially resectable recurrence, and maintain symptom support throughout.
