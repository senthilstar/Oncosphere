**HER2-positive metastatic breast cancer — select induction, maintenance and the next line**

## 1️⃣ Initial Assessment — Confirm Metastatic Biology and Organ Threat

- Biopsy an accessible metastatic site when feasible; reassess HER2 IHC/ISH, ER and PR. HER2-positive means IHC 3+ or IHC 2+/ISH amplified; HER2-low disease follows a separate pathway.
- Stage chest/abdomen and bone as appropriate; urgent brain MRI for neurological symptoms. Discuss screening MRI in selected high-risk patients according to local practice and access.
- **Record:**
  - ECOG, disease tempo, symptomatic visceral burden, bone pain and CNS symptoms.
  - Prior taxane, trastuzumab, pertuzumab, T-DM1/T-DXd; dates, recurrence interval, response and residual toxicity.
  - LVEF/cardiac history, respiratory symptoms/ILD, neuropathy and marrow/hepatic reserve.
- Define visceral crisis by organ dysfunction/rapid threat rather than visceral metastases alone; prompt active systemic therapy and parallel supportive care.

## 2️⃣ Fitness and Prior Exposure — Choose a Sustainable Whole Pathway

- Baseline echocardiography/LVEF and serial monitoring, commonly every 3 months during HER2 therapy; individualise with cardiology for pre-existing disease or decline.
- Before T-DXd: review CT chest, prior ILD/pneumonitis, oxygen requirement, lung comorbidity and ability to access urgent respiratory assessment.
- Long interval after adjuvant trastuzumab/taxane → consider untreated metastatic pathways; early relapse after adjuvant treatment or prior ADC → exposure-led selection, not automatic trial extrapolation.
- HR-positive biology permits endocrine maintenance; ovarian suppression is required with aromatase-inhibitor strategies in premenopausal patients.
- Compare T-DXd–pertuzumab with taxane–trastuzumab–pertuzumab (THP) using pulmonary risk, prior exposure, local indication, patient preference and full-course access.
- Explain that ongoing ADC treatment and brief taxane induction followed by antibody maintenance have different toxicity and financial trajectories.

## 3️⃣ Established First Line — Use THP with a Maintenance Plan

**📊 CLEOPATRA: Pertuzumab + Trastuzumab + Docetaxel**

- **Study design:** double-blind phase 3; n=808; previously untreated metastatic HER2-positive disease; pertuzumab versus placebo with trastuzumab–docetaxel.
- Pertuzumab 840 mg IV loading → 420 mg every 3 weeks; trastuzumab 8 mg/kg IV loading → 6 mg/kg every 3 weeks.
- Docetaxel 75 mg/m² IV every 3 weeks; escalation to 100 mg/m² permitted if tolerated; at least 6 cycles recommended → continue antibodies until progression/toxicity.
- End-of-study OS 57.1 vs 40.8 months (HR 0.69; 95% CI 0.58–0.82).
- ⚠️ Grade 3–4 neutropenia 49% vs 46%; monitor febrile neutropenia, diarrhoea, neuropathy and cardiac function.

[Primary publication](https://doi.org/10.1016/S1470-2045(19)30863-0).

- Weekly paclitaxel 80 mg/m² IV is a pragmatic alternative taxane backbone where appropriate; it was not the randomized CLEOPATRA regimen.
- Stop the taxane for cumulative toxicity/after adequate induction when appropriate; maintain HER2 blockade, adding endocrine therapy for HR-positive disease.

**🔑 Key insights**

- 1️⃣ THP has mature OS evidence.
- 2️⃣ Taxane discontinuation does not mean stopping effective HER2 blockade.
- 3️⃣ 💰 Quality-assured trastuzumab biosimilars can improve access.

## 4️⃣ New First-Line Option — Weigh T-DXd–Pertuzumab Benefit and ILD Risk

**📊 DESTINY-Breast09: T-DXd + Pertuzumab ✨**

- **Study design:** phase 3, three arms, n=1,157 overall; reported comparison T-DXd–pertuzumab n=383 versus THP n=387; untreated advanced/metastatic HER2-positive disease.
- T-DXd 5.4 mg/kg IV every 3 weeks + pertuzumab 840 mg loading → 420 mg every 3 weeks, until progression/toxicity; not a time-limited taxane course.
- February 2025 cutoff, published 2026: PFS 40.7 vs 26.9 months (HR 0.56; 95% CI 0.44–0.71); ORR 85.1% vs 78.6%; OS immature.
- ⚠️ Grade ≥3 AEs 63.5% vs 62.3%; adjudicated drug-related ILD/pneumonitis 12.1% vs 1.0%, including two fatal T-DXd events.

[Primary publication](https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2508668).

**🔑 Key insights**

- 1️⃣ Substantial PFS benefit supports an upfront option where indicated.
- 2️⃣ Post-first-line T-DXd sequencing is less well defined than the older ADC-naïve sequence.
- 3️⃣ 💰 Budget for ongoing ADC/pertuzumab and rapid lung assessment before starting.

## 5️⃣ When Preferred Induction Is Unsuitable — Preserve HER2 Blockade

- Pertuzumab inaccessible → trastuzumab + taxane is an access-driven alternative; acknowledge the forgone randomized pertuzumab benefit.
- Major ILD concern or limited pulmonary emergency access → an appropriate THP pathway may be favoured over T-DXd.
- Taxane intolerance → selected trastuzumab-based partner such as vinorelbine or capecitabine, with clear evidence/access qualification; attenuate for frailty.
- Selected indolent HR-positive/HER2-positive disease without organ threat → endocrine therapy plus HER2 blockade when chemotherapy is unsuitable or declined; reassess promptly if control is inadequate.
- Persistent severe frailty/organ dysfunction → symptom-directed management or carefully adapted treatment; do not force a trial-intensity regimen.
- New cardiac dysfunction → hold/modify according to the specific drug label and cardio-oncology assessment; assess reversibility before abandoning the entire HER2 pathway.

## 6️⃣ HR-Positive Maintenance — Consider Palbociclib after Trial-Matched Induction

**📊 PATINA: Palbociclib + Anti-HER2 + Endocrine Therapy**

- **Study design:** open-label phase 3; n=518; HR-positive/HER2-positive advanced disease without progression after 4–8 induction cycles; maintenance with or without palbociclib.
- Palbociclib 125 mg orally daily days 1–21 every 28 days + anti-HER2 therapy and endocrine therapy; continue until progression/toxicity.
- Published analysis, median follow-up 53.5 months: PFS 44.3 vs 29.1 months (HR 0.75; 95% CI 0.59–0.96).
- ⚠️ Grade 3 AEs 79.7% vs 30.6%; grade 4 AEs 10.0% vs 3.6%, mainly neutropenia. CBC surveillance and indicated holds/reductions (125 → 100 → 75 mg) are essential.

[Primary publication](https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2511218).

- These medians start at maintenance randomization, not first induction dose. Do not add them to induction medians or compare them directly with DESTINY-Breast09.
- Findings do not establish palbociclib maintenance after upfront T-DXd–pertuzumab; do not assume HR-negative eligibility.

**🔑 Key insights**

- 1️⃣ Choose maintenance only after confirming disease control and HR status.
- 2️⃣ An additional oral drug adds monitoring and cytopenia burden.
- 3️⃣ 💰 Discuss endocrine therapy and sustainable antibodies even when palbociclib is unaffordable.

## 7️⃣ After THP — Use T-DXd When ADC-Naïve and Pulmonary Safety Permits

**📊 DESTINY-Breast03: T-DXd versus T-DM1**

- **Study design:** open-label phase 3; n=524; previously treated with trastuzumab/taxane; T-DXd 5.4 mg/kg versus T-DM1 3.6 mg/kg IV every 3 weeks.
- Long-term 2024 analysis: investigator-assessed PFS 29.0 vs 7.2 months (HR 0.30; 95% CI 0.24–0.38); OS 52.6 vs 42.7 months (HR 0.73; 95% CI 0.56–0.94).
- 🎯 ORR 78.9% vs 36.9%; grade ≥3 treatment-emergent AEs 58.0% vs 52.1%.
- ⚠️ Adjudicated drug-related ILD 16.7% vs 3.4%; no grade 4–5 ILD in this analysis.

[Primary publication](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41591-024-03021-7).

- After first-line THP → T-DXd is an evidence-supported subsequent strategy if suitable. T-DM1 remains useful when T-DXd is inaccessible/contraindicated or in selected later sequencing.
- After upfront T-DXd → do not recycle the ADC-naïve pathway as prospectively validated; consider tucatinib-based treatment, T-DM1/other active HER2 therapy or a trial according to prior exposure and disease distribution.

## 8️⃣ Brain Metastases — Coordinate CNS and Extracranial Control

**📊 HER2CLIMB: Tucatinib + Trastuzumab + Capecitabine**

- **Study design:** randomized double-blind phase 2; n=612; prior trastuzumab, pertuzumab and T-DM1; allowed active/stable brain metastases; tucatinib versus placebo.
- Tucatinib 300 mg orally twice daily continuously + capecitabine 1,000 mg/m² orally twice daily days 1–14 every 21 days + trastuzumab every 3 weeks.
- Primary report: PFS 7.8 vs 5.6 months (HR 0.54; 95% CI 0.42–0.71); OS 21.9 vs 17.4 months (HR 0.66; 95% CI 0.50–0.88).
- [Final OS update](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.annonc.2021.12.005): 24.7 vs 19.2 months (HR 0.73; 95% CI 0.59–0.90).
- ⚠️ Diarrhoea, palmar–plantar toxicity and transaminase elevation; adjust capecitabine for renal function and review interactions.

[Primary publication](https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1914609).

- Symptomatic, large or threatening lesions → prompt steroids when indicated, neurosurgery/SRS/radiation review; do not defer urgent local control solely to try a systemic drug.
- Selected isolated CNS progression with controlled body disease → local treatment while continuing an effective systemic pathway may be reasonable.
- T-DXd also has intracranial activity, but there is no universal head-to-head CNS ranking against tucatinib; HER2CLIMB largely predates T-DXd exposure.

## 9️⃣ Regional Alternatives — Interpret PHILA against Its Own Comparator

**📊 PHILA: Pyrotinib + Trastuzumab + Docetaxel**

- **Study design:** double-blind phase 3 in China; n=590; untreated HER2-positive metastatic disease; pyrotinib versus placebo with trastuzumab/docetaxel.
- Pyrotinib 400 mg orally daily + trastuzumab 8 → 6 mg/kg IV every 3 weeks + docetaxel 75 mg/m² IV every 3 weeks.
- Initial analysis: investigator PFS 24.3 vs 10.4 months (HR 0.41; 95% CI 0.32–0.53).
- ⚠️ Grade ≥3 treatment-related AEs 90% vs 76%; grade ≥3 diarrhoea 46% vs 3%.
- Comparator lacked pertuzumab; these results do not prove superiority to THP or T-DXd–pertuzumab.

[Primary publication](https://doi.org/10.1136/bmj-2023-076065).

- Use only with current regional indication, reliable supply and diarrhoea management infrastructure; do not assume Chinese availability establishes CDSCO approval.

## 🔟 Other Maintenance and Salvage — Avoid Untested Drug Stacking

**📊 HER2CLIMB-05: Tucatinib + Trastuzumab–Pertuzumab Maintenance**

- **Study design:** double-blind phase 3; n=654; nonprogressing after first-line induction; tucatinib 300 mg orally twice daily versus placebo, with trastuzumab–pertuzumab maintenance.
- Investigator-assessed PFS 24.9 vs 16.3 months (HR 0.641; 95% CI 0.514–0.799); OS immature.
- ⚠️ Tucatinib-arm grade ≥3 diarrhoea 6.1%, ALT elevation 13.5%, AST elevation 7.1%; review liver tests and interactions.
- Maintenance population and time origin differ from upfront induction and HER2CLIMB salvage; check current indication and toxicity plan. [Primary publication](https://doi.org/10.1200/JCO-25-02600).
- Do not combine palbociclib and tucatinib maintenance merely because each has positive evidence; efficacy/safety of the stacked regimen are unestablished.
- Later lines → T-DM1 if appropriate, capecitabine–lapatinib, trastuzumab with another cytotoxic partner, neratinib–capecitabine or trials according to exposure and access.
- Re-biopsy discordant progression when feasible; persistent HER2 expression, organ reserve and patient goals determine whether ongoing HER2 targeting remains appropriate.
- Prior adjuvant T-DM1/T-DXd, rapid relapse and prior metastatic ADC exposure require an individualised sequence; document the reason for each change.

## 1️⃣1️⃣ Toxicity and Special Populations — Act Early to Preserve Options

- 🫁 T-DXd: premedicate with effective antiemetics; monitor CBC and pulmonary symptoms at every visit. New cough, fever or breathlessness → hold immediately and evaluate CT, infection and pneumonitis.
- Suspected grade 1 ILD → interrupt, specialist evaluation and consider steroids; restart only per label after resolution. Grade ≥2 symptomatic ILD → permanently discontinue and promptly start systemic corticosteroids.
- T-DXd dose reductions for relevant toxicity: 5.4 → 4.4 → 3.2 mg/kg; do not re-escalate. T-DM1 requires platelet/LFT and cardiac monitoring; the two ADCs are not interchangeable.
- Taxanes → assess neuropathy, cytopenias and febrile-neutropenia risk; reduce/stop before disability accumulates.
- Older adults → use physiological reserve and goals; premenopausal HR-positive patients → ovarian suppression with appropriate endocrine therapy. Pregnancy requires specialist review because systemic agents pose fetal risk.
- Continue cardiac surveillance; coordinate cardiology management when LVEF falls or heart-failure symptoms emerge.

## 1️⃣2️⃣ Palliation and India Practice — Make the Entire Sequence Affordable

- Painful bone disease → analgesia, selected radiotherapy and bone-modifying agent after dental/renal review; suspected cord compression → urgent MRI and local treatment pathway.
- Assess symptoms/laboratories each cycle and image at clinically appropriate intervals, commonly every 2–3 months; do not change therapy on a marker rise alone.
- 🇮🇳 Prioritise reliable HER2 IHC/ISH and ER/PR testing, cardiac monitoring and quality-assured trastuzumab biosimilars. Broad NGS is not required to establish HER2 eligibility.
- Generic taxanes/endocrine drugs and biosimilar trastuzumab form a lower cost tier; pertuzumab, ADCs and tucatinib add substantial recurring costs. Verify current biosimilar supply rather than assuming availability.
- Check current CDSCO indication separately for induction/maintenance/salvage, plus PM-JAY/state coverage and patient assistance; budget for emergency pulmonary care and repeated LVEF assessment.

**🔑 Key insights**

- 1️⃣ Choose induction and maintenance together, using the correct trial population.
- 2️⃣ CNS activity and prior ADC exposure change later sequencing.
- 3️⃣ 💰 A sustainable trastuzumab-based programme can be more deliverable than a high-cost regimen stopped prematurely.
