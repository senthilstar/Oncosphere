## Before cycle 1 — verify the metastatic biology

- Obtain a metastatic biopsy when feasible and reassess HER2 with IHC/ISH, together with ER and PR. Resolve discordant or equivocal results before anchoring the entire sequence to an old primary-tumour label.
- Establish disease distribution, symptoms and organ threat. Neurological symptoms need prompt brain MRI and an appropriate local-treatment assessment; threatened neurological function should not wait for a routine systemic review.
- Reconstruct prior taxane, trastuzumab, pertuzumab and antibody–drug conjugate exposure. Record why treatment stopped, the interval to recurrence and unresolved cardiac, pulmonary or nerve toxicity.
- Assess cardiac function with baseline LVEF and a plan for repeat monitoring. Review cardiovascular comorbidity, blood pressure, daily function, pregnancy potential and treatment goals.
- Before trastuzumab deruxtecan (T-DXd), review respiratory symptoms, prior lung disease and relevant imaging. Explain how to report new cough, fever or breathlessness and establish a rapid evaluation route.
- Discuss the treatment trajectory: induction, ongoing antibody or ADC treatment, endocrine therapy when relevant and the implications for the next line. The longest reported progression median is not a complete description of that trajectory.

## Choosing first line — select the whole pathway

| Situation | Preferred approach | Alternatives / qualification |
|---|---|---|
| Suitable for T-DXd and pertuzumab; access sustainable | Consider T-DXd + pertuzumab | Weigh pulmonary risk and uncertain later sequencing |
| Established taxane–antibody pathway preferred | Taxane + trastuzumab + pertuzumab (THP) | Mature survival evidence and defined maintenance approaches |
| Pertuzumab inaccessible | Trastuzumab + taxane | Explain the access-driven compromise |
| HR-positive disease after taxane induction | Antibody maintenance + endocrine therapy | Discuss an evidence-matched maintenance addition where appropriate |
| Prior early ADC exposure or rapid relapse | Individualised exposure-led pathway | Do not assume the untreated trial population fits |
| Symptomatic brain disease | Urgent CNS-directed assessment | Coordinate local and systemic treatment |

As of November 2026, trial evidence and local approval must be checked separately for the induction and maintenance choices. These trials support distinct treatment settings; they do not justify mixing all active agents into an untested combination.

## The evidence — induction and maintenance differ

- **CLEOPATRA:** THP versus trastuzumab–taxane with placebo: OS 57.1 versus 40.8 months; HR 0.69. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1016/S1470-2045(19)30863-0">[Lancet Oncol 2020]</a>
- **DESTINY-Breast09:** T-DXd–pertuzumab versus THP: PFS 40.7 versus 26.9 months; HR 0.56. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2508668">[N Engl J Med 2026]</a> Drug-related adjudicated ILD 12.1% versus 1.0%, including 2 deaths in the T-DXd group. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2508668">[N Engl J Med 2026]</a>
- **PATINA:** In HR-positive/HER2-positive maintenance after induction, adding palbociclib gave PFS 44.3 versus 29.1 months; HR 0.75. <a class="cite" href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41604639/">[N Engl J Med 2026]</a>
- **HER2CLIMB-05:** Tucatinib added to trastuzumab–pertuzumab maintenance: PFS 24.9 versus 16.3 months; HR 0.64. <a class="cite" href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41369677/">[J Clin Oncol 2026]</a>

Maintenance trials begin after induction and selection for disease control. Their medians should not be placed beside upfront-treatment medians as if the survival clock and population were identical. PATINA concerns hormone-receptor-positive disease; its findings are not an endocrine-independent strategy for receptor-negative cancer.

## Pitfalls / pearls

- **ILD vigilance:** new cough or breathlessness on T-DXd requires interruption and prompt investigation for drug-related pneumonitis, infection and progression. A reassuring previous visit does not remove the need to investigate new symptoms.
- **Cardiac monitoring:** continue scheduled LVEF review with HER2-directed treatment. Evaluate new oedema, dyspnoea or declining exercise capacity rather than assigning all fatigue to chemotherapy.
- **Taxane duration:** assess neuropathy, marrow tolerance and response when planning transition to maintenance. T-DXd is an ongoing ADC strategy, not a direct substitute for a brief taxane induction followed by the same maintenance plan.
- **Endocrine context:** in receptor-positive disease, include endocrine therapy in the maintenance discussion. Explain the extra monitoring, cytopenia and access implications before considering a palbociclib-containing approach.
- **CNS and body disease:** isolated intracranial progression with controlled extracranial disease may warrant local treatment. It does not automatically mean every effective systemic component must be abandoned.
- **Avoid unsupported stacking:** PATINA and HER2CLIMB-05 do not establish a combined palbociclib–tucatinib maintenance regimen. Apply the selected trial’s setting and safety requirements.

<p class="opinion">Opinion: discuss the pulmonary emergency plan before choosing T-DXd. A patient who cannot access rapid respiratory assessment may reasonably value the established taxane–antibody pathway differently from a patient with immediate specialist support.</p>

## After first line — prior ADC exposure changes the question

After THP, T-DXd can be considered if not previously used and pulmonary safety permits. Progression after initial T-DXd plus pertuzumab leaves a less well-defined sequence; do not present the older ADC-naive pathway as prospectively validated in this population.

Review CNS involvement, receptor findings, prior toxicity and available trials. A tucatinib–trastuzumab–capecitabine approach or another HER2-directed strategy may be appropriate according to prior exposure and local eligibility. Re-biopsy when feasible if the disease pattern raises concern about biological change. Preserve cardiac and pulmonary information at every handover, and continue symptom support alongside active therapy.
