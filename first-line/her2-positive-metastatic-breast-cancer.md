**HER2-positive metastatic breast cancer — select induction, maintenance and the next line**

## 1️⃣ Initial Assessment — Confirm Biology and Organ Threat

- Biopsy an accessible metastatic site when feasible; reassess HER2 IHC/ISH, ER and PR. HER2-positive means IHC 3+ or IHC 2+/ISH amplified; record the exact IHC score and reassess borderline or heterogeneous tissue; HER2-low (IHC 1+ or IHC 2+/ISH-negative) and ultralow (IHC 0 with detectable membrane staining) follow separate eligibility pathways. <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/761139s041s043lbl.pdf">[FDA 2026]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://www.fda.gov/drugs/drug-approvals-and-databases/fda-approves-fam-trastuzumab-deruxtecan-nxki-pertuzumab-unresectable-or-metastatic-her2-positive">[FDA 2025]</a>
- Stage chest/abdomen and bone as appropriate; urgent brain MRI for neurological symptoms. Discuss screening MRI in selected high-risk patients according to local practice and access.
- **Record:**
  - ECOG, disease tempo, symptomatic visceral burden, bone pain and CNS symptoms.
  - Prior taxane, trastuzumab, pertuzumab, T-DM1/T-DXd; dates, recurrence interval, response and residual toxicity.
  - LVEF/cardiac history, respiratory symptoms/ILD, neuropathy and marrow/hepatic reserve.
- Define visceral crisis by organ dysfunction/rapid threat rather than visceral metastases alone; prompt active systemic therapy and parallel supportive care.

**🔑 Key insights**

- 1️⃣ 🎯 Match the decision to disease extent and prior treatment.
- 2️⃣ 💰 Check access to the treatment and required monitoring.

## 2️⃣ Fitness and Biomarkers — Choose a Sustainable Pathway

- Baseline echocardiography/LVEF; trastuzumab label recommends reassessment every 3 months during treatment. Individualise for drug, cardiac risk and dysfunction with cardiology. <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/103792s5359lbl.pdf">[FDA 2026]</a>
- Before T-DXd: review CT chest, prior ILD/pneumonitis, oxygen requirement, lung comorbidity and ability to access urgent respiratory assessment.
- Long interval after adjuvant trastuzumab/taxane → consider untreated metastatic pathways; early relapse after adjuvant treatment or prior ADC → exposure-led selection, not automatic trial extrapolation.
- HR-positive biology permits endocrine maintenance; ovarian suppression is required with aromatase-inhibitor strategies in premenopausal patients.
- Compare T-DXd–pertuzumab with taxane–trastuzumab–pertuzumab (THP) using pulmonary risk, prior exposure, local indication, patient preference and full-course access.
- Explain that ongoing ADC treatment and brief taxane induction followed by antibody maintenance have different toxicity and financial trajectories.

**🔑 Key insights**

- 1️⃣ 🎯 Match the decision to disease extent and prior treatment.
- 2️⃣ 💰 Check access to the treatment and required monitoring.

## 3️⃣ Preferred First Line — Weigh T-DXd and Pertuzumab

**📊 DESTINY-Breast09: T-DXd + Pertuzumab ✨**

- **Study design:** phase 3, three arms, n=1,157 overall; reported comparison T-DXd–pertuzumab n=383 versus THP n=387; untreated advanced/metastatic HER2-positive disease. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2508668">[NEJM 2026]</a>
- T-DXd 5.4 mg/kg IV every 3 weeks + pertuzumab 840 mg loading → 420 mg every 3 weeks, until progression/toxicity; not a time-limited taxane course. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2508668">[NEJM 2026]</a>
- February 2025 cutoff, published 2026: blinded independent review PFS 40.7 vs 26.9 months (HR 0.56; 95% CI 0.44–0.71); ORR 85.1% vs 78.6%; OS immature; no proven OS advantage for upfront T-DXd over THP followed by T-DXd. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2508668">[NEJM 2026]</a>
- ⚠️ Grade ≥3 AEs 63.5% vs 62.3%; adjudicated drug-related ILD/pneumonitis 12.1% vs 1.0%, including two fatal T-DXd events. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2508668">[NEJM 2026]</a>

- 🎯 FDA measurable-disease ORR 87% vs 81%; the journal’s 85.1% vs 78.6% uses all randomized patients, so these are different denominators, not an update. <a class="cite" href="https://www.fda.gov/drugs/drug-approvals-and-databases/fda-approves-fam-trastuzumab-deruxtecan-nxki-pertuzumab-unresectable-or-metastatic-her2-positive">[FDA 2025]</a>
- 🇺🇸 FDA approved this first-line combination in December 2025; local availability and indications vary. <a class="cite" href="https://www.fda.gov/drugs/drug-approvals-and-databases/fda-approves-fam-trastuzumab-deruxtecan-nxki-pertuzumab-unresectable-or-metastatic-her2-positive">[FDA 2025]</a>

**🔑 Key insights**

- 1️⃣ Substantial PFS benefit supports an upfront option where indicated. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2508668">[NEJM 2026]</a>
- 2️⃣ Sequencing after first-line T-DXd is less well defined than the older ADC-naïve sequence. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2508668">[NEJM 2026]</a>
- 3️⃣ 💰 Budget for ongoing ADC/pertuzumab and rapid lung assessment before starting. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2508668">[NEJM 2026]</a>

## 4️⃣ When Upfront ADC Is Unsuitable — Choose THP or Adapt

**📊 CLEOPATRA: Pertuzumab + Trastuzumab + Docetaxel**

- **Study design:** double-blind phase 3; n=808; previously untreated metastatic HER2-positive disease; pertuzumab versus placebo with trastuzumab–docetaxel. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1016/S1470-2045(19)30863-0">[Lancet Oncol 2020]</a>
- Pertuzumab 840 mg IV loading → 420 mg every 3 weeks; trastuzumab 8 mg/kg IV loading → 6 mg/kg every 3 weeks. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1016/S1470-2045(19)30863-0">[Lancet Oncol 2020]</a>
- Docetaxel 75 mg/m² IV every 3 weeks; escalation to 100 mg/m² permitted if tolerated; at least 6 cycles recommended → continue antibodies until progression/toxicity. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1016/S1470-2045(19)30863-0">[Lancet Oncol 2020]</a>
- 🎯 Measurable-disease ORR 80.2% vs 69.3%. <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/125409s141lbl.pdf">[FDA 2026]</a>
- ⏱️ Investigator PFS 18.7 vs 12.4 months (HR 0.69; 95% CI 0.58–0.81). <a class="cite" href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23602601/">[Lancet Oncol 2013]</a>
- End-of-study OS 57.1 vs 40.8 months (HR 0.69; 95% CI 0.58–0.82). <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1016/S1470-2045(19)30863-0">[Lancet Oncol 2020]</a>
- ⚠️ Grade 3–4 neutropenia 49% vs 46%; monitor febrile neutropenia, diarrhoea, neuropathy and cardiac function. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1016/S1470-2045(19)30863-0">[Lancet Oncol 2020]</a>

- Paclitaxel 80 mg/m² IV weekly with trastuzumab–pertuzumab is an alternative supported by a single-arm study; it was not the randomized CLEOPATRA taxane. <a class="cite" href="https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6693713/">[Oncologist 2019]</a>
- Stop the taxane for cumulative toxicity/after adequate induction when appropriate; maintain HER2 blockade, adding endocrine therapy for HR-positive disease. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1016/S1470-2045(19)30863-0">[Lancet Oncol 2020]</a>

- Pertuzumab inaccessible → trastuzumab + taxane is an access-driven alternative; acknowledge the forgone randomized pertuzumab benefit.
- Major ILD concern or limited pulmonary emergency access → an appropriate THP pathway may be favoured over T-DXd.
- Taxane intolerance → selected trastuzumab-based partner such as vinorelbine or capecitabine, with clear evidence/access qualification; attenuate for frailty.
- Selected indolent HR-positive/HER2-positive disease without organ threat → endocrine therapy plus HER2 blockade when chemotherapy is unsuitable or declined; reassess promptly if control is inadequate.
- Persistent severe frailty/organ dysfunction → symptom-directed management or carefully adapted treatment; do not force a trial-intensity regimen.
- New cardiac dysfunction → hold/modify according to the specific drug label and cardio-oncology assessment; assess reversibility before abandoning the entire HER2 pathway.

**🔑 Key insights**

- 1️⃣ THP has mature OS evidence. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1016/S1470-2045(19)30863-0">[Lancet Oncol 2020]</a>
- 2️⃣ Taxane discontinuation does not mean stopping effective HER2 blockade. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1016/S1470-2045(19)30863-0">[Lancet Oncol 2020]</a>
- 3️⃣ 💰 Quality-assured trastuzumab biosimilars can improve access. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1016/S1470-2045(19)30863-0">[Lancet Oncol 2020]</a>

## 5️⃣ Maintenance — Consider Tucatinib after Induction

**📊 HER2CLIMB-05: Tucatinib + Trastuzumab–Pertuzumab Maintenance**

- **Study design:** double-blind phase 3; n=654; any HR status, nonprogressing after 4–8 cycles of taxane plus trastuzumab–pertuzumab induction; fewer than 6 taxane cycles required intolerance; tucatinib 300 mg orally twice daily versus placebo, with trastuzumab–pertuzumab maintenance. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1200/JCO-25-02600">[J Clin Oncol 2026]</a>
- Investigator-assessed PFS 24.9 vs 16.3 months (HR 0.641; 95% CI 0.514–0.799); OS immature. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1200/JCO-25-02600">[J Clin Oncol 2026]</a>
- ⚠️ Grade ≥3 treatment-emergent AEs 42.3% vs 24.4%; tucatinib-arm grade ≥3 diarrhoea 6.1%, ALT elevation 13.5%, AST elevation 7.1%; review liver tests and interactions. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1200/JCO-25-02600">[J Clin Oncol 2026]</a>
- Maintenance population and time origin differ from upfront induction and HER2CLIMB salvage; check current indication and toxicity plan. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1200/JCO-25-02600">[J Clin Oncol 2026]</a>
- 🇺🇸 FDA approved tucatinib with trastuzumab–pertuzumab after induction in October 2026; its label carries a hepatotoxicity boxed warning → monitor liver tests and follow interruption and discontinuation instructions. <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/213411s010lbl.pdf">[FDA 2026]</a>
- Tucatinib: check ALT, AST and bilirubin before treatment, every 2 weeks for the first 2 months, then every 3 weeks and as indicated. ALT/AST >3 × ULN with bilirubin >2 × ULN → permanently discontinue; follow the label for other abnormalities and rechallenge. <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/213411s010lbl.pdf">[FDA 2026]</a>
- PATINA and HER2CLIMB-05 have different populations and designs → do not compare maintenance PFS across trials. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1200/JCO-25-02600">[J Clin Oncol 2026]</a>
- Do not combine palbociclib and tucatinib maintenance merely because each has positive evidence; efficacy/safety of the stacked regimen are unestablished.
- Later lines → T-DM1 if appropriate, capecitabine–lapatinib, trastuzumab with another cytotoxic partner, neratinib–capecitabine or trials according to exposure and access. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1200/JCO-25-02600">[J Clin Oncol 2026]</a>
- Re-biopsy discordant progression when feasible; persistent HER2 expression, organ reserve and patient goals determine whether ongoing HER2 targeting remains appropriate. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1200/JCO-25-02600">[J Clin Oncol 2026]</a>
- Prior adjuvant T-DM1/T-DXd, rapid relapse and prior metastatic ADC exposure require an individualised sequence; document the reason for each change. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1200/JCO-25-02600">[J Clin Oncol 2026]</a>

**🔑 Key insights**

- 1️⃣ 🎯 Match the decision to disease extent and prior treatment.
- 2️⃣ 💰 Check access to the treatment and required monitoring.

## 6️⃣ Progression — Sequence by Prior ADC Exposure

**📊 DESTINY-Breast03: T-DXd versus T-DM1**

- **Study design:** open-label phase 3; n=524; previously treated with trastuzumab/taxane; T-DXd 5.4 mg/kg versus T-DM1 3.6 mg/kg IV every 3 weeks. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1038/s41591-024-03021-7">[Nat Med 2024]</a>
- Earlier 2024 analysis: investigator PFS 29.0 vs 7.2 months (HR 0.30; 95% CI 0.24–0.38); OS 52.6 vs 42.7 months (HR 0.73; 95% CI 0.56–0.94). <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1038/s41591-024-03021-7">[Nat Med 2024]</a>
- Earlier grade ≥3 treatment-emergent AEs 58.0% vs 52.1%; adjudicated drug-related ILD 16.7% vs 3.4%, with no grade 4–5 ILD. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1038/s41591-024-03021-7">[Nat Med 2024]</a>
- ⏱️ Final investigator PFS 29.0 vs 7.8 months (HR 0.33; 95% CI 0.26–0.41; final analysis). <a class="cite" href="https://aacrjournals.org/clincancerres/article/32/4_Supplement/PS5-01-30/773588">[Clin Cancer Res 2026]</a>
- 🫀 Final OS 56.4 vs 42.7 months (HR 0.74; 95% CI 0.59–0.94; final analysis). <a class="cite" href="https://aacrjournals.org/clincancerres/article/32/4_Supplement/PS5-01-30/773588">[Clin Cancer Res 2026]</a>
- 🎯 Investigator ORR 78.9% vs 36.9%. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1038/s41591-024-03021-7">[Nat Med 2024]</a>
- ⚠️ Final grade ≥3 treatment-emergent AEs 60.3% vs 52.1%. <a class="cite" href="https://aacrjournals.org/clincancerres/article/32/4_Supplement/PS5-01-30/773588">[Clin Cancer Res 2026]</a>
- ⚠️ Drug-related ILD 17.5% vs 3.1%, no grade 4–5. <a class="cite" href="https://aacrjournals.org/clincancerres/article/32/4_Supplement/PS5-01-30/773588">[Clin Cancer Res 2026]</a>

- After first-line THP → T-DXd is the preferred subsequent strategy if suitable. T-DM1 remains useful when T-DXd is inaccessible/contraindicated or in selected later sequencing. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1038/s41591-024-03021-7">[Nat Med 2024]</a>
- After upfront T-DXd → do not recycle the ADC-naïve pathway as prospectively validated; consider tucatinib-based treatment, T-DM1/other active HER2 therapy or a trial according to prior exposure and disease distribution. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1038/s41591-024-03021-7">[Nat Med 2024]</a>

**🔑 Key insights**

- 1️⃣ 🎯 Match the decision to disease extent and prior treatment.
- 2️⃣ 💰 Check access to the treatment and required monitoring.

## 7️⃣ HR-Positive Disease — Consider Palbociclib Maintenance

**📊 PATINA: Palbociclib + Anti-HER2 + Endocrine Therapy**

- **Study design:** open-label phase 3; n=518; HR-positive/HER2-positive advanced disease without progression after 4–8 induction cycles; maintenance with or without palbociclib. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2511218">[NEJM 2026]</a>
- Palbociclib 125 mg orally daily days 1–21 every 28 days + anti-HER2 therapy and endocrine therapy; continue until progression/toxicity. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2511218">[NEJM 2026]</a>
- Published analysis, median follow-up 53.5 months: PFS 44.3 vs 29.1 months (HR 0.75; 95% CI 0.59–0.96). <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2511218">[NEJM 2026]</a>
- FDA analysis: PFS HR 0.76 (95% CI 0.59–0.97); the label cautions that censoring limits interpretation of the medians. OS was immature. <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/207103s024lbl.pdf">[FDA 2026]</a>
- ⚠️ Grade 3 AEs 79.7% vs 30.6%; grade 4 AEs 10.0% vs 3.6%, mainly neutropenia. CBC surveillance and indicated holds/reductions (125 → 100 → 75 mg) are essential. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2511218">[NEJM 2026]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/207103s024lbl.pdf">[FDA 2026]</a>

- CBC before palbociclib, at each cycle start and on day 15 of the first 2 cycles; adjust for neutropenia per label. <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/207103s024lbl.pdf">[FDA 2026]</a>
- These medians start at maintenance randomization, not first induction dose. Do not add them to induction medians or compare them directly with DESTINY-Breast09. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2511218">[NEJM 2026]</a>
- Findings do not establish palbociclib maintenance after upfront T-DXd–pertuzumab; do not assume HR-negative eligibility.

**🔑 Key insights**

- 1️⃣ Choose maintenance only after confirming disease control and HR status. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2511218">[NEJM 2026]</a>
- 2️⃣ An additional oral drug adds monitoring and cytopenia burden. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2511218">[NEJM 2026]</a>
- 3️⃣ 💰 Discuss endocrine therapy and sustainable antibodies even when palbociclib is unaffordable. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2511218">[NEJM 2026]</a>

## 8️⃣ Brain Metastases — Coordinate CNS and Systemic Control

**📊 HER2CLIMB: Tucatinib + Trastuzumab + Capecitabine**

- **Study design:** randomized double-blind phase 2; n=612; prior trastuzumab, pertuzumab and T-DM1; allowed active/stable brain metastases; tucatinib versus placebo. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1914609">[NEJM 2020]</a>
- Tucatinib 300 mg orally twice daily continuously + capecitabine 1,000 mg/m² orally twice daily days 1–14 every 21 days + trastuzumab every 3 weeks. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1914609">[NEJM 2020]</a>
- Primary report: PFS 7.8 vs 5.6 months (HR 0.54; 95% CI 0.42–0.71); OS 21.9 vs 17.4 months (HR 0.66; 95% CI 0.50–0.88). <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1914609">[NEJM 2020]</a>
- 🫀 Updated final OS 24.7 vs 19.2 months (HR 0.73; 95% CI 0.59–0.90). <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1016/j.annonc.2021.12.005">[Ann Oncol 2022]</a>
- 🎯 Measurable-disease ORR 40.6% vs 22.8%; grade ≥3 adverse events 55.2% vs 48.7%. <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/213411s010lbl.pdf">[FDA 2026]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2020/213411Orig1s000MultidisciplineR.pdf">[FDA 2020]</a>
- ⚠️ Diarrhoea, palmar–plantar toxicity and transaminase elevation; adjust capecitabine for renal function and review interactions.

- Symptomatic, large or threatening lesions → prompt steroids when indicated, neurosurgery/SRS/radiation review; do not defer urgent local control solely to try a systemic drug.
- Selected isolated CNS progression with controlled body disease → local treatment while continuing an effective systemic pathway may be reasonable.
- 📊 HER2CLIMB active measurable brain metastases → intracranial ORR 47.3% vs 20.0%; exploratory updated analysis. <a class="cite" href="https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jamaoncology/fullarticle/2799133">[JAMA Oncol 2023]</a>
- 📊 DESTINY-Breast12: single-arm T-DXd in previously treated disease; brain-metastasis cohort n=263 → CNS ORR 71.7% in measurable CNS disease; no randomized comparator. <a class="cite" href="https://aacrjournals.org/clincancerres/article/31/12_Supplement/PS14-10/753287/Abstract-PS14-10-Effects-of-trastuzumab-deruxtecan">[Clin Cancer Res 2025]</a>
- Coordinate stereotactic radiation and ADC timing with radiation oncology; overlapping toxicity and optimal timing remain uncertain.
- T-DXd also has intracranial activity, but there is no universal head-to-head CNS ranking against tucatinib; HER2CLIMB largely predates T-DXd exposure. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1914609">[NEJM 2020]</a>

**🔑 Key insights**

- 1️⃣ 🎯 Match the decision to disease extent and prior treatment.
- 2️⃣ 💰 Check access to the treatment and required monitoring.

## 9️⃣ Regional Alternatives — Interpret PHILA against Its Comparator

**📊 PHILA: Pyrotinib + Trastuzumab + Docetaxel**

- **Study design:** double-blind phase 3 in China; n=590; untreated HER2-positive metastatic disease; pyrotinib versus placebo with trastuzumab/docetaxel. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1136/bmj-2023-076065">[BMJ 2023]</a>
- Pyrotinib 400 mg orally daily + trastuzumab 8 → 6 mg/kg IV every 3 weeks + docetaxel 75 mg/m² IV every 3 weeks. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1136/bmj-2023-076065">[BMJ 2023]</a>
- Initial investigator PFS 24.3 vs 10.4 months (HR 0.41; 95% CI 0.32–0.53). <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1136/bmj-2023-076065">[BMJ 2023]</a>
- ⏱️ Final investigator PFS 22.1 vs 10.5 months (HR 0.44; 95% CI 0.36–0.53). <a class="cite" href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41839514/">[BMJ 2026]</a>
- 🫀 OS medians not reached; HR 0.64 (95% CI 0.46–0.89). <a class="cite" href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41839514/">[BMJ 2026]</a>
- 🎯 Investigator ORR 83% vs 71% in the initial analysis. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1136/bmj-2023-076065">[BMJ 2023]</a>
- ⚠️ Grade ≥3 treatment-related AEs 90% vs 76%; grade ≥3 diarrhoea 46% vs 3%. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1136/bmj-2023-076065">[BMJ 2023]</a>
- Comparator lacked pertuzumab; these results do not prove superiority to THP or T-DXd–pertuzumab.

- Use only with current regional indication, reliable supply and diarrhoea management infrastructure; do not assume Chinese availability establishes CDSCO approval.

**🔑 Key insights**

- 1️⃣ 🎯 Match the decision to disease extent and prior treatment.
- 2️⃣ 💰 Check access to the treatment and required monitoring.

## 🔟 Toxicity — Act Early to Preserve Options

- 🫁 T-DXd: premedicate with effective antiemetics; monitor CBC and pulmonary symptoms at every visit. New cough, fever or breathlessness → hold immediately and evaluate CT, infection and pneumonitis.
- Suspected grade 1 ILD → interrupt, specialist evaluation and consider steroids; restart only per label after resolution. Grade ≥2 symptomatic ILD → permanently discontinue and promptly start systemic corticosteroids. <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/761139s041s043lbl.pdf">[FDA 2026]</a>
- T-DXd dose reductions for relevant toxicity: 5.4 → 4.4 → 3.2 mg/kg; do not re-escalate. T-DM1 requires platelet/LFT and cardiac monitoring; the two ADCs are not interchangeable. <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/761139s041s043lbl.pdf">[FDA 2026]</a>
- Taxanes → assess neuropathy, cytopenias and febrile-neutropenia risk; reduce/stop before disability accumulates.
- Older adults → use physiological reserve and goals; premenopausal HR-positive patients → ovarian suppression with appropriate endocrine therapy. Pregnancy requires specialist review because systemic agents pose fetal risk.
- Continue cardiac surveillance; coordinate cardiology management when LVEF falls or heart-failure symptoms emerge.

**🔑 Key insights**

- 1️⃣ 🎯 Match the decision to disease extent and prior treatment.
- 2️⃣ 💰 Check access to the treatment and required monitoring.

## 1️⃣1️⃣ Special Populations — Adapt Treatment to Reserve and Prior Exposure

- 🎯 Early relapse after adjuvant HER2 therapy or prior ADC → reassess drug-free interval, prior response and organ reserve; trial populations may not match.
- Older or frail patients → individualise intensity to function and goals; pregnancy → multidisciplinary assessment of fetal and maternal risks.
- HR-positive premenopausal patients → ovarian suppression with aromatase inhibitor-based endocrine treatment.

**🔑 Key insights**

- 1️⃣ Prior exposure changes the likely benefit of retreatment.
- 2️⃣ 💰 Match monitoring and treatment intensity to sustainable access.

## 1️⃣2️⃣ Palliation and India Practice — Make the Entire Sequence Affordable

- Painful bone disease → analgesia, selected radiotherapy and bone-modifying agent after dental/renal review; suspected cord compression → urgent MRI and local treatment pathway.
- Assess symptoms/laboratories each cycle; evaluate response every 2–4 months, including the previously used 2–3-month interval when appropriate, tailored to disease tempo and treatment; investigate new symptoms promptly. Do not change therapy on a marker rise alone. <a class="cite" href="https://www.annalsofoncology.org/article/S0923-7534%2826%2900902-6/abstract">[Ann Oncol 2026]</a>
- 🇮🇳 Prioritise reliable HER2 IHC/ISH and ER/PR testing, cardiac monitoring and quality-assured trastuzumab biosimilars. Broad NGS is not required to establish HER2 eligibility.
- Generic taxanes/endocrine drugs and biosimilar trastuzumab form a lower cost tier; pertuzumab, ADCs and tucatinib add substantial recurring costs. Verify current biosimilar supply rather than assuming availability.
- Check current CDSCO indication separately for induction/maintenance/salvage, plus PM-JAY/state coverage and patient assistance; budget for emergency pulmonary care and repeated LVEF assessment.

**🔑 Key insights**

- 1️⃣ Choose induction and maintenance together, using the correct trial population.
- 2️⃣ CNS activity and prior ADC exposure change later sequencing.
- 3️⃣ 💰 A sustainable trastuzumab-based programme can be more deliverable than a high-cost regimen stopped prematurely.
