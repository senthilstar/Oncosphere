**Locally advanced pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma — first-line management**

## 1️⃣ Initial Assessment — Establish Anatomy and Exclude Metastases

- 🎯 Biopsy-confirm pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma before systemic treatment → distinguish anatomical unresectability from medical inoperability and borderline resectability.
- Pancreas-protocol multiphasic CT and chest imaging → document arterial contact, venous occlusion and reconstructibility; liver MRI for indeterminate lesions. PET-CT does not replace anatomical staging.
- **Record:**
  - ECOG, weight, intake, sarcopenia, pain, diabetes and baseline neuropathy.
  - CBC, renal function, bilirubin, liver tests, albumin and CA19-9 after biliary drainage; cholestasis and cholangitis confound the marker, and some patients do not secrete it.
- Consider staging laparoscopy for suspected occult peritoneal disease or before major conversion surgery → expert pancreatic multidisciplinary review.

## 2️⃣ Fitness and Biomarkers — Choose a Deliverable Induction

- 🧬 Offer germline testing, including BRCA1/2 and PALB2, with counselling; preserve tumour tissue for MSI/MMR and broad molecular profiling.
- Record tumour contact in degrees, vessel contour, thrombosis and reconstructibility with expert radiology and surgical review. Resectable anatomy has no major arterial contact and limited venous contact without contour irregularity.
- Borderline pancreatic-head anatomy can include superior mesenteric artery contact ≤180°, selected reconstructible common hepatic artery contact or reconstructible venous involvement; >180° superior mesenteric or coeliac artery contact or unreconstructible portal–mesenteric occlusion suggests locally advanced disease. Body/tail anatomy and vascular reconstruction need specialist review. <a class="cite" href="https://www.xiahepublishing.com/2835-3315/CSP-2025-00029">[Cancer Screen Prev 2026]</a>
- Correct obstruction, infection and malnutrition → reassess performance status and organ reserve before choosing intensity.
- Fit, good organ function → modified FOLFIRINOX or gemcitabine–nab-paclitaxel; impaired reserve → individualised attenuation or gemcitabine alone; persistent poor performance → symptom-directed care.

## 3️⃣ Fit Patients — Start FOLFIRINOX and Plan Reassessment

- 💊 Modified FOLFIRINOX → oxaliplatin, irinotecan, leucovorin and infusional fluorouracil; modified protocols often omit the fluorouracil bolus and reduce irinotecan. Adjust for marrow reserve, bilirubin, neuropathy and supportive care access.

**📊 PRODIGE 4/ACCORD 11: FOLFIRINOX versus gemcitabine in metastatic disease ✨**

- **Study design:** phase 3; n=342; untreated metastatic disease, ECOG 0–1. <a class="cite" href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21561347/">[N Engl J Med 2011]</a>
- Original FOLFIRINOX every 14 days: oxaliplatin 85 mg/m², irinotecan 180 mg/m², leucovorin 400 mg/m², fluorouracil 400 mg/m² bolus then 2,400 mg/m² over 46 hours. <a class="cite" href="https://ascopubs.org/doi/10.1200/jco.2010.28.15_suppl.4010">[J Clin Oncol 2010]</a>
- ⏱️ OS 11.1 vs 6.8 months (HR 0.57; 95% CI 0.45–0.73); PFS 6.4 vs 3.3 months (HR 0.47; 95% CI 0.37–0.59); ORR 31.6% vs 9.4%. <a class="cite" href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21561347/">[N Engl J Med 2011]</a>
- ⚠️ Grade 3–4 neutropenia 45.7% and febrile neutropenia 5.4% with FOLFIRINOX. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(23)01366-1">[Lancet 2023]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21561347/">[N Engl J Med 2011]</a>
- A modified protocol may omit the bolus and use irinotecan 150 mg/m² with oxaliplatin 85 mg/m², leucovorin 400 mg/m² and infusional fluorouracil 2,400 mg/m² every 2 weeks; the cited regimen was tested after resection. Individualise induction and G-CSF by risk. <a class="cite" href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36048453/">[JAMA Oncol 2022]</a>

**📊 FOLFIRINOX: LAPC patient-level analysis ✨**

- **Study design:** pooled nonrandomised cohorts; survival analysis included 315 patients across 11 studies; subsequent local therapy and surgical selection varied. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1016/S1470-2045(16)00172-8">[Lancet Oncol 2016]</a>
- **Key outcomes:**
  - ⏱️ Median OS 24.2 months (95% CI 21.7–26.8); PFS 15.0 months (95% CI 13.8–16.2). <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1016/S1470-2045(16)00172-8">[Lancet Oncol 2016]</a>
  - 🎯 Pooled resection 25.9%; among resected patients, 78.4% had R0 margins → selected subgroup, not the probability of R0 for everyone starting treatment. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1016/S1470-2045(16)00172-8">[Lancet Oncol 2016]</a>
- ⚠️ Across mixed cohorts, 296 grade 3–4 events in 490 patients (60.4 events per 100 patients); this is an event rate, not a patient toxicity percentage. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1016/S1470-2045(16)00172-8">[Lancet Oncol 2016]</a>
- Heterogeneous cohorts → no randomised superiority claim against gemcitabine–nab-paclitaxel; monitor cytopenias, diarrhoea and neuropathy.
- 🔁 Repeat pancreas-protocol CT and chest imaging with symptoms, weight, fitness and CA19-9; multidisciplinary resectability review every 2–3 months, adjusted for tolerance and biology. <a class="cite" href="https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12546840/">[ESMO Open 2025]</a>

**🔑 Key insights**

- 1️⃣ LAPC cohort survival cannot be compared directly with metastatic trial survival.
- 2️⃣ 💰 Include infusion access, supportive medicines and reassessment when selecting treatment.

## 4️⃣ Alternative Induction — Choose Gemcitabine–Nab-Paclitaxel

**📊 MPACT: Gemcitabine–nab-paclitaxel versus gemcitabine in metastatic disease ✨**

- **Study design:** phase 3; n=861; untreated metastatic disease. <a class="cite" href="https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4631139/">[N Engl J Med 2013]</a>
- Nab-paclitaxel 125 mg/m² followed by gemcitabine 1,000 mg/m² IV on days 1, 8 and 15 every 28 days. <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2020/021660s047lbl.pdf">[FDA 2020]</a>
- ⏱️ Updated OS 8.7 vs 6.6 months (HR 0.72; 95% CI 0.62–0.83). <a class="cite" href="https://academic.oup.com/jnci/article-abstract/107/2/dju413/902453">[J Natl Cancer Inst 2015]</a>
- ⏱️ Original PFS 5.5 vs 3.7 months (HR 0.69; 95% CI 0.58–0.82); independently assessed ORR 23% vs 7%. <a class="cite" href="https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4631139/">[N Engl J Med 2013]</a>
- ⚠️ Grade ≥3 neutropenia 38% vs 27%; neuropathy 17% vs 1%. <a class="cite" href="https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4631139/">[N Engl J Med 2013]</a>

**📊 LAPACT: Gemcitabine–nab-paclitaxel in LAPC ✨**

- **Study design:** single-arm phase 2; n=107; nab-paclitaxel 125 mg/m² plus gemcitabine 1,000 mg/m² on days 1, 8 and 15 every 28 days, for six planned induction cycles. <a class="cite" href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31953079/">[Lancet Gastroenterol Hepatol 2020]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2020/021660s047lbl.pdf">[FDA 2020]</a>
- **Key outcomes:**
  - ⏱️ Median PFS 10.9 months (90% CI 9.3–11.6) and OS 18.8 months (90% CI 15.0–24.0); no comparator or comparative HR. <a class="cite" href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31953079/">[Lancet Gastroenterol Hepatol 2020]</a>
  - 🎯 Induction ORR 33.6%, disease control 77.6%; 58% completed induction, 17 underwent surgery and seven had R0 resection. <a class="cite" href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31953079/">[Lancet Gastroenterol Hepatol 2020]</a>
- ⚠️ Grade ≥3 induction neutropenia 33%, anaemia 11% and fatigue 10%. <a class="cite" href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31953079/">[Lancet Gastroenterol Hepatol 2020]</a>
- Conventional paclitaxel is not an interchangeable substitute; adjust for cytopenias, neuropathy and hepatic dysfunction.

**📊 NEOLAP: Continue induction versus switch to FOLFIRINOX ✨**

- **Study design:** phase 2; n=130 randomised after two cycles of gemcitabine–nab-paclitaxel → two further cycles versus four cycles of FOLFIRINOX. <a class="cite" href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33338442/">[Lancet Gastroenterol Hepatol 2021]</a>
- 🎯 Macroscopic surgical conversion 35.9% vs 43.9%; median OS 18.5 vs 20.7 months (HR for sequential treatment 0.86; 95% CI 0.55–1.36). <a class="cite" href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33338442/">[Lancet Gastroenterol Hepatol 2021]</a>
- ⚠️ Grade ≥3 induction adverse events 55% vs 53%; pathological downstaging improved in selected specimens, but conversion and survival did not significantly improve. <a class="cite" href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33338442/">[Lancet Gastroenterol Hepatol 2021]</a>
- 🔁 No routine switch solely to expose a responding tumour to both regimens; this was not an upfront regimen comparison.

**🔑 Key insights**

- 1️⃣ Choose between active combinations using fitness and toxicity; the LAPC studies do not establish their relative superiority.
- 2️⃣ 💰 Nab-paclitaxel access and supportive care costs matter to continuity.

## 5️⃣ Controlled Disease or Frailty — Adjust Treatment Intensity

- Consider up to 6 months of combination induction in a conversion strategy, with reassessment and treatment adapted to tolerance and biology. <a class="cite" href="https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12546840/">[ESMO Open 2025]</a>
- Stable or responding disease → multidisciplinary decision on continued induction, de-escalation, exploration or local radiation; no universal LAPC maintenance regimen is established.
- Cumulative neuropathy → reduce or stop the responsible drug; consider fluoropyrimidine or gemcitabine continuation with a surveillance and restart plan.
**📊 NAPOLI-3: NALIRIFOX versus gemcitabine–nab-paclitaxel in metastatic disease ✨**

- **Study design:** phase 3; n=770; previously untreated metastatic disease, ECOG 0–1. NALIRIFOX on days 1 and 15 every 28 days: liposomal irinotecan 50 mg/m², oxaliplatin 60 mg/m², leucovorin 400 mg/m² and fluorouracil 2,400 mg/m² over 46 hours. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(23)01366-1">[Lancet 2023]</a> <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2024/207793s018lbl.pdf">[FDA 2024]</a>
- ⏱️ Updated OS 11.1 vs 9.2 months (HR 0.84; 95% CI 0.72–0.98). <a class="cite" href="https://ascopubs.org/doi/10.1200/JCO.2024.42.16_suppl.4136">[J Clin Oncol 2024]</a>
- ⏱️ Original PFS 7.4 vs 5.6 months (HR 0.69; 95% CI 0.58–0.83); investigator ORR 41.8% vs 36.2%. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(23)01366-1">[Lancet 2023]</a>
- ⚠️ Grade ≥3 treatment-emergent adverse events 87% vs 86%; anticipate diarrhoea, electrolyte loss and neutropenia. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(23)01366-1">[Lancet 2023]</a>
- LAPC conversion superiority remains unproven; conventional and liposomal irinotecan are not interchangeable dose for dose.
- Frailty after drainage, infection treatment and nutrition optimisation → consider single-agent treatment or symptom-focused care according to recovery and goals.

**🔑 Key insights**

- 1️⃣ Reassess before continuing combination treatment or choosing a local strategy.
- 2️⃣ 💰 Account for drug access and supportive care when adapting intensity.

## 6️⃣ Progression — Sequence by Site and Prior Exposure

- 🔁 Rising CA19-9 → check stent function and cholangitis, then investigate progression; marker change alone does not establish progression.
- Isolated local progression without metastases → consider chemoradiation or stereotactic radiotherapy if anatomy, fitness and prior treatment permit.
- Distant progression → metastatic pathway: after FOLFIRINOX consider gemcitabine-based treatment; after gemcitabine consider fluoropyrimidine-based treatment including liposomal irinotecan when appropriate.
- 📊 NAPOLI-1 after gemcitabine in metastatic disease: final OS 6.2 vs 4.2 months with liposomal irinotecan–fluorouracil/leucovorin versus fluorouracil/leucovorin (HR 0.75; 95% CI 0.57–0.99; final analysis). <a class="cite" href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30654298/">[Eur J Cancer 2019]</a>
- 💊 Daraxonrasib → US FDA indication for metastatic pancreatic adenocarcinoma after prior systemic therapy or when multiagent systemic therapy is unsuitable; LAPC induction is outside this indication. This updates the earlier trial-only RAS inhibitor position. <a class="cite" href="https://www.fda.gov/drugs/cancer-approvals/fda-approves-daraxonrasib-metastatic-pancreatic-adenocarcinoma">[FDA 2026]</a>

**🔑 Key insights**

- 1️⃣ Reassess metastatic status and prior toxicity before choosing the next line.
- 2️⃣ 💰 Verify access to later-line drugs and molecular testing early.

## 7️⃣ BRCA or PALB2 — Select Platinum When Suitable

- 🧬 Pathogenic germline BRCA1/2 or PALB2 alteration → consider platinum-containing induction; assess renal function, hearing and neuropathy before cisplatin.

**📊 Germline BRCA/PALB2 trial: Cisplatin–gemcitabine with or without veliparib ✨**

- **Study design:** randomised phase 2; 50 evaluable patients with previously untreated stage III/IV disease; cisplatin–gemcitabine with versus without veliparib. <a class="cite" href="https://ascopubs.org/doi/10.1200/JCO.19.02931">[J Clin Oncol 2020]</a>
- Cisplatin 25 mg/m² plus gemcitabine 600 mg/m² IV on days 3 and 10 every 3 weeks; trial schedule in both arms. <a class="cite" href="https://ascopubs.org/doi/10.1200/JCO.19.02931">[J Clin Oncol 2020]</a>
- 🎯 With vs without veliparib: ORR 74.1% vs 65.2%; median PFS 10.1 vs 9.7 months and OS 15.5 vs 16.4 months; response did not significantly improve. <a class="cite" href="https://ascopubs.org/doi/10.1200/JCO.19.02931">[J Clin Oncol 2020]</a>
- ⚠️ Grade 3–4 neutropenia 48% vs 30%, thrombocytopenia 55% vs 9% and anaemia 52% vs 35% with vs without veliparib; monitor renal function. Advanced-disease results do not establish a LAPC conversion rate. <a class="cite" href="https://ascopubs.org/doi/10.1200/JCO.19.02931">[J Clin Oncol 2020]</a>
- Olaparib maintenance evidence is in platinum-sensitive germline BRCA-mutated metastatic disease → routine LAPC maintenance remains unproven.

**🔑 Key insights**

- 1️⃣ A hereditary finding can guide both treatment and family counselling.
- 2️⃣ 💰 Prioritise accessible germline testing and counselling.

## 8️⃣ Other Biomarkers — Match Therapy to the Setting

- 🧬 MSI-H/dMMR → consider immunotherapy according to the indication and clinical setting; unselected checkpoint therapy is not established.
- KRAS-wild-type tumour → seek fusions including NRG1, NTRK and RET, BRAF and other actionable alterations; use RNA fusion testing where feasible.
- NRG1 fusion with progression after systemic therapy → zenocutuzumab has a US indication for advanced unresectable or metastatic pancreatic adenocarcinoma; check local access and indication. <a class="cite" href="https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/761352s010lbl.pdf">[FDA 2026]</a>
- Other matched agents or clinical trials → apply alteration-specific and treatment-setting eligibility; avoid extrapolating metastatic results to curative-intent induction.

**🔑 Key insights**

- 1️⃣ Send adequate tissue early so results can inform progression decisions.
- 2️⃣ 💰 Sequence molecular testing to preserve tissue and minimise repeat procedures.

## 9️⃣ Radiation After Induction — Define the Local-Control Goal

**📊 LAP07: Chemoradiation versus continued chemotherapy ✨**

- **Study design:** phase 3; 449 enrolled; 269 without progression after four months of gemcitabine-based induction randomised to 54 Gy with capecitabine or continued chemotherapy. <a class="cite" href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27139057/">[JAMA 2016]</a>
- ⏱️ Median OS from first randomisation: 15.2 vs 16.5 months (HR 1.03; 95% CI 0.79–1.34); erlotinib also failed to improve OS. <a class="cite" href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27139057/">[JAMA 2016]</a>
- ⏱️ PFS from first randomisation 9.9 vs 8.4 months (HR 0.78; 95% CI 0.61–1.01). <a class="cite" href="https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jama/fullarticle/2518265">[JAMA 2016]</a>
- 🎯 Among patients with progression, locoregional progression 32% vs 46%; median interval before treatment reintroduction 6.1 vs 3.7 months. <a class="cite" href="https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jama/fullarticle/2518265">[JAMA 2016]</a>
- ⚠️ Grade 3–4 nausea 5.9% vs 0%; older induction limits extrapolation to modern regimens. <a class="cite" href="https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jama/fullarticle/2518265">[JAMA 2016]</a>

**📊 CONKO-007: Chemoradiation versus continued chemotherapy ✨**

- **Study design:** phase 3; 525 enrolled, 495 received induction, including 402 FOLFIRINOX; 336 nonprogressing patients randomised after three months to chemotherapy or 50.4 Gy with gemcitabine. <a class="cite" href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40802908/">[J Clin Oncol 2025]</a>
- 🎯 Overall R0 resection 25% vs 18%; among operated patients 69.4% vs 50.0%; OS HR 0.94 (95% CI 0.75–1.17) → no significant overall R0 or survival benefit. <a class="cite" href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40802908/">[J Clin Oncol 2025]</a>
- ⚠️ Operated-only margins reflect selection; discuss radiation for local pain, threatened structures or chemotherapy intolerance, not a promised survival gain.

**🔑 Key insights**

- 1️⃣ Radiation can address local control after systemic therapy in selected patients.
- 2️⃣ 💰 Consider travel, fractionation and access to safe planning.

## 🔟 Conversion Surgery — Reconsider Resectability After Induction

- 🎯 No metastases, favourable biology, adequate reserve and plausible complete resection → expert pancreatic surgical review of baseline and current imaging.
- Persistent perivascular tissue may represent fibrosis → lack of dramatic shrinkage alone does not exclude exploration; declining CA19-9 supports but does not guarantee favourable biology.
- Selected venous reconstruction can enable surgery; arterial resection is exceptional. Selected body/tail disease may allow distal pancreatectomy with coeliac-axis resection after specialist collateral-perfusion assessment.
- Avoid planned macroscopic residual debulking; survival in resected cohorts is affected by selection. After resection, individualise further chemotherapy to exposure, recovery, pathology and toxicity.

**📊 ESPAC5: Preoperative treatment in borderline resectable disease ✨**

- **Study design:** phase 2 feasibility trial; n=90; immediate surgery versus short-course gemcitabine–capecitabine, FOLFIRINOX or chemoradiation in borderline resectable disease. This does not establish a conversion regimen for unresectable LAPC. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1016/S2468-1253(22)00348-X">[Lancet Gastroenterol Hepatol 2023]</a>
- ⏱️ One-year OS: immediate surgery 39%, gemcitabine–capecitabine 78%, FOLFIRINOX 84%, chemoradiation 60%; exploratory comparison in a small feasibility trial. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1016/S2468-1253(22)00348-X">[Lancet Gastroenterol Hepatol 2023]</a>
- ⚠️ Grade ≥3 adverse events occurred in 17 of 50 patients in the combined neoadjuvant groups. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1016/S2468-1253(22)00348-X">[Lancet Gastroenterol Hepatol 2023]</a>

**📊 PRODIGE 24: Adjuvant modified FOLFIRINOX versus gemcitabine ✨**

- **Study design:** phase 3; n=493; selected patients after macroscopic resection. <a class="cite" href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36048453/">[JAMA Oncol 2022]</a>
- ⏱️ Final median disease-free survival 21.4 vs 12.8 months (HR 0.66; 95% CI 0.54–0.82); OS 53.5 vs 35.5 months (HR 0.68; 95% CI 0.54–0.85). These are postoperative results, not LAPC induction estimates. <a class="cite" href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36048453/">[JAMA Oncol 2022]</a>
- ⚠️ Grade 3–4 adverse events 75.9% vs 52.9%. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1809775">[N Engl J Med 2018]</a>

**🔑 Key insights**

- 1️⃣ Review surgical options with baseline imaging, disease behaviour and recovery together.
- 2️⃣ 💰 Plan access to specialist review and postoperative care before exploration.

## 1️⃣1️⃣ Special Situations — Select Safe Local Treatment and Histology Pathways

**📊 SMART: MR-guided adaptive radiotherapy ✨**

- **Study design:** single-arm phase 2; n=136, mixed locally advanced and borderline disease; after ≥3 months chemotherapy without distant progression and CA19-9 ≤500 U/mL → 50 Gy in five fractions. <a class="cite" href="https://www.thegreenjournal.com/article/S0167-8140%2823%2909371-4/fulltext">[Radiother Oncol 2024]</a>
- ⏱️ Two-year OS 53.6% from diagnosis and 40.5% from radiation; 34.6% underwent surgery → mixed, selected cohort without a comparative survival estimate. <a class="cite" href="https://www.thegreenjournal.com/article/S0167-8140%2823%2909371-4/fulltext">[Radiother Oncol 2024]</a>
- ⚠️ Late grade ≥3 toxicity possibly attributed to treatment 11.5%; ulceration, bleeding and perforation are concerns. Dose requires MR-guided adaptation, organ constraints and experienced delivery. <a class="cite" href="https://www.thegreenjournal.com/article/S0167-8140%2823%2909371-4/fulltext">[Radiother Oncol 2024]</a>
- SBRT requires image guidance, motion management and stomach, duodenal and bowel constraints; active ulceration or close bowel contact may prevent safe ablative dosing.
- Irreversible electroporation and radiofrequency ablation → randomised survival benefit is unestablished; favour trials.
- Neuroendocrine and acinar cancers → distinct diagnostic and treatment pathways; do not apply this ductal pathway automatically.

**🔑 Key insights**

- 1️⃣ Match local treatment to anatomy, expertise and a specific goal.
- 2️⃣ 💰 Safe planning and follow-up are part of the cost of local treatment.

## 1️⃣2️⃣ Palliation and Follow-up — Preserve Function and Access

- 🩸 Obstructive jaundice → durable drainage and stent surveillance; fever or rigors with cholestasis → urgent assessment for cholangitis.
- Pancreatic enzyme replacement with meals and snacks, dietitian input, diabetes care and activity as feasible → support weight and treatment tolerance.
- Analgesia and early palliative care; consider coeliac plexus neurolysis for refractory visceral pain and endoscopic or surgical palliation for gastric outlet obstruction.
- Assess thromboprophylaxis during chemotherapy against bleeding risk, renal function, platelet count and interactions; plan imaging and clinical follow-up after any treatment break.
- 💰 Generic FOLFIRINOX components may improve access; account for infusion pumps, antiemetics, growth factors, nutrition and travel.
