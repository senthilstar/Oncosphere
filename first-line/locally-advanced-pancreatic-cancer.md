## Before cycle 1 — define unresectability properly

- Obtain tissue confirmation of pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma before systemic treatment. Neuroendocrine and acinar tumours require different pathways.
- Arrange pancreas-protocol imaging and chest staging, with further liver assessment for indeterminate lesions. Locally advanced disease excludes distant metastasis; a metastatic lesion changes both intent and interpretation of the evidence.
- Have an expert pancreatic multidisciplinary team assess arterial involvement and the possibility of venous reconstruction. Venous contact or occlusion alone does not settle unresectability. Distinguish anatomical unresectability from borderline anatomy and medical inoperability.
- Assess performance, weight trajectory, muscle loss, intake, pain, diabetes and baseline neuropathy. Obtain blood count, renal function, bilirubin, liver tests and albumin; poor nutrition can undermine an otherwise reasonable induction plan.
- Offer germline testing, including BRCA and PALB2, and retain tumour material for focused or broader profiling. Hereditary findings can affect counselling and later treatment discussions.
- Establish biliary drainage when required. Treat infection and support nutrition, including pancreatic enzymes when malabsorption is suspected. These interventions can change whether combination chemotherapy is tolerable.

## Choosing first line — induction with a reassessment plan

| Situation | Preferred approach | Alternatives / qualification |
|---|---|---|
| Fit for intensive induction | Modified FOLFIRINOX | Tailor to organ reserve and likely tolerance |
| FOLFIRINOX unsuitable; combination still feasible | Gemcitabine–nab-paclitaxel | Account for neuropathy, marrow reserve and access |
| Reduced reserve | Individualised lower-intensity treatment | Reassess reversible nutritional or obstructive causes |
| Disease controlled but still unresectable | Continue or adapt systemic treatment | Selected radiation for a defined local-control goal |
| Favourable response and plausible complete resection | Expert conversion-surgery assessment | Compare baseline and current scans together |
| New distant progression | Metastatic-disease pathway | Reconsider intent and subsequent systemic options |

Agree on reassessment before the first prescription. Review imaging, symptoms, tumour-marker trajectory and physiological reserve together. A marker trend after drainage may be more interpretable than the obstructed baseline value; it still cannot substitute for staging.

## The evidence — avoid metastatic extrapolation

- **FOLFIRINOX patient-level meta-analysis:** Pooled median OS 24.2 months and PFS 15.0 months from treatment initiation. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1016/S1470-2045(16)00172-8">[Lancet Oncol 2016]</a> Selected nonrandomised cohorts limit comparative interpretation.
- **LAPACT:** Gemcitabine–nab-paclitaxel: median OS 18.8 months, PFS 10.9 months; surgery in 16%. <a class="cite" href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31953079/">[Lancet Gastroenterol Hepatol 2020]</a> Single-arm evidence.
- **NEOLAP:** Sequential switch to FOLFIRINOX versus continued gemcitabine–nab-paclitaxel: conversion 43.9% versus 35.9%, not significant; OS 20.7 versus 18.5 months. <a class="cite" href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33338442/">[Lancet Gastroenterol Hepatol 2021]</a>
- **LAP07:** Chemoradiation after controlled induction: OS 15.2 versus 16.5 months; HR 1.03, without survival gain. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1001/jama.2016.4324">[JAMA 2016]</a>
- **CONKO-007:** Overall complete-margin resection 25% versus 18%, not significant; among operated patients 69.4% versus 50.0%. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1200/JCO-24-01502">[J Clin Oncol 2025]</a> OS HR 0.94, without a survival difference. <a class="cite" href="https://doi.org/10.1200/JCO-24-01502">[J Clin Oncol 2025]</a> Operated-only results require selection caution.

The induction datasets differ in patient selection, subsequent radiation and surgical exploration. They cannot establish that an upfront regimen is superior by comparing their reported medians. NEOLAP evaluates a sequential strategy after shared induction; it is not a direct initial FOLFIRINOX-versus-gemcitabine–nab-paclitaxel comparison.

## Pitfalls / pearls

- **Do not freeze the anatomical label:** persistent tissue around vessels after chemotherapy may include fibrosis. Lack of dramatic shrinkage should not automatically block expert reassessment when clinical and biological findings improve.
- **Do not promise conversion:** patients reaching surgery are selected by anatomy, response and fitness. Their outcomes do not establish what surgery would achieve for every nonprogressing patient.
- **Radiation has a specific purpose:** discuss local pain, threatened structures, chemotherapy intolerance and a potential treatment break. Absence of an overall survival gain does not erase a local-control role; local control does not prove longer survival.
- **Protect bowel safety:** radiation planning must respect adjacent stomach and duodenum. Avoid treating a more ambitious dose as intrinsically better.
- **Protect function:** cumulative neuropathy, diarrhoea, marrow suppression and weight loss can close a surgical window. Adapt treatment before disabling toxicity accumulates.
- **Supportive care is concurrent:** pancreatic enzymes, analgesia, diabetes management and stent surveillance should continue during response. Consider coeliac intervention for refractory visceral pain and assess thrombosis risk individually.

<p class="opinion">Opinion: set the next pancreatic-team review when induction is started. A response is useful only if it leads to a timely decision about continued treatment, surgery or a defined local intervention.</p>

## After first line — choose the next intent

For a responding patient, revisit resection rather than assuming chemotherapy must continue unchanged. If disease remains local but unresectable, weigh continued treatment, de-escalation or radiation against cumulative toxicity and personal goals. No universal maintenance programme has been established for this setting.

With metastatic progression, use prior drug exposure and residual fitness to choose the subsequent systemic pathway. Evidence from metastatic maintenance or salvage studies must be labelled as extrapolation when discussed for nonmetastatic disease. Revisit germline and tumour results for trial opportunities, while maintaining nutritional, drainage and symptom support.
