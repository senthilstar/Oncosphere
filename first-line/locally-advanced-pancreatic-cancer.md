**Locally advanced pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma — induction and reassessment**

## 1️⃣ Initial Assessment — Separate Local Disease from Metastatic Disease

- Confirm pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma by core biopsy before systemic therapy; acinar, neuroendocrine and other histologies follow separate pathways.
- Pancreas-protocol multiphase CT + chest staging; MRI for indeterminate liver lesions. Consider staging laparoscopy for a high risk of occult peritoneal disease before a major local intervention.
- **Record:**
  - ECOG, frailty, weight/sarcopenia, oral intake, pain and diabetes.
  - Arterial/venous contact and reconstructibility, biliary drainage, metastatic sites if present.
  - CBC, creatinine, bilirubin/LFT, albumin and CA19-9 after relief of obstruction.
- Distant disease → metastatic intent. Anatomically unresectable M0 disease → systemic induction with planned expert surgical reassessment; distinguish medical inoperability.

## 2️⃣ Resectability and Fitness — Define the Barrier Before Induction

- Describe tumour contact in degrees, vessel contour, thrombosis and length; use an experienced pancreatic radiologist and surgeon.
- Resectable anatomy generally has no major arterial contact and absent/minimal SMV–portal vein contact without deformity.
- Borderline anatomy may include limited SMA contact (≤180°), selected common hepatic artery contact permitting reconstruction, or reconstructible venous involvement.
- Locally advanced anatomy commonly includes extensive SMA/coeliac encasement (>180°) or unreconstructible SMV–portal occlusion. Body/tail exceptions and reconstructibility require centre-specific review.
- Do not use venous involvement alone to declare unresectability. Compare pretreatment/current scans; post-treatment vessel-associated tissue can be fibrosis.
- Drain jaundice, treat infection and correct malnutrition; pancreatic enzyme replacement for exocrine insufficiency. Reassess apparent frailty after these interventions.

## 3️⃣ Fit Patients — Start Modified FOLFIRINOX with a Review Date

**📊 FOLFIRINOX: Distinguish Metastatic Randomization from LAPC Evidence**

- **Study design:** PRODIGE 4/ACCORD 11 phase 3; n=342; metastatic disease, ECOG 0–1; FOLFIRINOX versus gemcitabine.
- Original IV schedule every 14 days: oxaliplatin 85 mg/m², irinotecan 180 mg/m², leucovorin 400 mg/m², 5-FU 400 mg/m² bolus then 2,400 mg/m² over 46 hours.
- Metastatic OS 11.1 vs 6.8 months (HR 0.57; 95% CI 0.45–0.73); PFS 6.4 vs 3.3 months.
- ⚠️ Grade 3–4 neutropenia 45.7%; febrile neutropenia 5.4% with FOLFIRINOX.

[Primary publication](https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1011923).

- **LAPC patient-level meta-analysis:** 315 patients contributed survival data; pooled OS 24.2 months (95% CI 21.7–26.8), PFS 15.0 months (95% CI 13.8–16.2); selected nonrandomized cohorts, not a comparative survival estimate. [Publication](https://doi.org/10.1016/S1470-2045(16)00172-8).
- Common modified induction: omit 5-FU bolus and use irinotecan 150 mg/m², preserving the other doses; individualise for reserve and local protocol. G-CSF by febrile-neutropenia risk.
- Reassess every 8–12 weeks; consider roughly 4–6 months of systemic induction before a local strategy, guided by tolerance and disease behaviour.

**🔑 Key insights**

- 1️⃣ FOLFIRINOX is a practical induction reference for fit LAPC.
- 2️⃣ Metastatic trial survival medians do not predict LAPC outcomes.
- 3️⃣ 💰 Generic components reduce acquisition cost; pumps, antiemetics and growth-factor support still need funding.

## 4️⃣ Alternative Combination — Use Gemcitabine–Nab-Paclitaxel When Appropriate

**📊 MPACT: Metastatic Evidence Supporting the Backbone**

- **Study design:** phase 3; n=861; untreated metastatic adenocarcinoma; gemcitabine–nab-paclitaxel versus gemcitabine.
- Nab-paclitaxel 125 mg/m² + gemcitabine 1,000 mg/m² IV days 1,8,15 every 28 days.
- Original OS 8.5 vs 6.7 months (HR 0.72; 95% CI 0.62–0.83); PFS 5.5 vs 3.7 months; ORR 23% vs 7%.
- ⚠️ Grade ≥3 neutropenia 38% vs 27%; neuropathy 17% vs 1%.

[Primary publication](https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1304369).

- **LAPACT:** phase 2 single-arm LAPC study; n=107; same schedule for 6 induction cycles. OS 18.8 months, PFS 10.9 months; 17 patients underwent surgery. Selected single-arm outcomes cannot rank this regimen against FOLFIRINOX. [Publication](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31953079/).
- **NEOLAP:** randomized phase 2 after shared gemcitabine–nab-paclitaxel induction; continued backbone versus sequential FOLFIRINOX did not establish a significant conversion advantage. It was not an initial-regimen comparison.
- Hold/reduce for cytopenias, neuropathy or liver dysfunction; conventional paclitaxel is not an interchangeable substitute for nab-paclitaxel.

## 5️⃣ NALIRIFOX — Label the Metastatic Extrapolation Explicitly

**📊 NAPOLI-3: NALIRIFOX versus Gemcitabine–Nab-Paclitaxel**

- **Study design:** open-label phase 3; n=770; previously untreated metastatic disease; ECOG 0–1.
- NALIRIFOX IV days 1,15 every 28 days: liposomal irinotecan 50 mg/m², oxaliplatin 60 mg/m², leucovorin 400 mg/m², 5-FU 2,400 mg/m² over 46 hours.
- 📈 OS 11.1 vs 9.2 months (HR 0.83; 95% CI 0.70–0.99); PFS 7.4 vs 5.6 months (HR 0.69; 95% CI 0.58–0.83).
- ⚠️ Grade ≥3 treatment-emergent AEs 87% vs 86%; diarrhoea, electrolyte loss and neutropenia require proactive care.

[Primary publication](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(23)01366-1).

- Metastatic evidence supports activity; no equivalent phase 3 LAPC conversion/survival result establishes superiority over modified FOLFIRINOX here.
- Liposomal and conventional irinotecan have different doses/formulations; never substitute milligram for milligram.
- Reduced reserve → attenuated doublet or gemcitabine alone; very poor irreversible performance → symptom-focused care.

## 6️⃣ Response and Maintenance — Reassess Biology Before Continuing

- Repeat pancreas-protocol CT and chest imaging with CA19-9, weight, symptoms and fitness. CA19-9 decline after drainage supports favourable biology; normal/absent secretion limits its usefulness.
- New distant disease → change intent; persistent vessel contact without progression does not automatically exclude surgical exploration.
- Controlled local disease → MDT decision: additional chemotherapy, de-escalation, conversion exploration or radiation for a defined purpose.
- Cumulative oxaliplatin/nab-paclitaxel neuropathy → reduce/stop the neurotoxic component; fluoropyrimidine or gemcitabine continuation may be pragmatic. No universal LAPC maintenance regimen or duration is established.
- Treatment breaks require a surveillance/restart plan; marker rise alone is insufficient to define radiological progression.

## 7️⃣ Chemoradiation — Use Local Control Without Promising Survival Gain

**📊 LAP07: Chemoradiation after Controlled Induction**

- **Study design:** phase 3; 449 initially randomized; 269 nonprogressing patients entered the second randomization after gemcitabine-based induction; continued chemotherapy versus capecitabine-based radiation.
- Chemoradiation: 54 Gy with capecitabine; median OS 15.2 vs 16.5 months with continued chemotherapy (HR 1.03; 95% CI 0.79–1.34).
- Local progression 32% vs 46%; no demonstrated OS advantage.
- Radiation safety includes nausea, gastric/duodenal injury and constraints from nearby bowel.

[Primary publication](https://doi.org/10.1001/jama.2016.4324).

- Selected controlled M0 disease with local pain, threatened structures or chemotherapy intolerance → discuss consolidative chemoradiation.
- Do not stop effective systemic therapy prematurely to irradiate occult systemic disease.

## 8️⃣ Conversion Surgery — Review Resectability after Induction

**📊 CONKO-007: Chemoradiation versus Continued Chemotherapy**

- **Study design:** phase 3; 525 enrolled, 495 received induction, 336 nonprogressing patients randomized; mostly FOLFIRINOX induction.
- Chemoradiation used 50.4 Gy with gemcitabine versus continued chemotherapy.
- Overall R0 resection 25% vs 18%, not statistically significant; among operated patients 69.4% vs 50.0%.
- 📈 OS HR 0.94 (95% CI 0.75–1.17); no demonstrated survival gain.
- Operated-only margins describe a selected subgroup, not the chance of benefit for every enrolled patient.

[Primary publication](https://doi.org/10.1200/JCO-24-01502).

- Favourable biology + no distant progression + plausible complete resection + adequate reserve → expert high-volume surgical review.
- Arterial resections and extensive vascular reconstruction require careful selection; exploration must have a credible oncological goal.

## 9️⃣ BRCA/PALB2 and KRAS-Wildtype Disease — Test Early for Actionable Biology

- Offer germline testing to pancreatic cancer patients, including BRCA1/2 and PALB2, with counselling and family implications.
- Pathogenic BRCA/PALB2 finding → favour an appropriate platinum-containing strategy when fit; gemcitabine–cisplatin can be considered in selected hereditary disease.
- Olaparib maintenance evidence comes from platinum-sensitive germline BRCA-mutated metastatic disease; it is not an established curative-intent LAPC maintenance standard.
- Tumour NGS, especially KRAS-wildtype → search for NTRK/RET/NRG1 fusions, BRAF and other actionable alterations; include RNA fusion testing where feasible.
- MMR/MSI testing → rare dMMR/MSI-H disease may qualify for immunotherapy in the appropriate advanced setting. Routine unselected checkpoint therapy has no established role.

## 🔟 Borderline and Resected Disease — Keep the Evidence Setting Separate

**📊 ESPAC-5: Support Preoperative Treatment in Borderline Anatomy**

- Randomized phase 2; n=90; borderline-resectable cancer; immediate surgery versus short-course gemcitabine–capecitabine, FOLFIRINOX or chemoradiation.
- Supports multidisciplinary neoadjuvant treatment in borderline disease; does not establish an unresectable LAPC conversion regimen. [Primary publication](https://doi.org/10.1016/S2468-1253(22)00348-X).

**📊 PRODIGE 24: Use Adjuvant Evidence after Resection**

- Phase 3; n=493; resected patients selected for postoperative fitness; modified FOLFIRINOX versus gemcitabine.
- Supports adjuvant modified FOLFIRINOX in suitable resected patients; do not quote its survival as an LAPC induction outcome. [Primary publication](https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1809775).

- After conversion resection, integrate preoperative exposure, recovery, pathology and residual toxicity into postoperative treatment; evidence does not mandate a fresh full adjuvant course for everyone.

## 1️⃣1️⃣ SBRT and Progression — Match Local Treatment to the Failure Pattern

- Selected nonprogressing LAPC → SBRT at an experienced centre for local control or treatment interval; fractionation/dose must respect stomach, duodenum and bowel constraints.
- Image guidance, motion management and suitable geometry matter; encasement of bowel/active ulceration may preclude safe ablative dosing. SBRT is not a proven universal survival-improving replacement for systemic therapy.
- Distant progression after FOLFIRINOX → gemcitabine-based treatment if fit; after gemcitabine-based therapy → fluoropyrimidine-based salvage, including liposomal irinotecan where appropriate. These are metastatic extrapolations.
- Acinar/neuroendocrine histology, severe frailty and hereditary disease need separate specialist pathways; avoid irreversible local procedures without a clear benefit.

## 1️⃣2️⃣ Palliation and India Practice — Preserve Nutrition and Access

- Pancreatic enzymes with meals/snacks, dietetic input and diabetes management; consider coeliac plexus intervention for refractory visceral pain.
- Stent surveillance, urgent fever pathway, gastric outlet obstruction management and individualised thrombosis prevention; early supportive care throughout treatment.
- 🇮🇳 Prioritise pancreas-protocol imaging, expert MDT review and germline BRCA/PALB2 testing over repeated low-yield expensive scans. Preserve tissue for focused molecular testing.
- Generic FOLFIRINOX components are a lower acquisition-cost option; nab-paclitaxel and liposomal irinotecan usually add cost. Include infusion-pump access, growth factors, nutrition and travel.
- Check current CDSCO indications and PM-JAY/state packages; international metastatic approvals do not establish a LAPC indication.

**🔑 Key insights**

- 1️⃣ Set the next surgical review when induction begins.
- 2️⃣ Track CA19-9, anatomy and patient recovery together.
- 3️⃣ 💰 Choose a regimen whose supportive care and full reassessment pathway are affordable.
