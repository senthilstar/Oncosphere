Historical Perspectives

Explore the evolving understanding of cancer through history, from early theories to modern advancements, shaping the way we diagnose, treat, and research this complex disease.

  1. 1940s — nitrogen mustard and Farber’s antifolate remissions in childhood leukaemia open the chemotherapy era.
  2. 1998 — trastuzumab (HER2) approved by the US FDA: targeted therapy arrives.
  3. 2001 — imatinib for CML: a pill that targets the driver (BCR-ABL).
  4. 2011 — ipilimumab: first checkpoint inhibitor to improve survival in melanoma.
  5. 2017 — first tissue-agnostic approval (pembrolizumab, MSI-H/dMMR).
  6. 2020s — antibody–drug conjugates, organ preservation with immunotherapy, ctDNA-guided care.
